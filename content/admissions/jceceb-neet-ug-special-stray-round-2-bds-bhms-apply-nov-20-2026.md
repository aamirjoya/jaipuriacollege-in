**Today, 20 November 2026, is the last day to apply for Jharkhand's Special Stray Vacancy Round-II for BDS and BHMS seats. The Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (JCECEB) opened fresh applications on 17 November, and the window closes at the end of today. If you are a NEET UG 2026 candidate who has not applied in Jharkhand yet, this is your last day to get in.**

The board issued two notices for this round on 17 November, Advt. No. JCECEB/46/26-125 and JCECEB/46/26-126. The round follows directions the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) in New Delhi issued on 16 November 2026 for state counselling. It covers seats that stayed vacant after the earlier rounds, and it is open to new candidates only.

## Three colleges, two courses, one last window

This round covers BDS and BHMS seats under the state quota at two colleges: Government Homeopathic Medical College and Hospital, Paraspani, Godda, and Awadh Dental College and Hospital, Jamshedpur. BDS seats under the All India quota are on offer at Vananchal Dental College and Hospital, Garhwa, and again at Awadh Dental College and Hospital, Jamshedpur.

The board displayed the vacant seat matrix on its website on 17 November, the same day applications opened. The notices themselves do not give vacancy numbers, so check the seat matrix on the portal before you fill your choices. You will know exactly which seats are open in each college.

## Merit list tomorrow, choices till 23 November

The schedule moves fast after today. The state merit list for this round will be published on 21 November 2026, and choice filling starts the same day. You will have three days, from 21 to 23 November, to fill and lock your choices.

- **State merit list:** 21 November 2026
- **Choice filling:** 21 to 23 November 2026
- **Provisional seat allotment letter:** 24 to 28 November 2026
- **Document verification and admission:** 25 to 28 November 2026

The provisional allotment letter will be issued between 24 and 28 November. If you get a seat, you must report to the allotted college with your original documents for verification and admission between 25 and 28 November. Keep those four days free.

## Who should apply, and who should not

This round is for new candidates. If your name is already on the state merit list released on 6 November 2026, you do not need to apply again. The board has said this clearly in its notice.

> If your name is already on the 6 November merit list, do not apply again. This round is only for candidates who have not applied yet.

The rules on age, education, residence and reservation are the same as in the board's earlier notices from August 2026. The counselling follows MCC guidelines, and the board has said it will issue fresh notices if those guidelines change. Read the notice on the <a href="https://jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in/IMP_l/1465.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JCECEB notice</a> before you apply, so you know the exact terms.

Stray vacancy rounds like this one fill the last seats of the admission season. In Maharashtra, for example, colleges had filled 99.56% of MBBS and [BDS seats](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-2026-50-mbbs-bds-seats-vacant-1310-ayush-seats/) by mid-November, leaving only a handful open. Jharkhand's round is the same kind of final mop-up, and it is aimed at students who missed the earlier windows.

## How to apply in five steps

1. Open the <a href="https://jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JCECEB website</a> and find the notice for Special Stray Vacancy Round-II, Advt. 125 and 126, dated 17 November 2026.
2. Read the vacant seat matrix displayed on 17 November and shortlist the colleges you want.
3. Fill the online application form for new candidates and upload the documents the form asks for.
4. Submit the form and save the confirmation page or receipt.
5. Check the website on 21 November for the state merit list, then fill your choices between 21 and 23 November.

Keep your NEET UG 2026 scorecard and your Class 10 and 12 marksheets ready before you start the form. A slow start on the last day is the most common reason students miss a deadline.

One warning: the notices give dates only. They do not state a closing time for today, and they do not mention an application fee. Do not wait for the evening to apply. Fill the form in the morning and keep the confirmation safe.
