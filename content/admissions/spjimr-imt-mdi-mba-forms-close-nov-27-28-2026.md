**SPJIMR Mumbai and IMT Ghaziabad close their MBA applications today, November 27, 2026. MDI Gurgaon closes tomorrow, November 28, at 5 pm.** All three institutes accept [CAT 2026](/admissions/cat-2026-2-84-lakh-registrations-nov-29-exam/) scores, and the CAT exam is on November 29. The forms close before the exam, so anyone who waits for the test first will miss the chance to apply.

This is the last big [MBA admissions](/admissions/) deadline cluster before CAT 2026. [FMS Delhi](/admissions/fms-delhi-mba-admission-2027-29-nov-25-deadline/) already shut its form on November 26 after a one-day extension. SPJIMR and IMT shut today. MDI is the only one of the three still open tomorrow, and only till 5 pm.

## Three deadlines at a glance

| Institute | Last date | Form fee | Scores accepted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SPJIMR Mumbai | November 27, 2026 | Rs 2,500 (one programme), Rs 4,000 (both) | CAT 2026, GMAT |
| IMT Ghaziabad | November 27, 2026 | Rs 3,500 plus bank charges | CAT 2026, XAT 2027, GMAT |
| MDI Gurgaon | November 28, 2026, 5 pm | Rs 3,000 including GST | CAT 2026, GMAT for PGDM-IB |

## SPJIMR Mumbai: 360 seats across two programmes

SPJIMR runs two programmes for the 2027-29 batch. The PGDM has 240 seats for Indian nationals and 36 seats for foreign nationals, OCIs and PIOs. The PGDM (BM) has 120 seats. One application form covers both programmes, so you can apply to either or both in a single submission.

The fee is Rs 2,500 for one programme and Rs 4,000 if you apply to both. You need a bachelor's degree with at least 50 per cent marks. SPJIMR accepts CAT 2026 or GMAT scores, with GMAT scores taken between January 1, 2024 and December 10, 2026 counting as valid.

The form opened on September 15, 2026. After it closes, SPJIMR will release a profile-based shortlist in late December 2026 or early January 2027. The programme starts in June 2027. Apply on the <a href="https://pgdmadmissions.spjimr.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official SPJIMR admissions portal</a>.

## IMT Ghaziabad: one form for three campuses

IMT Ghaziabad offers five two-year, full-time residential PGDM programmes for the 2027-29 batch: PGDM, PGDM Marketing, PGDM Financial Management, PGDM Banking and Financial Services, and the Dual Country Programme. One common application covers the Ghaziabad, Nagpur and Hyderabad campuses.

The fee is Rs 3,500 plus bank charges. IMT accepts CAT 2026, XAT 2027 and GMAT scores. If you are applying with GMAT, you can submit the score by January 15, 2027. The selection process runs through a Critical Thinking Test and Personal Interview, with slot booking in the first week of January 2027 and interviews starting January 8 across 22 cities.

Apply on the <a href="https://www.imt.edu/admissions/pgdm/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official IMT admissions page</a>. The form has been open since the last week of August 2026.

> The forms close before the exam, and that order catches first-time applicants each year.

## MDI Gurgaon: tomorrow, 5 pm sharp

MDI Gurgaon gives you one more day. Its unified form covers five programmes: PGDM, PGDM-HRM, PGDM-IB, PGDM-Business Analytics and PGDM-BFS. The same form also covers the PGDM programme at MDI Murshidabad.

The fee is Rs 3,000 including GST. If you apply to both the Gurgaon and Murshidabad campuses, the fee is Rs 3,590. NRI and international candidates pay Rs 8,496. You need at least 50 per cent marks in both Class 10 and Class 12. Final-year students can apply if they finish their degree by June 30, 2027.

MDI shortlists on CAT 2026 scores, then runs a written analysis test, group discussion and personal interview between February and April 2027. Apply on the <a href="https://www.mdi.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MDI website</a> before 5 pm tomorrow.

## FMS is shut, IIFT is still open

If you miss today, not every door is closed. FMS Delhi shut on November 26 after extending its deadline by a day from November 25. IIFT extended its MBA (IB) deadline to December 8, 2026. XLRI Jamshedpur closes on December 6 through XAT 2027, and IMI Delhi stays open till December 19. But for SPJIMR, IMT and MDI, there is no second chance in this cycle. The portals close on the dates above.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the last date to apply for SPJIMR PGDM 2027-29?

November 27, 2026. Apply on the official portal pgdmadmissions.spjimr.org. One form covers both the PGDM and PGDM (BM) programmes.

### What is the last date for MDI Gurgaon MBA admission 2027?

November 28, 2026, at 5 pm IST. The application fee is Rs 3,000 including GST, and one unified form covers all five PGDM programmes.

### Can I apply to IMT Ghaziabad with XAT 2027 scores?

Yes. IMT Ghaziabad accepts CAT 2026, XAT 2027 and GMAT scores. GMAT scores can be submitted by January 15, 2027.

### Do I need my CAT 2026 score before applying?

No. The forms close before the exam on November 29, 2026, so you apply with your CAT registration details. The score is used later, at the shortlist stage.
