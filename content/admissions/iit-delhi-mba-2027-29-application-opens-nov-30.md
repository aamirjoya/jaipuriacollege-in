**The Department of Management Studies (DMS) at IIT Delhi opens MBA applications tomorrow, November 30, 2026. The form stays open till January 25, 2027, and admission is based on CAT 2026 scores. The two-year MBA costs Rs 12 lakh in tuition.**

[CAT 2026](/admissions/cat-2026-2-84-lakh-registrations-nov-29-exam/) is on November 29, a day before the DMS form opens. About 2.84 lakh candidates have registered for the exam, which IIM Indore is conducting in three sessions across 170 cities. Anyone who sat for CAT can apply to DMS without waiting for the result. The department shortlists on the CAT score and calls candidates for interviews at IIT Delhi from February 22 to 26, 2027. Final results come in the first week of May 2027.

## Key dates for DMS IIT Delhi MBA 2027

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Online application opens | November 30, 2026 |
| Last date to apply | January 25, 2027 |
| Personal interview | February 22 to 26, 2027 |
| Admission results | First week of May 2027 |

All four dates are tentative, and the department page says so. Check the official page before you pay the fee.

## Who can apply

You need a bachelor's degree of at least three years after Class 12, from a recognised university. The degree must carry 60 per cent marks in aggregate, or a CGPA of 6.00 on a 10-point scale. SC, ST and PwD candidates need 55 per cent, or 5.50 CGPA.

Final-year students can apply too. If you get selected, you join provisionally and submit proof of completing the degree by the date DMS sets.

Only CAT 2026 scores count for shortlisting. There is no separate DMS entrance test. The interview is held at IIT Delhi, in person.

## What the MBA costs

DMS lists the total tuition fee as Rs 12 lakh for the full two-year programme, paid in four equal instalments, one per semester. For the 2026-28 batch, the per-semester break-up is Rs 3,00,000 in tuition, plus Rs 22,400 in other charges and Rs 8,600 as institute residence fee. The department page lists only the other charges and residence fee for SC, ST and PwD candidates.

The exact amount and payment mode for the new batch will appear in the offer of admission. The department links a detailed fee break-up on the <a href="https://home.iitd.ac.in/curriculum.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IIT Delhi curriculum page</a>.

## How to apply

1. Take CAT 2026 on November 29. The score is the first filter, so there is no DMS form without a CAT registration.
2. When the form opens on November 30, apply on the <a href="https://dms.iitd.ac.in/admission-mba/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official DMS admissions page</a>. Keep your CAT registration number and graduation marks ready.
3. Pay the application fee online. The department says the processing fee is non-refundable.
4. Wait for the shortlist. DMS calls shortlisted candidates for interviews at IIT Delhi between February 22 and 26, 2027.
5. Results come in the first week of May 2027.

The MBA (Telecom) programme follows the same window. Its application dates are listed as November 30, 2026 to January 25, 2027.
