**Only 50 MBBS and BDS seats are still empty in Maharashtra. The state has filled 11,431 of 11,481 seats for 2026-27, a fill rate of 99.56 per cent, after nine rounds of counselling. And for students who missed a medical or dental seat, there is still a real chance left: the State CET Cell has released a provisional seat matrix with 1,310 BAMS, BHMS and BUMS seats across 208 colleges.**

The numbers come from a press release the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test (CET) Cell issued on November 18. It brings one of the longest medical admission seasons in the state to a close, with three centralised rounds, three stray vacancy rounds and three special stray vacancy rounds.

## Government colleges are full; 30 MBBS and 20 BDS seats stay empty

Government medical and dental colleges in Maharashtra have filled every single seat this year. MBBS admissions stand at 8,834 of 8,864 seats, a fill rate of 99.66 per cent. BDS admissions stand at 2,597 of 2,617 seats. The 50 seats that remain are 30 MBBS and 20 BDS seats, all of them in private colleges.

The last round was the third special stray vacancy round. Its selection list came out earlier this week, and the last date for candidates to join their allotted colleges was November 16. The CET Cell has not fixed a date for any more MBBS or BDS rounds yet. It has said further rounds will be announced as required, so students who are still waiting should watch the official portal instead of trusting WhatsApp forwards or coaching centre rumours.

> "Further counselling rounds to fill the remaining vacant seats will be announced as required."
>
> Maharashtra State CET Cell

## A new college joined the matrix at the last minute

One college made a late entry into this year's [Maharashtra NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) and changed the final numbers. Ideal Institute of Medical Science at Wada in Palghar joined the process with 50 MBBS seats, split into 38 general category seats and 12 women category seats.

The institute had approval from the National Medical Commission but was still waiting for affiliation from the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences in Nashik. Seats allotted at this college depend on that affiliation coming through. Students who took a seat there should confirm the affiliation status with the college before paying fees.

## 1,310 AYUSH seats are open across 208 colleges

The bigger opening right now is in AYUSH courses. The CET Cell has released a provisional seat matrix for BAMS, BHMS and BUMS courses in government, government-aided, private and minority colleges. The matrix shows 1,310 balance seats in all, against a total intake of 16,427 seats across 208 colleges.

The course-wise split is:

- **BAMS:** 586 balance seats, 73 in government and government-aided colleges and 513 in private and minority colleges
- **BHMS:** 712 balance seats, 12 in government colleges and 700 in private and minority colleges
- **BUMS:** 12 balance seats, 1 in a government-aided college and 11 in private and minority colleges

BHMS has the largest number of open seats, and most of them are in private colleges. BAMS has the best government-college opening, with 73 seats still on the table. The matrix is provisional, which means the seat position can change if colleges send fresh information to the CET Cell. Check the latest version on the portal before you fill your choices.

## How to check the AYUSH seat matrix

- Go to the <a href="https://medical2026.mahacet.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official NEET UG counselling portal</a> at medical2026.mahacet.org
- Look for the provisional seat matrix link for BAMS, BHMS and BUMS courses
- Download the PDF and filter it by course, college type and category
- Note the balance seats in government colleges first, then compare private college options
- Keep your documents and fee payment ready, because AYUSH choice filling windows are usually short

Students who took part in the [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray vacancy round 3](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-3-list-103-seats-report-today-nov-16/) already know how fast these rounds move. The third special stray round gave candidates barely a day between choice filling and the joining deadline. AYUSH rounds are likely to move at the same speed.

## What this means for NEET UG 2026 aspirants

Maharashtra has nearly closed its MBBS and BDS admissions for this year. With 99.56 per cent of seats filled and government colleges at full strength, the 50 vacant seats are the only medical and dental opening left, and they will go fast if another round is announced.

AYUSH is where the real opportunity sits now. More than 1,300 seats are open, and the demand for BAMS and BHMS seats is lower than for MBBS, so cut-offs are likely to be within reach for many candidates who missed a medical seat. Students who qualified in NEET UG 2026 and have not taken admission anywhere should look at the matrix today, shortlist colleges by fees and location, and stay ready for the choice filling notice.

For students eyeing other medical courses, the [NEET UG Round 6](/admissions/neet-ug-round-6-bds-bsc-nursing-registration-nov-17-2026/) counselling for BDS and BSc Nursing seats is also running at the national level. Check both the state and national portals, because a seat in one round can close your options in the other.
