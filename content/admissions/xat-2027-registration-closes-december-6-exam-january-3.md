**MBA aspirants have 23 days left to register for XAT 2027. The form closes on Sunday, December 6, 2026, and the exam is on Sunday, January 3, 2027.** XLRI Jamshedpur runs the test for the Xavier Association of Management Institutes, and more than 250 B-schools across India accept the score.

Registration opened on July 15, 2026, on the official portal, xatonline.in. One form covers both the XAT test and the XLRI programmes you want to apply to, so you do not need to fill a separate form for XLRI. The fee is Rs 2,300 for Indian candidates, plus Rs 200 for each XLRI programme you add. The fee is not refunded once paid.

The timing suits [CAT 2026](/admissions/cat-2026-2-84-lakh-registrations-nov-29-exam/) takers well. CAT is on November 29, a week before the XAT form closes, so you can finish one exam and then decide on XAT with a clear head. [SNAP 2026](/admissions/snap-2026-registration-closes-november-25-tests-december-13-19-26/) closes earlier, on November 25, while [FMS Delhi MBA admission](/admissions/fms-delhi-mba-admission-2027-29-nov-25-deadline/) also ends on November 25.

## What is new in XAT 2027

XLRI has announced four changes for this cycle, and the biggest one helps every candidate. You can now leave 12 questions unanswered without any penalty. Last year the limit was 8.

The PGDM in Human Resource Management at XLRI Jamshedpur will take 240 students this year, up from 180. Seventeen new institutes have also joined as XAT associate institutes, so the score now opens doors at more B-schools than before.

## XAT 2027 dates to remember

Keep these dates in front of you while you plan. The admit card date is still marked tentative by XLRI.

- **December 6, 2026:** Last date to register for XAT 2027 and XLRI programmes
- **December 20, 2026 (tentative):** Admit card download begins
- **January 3, 2027:** XAT 2027 exam, 2 PM to 5 PM, single slot
- **Late January 2027:** Result expected, about three weeks after the test

## The paper: around 95 questions in 3 hours

XLRI has not yet published the section-wise split for 2027, but the test runs for three hours with no sectional time limits. You can move between sections in any order and switch back when you want. A scientific calculator is available on screen.

Last year's paper had 95 questions in two parts. Part 1 gave 170 minutes for Verbal Ability and Logical Reasoning (26 questions), Decision Making (21 questions) and Quantitative Aptitude and Data Interpretation (28 questions). Part 2 gave 10 minutes for General Knowledge (20 questions).

> The first 12 questions you leave blank cost you nothing. The 13th one costs 0.10 marks. That single rule should shape how you attempt the paper.

## Marking: 12 questions you can skip for free

The marking scheme rewards careful attempts and punishes blind guessing. A correct answer in Part 1 earns 1 mark. A wrong answer loses 0.25 marks. The first 12 questions you leave unanswered across Part 1 cost nothing. Each one after that costs 0.10 marks.

General Knowledge works differently. It has no negative marking, and it does not count in your overall percentile or cut-off. XLRI uses the GK score only at the final selection stage. So in Part 1, skip a question you cannot solve rather than guess. In GK, answer everything, since a wrong answer costs nothing there.

## Who can apply for XAT 2027

You need a bachelor's degree of at least three years in any subject from a recognised university. Final-year students can apply too, as long as their final examinations finish by June 11, 2027. XLRI sets no minimum percentage and no age limit for XAT, though individual B-schools may set their own conditions. NRI, foreign, OCI and PIO candidates can apply to XLRI through GMAT instead of XAT.

## How to apply in 5 steps

1. Go to the <a href="https://xatonline.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official XAT portal</a> and click Register. Enter your name, email ID and mobile number carefully. You cannot change them later.
2. Verify your email from the link XLRI sends. Your login details will arrive by email.
3. Fill in your academic and work details, choose your test cities, and select the XLRI programmes you want.
4. Upload your photograph and signature in the sizes the portal asks for.
5. Pay the fee online and submit. The form saves at every stage, so you can finish it in more than one sitting. After submission, changes are possible only through the "Any Query? Ask Us" option on your dashboard.

Apply on the <a href="https://applications.xatonline.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official XAT 2027 application page</a> well before December 6. The last two days of any big exam form see heavy traffic, and a failed payment at 11 PM can cost you the whole attempt.

## What XAT 2027 opens for you

XLRI shortlists candidates for interview on XAT scores alone for its PGDM in Business Management and Human Resource Management programmes. The final list weighs your XAT score, academic record, work experience, interview and group discussion. Beyond XLRI, the score is accepted by XAMI member institutes such as LIBA Chennai, XIM University Bhubaneswar and XIME Bengaluru, plus around 140 associate institutes including IMT Ghaziabad, Goa Institute of Management, TAPMI Manipal and MICA Ahmedabad.
