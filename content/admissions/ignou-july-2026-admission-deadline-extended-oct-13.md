**The IGNOU July 2026 admission deadline is now October 13, 2026. Indira Gandhi National Open University extended the last date for fresh admission applications, announcing the change on October 1. Students get 11 extra days to finish their forms for distance and online programmes.**

This is one of the biggest admission windows still open this month. IGNOU is India's largest open university, and its July session draws lakhs of applicants every year, from fresh Class 12 pass-outs to working professionals earning a degree alongside their job.

## The extension covers ODL and online programmes

The revised deadline applies to all notified programmes offered through Open and Distance Learning (ODL) and online modes. That includes undergraduate, postgraduate, PG diploma and diploma programmes listed in the university's July 2026 admission notice.

Two categories are left out. Semester-based programmes and certificate programmes are not covered by the extension, so students applying to those must follow the dates in the original notice. If you are unsure which category your course falls in, check the programme list on the official admission portal before paying.

## Who can apply, and what the prospectus says

Eligibility is programme-specific. Each course lists its own qualifying marks and background in the Common Prospectus, and the portal will not let you submit a form for a programme whose conditions you do not meet. Read the prospectus entry for your course before you register, because eligibility rules and required certificates differ widely across programmes.

Reserved category and PwD applicants should keep their certificates ready. The application asks for category and disability certificates where applicable, and a missing certificate at upload time is one of the most common reasons forms get stuck.

## How to apply before October 13

IGNOU accepts applications only online, through two official portals: the <a href="https://ignouadmission.samarth.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ODL admission portal</a> for distance programmes and the <a href="https://ignouiop.samarth.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">online programmes portal</a> for fully online courses. Links to both are also posted on <a href="https://ignou.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IGNOU's official website</a>.

Here is the process, step by step:

- Open the portal for your mode of study and register as a new applicant.
- Enter your personal and contact details, then verify your email and mobile number.
- Select your programme, Regional Centre and Study Centre.
- Upload a recent photo, signature, valid ID proof and your qualification certificates and marksheets.
- Pay the Rs 300 application fee plus the programme fee using UPI, net banking or a credit or debit card.
- Download the confirmation page and keep a printed copy for your records.

Keep your documents scanned before you begin. Our [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) covers the papers most institutions ask for, and the same list will get you through the IGNOU upload page without last-minute stress.

## Fee and refund rules you should know

The Rs 300 application fee is paid along with the programme fee at the time of application. If your admission is not confirmed, the fee is refunded. Once admission is confirmed, the rules tighten: cancelling after confirmation leads to a deduction of 15 percent of the programme fee, capped at Rs 2,000. No refund is available after 60 days from the closing date.

Track your application status on the portal until the confirmation arrives. If something is flagged, your Regional Centre will raise a query, and the earlier you respond, the smoother the process runs.

> Eleven extra days sounds comfortable, but IGNOU's portal slows down badly in the last two days of every deadline. Fill the form this week, not on October 13.

## Why this extension matters right now

October is deadline season, and students juggling multiple applications can easily let one slip. The [GATE 2027 registration](/admissions/gate-2027-registration-last-date-oct-5/) closes on October 5, and other exam cut-offs land in the same week, so calendars are already packed. IGNOU's extension to October 13 gives breathing room to students who kept distance learning as their backup plan.

Missing documents remain the biggest reason applications stall. A blurry photo, a missing category certificate or a name mismatch between your ID and your marksheet can push you into a query cycle that eats up days.
