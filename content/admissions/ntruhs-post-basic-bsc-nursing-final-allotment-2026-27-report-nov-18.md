**Dr NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada, has released the final allotment list for Post Basic BSc Nursing 2026-27.** The 3rd and final phase college-wise allotment covers 47 colleges across Andhra Pradesh, and every candidate who got a seat must report to the allotted college by 3 PM on November 18, 2026.

This is the last round of counselling for the two-year Post Basic BSc Nursing course under the Competent Authority Quota. The university filled 440 vacant seats in this phase, and there is no later round in the schedule. If your name is on the list, the next two days decide whether you keep the seat.

## 440 seats were on offer in the final phase

The university opened this phase to fill seats that stayed empty after the second round. As per the official notice dated November 7, 2026, the vacancies were 381 unfilled seats from phase two and 59 seats left by candidates who did not join after phase two. That added up to 440 seats.

Candidates exercised their web options from 8 PM on November 7 to 2 PM on November 9, 2026. The allotment list released today is the result of that process. It is published college by college as a PDF on the official notification site, <a href="https://drntr.uhsap.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">drntr.uhsap.in</a>.

Each entry shows the registration ID, date of birth, score, name, gender, category, local area, service type and counselling phase. The registration IDs in this round begin with "UG26CQ03". Use Ctrl+F in the PDF to find your ID fast.

## Report by 3 PM on November 18 or lose the seat

The reporting deadline is firm. Candidates who received a seat must reach their allotted college on or before 3 PM on November 18, 2026, to complete the admission process. Carry your original certificates, allotment letter and fee receipt. Download the allotment letter after paying the university fee through the payment gateway, and keep a printout ready.

> "Candidates have to report at the allotted college on or before 3 PM on 18 November 2026. Classes have already commenced from 22 October 2026."

Classes for the course started on October 22, 2026. That means allotted candidates will join classes that are already running, so there is no time to waste after reporting. Contact your allotted college before you travel to confirm the reporting desk, the documents they want and the fee you have to pay at the time of joining.

The university also sends an SMS to the registered mobile number of candidates who get a seat. If you exercised web options in this phase, check your phone and the official website both.

## How to check your allotment in the list

Follow these steps to find your name and college:

- Open the official NTRUHS notification site at <a href="https://drntr.uhsap.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">drntr.uhsap.in</a>.
- Go to the Notifications section and find Post Basic BSc Nursing 2 YDC 2026-27.
- Click the link for the 3rd and Final Phase College-Wise Allotments.
- Open the allotment PDF and press Ctrl+F.
- Type your registration ID, which begins with UG26CQ03.
- Check your name, score and category, then note the college heading above your row.

Read every detail twice before you leave home. A wrong college name or a missed document can waste a full day, and with the deadline only two days away, there is no room for a second trip.

## No more rounds after this one

This phase is called final for a reason. The notification is titled "College-wise Allotments of 3rd & Final Phase Counselling" and it does not mention any later round. Candidates who did not get a seat in this phase should watch the university website for stray vacancy notices, but the regular counselling for Post Basic BSc Nursing 2026-27 ends here. The [NEET PG Round 2 provisional result](/results/neet-pg-round-2-provisional-result-nov-16-final-nov-17/) also came out today, with its final result due on November 17, and candidates allotted in the [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray round 3](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-3-list-103-seats-report-today-nov-16/) have to report today as well. For more updates, see our [admissions](/admissions/) page.

Post Basic BSc Nursing is a two-year degree course for nurses who already hold a GNM diploma. It opens the door to senior staff nurse posts and teaching roles. For the candidates who got a seat in this final phase, the course is now within reach. The only step left is to report on time.
