**The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has released the round 1 mock seat allotment for Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) admissions 2026, and every registered candidate can now see an early hint of which college they may get.** The Karnataka KEA D.Pharm mock allotment came out on October 27, 2026 on the official KEA portal. It is not the final result. It is a trial run based on your merit rank and the college options you submitted, and it tells you where you stand before the real allotment.

If you applied for D.Pharm through KEA counselling, open your mock allotment today and read it carefully. The college shown there is indicative and can change in the final allotment. Still, it is the best clue you have right now about your chances.

## What the mock allotment actually means

A mock allotment is a practice round. KEA runs your rank and your option list through the same seat allotment logic used for the final round, then shows you the result.

Think of it this way. If the mock allotment shows a college you are happy with, your options are working well. If it shows a college far below your first choices, or no seat at all, your rank may not be enough for your top options in this round.

One thing to keep clear: a mock allotment does not reserve a seat for you. No fee payment and no college reporting happen on the basis of this list. Only the final allotment creates a real seat.

## How to check your KEA D.Pharm mock allotment

Keep your login details ready before you start.

- Visit the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA website</a> and open the Diploma in Pharmacy admission section
- Find the link for the D.Pharm 2026 round 1 mock seat allotment result
- Enter your login details if the portal asks for them
- Check the college and course shown against your name and rank
- Download or save a copy of the allotment details for your records

Read every line of the allotment carefully. Check the college name, the course, and the category under which the seat is shown. Note down anything that looks wrong, so you can compare it with the final allotment later.

## The option change window has closed: what happens now

KEA had allowed candidates to add, delete or modify their college options, and to submit objections to the mock allotment, until 1 pm on October 28, 2026. That deadline has now passed. Candidates who used the window will have their updated options considered for the final round. Everyone else will go into the final allotment with the options already on record.

This is the part many students miss. After the mock round, the final allotment is prepared from the frozen option list. There is no second chance to reorder choices once the final result is out, unless KEA opens a fresh round later. If you reviewed your mock allotment and fixed your options before the deadline, your part is done for now.

> The mock allotment is not a seat. It is a mirror that shows you where your rank and choices stand today, so the final list does not catch you by surprise.

## What to do while you wait for the final allotment

Use this waiting time well. First, keep your documents ready. Admission reporting usually needs your marksheets, rank details, category certificate if applicable, and identity proof. Our [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) covers what most colleges ask for at the counter.

Second, check the official KEA portal once a day. KEA has not announced the final round 1 allotment date yet, and the notice will appear on the portal first. Do not trust allotment PDFs forwarded in WhatsApp groups. Fake lists do the rounds every counselling season, and the portal is the only source that counts.

Third, watch other pharmacy rounds too. The [Maharashtra M.Pharm CAP round 4 allotment](/admissions/maharashtra-m-pharm-cap-round-4-allotment-2026/) shows how fast later rounds move, and you can follow all counselling updates in our [admissions](/admissions/) section. When the KEA final list drops, the fee payment and reporting window is usually short.

## Key dates for KEA D.Pharm round 1

- **Mock allotment released:** October 27, 2026
- **Last date to modify options and submit objections:** October 28, 2026, 1 pm (closed)
- **Final round 1 allotment result:** To be announced by KEA
- **Official portal:** cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea

A good mock allotment is a strong sign, but the final list is what decides your college. Check the portal, keep your papers ready, and act fast when the final allotment is published.
