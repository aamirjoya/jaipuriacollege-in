**The Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, has released the [Kerala NEET PG 2026](/results/kerala-neet-pg-state-merit-list-2026-complaints-oct-29/) round 2 final allotment result today, 26 November 2026.** Candidates who got a seat in this second phase of counselling can check the allotment list on the official website and download their allotment memo from the candidate portal. Reporting to the allotted college starts today and ends on 30 November at 5 PM.

The allotment list is out as a PDF on the official website. It names the candidates who got seats, along with the college, the course, and the allotment category. The memo is separate. You need to log in to the candidate portal to get your own memo, and you must carry a printout of it when you report.

> "Candidates must report to their respective allotted colleges between November 26 and November 30, 2026, before 5:00 PM, to complete the admission formalities."
>
> CEE Kerala notice, 26 November 2026

## Important dates for Kerala NEET PG round 2

| What happens | Date |
|---|---|
| Final allotment result released | 26 November 2026 |
| Allotment memo available for download | 26 November 2026 |
| Physical reporting at allotted colleges | 26 to 30 November 2026 |
| Reporting deadline | 30 November 2026, before 5 PM |

CEE published a provisional list on 23 November and took complaints till 1 PM on 24 November. The final list came out after those complaints were checked. If your name was on the provisional list, check the final list again. A few names can change between the two lists.

The reporting window runs five days, 26 to 30 November. That is enough time to arrange documents, but a last-day rush at the college counter is common in every round. Candidates who travel from another district should plan the trip a day early if they can.

Kerala runs its own state counselling separately from the all-India MCC rounds. The all-India [NEET PG 2026 round 2 final allotment](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-final-allotment-20707-seats-report-nov-25/) covered 20,707 seats, and its reporting window closed on 25 November. Kerala candidates follow the state schedule in the table above, not the MCC one.

## How to download the allotment memo

1. Open <a href="https://cee.kerala.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cee.kerala.gov.in</a>, the official CEE Kerala website.
2. Go to the PG Medical 2026 section.
3. Click Login and enter your application number and password.
4. Open the Allotment Memo link.
5. Download the memo and take a clear printout.

Check every detail on the memo before you print it. The college name, course, and category on it decide where you report. If anything looks wrong, raise it with CEE Kerala before you travel.

## Documents to carry for reporting

- Allotment memo, printed
- Data sheet of PG Medical 2026 issued by CEE Kerala
- Proof of date of birth: SSLC certificate, senior secondary certificate, passport, or birth certificate
- MBBS degree certificate or pass certificate
- House surgency (CRRI) completion certificate
- NEET PG 2026 score sheet
- Original transfer certificate from the institution you last attended
- Relieving order from the institution you last worked at, if you are in government service
- Originals of all documents and certificates you uploaded while applying

Carry originals for verification. Keep a set of photocopies with you as well. The college will verify the originals at the counter, and you pay the admission fee at the allotted college during admission.

## If you got a better college in this round

Candidates who moved to a new college in phase 2 must join the new college. The seat from phase 1 will not stay open for you. This matters if you were happy with your earlier college and were hoping to keep it as a backup. The new allotment replaces the old one, so report to the college named in your latest memo.

Do not wait till the last day to report. Colleges see a rush on the final day, and a missing paper can mean a wasted trip. Go early, finish the verification, and keep copies of your allotment memo, fee receipt, and admission papers for your records.

## If your name is not on the list

If your name is not on this list, counselling is not over for you yet. CEE Kerala posts notices for later rounds on the same website, and stray vacancy rounds follow when seats stay empty after reporting. Keep checking cee.kerala.gov.in for the next notice instead of trusting forwards on social media. Do not pay anyone who promises a seat in exchange for money. Allotment happens only through the official counselling process.
