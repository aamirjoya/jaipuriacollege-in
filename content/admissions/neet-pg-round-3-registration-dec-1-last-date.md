**The Medical Counselling Committee opened NEET PG 2026 Round 3 registration on 26 November at 6 PM. Choice filling starts today, 27 November. You can register and pay the fee till 1 December, and the Round 3 result will come out on 4 December.**

This round covers the 50% All India Quota, deemed universities, central universities, DNB seats, and Armed Forces Medical Services seats. It is the last round in which you can move to a better seat. After this, only the stray vacancy round remains, and that runs from 16 to 24 December.

## Round 3 dates: the full schedule

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration and fee payment | 26 November to 1 December 2026 |
| Fee payment closes | 1 December, 3 PM |
| Choice filling | 27 November to 2 December 2026 |
| Choice locking | 1 December, 4 PM to 2 December, 10 AM |
| Seat allotment processing | 2 to 3 December 2026 |
| Round 3 result | 4 December 2026 |
| Reporting and joining | 5 to 12 December 2026 |

The PwD portal stays open till 6 PM on 28 November for the Primary Assessment Board, and till 6 PM on 1 December for the Appellate Board. If you need a disability certificate for this round, finish that step before the 28 November deadline.

## Who can register in Round 3

Fresh candidates who have not registered so far can register now. Candidates who took part in Round 1 or Round 2 and want a better seat can also take part. If you already registered in an earlier round, do not register again. Log in with the same details you used before.

One group needs to be careful. MCC issued a notice on 23 November about resignation from Round 1 or Round 2 seats. Read that notice on the MCC portal before you decide anything about a seat you already hold. The notice is listed under News and Events on the PG Medical Counselling page.

The registration fee for All India Quota and central university seats is Rs 1,000 for general category candidates. A refundable security deposit also applies, and the amount depends on the type of seat you choose. Check the fee details on the portal before you pay.

## Round 3 has one rule you cannot ignore

There is no float option in Round 3. In the earlier rounds, you could accept a seat and still try for a better one. That option is gone now. The seat you get in this round is the seat you must join.

Joining is compulsory, and a seat you accept here cannot be resigned. You also cannot take part in any later round after joining a Round 3 seat. That makes the choice list more important than in any earlier round.

> Fill choices only for colleges and courses you are ready to join. A seat you do not want is worse than no seat in this round, because you cannot give it back.

Look at the [Round 2 final allotment](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-final-allotment-20707-seats-report-nov-25/) to see where the cutoffs landed last time. In that round, 20,707 seats were allotted, and the last All India Open rank admitted was 1,33,639. That gives you a rough idea of where your rank stands.

## How to register and fill choices

- Go to the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/pg-medical-counselling/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC PG Medical Counselling page</a>.
- Click on New Registration for NEET PG Counselling 2026 under the Candidate Activity Board.
- Register with a valid email ID and mobile number, or log in if you registered in an earlier round.
- Fill the form and pay the fee before 3 PM on 1 December.
- Fill your choices between 27 November and 2 December, with your most wanted college and course first.
- Lock your choices between 4 PM on 1 December and 10 AM on 2 December. If you do not lock them, the system locks your last saved list on its own.

Pay the fee on 26 or 27 November if you can. Payment closes at 3 PM on 1 December, and a failed bank payment on the last afternoon can cost you the whole round. The MCC portal also hosts a Candidate User Guide for NEET PG Counselling 2026, which walks through each step.

## Documents to keep ready

- NEET PG admit card and scorecard or rank letter issued by NBE
- MBBS first, second, and third professional exam mark sheets
- MBBS degree certificate or provisional certificate
- Internship completion certificate
- Class 10 certificate or birth certificate as proof of date of birth
- Valid photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, or driving licence
- Category certificate, if applicable
- PwD certificate, if any
- NRI or OCI documents in original, if applicable

You will need the allotment letter from your candidate login at the time of reporting. Download it as soon as the result is out on 4 December and keep a printout ready.

## The seat matrix is still awaited

MCC had not published the Round 3 seat matrix at the time of writing. The matrix shows how many seats are open in each college and course, and it decides how you should order your choices. Check the portal before you start filling choices.

One number gives a hint of what to expect. More than [10,105 seats are blocked](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-10105-seats-blocked-float-rule-review/) after Round 2, held by candidates who also have an allotment in state counselling. Some of those seats may come back into play in Round 3. The government is also looking at the float rule after this round, so read the latest MCC notices before you lock your list.
