**IIT Kharagpur has done something no IIT has done before. From the 2026-27 session, the institute will admit its first batch of MD students. The National Medical Commission has cleared six postgraduate medical programmes, and 12 seats are up for grabs through NEET PG Round 2 counselling.**

An IIT is known for engineering. Now one will train doctors too. The first batch will study at the Dr B C Roy Multi-Speciality Medical Research Centre in Kharagpur. The centre also goes by its newer name, the Dr Shyama Prasad Mookerjee Super Speciality Hospital.

The news broke on November 12. Round 2 is open right now, and these 12 seats will sit in its seat matrix.

## Six MD courses, two seats each

The institute will offer six MD specialisations. Each one has two seats for the first batch.

| MD programme | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| General Medicine | 2 |
| Radiology | 2 |
| Physiology | 2 |
| Pathology | 2 |
| Community Medicine | 2 |
| Biochemistry | 2 |
| **Total** | **12** |

Two seats per subject means the fight for each one will be sharp. Radiology and General Medicine usually draw the highest cut-offs, so the closing ranks here could sit very high.

The plan has been in the works for over a year. The proposal first went to the institute's Board of Governors on September 23, 2025. The board cleared it, and the NMC approval followed.

## How to apply: NEET PG Round 2

Admission runs through the Medical Counselling Committee, not through IIT Kharagpur directly. You need a valid NEET PG 2026 score and a place in Round 2 counselling.

1. Go to the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/pg-medical-counselling/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC PG counselling portal</a> and log in with your NEET PG 2026 roll number.
2. Register for Round 2 if you have not registered already. Registration stays open till 3 PM on November 14, 2026.
3. Fill your choices from the live seat matrix. Look for the listing "Dr B C Roy Multi-Speciality Medical Research Centre, Kharagpur, under the aegis of IIT Kharagpur".
4. Lock your choices between 4 PM on November 14 and 11:55 PM on November 15.

The [NEET PG Round 2 final result](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-revised-dates-nov-17-result/) comes on November 17, after a provisional result on November 16. Reporting and joining run from November 18 to 24.

Round 1 candidates need not register again. They can go straight to choice filling. Fresh candidates must register before November 14.

> For the first time, an IIT will train doctors, not just engineers. And the first batch is just 12 students.

## Why this is a big deal for medical aspirants

IIT Kharagpur was set up in 1951. In 75 years, no IIT has offered a clinical medical degree. Medical colleges trained doctors, IITs trained engineers. That line has now been crossed.

The mix is the real story. IIT Kharagpur already runs research in medical science and technology. A doctor who trains inside an IIT campus works next to engineers and researchers building medical devices and AI tools. For a student who wants to work where medicine meets technology, there is no other seat like this in India right now.

The [9,783 new PG seats](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-9783-new-seats-total-68305/) approved this year take the total to 68,305. IIT Kharagpur's 12 are a small part of that total, but they are the only ones inside an IIT. That tag alone will push the cut-offs up.

## What to check before you lock your choices

Twelve seats leave no room for a casual pick. Before you place the B C Roy centre in your choice list, check three things.

First, read the seat matrix on the MCC portal. It shows the exact seat type and the reservation roster for each of the 12 seats. A seat you cannot claim under your category is a wasted choice.

Second, look at the fee and bond rules. No separate fee notice is out for these MD seats yet. Check the MCC portal and the <a href="https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IIT Kharagpur website</a> for the fee structure and any service bond before you lock the choice.

Third, remember the Round 2 rules. From Round 2 onwards, there is no free exit. If you take a seat and do not join, you lose the security deposit. Fill only the choices you are ready to accept.
