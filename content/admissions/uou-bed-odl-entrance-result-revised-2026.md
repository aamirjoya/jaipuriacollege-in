**Two candidates, one mix-up. Uttarakhand Open University has corrected the B.Ed ODL entrance exam result after finding that the scores of two roll numbers were swapped. If you downloaded your result before September 30, you need to check it again.**

The B.Ed (ODL) entrance examination was held on September 20, 2026. UOU declared the result on September 26 along with the final answer key, released the merit list on September 28, and began issuing counselling notices for the 2026-28 session. Four days after the first result, the Controller of Examinations signed a revision notice that changed the outcome for two candidates. The timeline matters here, because counselling is already in progress.

## What the September 30 revision notice says

The official revision notice, letter number यूओयू/परीक्षा/447 (01)/2026/763, says the correction followed applications received from candidates. Two roll numbers were affected:

- Roll No. 21260140: first shown with 70 marks, now marked Absent
- Roll No. 21260141: first shown as Absent, now marked with 70 marks

In simple terms, one candidate's score had landed against the other candidate's roll number. The notice fixes this swap and was sent for publication on the university website. You can read the <a href="https://uou.ac.in/announcement/2026/09/6535" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official B.Ed ODL result revision notice</a> yourself before trusting any second-hand copy.

The circular is signed by Prof. Somesh Kumar, Controller of Examinations, and it carries no new instructions beyond the correction. That matters because it means every other candidate's result stands exactly as declared on September 26. If your roll number is not one of these two, your score is unchanged. Even so, confirm the latest notice on the portal rather than relying on a screenshot you saved earlier.

## Why this matters while counselling is on

Counselling for the B.Ed ODL 2026-28 session is already underway. UOU released category-wise merit lists on September 28 for General, OBC, EWS, SC and ST candidates, and counselling notices have followed since. Everything downstream, your reporting date, your documents, and your admission itself, flows from the result on record.

For the two affected candidates, the picture changes completely. For everyone else, the correction is a warning: work only from the latest official notice, not from a result you downloaded a week ago. If you need a refresher, read our guide on [how to check a university result online](/results/how-to-check-university-result-online/) so you do not miss any step on the portal.

> A result downloaded on September 26 is not the latest word anymore. Open the September 30 notice on the official portal and confirm your status before you report for counselling.

## What to do before your counselling turn

1. Visit the <a href="https://uou.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Uttarakhand Open University portal</a> and go to the Announcements section.
2. Open the September 30 revision notice and check the corrected roll numbers against yours.
3. Match your result with the September 28 merit list for your category.
4. Open the counselling notice that applies to you and note the reporting date, time, venue and document list.
5. Keep originals and photocopies of every document ready in one folder.

## Documents to carry for counselling

Carry your qualifying degree marksheets and certificate, the entrance admit card, the latest result printout, a photo ID such as Aadhaar, and your category certificate if you applied under a reserved category. Take originals plus self-attested photocopies of each.

A small name or spelling mismatch between your ID and your marksheets is one of the most common reasons candidates are sent back from the verification desk. Sort that out before your turn, not on the day. ## What happens after counselling

After document verification, selected candidates complete fee payment and other admission formalities as the university instructs. If you have already attended counselling, recheck your updated status on the official portal so nothing is left pending. UOU's announcements page also carries the September 28 merit list notice and the September 29 notice for qualified candidates.

Notice how fast the sequence has moved: merit list on September 28, instructions for qualified candidates on September 29, and the result revision on September 30, with counselling notices for different reporting dates issued alongside. When a university moves at this pace, the announcements page is the one place to check before you leave home.
