**The Indian Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT) has moved the last date to apply for its MBA 2027-29 programmes to December 8, 2026. The portal at applyadmission.net/iift2027 stays open till 11:59 pm that night, and both registration and fee payment must be done by then.**

The earlier deadline was November 25. The new date, which appeared on the IIFT website and the admission portal on November 26, gives candidates 13 more days. That gap matters because [CAT 2026](/admissions/cat-2026-2-84-lakh-registrations-nov-29-exam/) falls on November 29, right in the middle of it. Most top [MBA admissions](/admissions/) closed their forms before the exam, so applicants had to commit blind. IIFT is the rare case where you can sit the test first and then decide.

## What the extension changes for you

If you were holding back because you were unsure about your CAT attempt, the pressure is off for now. You can take the exam on November 29, see how it went, and then file the IIFT form before December 8. The extension does not change anything else. The programmes, the fees, the campuses and the selection process stay the same. IIFT has not given a reason for the extension, and the institute is yet to announce the shortlist and interview schedule for this batch.

One point needs care. The portal closes at 11:59 pm on December 8, and that cutoff covers payment too. A form that is filled but unpaid after that time will not count. Do not leave the payment for the last hour, when servers slow down.

> The IIFT portal closes at 11:59 pm on December 8, 2026. Registration and fee payment both end at the same time, so finish and pay well before the last evening.

## The two programmes and their seats

IIFT offers two MBA programmes for the 2027-29 batch. Together they carry 780 seats.

| Programme | Campuses | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MBA (International Business) | Delhi, Kolkata, Kakinada, GIFT City | 720 |
| MBA (Business Analytics) | Delhi only | 60 |

The International Business programme is the older and larger of the two, and it runs across four campuses. Business Analytics is a smaller programme, taught only in Delhi, for candidates who want a management degree built around data and analytics.

## What you pay for each form

The fee depends on the programme and your category. It is charged per form, and the two programmes need separate forms.

| Category | MBA (International Business) | MBA (Business Analytics) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General, OBC-NCL, EWS | Rs 3,000 | Rs 2,000 |
| SC, ST, PwD, transgender | Rs 1,500 | Rs 1,000 |

If you apply to both programmes, you pay twice, once for each form. The fee is paid online through the portal while you submit the form.

## What happens after December 8

The schedule on the official admission portal lays out the rest of the cycle. IIFT does not hold its own entrance test. It will use CAT 2026 scores to shortlist candidates, so your CAT result decides whether you reach the next round.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Online application opened | October 12, 2026 |
| Last date to register and pay | December 8, 2026, 11:59 pm |
| CAT 2026 exam | November 29, 2026 |
| Shortlist for the second round | Last week of January 2027 |
| Group discussion and interview | February 2027 onwards |
| Final result | Last week of April 2027 |
| Programme begins | June 2027 |

The shortlist comes about seven weeks after the form closes. Candidates who make it will face a group discussion and an interview from February onwards, and the final list is expected in the last week of April 2027.

## How to apply on the official portal

The whole process is online. Keep your CAT 2026 registration number and your academic records ready before you start.

1. Open the official admission portal at <a href="https://applyadmission.net/iift2027" target="_blank" rel="noopener">applyadmission.net/iift2027</a>.
2. Read the guidelines on how to fill the form before you start.
3. Pick your programme, MBA (IB) or MBA (BA). For both, you will need to register and apply separately.
4. Register with your name, email ID and mobile number, then fill in your personal, academic and CAT 2026 details.
5. Pay the application fee online. The portal shows the amount based on your programme and category.
6. Submit the form and save a copy of it along with the payment receipt.

If something goes wrong with the form, the admission helpdesk can be reached at 011-39147213 or by email at mba_admission@iift.edu. The institute's main site is <a href="https://www.iift.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">iift.ac.in</a>.

## The timing is kind to CAT takers this year

[FMS Delhi](/admissions/fms-delhi-mba-admission-2027-29-nov-25-deadline/) shut its form on November 26 after a one-day extension. SPJIMR and IMT closed today, November 27, and MDI Gurgaon closes tomorrow, November 28, at 5 pm. Against that rush, IIFT stands apart. It is the one big name that still lets you apply after the CAT exam, with the score in hand and a clear head. That is worth using if IIFT is on your list. Treat December 8 as the final date, because nothing in the notice points to another extension.
