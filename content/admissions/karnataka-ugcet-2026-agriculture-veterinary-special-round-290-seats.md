**Around 290 seats are still open in Karnataka's BSc Agriculture, BVSc (Veterinary) and Fisheries courses, and the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has opened a special round to fill them.** The seat matrix comes out today, November 16, and option entry starts tomorrow, November 17. Students who missed a seat in the earlier rounds now get one more chance, but the window is short.

The Karnataka UGCET 2026 special round covers vacant seats in BSc (Hons) Agriculture, BVSc and Animal Husbandry, and B.F.Sc (Fisheries) courses across the state's agriculture and veterinary universities. KEA decided on the extra round after the agriculture and veterinary universities asked it to fill the seats left vacant after the earlier rounds. The authority said the full seat matrix, with college-wise and course-wise vacancies, would be released on November 16.

## The dates you cannot miss

The schedule leaves no room for delay.

- **Seat matrix:** November 16, after 1 pm
- **Advance fee of Rs 35,000:** November 17 and 18, during banking hours
- **Option entry:** November 17 to November 19, 10 am
- **Provisional allotment result:** November 19, after 5 pm
- **Final allotment result:** November 20, after 10 am
- **Fee payment and reporting to college:** by November 21, 5:30 pm

Karnataka is running more than one mop-up round this week. The [Karnataka NEET UG special stray round](/admissions/karnataka-neet-ug-special-stray-round-allotment-20-mbbs-seats/) for MBBS seats is also under way, and the [Maharashtra NEET UG Round 3](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-3-list-103-seats-report-today-nov-16/) reporting window closes today, November 16. Students tracking [college admissions](/admissions/) in Karnataka should treat this special round as a separate process with its own dates and rules.

## Who can apply, and who cannot

Not every student can take part in this round. KEA has set clear conditions, and breaking them can cost you the seat even after allotment.

Students who have already taken admission in another course can take part, but only after getting a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their college. Get the NOC first, then enter your options. Without it, your entry does not count.

Some students are kept out of this round. If you were allotted an agriculture or veterinary seat earlier and then cancelled it, you cannot apply for the same course again. Students who have joined medical, dental or AYUSH courses cannot take part either. The same rule applies to students who got a seat in the fourth or later rounds of counselling.

Money is another point to note. The fee you paid for any other course will not be adjusted against the new seat. If you get a seat in this round, the fresh fee has to be paid in full. The Rs 35,000 advance deposit is the only amount that gets adjusted against the total fee.

## How to take part in the special round

Follow these steps in order, and do not leave anything for the last hour. The KEA portal slows down near the deadline, and a missed step cannot be fixed later.

1. Check the seat matrix on the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA portal</a> once it is out today, and note the colleges and courses with vacant seats.
2. Pay the advance deposit of Rs 35,000 on November 17 or 18, during banking hours.
3. If you have already joined another course, get an NOC from your college before you enter options.
4. Enter your options online between November 17 and 10 am on November 19.
5. Check the provisional allotment on November 19 evening, and the final list on November 20.
6. If you get a seat, pay the fee and report to the college by 5:30 pm on November 21 with your original documents.

> "This is a short window. Option entry closes at 10 am on November 19, and there is no second chance in this round."

## What the Rs 35,000 advance covers

Every candidate who wants to join must pay an advance deposit of Rs 35,000. The amount is the same for all categories. KEA will adjust the deposit against the total course fee after allotment. Keep the payment receipt safe, because you will need it at the time of admission. The payment window is only two days, November 17 and 18, and it runs during banking hours, so plan the visit to the bank in advance.

## Why this round matters for agriculture aspirants

The round comes after the earlier UGCET rounds left around 290 seats vacant in these three course groups. For students who want a career in agriculture, animal care or fisheries, this is a direct route into a government college without waiting for next year. BSc Agriculture, BVSc and Fisheries courses lead to steady government and private sector work in farming, animal husbandry and food production, and the seats in this round are in state agriculture and veterinary universities.

KEA Executive Director H. Prasanna said the details of the available seats would be announced on November 16. That is the day the seat matrix goes live. Once you see the matrix, match the colleges against your rank and your choices, and fill your options in order of preference. A careful option list matters more than speed, but speed matters too, because the entry window closes on November 19 morning.
