**Students eyeing professional courses under the University of Mumbai have got one more window. The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) has extended the Centralised Admission Process (CAP) admission deadline for 2026-27 to October 16, 2026, giving late applicants a final chance to lock their seats in colleges affiliated with Mumbai University.**

The extension was announced on the CET Cell's directions on October 7, 2026. It covers the seats filled through the CAP in MU-affiliated institutions. If you had missed the earlier rounds or were waiting to complete your application, this is the window you should not let slip.

## Which Mumbai University courses come under the new October 16 deadline

The extended deadline applies to professional and management courses admitted through the CET Cell's Centralised Admission Process for the 2026-27 academic year. These include:

- **B.E (Bachelor of Engineering)** — through MHT CET CAP
- **BMS, BBA and BCA** — through MAH BBA/BMS/BBM and BCA CET CAP
- **Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE)** — through MAH DSE CAP
- **MCA and MMS** — through MAH MCA and MBA/MMS CET CAP
- **M.E (Master of Engineering)** — through MHT CET MTech CAP

Seats in these courses are allotted on CET merit through the centralised process. Colleges cannot fill CAP seats on their own, so every applicant must complete the process on the official CET Cell portal before the revised cut-off.

## How to apply before October 16

The CAP is fully online. Do not wait till the last evening, because document uploads and payment confirmations sometimes take time.

1. Go to the official CET Cell portal <a href="https://mahacet.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mahacet.org</a> and open the CAP section for your course.
2. Register with your CET application ID and complete the online application form.
3. Upload scanned copies of your mark sheets, CET scorecard, domicile and category documents if applicable.
4. Fill your college and course choices carefully and lock them before the deadline.
5. Pay the counselling fee online and download the confirmation receipt.

After the October 16 deadline, the CAP rounds move ahead with seat allotment. Students who complete their process on time will be part of the subsequent allotment and reporting stages.

> Missing the October 16 cut-off means sitting out of the centralised process for these courses this year. Colleges do not get to admit CAP seats on their own, so there is no backdoor round after the portal closes.

## Why this extension matters for students

Admission season in Maharashtra has been tight this year. Vacant seats have lingered in several rounds, and many students have been waiting on documents or decisions from other counsellings. This extension gives such students a second wind. Students who were tracking [MAH CET counselling updates](/admissions/mhcet-3-year-llb-round-4-merit-list-2026/) should note that each course has its own CAP schedule on the CET Cell portal, so check your course's exact timeline rather than assuming a common one.

For regular merit-based undergraduate and postgraduate admissions under MU, the university runs its own schedule through its admission portal and the official <a href="https://mu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mu.ac.in</a> website. The October 16 extension is specific to the CET Cell's Centralised Admission Process for professional courses.

## Documents to keep ready

Before you sit down to fill the CAP application, keep these scanned and ready in the prescribed size and format:

- CET 2026 scorecard and application form
- Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets
- Graduation mark sheet for PG courses like MCA, MMS and M.E
- Domicile certificate and category or caste certificate, if applicable
- Passport-size photograph and signature
- Aadhaar card or other valid identity proof

Incomplete or wrongly sized uploads are the most common reason applications get stuck. Double-check every file before you submit.
