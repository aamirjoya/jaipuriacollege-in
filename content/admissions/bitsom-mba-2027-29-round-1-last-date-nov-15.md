**The clock is running out for MBA aspirants eyeing BITS School of Management. BITSoM MBA admission 2027-29 Round 1 applications close today, 15 November 2026. The form has been open since 19 August, and the window shuts at the end of the day. If you have been putting it off, this is the last chance to apply in the first round.**

## Round 1 ends today: the dates you need

BITS School of Management in Mumbai runs its [MBA admissions](/admissions/) in two rounds. Round 1 opened on 19 August 2026 and closes on 15 November 2026, as per the <a href="https://www.bitsom.edu.in/admissions-timelines/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official admission timeline</a>. Students who apply with a GMAT or GRE score will face personal interviews in November and December 2026. Results for these candidates come out on 15 January 2027.

[CAT 2026](/admissions/cat-2026-2-84-lakh-registrations-nov-29-exam/) applicants get more time on one front. The CAT 2026 exam is on 29 November, and BITSoM lets these candidates submit their score by 12 January 2027. Their interviews run from January to February 2027, and their results are declared on 15 March 2027. The last date to submit a GMAT or GRE score is 31 March 2027. Classes for the 2027-29 batch begin on 21 May 2027.

## Who can apply for the BITSoM MBA

Any graduate can apply. BITSoM accepts a bachelor's degree in any subject from a recognised university. Final-year students can also apply, as long as they finish their degree before the programme starts.

The school accepts four test scores: CAT, GMAT, GMAT Focus Edition, and GRE. XAT scores are not accepted. You can apply with any one of these four scores, and you can also submit your form first and add your score later. But the score has to reach BITSoM by the deadline for your round, or the application will not move ahead.

## What the two-year MBA costs

The BITSoM MBA is a two-year, full-time residential programme. The total fee is Rs 28.55 lakh. That covers tuition of Rs 23.85 lakh, a one-time admission fee of Rs 1.5 lakh, and on-campus stay of Rs 3 lakh for two years. A refundable security deposit of Rs 20,000 is charged separately.

The application fee is Rs 3,000. Students should plan for living costs and travel on top of the fee, and look at education loans and scholarships early if they need support. The campus sits on Kalyan-Murbad Road in Thane district, near Mumbai, and students stay on campus for the full two years.

## How to apply before the window closes

The whole process runs online on the official BITSoM admissions portal. Keep your documents and test details ready before you start, so you do not get stuck halfway.

- Go to the <a href="https://admissions.bitsom.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BITSoM admissions portal</a> and register with your email ID and mobile number.
- Fill in your academic details, work experience, and test score information.
- Write and submit the essays asked in the form.
- Pay the application fee of Rs 3,000 online.
- Submit the form before the portal closes on 15 November 2026.

Once you submit, you can track your application on the portal. BITSoM informs shortlisted candidates by email, and the final decision also comes by email after the personal interview.

## What happens after you apply

Shortlisted candidates face a personal interview. The panel usually includes industry leaders, senior faculty, and BITSoM alumni. The final offer depends on your test score, your essays, the interview, and your academic record and work experience.

> "Round 1 usually has the widest seat availability. That is why the first deadline matters most for serious applicants."

## Missed today? Round 2 opens tomorrow

If you cannot finish the form today, all is not lost. Round 2 opens on 16 November 2026 and runs till 24 January 2027. Interviews for Round 2 run from January to April 2027, and results come out on 15 April 2027. You can apply in both rounds with CAT, GMAT, GMAT Focus, or GRE scores.

Still, Round 1 is the safer bet. The first round gives you the earliest result and the widest choice of seats. If BITSoM is on your list, finish the form today.
