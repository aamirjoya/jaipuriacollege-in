**Registration for the second phase of final Tamil Nadu AYUSH counselling opens today, November 22.** The window closes at 5 pm tomorrow, November 23. That gives NEET UG 2026 qualified candidates just over a day to apply for BAMS, BSMS, BUMS and BHMS seats that are still vacant in government and self-financing colleges across the state.

The Directorate of Indian Medicine and Homoeopathy announced the round on November 21. It comes after three online counselling rounds and the first phase of final counselling, which still left seats empty in several colleges. This is the last round of the 2026-27 admission cycle for these courses, so candidates who miss this window will have to wait for next year.

## Four courses, four quotas: what this round covers

The counselling covers four undergraduate AYUSH courses. Each one runs for five and a half years. Seats are open under four quotas: government quota, government-surrendered seats, management quota, and the All India quota. Both government colleges and self-financing AYUSH institutions in Tamil Nadu are part of this round.

Before you apply, check the vacancy list on the official portal. It shows which colleges still have seats and in which course. The Directorate has also put the counselling timetable and the information brochure on the same portal. Read the brochure first. It carries the rules for this round, and the portal is the only place to apply.

## Who can apply in this round

You can apply if you passed Class 12 in the science stream and scored the qualifying marks in NEET UG 2026. That is the same rule that applied in the earlier rounds. There is no separate entrance test for this round.

One rule needs care. If you want a seat under more than one quota, you must file a separate application for each quota. A single form does not cover two quotas. So if you plan to apply under both the government quota and the management quota, submit two forms and pay the fee for each.

## What you pay: the fee for each quota

The fee depends on the quota you apply under.

- **Government quota:** Rs 500 for candidates from the Open Competition, Backward Classes, Backward Classes Muslim, and Most Backward Classes and Denotified Communities categories. Candidates from the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Caste Arunthathiyar, and Scheduled Tribes get the prescribed fee concession.
- **All India quota and management quota:** Rs 1,000 for every candidate, no matter the community category.

All payments are online only. The Directorate takes the application fee, the counselling fee, and the advance tuition fee through the portal. Keep the payment receipt safe. You will need it at the time of reporting.

## How to apply in five steps

1. Open the official portal at <a href="https://www.tnayushselection.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnayushselection.org</a>.
2. Download the vacancy list, the counselling timetable, and the information brochure. Check which colleges have seats in your course.
3. Fill the online application form and upload your original certificates within the deadline.
4. Pay the fee online. Rs 500 for the government quota, Rs 1,000 for the All India and management quotas.
5. Submit the form before 5 pm on November 23 and save a copy of the submitted form and the payment receipt.

Do not leave it for the last hour. Portals slow down when thousands of students log in at once, and a failed payment at 4:55 pm leaves no time to try again.

## Three things to check before you submit

First, your certificates. Upload the originals the portal asks for, and make sure the name and date of birth match your NEET UG 2026 records. A mismatch can hold up your admission later.

Second, your quota. Pick the quota you are sure about. If you apply under two quotas, file two separate forms. Do not assume one form covers both.

Third, the schedule after registration. The Directorate will publish the allotment and certificate verification dates on the portal. Keep checking the official site after November 23 so you do not miss the next step.
