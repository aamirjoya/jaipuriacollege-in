**Today is the day Bihar medical aspirants have been waiting for.** The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) has scheduled the Bihar NEET UG 2026 stray vacancy round seat allotment result for today, October 27, 2026. Students who took part in the Undergraduate Medical Admission Counselling (UGMAC) 2026 stray vacancy round can check whether they have got a seat through the <a href="https://bceceboard.bihar.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BCECEB portal</a>.

This round covers MBBS, BDS, B.V.Sc. and A.H., and B.F.Sc. seats in government and private colleges across Bihar. It is the last round of this year's state counselling. Download your allotment order by October 29 and report for verification on October 28 or 29. A seat missed in this window will not come back.

## What the schedule says: allotment today, reporting in two days

BCECEB revised this round's calendar after students reported trouble with the portal during registration. Fresh registration closed on October 24, and the stray vacancy rank card came out on October 25. Choice filling and locking closed the same night, on October 25 at 11:59 pm.

Now the calendar runs like this:

- Provisional seat allotment result: October 27, 2026
- Allotment order download: October 27 to October 29, 2026
- Document verification and reporting: October 28 and October 29, 2026

Keep the board's own schedule notice open while you check your result. Boards often upload the result file in stages during the day, and the download window stays open till October 29.

## How to check your allotment and download the letter

Follow these steps once the result link appears on the portal:

1. Go to the <a href="https://bceceboard.bihar.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BCECEB website</a>.
2. Open the UGMAC 2026 section on the homepage.
3. Find the link for the stray vacancy round seat allotment result or allotment order.
4. Log in with your UGMAC ID and date of birth or password, if the link asks for login.
5. Check the college and course allotted to you, and read every detail on the screen.
6. Download the provisional allotment letter and take at least two printouts.

One printout goes into your reporting file. Keep the second one at home as a backup. Your name, roll number, allotted college, course, and quota must all match your other records. If anything looks wrong, contact the board's helpdesk at once instead of waiting till reporting day.

> "This is the final round of Bihar medical counselling for 2026. A seat allotted today has to be reported to on October 28 or 29, because there is no next round to fall back on."

## Documents to carry for verification on October 28 and 29

Reporting means physical verification at the allotted college or reporting centre. Carry originals plus self-attested photocopies of each document. Students who went through [Bihar UGMAC counselling](/admissions/bihar-ugmac-2026-round-3-registration-oct-6/) in earlier rounds will know this drill, but the stray round leaves no room for a second trip.

- NEET UG 2026 admit card and scorecard
- Bihar UGMAC 2026 rank card
- Provisional seat allotment letter downloaded from the BCECEB portal
- Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates
- Bihar residential or domicile certificate, where required
- Caste or category certificate, if applicable
- Valid government photo ID, such as Aadhaar
- Passport-size photographs

Check the full [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) before you pack your folder. A missing paper on October 28 leaves you just one more day, October 29, to fix it.

## Who could take part in this round

Only students who completed the stray round process could get a seat today. That meant fresh registration by October 24, fee payment, and choice filling and locking by October 25 night. Seats are allotted on NEET UG 2026 merit, the choices each student locked, and the seats actually vacant in each college and course.

Students who did not register or lock choices in this round will not appear in today's allotment. The board had made it clear in its revised notice that no further concession on dates would follow, so this round closed strictly on its own calendar. Earlier UGMAC notices are listed in the [admissions](/admissions/) section.

## If your name is not on the list

First, search carefully. Check the result for your exact course and quota, and confirm that you are looking at the stray vacancy PDF and not an older round's list. A spelling slip in a search can hide a seat that is really there.

If no seat is allotted, this year's Bihar state counselling ends here for you. Other states ran their own stray rounds this month, and the [West Bengal stray vacancy result](/results/wb-neet-ug-2026-stray-vacancy-seat-allotment-result/) came out on October 23, but each state's counselling is separate. You cannot carry a Bihar application into another state's round after its own schedule has closed.

## What happens after you report

Once your documents are verified and the admission fee is paid at the college, your seat is confirmed. Ask the college office for a receipt and a copy of the admission confirmation, and keep both safe. Classes and hostel allotment follow the college's own notice, so check the college website after reporting instead of relying on rumours in student groups.

Two dates decide everything now: download the letter by October 29, and report on October 28 or 29.
