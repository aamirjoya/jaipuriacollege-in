**Registration for the West Bengal AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2 opens today, November 21, at 12 noon.** This is the last round of UG AYUSH counselling in the state for the 2026-27 session, and it covers BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BNYS seats in government and private colleges. If you qualified in NEET UG 2026 and still have no seat, this is your final chance.

The window is short. Registration closes on November 22 at 6 PM, so you have just over a day to fill the form and pay the fee. The West Bengal Medical Counselling Committee (WBMCC) runs the whole process online through its official portal, wbmcc.nic.in.

At noon today, the link goes live on the portal. Fee payment stays open till midnight on November 22. Candidates who leave it for the last hour often run into slow servers or payment failures, and the portal does not accept late forms. Fill the form today, pay the fee, and save the receipt.

This round is open to two groups. Fresh candidates who never registered in the earlier rounds can sign up now. Candidates who registered before but got no seat can also take part, and they do not have to pay the fee again. One rule stays fixed across stray rounds: no choice upgradation is allowed once a seat is allotted in this round.

## WB AYUSH Stray Round 2: all the dates in one place

- **Online registration and fee payment:** November 21 (12 noon) to November 22 (6 PM; fee till midnight)
- **Document verification:** November 23, 11 AM to 4 PM
- **Verified list and seat matrix:** November 23, after 5 PM
- **Choice filling and locking:** November 23 (6 PM) to November 24 (2 PM)
- **Allotment result:** November 27, after 4 PM
- **Reporting and admission at the allotted college:** November 28 and 30, 11 AM to 4 PM

The schedule comes from the official WBMCC notice, and the committee calls it tentative. Check the portal before each step in case a date moves.

## Who this round is for

Only NEET UG 2026 qualified candidates can take part. The counselling covers state quota, management quota and private college seats for BAMS, BHMS, BUMS and BNYS. Seats left vacant after Round 3 and Stray Round 1 flow into this round, so the seat matrix that comes out on November 23 evening is the document that matters most. Read it before you fill your choices.

Other states are running their own last rounds at the same time. The [MP AYUSH Stray Round 2 merit list](/results/mp-ayush-stray-round-2-merit-list-nov-19-2026/) came out on November 19, and the [Jharkhand BDS, BHMS stray round 2](/admissions/jceceb-neet-ug-special-stray-round-2-bds-bhms-apply-nov-20-2026/) closed applications on November 20. If you are tracking more than one state, keep each state's dates separate, because the portals and deadlines do not match.

## Documents to keep ready before you log in

Verification happens on November 23 at the designated college, from 11 AM to 4 PM. Keep these papers ready now, not on the morning of verification:

- NEET UG 2026 scorecard and admit card
- Class 10 certificate as age proof and Class 12 marksheet
- Domicile certificate, as per West Bengal state quota rules
- Category or EWS certificate, if it applies to you
- A valid government photo ID, such as voter ID, passport, PAN card or driving licence
- College fees and bond papers, as the reporting rules ask for them

Check that the name and date of birth match across every document. A mismatch at the verification desk can cost you the seat even after allotment.

## How to register in six steps

1. Open the <a href="https://wbmcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official WBMCC portal</a> and find the UG AYUSH counselling section
2. Click the Stray Vacancy Round 2 registration link, which goes live at 12 noon today
3. Fill in your NEET UG 2026 roll number, rank and personal details
4. Pay the registration fee online and save the payment receipt
5. Note your registration number and keep the login details safe
6. Return to the portal on November 23 evening to check the verified list and seat matrix

After verification, choice filling opens on November 23 at 6 PM and closes on November 24 at 2 PM. List your colleges in true order of preference, because there is no upgrade step in a stray round. The allotment result comes out on November 27 after 4 PM, and reporting at the allotted college happens on November 28 and 30 between 11 AM and 4 PM.
