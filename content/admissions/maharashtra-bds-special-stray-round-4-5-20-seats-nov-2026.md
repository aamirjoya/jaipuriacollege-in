**If you are still waiting for a BDS seat in Maharashtra, your chance has not ended. The State CET Cell has announced two more special stray vacancy rounds for 20 vacant BDS seats. Preference filling for Round 4 starts today, November 20, and closes tomorrow at 3 PM. The result will be out on November 21.**

These 20 seats are the last ones left after a long admission season. Maharashtra has already run three CAP rounds and six stray vacancy rounds for NEET UG 2026. Even after all that, 20 BDS seats stayed empty. The CET Cell decided to hold two more rounds after the Medical Counselling Committee extended the admission cut-off date.

This is a BDS-only round. MBBS seats are not part of it. Only Group A admissions are covered, and every candidate must file fresh preferences. Students who want one of these seats should act now, because Round 4 moves fast.

## Round 4 and Round 5: dates you must not miss

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Round 4 preference filling | November 20-21, 2026, till 3 PM |
| Round 4 result | November 21, 2026 |
| Round 4 joining | November 22-24, 2026 |
| Round 5 seat matrix | November 24, 2026 |
| Round 5 preference filling | November 25, 2026, till 11:59 PM |
| Round 5 result | November 26, 2026 |
| Round 5 joining | November 27-28, 2026 |

You have less than two days to fill your choices for Round 4, and the result comes the next day. If you get a seat, you must report to the college between November 22 and 24. Round 5 follows the same pattern a few days later. Its seat matrix comes out on November 24, preference filling runs on November 25, and the result is due on November 26.

## Who can take part, and who cannot

You can apply if you qualified in NEET UG 2026 and registered for the Maharashtra state admission process, but did not get a seat in any Group A CAP round. The CET Cell has also listed who cannot apply. Students who joined an MBBS or BDS seat by November 16, 2026 are out. So are students who got a seat through the All India Quota stray vacancy rounds. And if you were allotted a seat in Round 3 or a later stray round and did not join, you cannot take part in these rounds either.

The [Maharashtra NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) process this year has run nine rounds in all: three CAP rounds, three stray vacancy rounds, and three special stray vacancy rounds. These two new rounds take that count to eleven, which shows how hard the CET Cell is working to fill every last seat.

## Your old choices will not work

> "Choices submitted during earlier stray vacancy rounds will not remain valid." (State CET Cell notice)

This is the part many students miss. Every special stray round needs fresh choices. The preferences you filled in the [special stray vacancy round 3](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-3-list-103-seats-report-today-nov-16/), or in any earlier round, are cancelled for this round. If you do not fill fresh choices by 3 PM on November 21, you will not be considered, even if you were eligible in the last round.

## How to fill your preferences for Round 4

1. Go to the official counselling portal at <a href="https://medical2026.mahacet.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">medical2026.mahacet.org</a>.
2. Log in with your NEET UG 2026 credentials.
3. Check the Round 4 seat matrix and the fee structure of each college before you choose.
4. Fill your college preferences in order and save them before 3 PM on November 21.
5. Check the result on November 21 on the same portal.

Check the fee structure before you submit your choices. The 20 seats are in private institutions, and fees vary from college to college. Once a seat is allotted, joining is mandatory.

## Why 20 seats are still empty

Maharashtra has 2,617 BDS seats this year. Of these, 2,597 have been filled, leaving 20 vacant. The government colleges filled all their BDS seats. The 20 open seats are in private institutions: one seat in a private dental college and 19 seats in a private medical university. These seats stayed empty through nine rounds of counselling, which is why the CET Cell has opened two more.

At the national level, the Medical Counselling Committee has also published the [all-India Round 6 final allotment](/results/neet-ug-round-6-provisional-allotment-out-final-result-today-nov-20-2026/) for BDS and BSc Nursing seats today. That round and the state rounds together close the 2026 admission season for dental courses.

## What to carry when you report

Students who get a seat must report in person at the allotted college with original documents and the prescribed fees. Carry your NEET UG 2026 admit card and scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, and your allotment letter. Fees can be paid by demand draft, NEFT or RTGS. Joining is mandatory. If you do not report, you may face penalties and disqualification under the information brochure.

Keep checking the official CET Cell website at <a href="https://mahacet.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mahacet.org</a> for the seat matrix and any fresh notice. The Medical Counselling Committee website at <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a> carries the all-India counselling updates. Do not trust forwards or social media posts for dates. The CET Cell notice is the only source that counts.
