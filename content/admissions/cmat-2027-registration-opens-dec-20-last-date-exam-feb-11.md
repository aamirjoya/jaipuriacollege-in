**The National Testing Agency has opened CMAT 2027 registration. You can apply online till December 20, 2026, up to 11:50 PM, without paying any late fee. The exam for MBA and PGDM admissions will be held on February 11, 2027.**

CMAT is the national entrance test for management programmes at AICTE-affiliated and participating institutes across the country. The form opened on November 23, so the window is already live. If you are planning an MBA next year, this is one of the main exams on the calendar, and the fee is lower than most other MBA entrance tests.

## All the CMAT 2027 dates in one place

NTA has released the full schedule in one go, from registration to result. Keep this table saved, because every date below comes from the official CMAT 2027 information bulletin.

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration opens | November 23, 2026 |
| Last date to apply without late fee | December 20, 2026, up to 11:50 PM |
| Last date to pay the fee | December 21, 2026, up to 11:50 PM |
| Late fee window, Rs 500 extra | December 22 to 25, 2026 |
| Last date to pay fee with late fee | December 25, 2026, up to 11:50 PM |
| Correction window | December 26 to 28, 2026 |
| Exam city announcement | January 21, 2027 |
| Admit card release | February 4, 2027 |
| CMAT 2027 exam | February 11, 2027 |
| Provisional answer key | February 19 to 21, 2027 |
| Result declaration | February 25, 2027 |

The correction window matters more than it looks. From December 26 to 28, you can fix mistakes in the form you already submitted. After that, NTA will not take correction requests. Check your name, date of birth and photo carefully before the window closes.

## Who can apply, and what it costs

You need a bachelor's degree in any subject to apply for CMAT 2027. The test is for admission to MBA and PGDM programmes, so it suits graduates from any stream who want to move into management.

The fee depends on your category. General category male candidates pay Rs 2,500. General category female candidates pay Rs 1,250. Candidates from Gen-EWS, SC, ST, PwD, PwBD and OBC-NCL categories pay Rs 1,250, and so do third gender candidates. Processing charges and GST may be added on top, so the final amount on the payment page can be a little higher than the fee itself.

Pay the fee by December 21 if you apply in the regular window. A form without fee payment does not count, even if you filled in every detail. Download the confirmation page after payment and keep it safe.

## What the exam looks like

CMAT 2027 will be a computer-based test in English, and it will last three hours. NTA has not yet announced the shift timings. Your exam centre, date and shift will be printed on the admit card, which you can download from February 4, 2027.

The paper tests five areas. Quantitative Technique and Data Interpretation covers maths and data. Logical Reasoning tests how you work through patterns and puzzles. Language Comprehension checks reading and English. General Awareness covers current affairs and general knowledge. Innovation and Entrepreneurship is the fifth section, and it sets CMAT apart from other MBA tests, since it asks about business ideas and startups.

> Submit only one form. NTA has said it will take strict action, even at a later stage, against candidates who submit more than one application for CMAT 2027.

If you are also preparing for other MBA tests, the dates sit close together. [XAT 2027](/admissions/xat-2027-registration-closes-december-6-exam-january-3/) registration closes on December 6, and its exam is on January 3, 2027. That gives you a January exam first, then CMAT in February. Plan your mock tests around both dates instead of cramming one after the other.

## How to apply in 5 steps

1. Go to the <a href="https://cmat.nta.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CMAT portal</a> and open the CMAT 2027 registration link.
2. Register with your personal and contact details, then fill in the full application form.
3. Upload the documents the form asks for, in the size and format given on the portal.
4. Pay the fee online before the deadline for your window.
5. Submit the form and download the confirmation page. Save a printout with you.

Fill the form in one sitting if you can. Keep your degree details, a scanned photo and signature, and a working mobile number and email ready before you start. The portal will send updates to the contact details you give, so use an email and phone number you check every day.

Treat the late fee window as a safety net. Apply before December 20 if you can, and use the late window only if you genuinely miss the first deadline. The portal gets slow in the last hours, and a failed payment on December 25 leaves no time to try again.
