**The clock is running out for JEE Main 2027. Registration for Session 1 closes tomorrow, November 22, at 11:50 PM. By November 19, the National Testing Agency had received 10,47,083 forms, and 8,38,138 students had paid the fee. If you have not applied yet, tonight is the time to finish it.**

Registration opened on October 24, and it has stayed open for nearly a month. The rush in the last few days has been heavy. NTA has asked students not to wait till the last hours, because the portal slows down when lakhs of students log in at once. Your form counts only after you pay the fee and download the confirmation page.

## Missed November 22? You get 4 more days, but it costs Rs 500 extra

Missing the regular deadline does not end your chance. NTA will keep the window open from November 23 to 26 for late applications, with an extra fee of Rs 500 for every form, whatever your category. The last date to pay the late fee is November 26, up to 11:50 PM.

Once the late window closes, NTA will open the correction window from November 27 to 29. During these three days, you can edit the details the form allows you to change. It is the last chance to fix errors before the exam process moves ahead.

There is one more option this year. If you made a mistake in your form, you can submit a fresh application. But be careful. If the fresh form is left incomplete, without fee payment or confirmation page, your earlier completed form stays valid. Do not start a fresh form unless you are sure you will finish it.

## 10.47 lakh forms so far, and the rush is still on

The numbers show how big this exam is. NTA shared on its official X account that registrations had crossed 5.72 lakh by November 10, with over 4.07 lakh students having paid the fee. By November 19, the count had jumped to 10,47,083 registrations and 8,38,138 fee payments.

This year, the login process has changed. Students now sign in through Meri Pehchaan, using DigiLocker, e-Pramaan or JanParichay. NTA has asked students to use their own DigiLocker login and finish re-KYC before filling the form. Your name and date of birth in the form must match your Class 10 certificate, or the form can be held up later. Our report on the [DigiLocker login advisory](/admissions/jee-main-2027-nta-digilocker-advisory-nov-26-deadline/) explains the three checks NTA wants every student to finish first.

> If you have not filled the form yet, do it tonight. After 11:50 PM tomorrow, every application costs Rs 500 more.

## JEE Main 2027 Session 1: all the dates in one place

Keep this list safe. Every date below comes from the official NTA schedule for Session 1.

- **Registration opened:** October 24, 2026
- **Last date to apply without late fee:** November 22, 2026, up to 11:50 PM
- **Late fee window:** November 23 to 26, 2026, up to 11:50 PM
- **Correction window:** November 27 to 29, 2026
- **Exam city intimation:** December 30, 2026
- **Admit card release:** January 13, 2027
- **Session 1 exam:** January 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28, 2027

Students who want a second shot do not have to wait long. Session 2 registration runs from January 5 to February 3, 2027, and the exam is set for April 2 to 7, 2027. You can appear in one session or both, and the better score counts for the final rank.

## Who can apply, and what is the fee

Class 12 students who passed in 2025 or 2026 can apply, and so can those appearing for Class 12 in 2027. There is no upper age limit for this exam. JEE Main is held for B.E. and B.Tech seats, along with B.Arch and B.Planning, at NITs, IIITs and other participating institutes. It is also the gateway to [JEE Advanced 2027](/admissions/jee-advanced-2027-exam-date-may-23-iit-delhi-eligibility/), which IIT Delhi will hold on May 23, 2027, for the top 2.5 lakh JEE Main candidates.

The exam is held in 13 languages, including English, Hindi and most major regional languages. The fee below is for one paper at a centre in India, charged per session.

- General, male: Rs 1,000
- General, female: Rs 800
- EWS and OBC-NCL, male: Rs 900
- SC, ST, PwD and third gender: Rs 500

## How to apply in 5 steps

1. Go to the <a href="https://jeemain.nta.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JEE Main portal</a> and open the Session 1 registration link.
2. Sign in through Meri Pehchaan with DigiLocker, e-Pramaan or JanParichay.
3. Fill in your personal, academic and contact details. Check the spelling of your name and your date of birth against your Class 10 certificate.
4. Upload your photo, signature and other documents in the size and format the form asks for.
5. Pay the fee online, then download and save the confirmation page. Keep a printout with you.

Pay the fee today, not tomorrow night. The payment link stays open till 11:50 PM on November 22, but a failed payment at the last minute leaves no time to try again. If the payment goes through and the confirmation page is in your hand, your place in the January exam is set.
