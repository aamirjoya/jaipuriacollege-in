**Haryana BDS Round 6 registration closes tonight, November 24, 2026, at 11:59 PM. Pt. B.D. Sharma University of Health Sciences (UHS), Rohtak, is running this round for BDS seats in private dental colleges, and the tentative seat matrix lists only two vacancies.**

The round covers BDS admission for the 2026-27 session in four private dental institutions across Haryana. It is a BDS-only round. MBBS seats are not part of it. Students who want one of the two seats must finish registration, fill fresh choices and lock them before midnight tonight on the <a href="https://uhsrugcounselling.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Haryana counselling portal</a>.

## Only two seats in the matrix

| College | Category | Seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swami Devi Dayal Hospital and Dental College, Panchkula | BC-B | 1 |
| Yamuna Institute of Dental Sciences and Research, Gadholi, Yamunanagar | Management | 1 |

The matrix is dated November 21, 2026, and the university calls it tentative. It shows zero vacancies in every other college and category listed, and no government BDS seats at all. Students should check the portal once more before locking choices, in case the university issues a revised matrix.

Four private dental institutions are part of this round: SGT University, Budhera, Gurugram; Maharishi Markandeshwar University, Sadopur, Ambala; Al-Falah University; and Sanskaram School of Medical Sciences, Jhajjar. Only two of them have a vacant seat in the current matrix.

## Five dates to remember

| What happens | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh registration, editing, choice filling and locking | November 19 to 24, 2026, till 11:59 PM |
| Provisional seat allocation and grievances | November 25, 2026 |
| Provisional tuition fee payment online | November 25 to 26, 2026, till 5:00 PM |
| Physical document verification at UHS Rohtak | November 27, 2026 |
| Last date of joining the allotted college | November 28, 2026, till 4:00 PM |

Everything after tonight moves fast. The provisional allocation comes out on November 25, a day after registration closes. Students who get a seat have two days to pay the provisional tuition fee online. Document verification follows on November 27 at UHS Rohtak, and joining must be complete by 4 PM on November 28.

Other states are closing their BDS rounds this week too. The [Maharashtra BDS special stray rounds](/admissions/maharashtra-bds-special-stray-round-4-5-20-seats-nov-2026/) cover 20 seats, with the Round 5 seat matrix due on November 24. At the national level, [NEET UG Round 6](/admissions/neet-ug-round-6-bds-bsc-nursing-registration-nov-17-2026/) for BDS and B.Sc Nursing has already closed, with its result out on November 20.

## Who can take part, and who cannot

Candidates who registered in earlier Haryana rounds do not need to register again, but they must fill fresh Round 6 choices. Choices from earlier rounds are null and void for this round, so an old list will not work.

Some students are kept out of Round 6 by the official rules. Candidates who were allotted a seat in Haryana Round 3, the stray round, the special stray round, or BDS Round 5 cannot take part. Students who have already joined a seat through the all-India quota or any state counselling up to those rounds are also out, even if they resigned later.

A security deposit of Rs 1,00,000 applies to candidates opting for a state quota seat in a private dental college. The amount is paid online. If a candidate is allotted a seat as per preference and does not join, the security amount is forfeited and eligibility for further rounds is lost. The BC-B seat should be picked only by candidates who meet the official category rules. The Management seat needs a separate affordability check, so verify the official fee structure and all mandatory charges before locking it.

UHS Rohtak has also warned students against direct admissions by private colleges.

> "Any admissions made by any private medical/dental educational and research institutes including those under private university in the state at its own level shall not be valid and such admission shall be considered as null and void."

## How to register and lock your choices

1. Open the official Haryana counselling portal and log in with your candidate ID.
2. Complete fresh registration, or edit your details if you are already registered.
3. Pay the Rs 1,00,000 security deposit online if you are opting for a state quota seat in a private dental college.
4. Fill a fresh list of BDS choices in the order you prefer.
5. Lock your choices before 11:59 PM on November 24. Choices that are not locked do not count.

Personal appearance with original documents before the admission committee at UHS Rohtak is mandatory at the verification stage. The university says candidature will not be considered without it. For updates, the university asks candidates to keep checking the <a href="https://www.uhsr.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UHS Rohtak website</a> and the counselling portal.
