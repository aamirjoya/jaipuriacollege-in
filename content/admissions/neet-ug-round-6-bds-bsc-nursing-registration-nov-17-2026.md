**Registration for NEET UG Round 6 counselling opened today, 17 November 2026. This round covers BDS and B.Sc Nursing seats only. If you want one of these seats, you must register by 3 pm on 18 November. The seat allotment result will be declared on 20 November.**

The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) announced this round in a notice dated 30 October 2026. The same notice covered the MBBS [special stray vacancy round](/admissions/mp-neet-ug-2026-special-stray-vacancy-round-choice-filling-nov-11/) and the fifth round for BDS and B.Sc Nursing. Those rounds ran from 2 to 4 November, and their results came out on 7 November. Reporting for those rounds ended on 16 November.

Round 6 is a separate window. It exists to fill BDS and B.Sc Nursing seats that are still vacant after the earlier rounds. It is not a fresh MBBS round, and students waiting for an MBBS seat should not count on it. Only candidates who qualified in NEET UG 2026 and meet the MCC rules for this round can take part. Read the official notice on the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC portal</a> before you register, because the notice is the final word on who can apply.

## The dates that matter: registration ends 18 Nov at 3 pm

Here is the full schedule for [NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) Round 6, as given in the MCC notice:

- Registration opens: 17 November 2026
- Registration closes: 18 November, 3 pm
- Fee payment: 17 November (10 am) to 19 November (6 pm)
- Choice filling: 17 November to 19 November (9 am)
- Choice locking: 18 November (4 pm) to 19 November (9 am)
- Seat allotment processing: 19 November
- Result: 20 November 2026
- Reporting at allotted college: 21 to 28 November (up to 5 pm on 28 November)

The date that catches students out is 18 November. The round is often described as running from 17 to 19 November, but that is the combined window for registration and payment. Registration itself shuts at 3 pm on 18 November. Fee payment stays open till 6 pm on 19 November, but that extra day does not help if you never registered.

> Registration closes at 3 pm on 18 November. The fee window stays open a day longer, but it cannot save a form that was never submitted.

Fill your choices early and lock them before the deadline. Choice filling closes at 9 am on 19 November, and the locking window runs from 4 pm on 18 November to 9 am on 19 November. If you do not lock your choices, the system takes the last saved order. Put your most wanted colleges at the top, and keep a few safe options lower down the list.

## How to register for NEET UG Round 6

Go to the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/ug-medical-counselling" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UG medical counselling section of the MCC website</a> and register with your NEET UG 2026 roll number and other details. Then pay the counselling fee inside the payment window. Candidates who took part in earlier rounds should read the Round 6 notice to check whether they need fresh registration and fresh choice filling for this round.

After registration, fill in your college and course choices in order of preference. Check the seat matrix on the portal before you finalise the list. It shows which colleges have vacant BDS and B.Sc Nursing seats in this round. Lock your choices before 9 am on 19 November.

The seat allotment result will be published on 20 November on the MCC portal. If you get a seat, download the allotment letter and report to the college between 21 and 28 November. Reporting closes at 5 pm on 28 November. Carry your NEET UG admit card and scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, allotment letter, photo ID, and passport-size photos. Our [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) covers the full list of papers most colleges ask for at reporting.

## What this round means for BDS and nursing aspirants

For many students, Round 6 is the last open door to a BDS or B.Sc Nursing seat this year. The MBBS special stray round has already closed, and its reporting ended on 16 November. Students who missed the fifth round for BDS and nursing now get one more chance, but only if they act before the 18 November deadline.

Keep one thing in mind. A seat in this round still needs the same reporting and document checks as any other round. Arrange your papers now, so a missing certificate does not cost you a seat you have already won.
