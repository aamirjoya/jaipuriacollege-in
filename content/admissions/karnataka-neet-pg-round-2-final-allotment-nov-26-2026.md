**The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has released the final seat allotment result for round 2 of [Karnataka NEET PG 2026 counselling](/admissions/) today, 26 November 2026.** The list went live after 11 AM on the official KEA portal. If you took part in this round, check the PDF now. The fee window is already open and closes on 30 November at 4 PM. You must report to your allotted college by 1 December.

This round covers MD and MS seats in Karnataka medical colleges. The final list is the document that decides your admission. Check your name, course, and college in it, even if you already saw the provisional list.

## The final list replaces the provisional one

KEA published the provisional round 2 list on 25 November, after 5 PM. The final list came a day later, and it is the only one that counts. A seat that appeared in the provisional list means nothing until it shows up in the final allotment.

KEA has put up the final list in English and Kannada. The seat is confirmed only after you pay the fee and report in person to the college. Downloading the PDF alone does not lock your seat.

## Round 2 dates you cannot miss

| What you need to do | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Final allotment published | 26 November 2026, after 11 AM |
| Download challan and pay the fee | 26 November, 11 AM to 30 November, 4 PM |
| Deposit original documents at KEA, Bengaluru | 26 to 30 November (book a slot first) |
| Download the admission order | By 30 November 2026 |
| Report to the allotted college | 26 November to 1 December 2026 |

The fee deadline is the one most candidates trip on. The challan window shuts at 4 PM on 30 November, and the banks close with it. Pay a day early if you can.

## How to check your allotment

1. Go to the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">KEA portal</a>.
2. Open the PGNEET 2026 Medical (MD/MS) section.
3. Click the link for the second round final seat allotment.
4. Open the PDF and search for your NEET PG roll number or KEA application number.
5. Note the college and course, then download the allotment letter from the same portal.

Keep a printout of the allotment letter. You will need it at the college, along with the fee receipt and your original documents.

> The final list is the only one that counts for admission. A seat on the provisional list means nothing until it appears in the final allotment.

## After allotment: four things to finish

First, download the challan from the KEA portal and pay the fee before 30 November, 4 PM. Second, book a slot and deposit your original documents at the KEA office in Bengaluru between 26 and 30 November. Third, download your admission order by 30 November. Fourth, report to the allotted college by 1 December with the admission order and your originals.

Do the steps in that order. The college cannot complete your admission without the admission order, and KEA will not issue it until the fee is paid and the documents are deposited.

## If you chose Choice 2 in round 1

Candidates who picked Choice 2 in [round 1](/admissions/karnataka-neet-pg-2026-round-1-final-allotment-3268-seats-report-nov-14/) and got a new seat in round 2 do not pay the full fee again. KEA adjusts the fee you already paid in round 1 against the fee for the new seat. You pay only the balance, if there is one, by 30 November at 4 PM. Check the challan before paying. It shows the exact amount due after the adjustment.

## What if you did not get a seat

KEA has not announced the schedule for the next round of Karnataka NEET PG counselling yet. Keep checking the KEA portal rather than trusting forwarded messages. ## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I check the Karnataka NEET PG 2026 round 2 final allotment?

Check it on the official KEA portal at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea. Open the PGNEET 2026 Medical (MD/MS) section, click the second round final seat allotment link, open the PDF, and search for your NEET PG roll number or KEA application number.

### What is the last date to pay the fee after Karnataka NEET PG round 2 allotment?

The fee must be paid by 30 November 2026, 4 PM. The challan download and payment window opened on 26 November at 11 AM.

### When do I report to the allotted college?

Report to your allotted medical college between 26 November and 1 December 2026. Carry your original documents and the admission order.

### I chose Choice 2 in round 1 and got a new seat in round 2. Do I pay the full fee again?

No. The fee you paid in round 1 is adjusted against the fee for the newly allotted seat. You pay only the balance, if any, by 30 November 2026, 4 PM.
