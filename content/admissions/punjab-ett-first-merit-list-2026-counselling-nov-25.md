**The Punjab ETT first merit list is out today, 21 November 2026.** SCERT Punjab has published the list for [D.El.Ed. admission](/admissions/) to the two-year Diploma in Elementary Education programme for the 2026-28 session. Candidates who took the entrance test can now check their merit rank and prepare for counselling. Government DIETs begin counselling on 25 November, and private colleges start from 28 November.

The list comes a day after the [Punjab ETT result](/results/) was declared on 20 November. Guru Nanak Dev University, Amritsar, conducted the entrance test on 15 November on behalf of SCERT Punjab. A total of 4,968 candidates qualified. Anmoldeep Kaur topped the test with 144 marks out of 150. Mankhushpreet Singh stood second with 139 marks, and Bharti Devi took the third spot with 138 marks.

The final answer key for Sets A, B, C and D is available on the <a href="https://ps.ctestservices.com/etadee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official exam portal</a>. Candidates who have not checked their scorecard yet can log in there with their registration details.

The D.El.Ed. course runs for two years and trains teachers for primary schools across Punjab. Graduates take up teaching posts in government and private primary schools after finishing the course and meeting the state rules for teacher recruitment. That is why this merit list matters so much to candidates who want a teaching career in the state.

## Counselling starts November 25 at government DIETs

SCERT Punjab has issued a public notice for the first counselling. Shortlisted candidates must attend counselling at their district DIET on the dates given for their merit position. The notice carries the reporting dates and the venue details, so read it in full before you travel.

> "Counselling for government DIETs starts on November 25. Private colleges follow from November 28. The first seat allotment is expected in December."

Seats in government DIETs and private D.El.Ed. colleges will be filled on merit, keeping in mind eligibility, category-wise seats and the number of seats available. A name on the merit list does not by itself confirm a seat. Admission is completed only after document verification and fee payment at the allotted college.

The merit list carries each candidate's name, roll number or application number, entrance marks, merit rank, category and counselling eligibility. Candidates should check every detail in the list before the counselling day. If a name, mark or category looks wrong, contact the admission office through the portal at once instead of waiting for the reporting date.

## How to check your name in the merit list

Follow these steps on the <a href="https://punjabdeledadmission.gndu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official D.El.Ed. admission portal</a>:

- Open punjabdeledadmission.gndu.ac.in and look for the first merit list link for the 2026-28 session.
- Open the list and search for your name or roll number.
- Check your marks, merit rank and category in the list.
- Note the counselling date and the DIET named against your rank.
- Read the counselling notice on <a href="https://www.ssapunjab.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ssapunjab.org</a> for the reporting time and venue.

Do not miss the counselling date given for your merit position. Admission runs on the published schedule, and a missed date can cost you the seat even if your rank is good.

## Documents to keep ready for counselling

Keep originals and photocopies ready. The counselling notice lists the final set of documents, but candidates should carry these:

- Punjab ETT entrance exam admit card
- Punjab ETT result or scorecard
- Class 10 certificate and marksheet
- Class 12 certificate and marksheet
- Graduation documents, if applicable
- Category certificate, if applicable
- Punjab domicile certificate, if required
- Valid photo ID proof
- Passport-size photographs
- Counselling registration or application details

Check that the name and date of birth match across every document. A mismatch at the verification desk can hold up admission even after a seat is allotted.

## What happens after counselling

After the first round of counselling, SCERT Punjab will publish the first seat allotment in December 2026. Allotted candidates will then report to their college for physical verification of documents and payment of the course fee. The exact allotment date will be announced on the official admission portal.

Teacher aspirants have had a busy week. The [UP Special TET result](/results/up-special-tet-result-2026-38902-qualified/) also came out this week, with 38,902 in-service teachers qualifying. For Punjab candidates, the next four days matter most. Check the list today, read the counselling notice, and keep your documents in one folder so nothing is left for the last morning.
