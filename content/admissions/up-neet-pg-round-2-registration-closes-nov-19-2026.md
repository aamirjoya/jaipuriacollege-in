**If you are aiming for a postgraduate medical seat in Uttar Pradesh, today is the last day to act. Registration for UP NEET PG 2026 Round 2 closes at 11 AM on 19 November, and the state merit list comes out later the same day.**

The Directorate General of Medical Education and Training (DGME), Uttar Pradesh, opened the Round 2 window on 16 November at 2 PM. The round covers state-quota seats in MD, MS, Diploma, and DNB courses at government and private medical colleges, institutes, and universities across the state. The [UP NEET PG Round 2 counselling](/admissions/) runs on the official portal, upneet.gov.in.

## Two cut-offs today, and they are not the same

This is the detail that trips up candidates every year. The form and the fee have different closing times on 19 November.

The online registration form closes at 11 AM. The fee and security deposit payment window stays open three hours longer, till 2 PM. A form submitted after 11 AM will not be accepted, even if the fee is paid. So finish the form first, then pay.

The registration fee is Rs 3,000, and it is non-refundable. The security deposit also has to be paid by 2 PM today. Its amount depends on the type of seat and college you are applying for, so check the figure shown on the portal for your category before you pay.

## Who can register in Round 2

Any candidate who qualified in NEET PG 2026 and wants a state-quota PG medical seat in Uttar Pradesh can register. This includes fresh candidates who did not take part in Round 1, and candidates who took part in Round 1 but did not get a seat or did not join one.

Your NEET PG score and rank decide your place in the state merit list. The list will show your roll number, name, category, NEET PG marks, and rank. It will be published as a PDF on upneet.gov.in. No separate time has been announced for the merit list, so check the portal through the day.

> "Submit the form before 11 AM. The fee window stays open till 2 PM, but the form itself closes at 11 AM. Do not leave either step for the last hour."

## What happens after today

Once the merit list is out, the next step is choice filling. Candidates in the merit list can fill and lock their college and course choices from 20 November at 11 AM till 25 November at 11 AM. Your choices decide which seat you can get, so list them in your real order of preference and lock them before the window ends.

The Round 2 seat allotment result is scheduled for 26 November 2026. Candidates who get a seat will then have to report to the allotted college and complete admission formalities. The reporting schedule will be published on the portal, so keep checking it after 26 November.

## This is the state round, not the all-India round

Many candidates are mixing up two different processes right now, so here is the clear picture. The all-India [NEET PG Round 2 result is on hold](/results/neet-pg-round-2-result-on-hold-madras-hc-order-nov-18-2026/) after the Madras High Court passed an interim stay on 17 November, and the Medical Counselling Committee has paused seat processing till the court gives further directions. That hold applies to the all-India quota counselling run by MCC.

The UP state round is separate. It is run by DGME Uttar Pradesh on its own portal, and it is going ahead as per the schedule in the 13 November notice. The hold on the all-India round does not stop UP state counselling.

Karnataka is running its own state round on a similar track. The [Karnataka NEET PG Round 2 option entry](/admissions/karnataka-neet-pg-round-2-option-entry-closes-nov-19-2026/) also closes today, 19 November, at 10 AM, with its provisional allotment list due on 20 November.

## How to register in five steps

- Go to the <a href="https://upneet.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UP NEET portal</a> and open the PG 2026 registration link.
- Log in or register with your NEET PG 2026 details, and fill in your personal and academic information.
- Upload the documents the portal asks for, and check every entry before you submit.
- Pay the Rs 3,000 registration fee and the security deposit online before 2 PM today.
- Save the confirmation page and the payment receipt. You will need both for choice filling and reporting.

If the portal is slow, do not keep refreshing the payment page. Wait for the confirmation screen, and check your payment status on the portal before you try again. A failed payment can still show as pending for a while.

The full Round 2 schedule, including the brochure and notices, is posted on the <a href="https://www.dgme.up.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DGME Uttar Pradesh website</a>. If any date changes, the revised notice will appear there first.
