**The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has published the final seat allotment for the NEET UG 2026 special stray vacancy round. Of the 47 MBBS seats on offer, 20 went to candidates under the management quota. The other 27 are still empty. If your name is on the list, today, November 14, is your last day to pay the fee and join your college.**

KEA released the result on November 13. The allotment list is a PDF on the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA portal</a>. You can search it by your name or rank and download a copy for your records. This was the last round of [Karnataka NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) for this session, and it covered MBBS seats only. All BDS seats had already been filled in earlier rounds.

## 20 seats filled, 27 left vacant

The numbers tell the story of this round. KEA had 47 medical seats to fill. It allotted 20 of them, all under the MBBS-MNG (management quota) category. That leaves 27 seats vacant, including 15 management seats in government medical colleges.

Two colleges took the biggest share. Bagalkote Medical College and Research Institute in Navanagar, Bagalkote, got seven seats. PES University Institute of Medical Sciences and Research in Electronic City, Bengaluru, got five. The other eight seats went to candidates at other colleges named in the allotment list.

> "Of the 47 medical seats available, 20 have been allotted, while 27 remain vacant." (KEA notice, as reported by The Telegraph)

## Why KEA ran this extra round

Four stray vacancy rounds had already taken place before this one. The fourth round allotment had come out on October 26. Even after that, 47 MBBS seats were still empty. KEA then announced an extended special stray vacancy round to give those seats one more chance to find students.

Candidates who wanted a seat in this round had to pay an advance deposit of Rs 25 lakh by 4 pm on November 12. After that, they could enter their college options till 10 am on November 13. KEA published the allotment result later the same day, after 12.30 pm.

The same week has been busy for medical admissions in Karnataka. The [Karnataka NEET PG round 1 allotment](/admissions/karnataka-neet-pg-2026-round-1-final-allotment-3268-seats-report-nov-14/) gave MD and MS seats to 3,268 candidates, and reporting for that round also ends on November 14. In Maharashtra, the [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray round 1](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-1-result-nov-8-2026/) selection list came out last week, and its round 3 list is due today as well.

## What to do if you got a seat

Time is short. Follow these steps today, November 14, and do not leave anything for tomorrow.

- Open the official KEA portal at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in and download the special stray vacancy round allotment PDF.
- Check your name, rank, allotted college and course in the list.
- Pay the prescribed fee as per the instructions in the notice.
- Report to the allotted college in person with your original documents, including the allotment letter, NEET UG scorecard, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, and Aadhaar card. Carry two sets of photocopies as well, since the college keeps one set at the counter.
- Keep the fee receipt and the admission confirmation slip safe. You will need them later.

KEA has said candidates must complete the admission process by November 14. If you miss the deadline, you can lose the seat. The authority has not announced any extension for this round.

## What happens to the 27 empty seats?

KEA has not said yet whether these 27 seats will go into another round. The seats include management quota places in government medical colleges, which are usually in high demand. For now, candidates who did not get a seat should keep checking the official KEA portal for any fresh notice. Do not trust any message on social media that claims a new round has started. The portal is the only place where a real notice will appear first.

This round closes a long counselling season for Karnataka MBBS aspirants. Four stray rounds, one extended round, and still 27 seats left over. For the 20 candidates who got a seat, the work is simple. Pay the fee, reach the college today, and start your MBBS journey.
