**If you qualified AIAPGET 2026 and still want a PG seat in Ayurveda or Homoeopathy, today is your last chance at IP University. Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University has opened PG Ayurveda, Homoeopathy and Master of Prosthetics and Orthotics (MPO) admissions for the 2026-27 session, and applications close today, November 12. The merit list comes out tomorrow, November 13, and offline counselling is on November 16.**

This is a fresh window for candidates who missed the earlier rounds. The university has issued separate notices for each programme, so read the notice for your course before you apply. The details below come from the university notices as reported by The Times of India, Education Times and Amar Ujala.

## Who can apply for IPU PG Ayurveda and Homoeopathy

Only candidates who have qualified the All India AYUSH Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET) 2026 can apply for the PG Ayurveda and Homoeopathy seats. These admissions run through offline counselling for the 85% institutional quota seats at the affiliated colleges, according to the Education Times report.

PG Ayurveda seats are offered at Chaudhary Brahm Prakash Ayurved Charak Sansthan in Najafgarh. PG Homoeopathy seats are at Dr. B. R. Sur Homoeopathic Medical College in Moti Bagh. Both are well-known Delhi institutions, so expect strong competition for the limited seats.

If you are an AIAPGET-qualified candidate, submit your application today, November 12. The university will release the merit list of eligible candidates on November 13. Offline counselling for both programmes will then take place on November 16. Keep your AIAPGET scorecard, degree certificate and other documents ready, because counselling moves fast once the merit list is out.

## IPU MPO admission 2026: form, fee and entrance test

The Master of Prosthetics and Orthotics (MPO) programme has a different process. Interested candidates must submit their completed application forms by November 12, 2026, to the University Facilitation Centre at the university's Dwarka Campus.

According to the Times of India report, MPO applicants must attach a demand draft of Rs 2,500, issued in favour of the University Registrar. Admission to the MPO programme will be decided through an entrance examination conducted by the university itself. Read the MPO admission notice carefully before you submit the form, because the notice carries the exam date and the full list of documents.

> "Applications for the postgraduate Ayurveda and Homoeopathy programmes can be submitted offline on November 12, 2026. The university will release the merit list on November 13, followed by offline counselling for both programmes on November 16."

## IPU PG admission 2026: key dates at a glance

- **November 12, 2026:** Last date to apply for PG Ayurveda, Homoeopathy and MPO
- **November 13, 2026:** Merit list for PG Ayurveda and Homoeopathy
- **November 16, 2026:** Offline counselling for PG Ayurveda and Homoeopathy

## How to apply and where to check the notice

Go to the university website at <a href="https://ipu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ipu.ac.in</a> or the admission portal at <a href="https://ipu.admissions.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ipu.admissions.nic.in</a> and open the admission notice for your programme. The notice is the final word on eligibility, documents and the exact mode of submission, so do not rely on second-hand summaries alone.

This IPU window is one of the last PG medical admission chances of the season. The all-India picture is moving too: the [NEET PG 2026 Round 2 counselling](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-revised-dates-nov-17-result/) schedule has been revised, with the final result now due on November 17, while the [MP NEET UG special stray vacancy round](/admissions/mp-neet-ug-2026-special-stray-vacancy-round-choice-filling-nov-11/) is also closing choice filling today. If you are tracking more than one counselling, keep a simple list of dates and check each portal every morning. A single missed deadline can cost a whole year.

## What to do today

- Download the admission notice for your programme from <a href="https://ipu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ipu.ac.in</a> and read it fully.
- Keep your AIAPGET 2026 scorecard, degree and internship documents, ID proof and photographs ready.
- Submit the PG Ayurveda or Homoeopathy application today, November 12, and keep a copy of what you submit.
- For MPO, reach the University Facilitation Centre at the Dwarka Campus with the filled form and the demand draft before the counter closes.
- Check the merit list tomorrow, November 13, and report for offline counselling on November 16 with all original documents.

Do not wait for the evening. Counters close, websites slow down, and a last-hour rush is the most common reason good candidates miss out. If you are eligible, apply now.
