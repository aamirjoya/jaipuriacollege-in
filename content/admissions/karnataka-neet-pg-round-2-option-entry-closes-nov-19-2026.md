**The clock is ticking for Karnataka NEET PG aspirants. The option entry window for Round 2 counselling closes tomorrow, 19 November, at 10 AM. If you have not locked your college choices by then, the portal will not let you change them later.**

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) opened the window on 17 November at 11 AM. Since then, eligible candidates have been able to add, change, or delete their college and course choices for MD, MS, and PG Diploma seats. The [Karnataka NEET PG Round 2 counselling](/admissions/) runs on the official portal, cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.

## The deposit deadline has already passed

Here is the part many candidates miss. Only those who paid the caution deposit can enter options in Round 2. Candidates who picked Choice 3 in Round 1, and those who entered options in Round 1 but got no seat, had to pay Rs 1,50,000 as caution deposit. For SC and ST candidates, the amount was Rs 50,000.

The last date to pay was today, 18 November, till 4 PM. That window is now closed. If you did not pay in time, the portal will not allow option entry for this round. Candidates who picked Choice 2 in Round 1 did not have to pay the deposit, and they cannot rearrange their options in this round either.

KEA displayed the Round 2 seat matrix on 17 November after 11 AM, so candidates could see the open seats before fixing their order of preference.

> "Check the seat matrix first, then fix your choices in order of preference. Once the window closes at 10 AM tomorrow, no changes are possible."

## What happens after 19 November

The schedule after option entry is short and tight. KEA will publish the provisional allotment list on 20 November, after 11 AM. The final allotment result will follow on 21 November.

Candidates who get a seat in Round 2 must pay the fee and complete the choice process between 23 and 26 November. They also need to deposit their original documents at the KEA office in Bengaluru between 23 and 27 November. You have to book a slot for the document deposit, so do not walk in without one.

This state round moves on its own track. The all-India [NEET PG Round 2 result is on hold](/results/neet-pg-round-2-result-on-hold-madras-hc-order-nov-18-2026/) after the Madras High Court passed an interim order on 17 November, and the Medical Counselling Committee has paused seat processing till the court gives further directions. Karnataka's state counselling, run by KEA, is continuing as per its own schedule.

## How to fill your choices before 10 AM tomorrow

Follow these steps on the KEA portal:

- Open <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cetonline.karnataka.gov.in</a> and log in with your PGET 2026 credentials.
- Open the Round 2 option entry link and check the seat matrix shown for your category.
- Add your preferred colleges and courses in order, with your most wanted college at the top.
- Remove any college you would not join even if allotted, because an allotment in Round 2 has to be accepted.
- Save your choices and take a printout or screenshot of the final list for your records.

Candidates who got no seat in Round 1 will see their Round 1 options after they log in and give consent on the portal. From there, they can change the order, add new options, or delete ones they no longer want.

## Three things to check tonight

First, confirm your caution deposit payment shows as successful on the portal. A failed payment means no option entry, even if the money left your account. Second, check the fee details of every college on your list, because the fee must be paid between 23 and 26 November if you get a seat. Third, keep your original documents ready now, since the document deposit window at KEA Bengaluru opens on 23 November and slots fill fast.

Karnataka ran a separate [special stray round for NEET UG seats](/admissions/karnataka-neet-ug-special-stray-round-allotment-20-mbbs-seats/) earlier this month, and that round also worked on a tight two-day window. The lesson from that round was the same: candidates who waited till the last hour faced slow portals and missed slots.

If you are still deciding between two colleges, put the one you truly prefer first. A higher preference that you later regret cannot be undone once the allotment is out. Use tonight to check the seat matrix one more time, talk to your family about fees and distance, and lock your list well before 10 AM tomorrow.
