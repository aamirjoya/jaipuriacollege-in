**Today, 25 November, is the last day to fill and lock choices for Bihar NEET PG 2026 Round 2 counselling. The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) has revised the schedule for MD, MS, PG Diploma and DNB seats in government and private medical colleges across the state. The provisional seat allotment result will come out on 28 November.**

The board issued the revised dates in notice BCECEB(PGMAC)-2026/05 on 21 November. The notice changes the Round 2 dates that were announced three days earlier. All other terms stay the same as in the board's main counselling notice, BCECEB(PGMAC)-2026/01, dated 18 October 2026. Choice filling is open to candidates who registered for PGMAC 2026 in the combined Round 1 and Round 2 registration window.

## The revised dates at a glance

| Round 2 event | Revised date |
| --- | --- |
| Resignation through free exit from Round 1 | 22 to 23 November 2026 |
| Online choice filling and editing | 24 November 2026 |
| Last date for choice filling, editing and locking | 25 November 2026 |
| Provisional seat allotment result | 28 November 2026 |
| Download of provisional allotment order | 28 November to 1 December 2026 |
| Document verification and admission | 30 November to 1 December 2026 |

## What the new dates mean for you

The free exit window closed on 23 November. Candidates who held a Round 1 seat and wanted to leave it without penalty had those two days to resign. That window is now over, and the board has moved on to Round 2 choice filling.

Choice filling opened on 24 November and ends today. The 25 November deadline covers filling, editing and locking, so finish all three steps today. A choice list that is filled but left unlocked does not count for allotment. Once the window closes, the board will process the locked choices and publish the provisional allotment result on 28 November.

The state round runs alongside the national counselling. The Medical Counselling Committee has already released the [NEET PG Round 2 final allotment result](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-final-allotment-20707-seats-report-nov-25/), with 20,707 seats allotted, and candidates who got a seat there must report by 25 November, 10 PM. More than [10,105 seats are blocked](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-10105-seats-blocked-float-rule-review/) after Round 2, held by candidates who also hold a state allotment, and the government is looking at the float rule after this round.

> Fill, edit and lock your choices today. The 25 November deadline covers all three steps, and the allotment on 28 November runs on the locked list.

## Round 1 closing ranks to guide your choices

Round 1 closing ranks show how far down the merit list each branch went in the last round. Treat them as a guide. Seats, choices and the number of candidates change in Round 2, so the cutoffs will move. Compare your rank with the closing rank in your own category, not another category's column.

| Branch (government colleges) | Round 1 closing rank, UR general |
| --- | --- |
| MD Dermatology, Venereology and Leprosy | 1,391 |
| MD Radio Diagnosis | 2,640 |
| MD General Medicine | 4,610 |
| MD Paediatrics | 8,319 |
| MS Obstetrics and Gynaecology | 10,071 |
| MS General Surgery | 11,507 |

DNB seats closed much further down the list in Round 1. DNB Dermatology and Venereology closed at 12,759 and DNB General Medicine at 43,374 in the general category. If your rank sits near a branch's closing rank, keep that branch in your list. If it sits far below it, keep it only if you genuinely want that seat, and add realistic options below it.

## How to fill and lock your choices

1. Open the <a href="https://bceceboard.bihar.gov.in/PGMACIndex.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official PGMAC page on the BCECEB portal</a> and log in through the PGMAC 2026 candidate login.
2. Fill your college and course choices in order of preference, with the branch you want most at the top.
3. Save the list, read it once, then lock it. Keep a printout or screenshot of the locked list.
4. Check the provisional allotment result on 28 November on the same portal.
5. If you get a seat, download the allotment order between 28 November and 1 December, then report for document verification on 30 November or 1 December with the documents named in the order.

If the portal does not open or your login fails, write to the board's helpdesk at helpdesk.bceceboard@bihar.gov.in. BCECEB also ran the [Bihar NEET UG stray vacancy round](/admissions/bihar-neet-ug-stray-vacancy-allotment-result-oct-27-2026/) for undergraduate seats last month, and the same portal carries both UG and PG counselling notices.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does Bihar NEET PG Round 2 choice filling close?**
Choice filling, editing and locking for Round 2 ends on 25 November 2026. Fill and lock your choices on the official BCECEB portal before the window closes.

**When will the Bihar NEET PG Round 2 result come out?**
BCECEB will publish the provisional seat allotment result on 28 November 2026. The allotment order can be downloaded from 28 November to 1 December.

**Who can fill choices in Bihar NEET PG Round 2?**
Candidates who completed PGMAC 2026 registration in the combined Round 1 and Round 2 window can fill choices. Only NEET PG 2026 qualified candidates are eligible.

**When is document verification and admission for Round 2?**
Document verification and admission will take place on 30 November and 1 December 2026. Follow the instructions in your allotment order for the reporting centre and documents.
