**MICA Ahmedabad has kept the MICAT 2027 registration window open since September, and it shuts on 21 November 2026 at 11:50 PM.** That leaves seven days for MBA aspirants who want a seat at the Mudra Institute of Communications, Ahmedabad. MICAT I, the first round, takes place on 5 December, and the admit card comes out on 3 December.

MICAT is the institute-level entrance test for MICA, mandatory for every programme in the 2027 intake. The flagship courses are the two-year PGDM and PGDM-C, and MICA is known across India for advertising, media and brand management.

## 21 November is the last date, and a second round follows

Missing the 21 November deadline does not end your chances. MICA runs the test twice in one cycle. MICAT II registration opens on 25 November 2026 and runs till 30 January 2027. The second test is on 12 February 2027, with the admit card out on 10 February and scores on 28 February.

You register and pay separately for each round, and MICA considers the better of your two MICAT scores if you take both. That makes the December attempt a low-risk shot. The admit card is out on 3 December, scores on 18 December, and interviews follow in March 2027.

## Who can apply, and what it costs

Any graduate can apply. You need a bachelor's degree in any discipline, with at least 50 per cent marks or an equivalent CGPA. Final-year students can apply too, with documents due by 30 June and 31 December 2027.

The fee is Rs 2,500 for one programme, plus Rs 500 for each additional programme. It is non-refundable, and students who apply for both MICAT rounds pay the fee twice.

One rule catches many first-time applicants. For the PGDM, PGDM-C and PGCM-SM programmes, a MICAT score alone is not enough. You also need a valid CAT 2026, XAT 2027 or GMAT score, added to your form by 30 January 2027. It carries 20 per cent weight in the final merit list, as per reports. The other programmes need MICAT alone.

> MICAT is not a replacement for CAT. It sits next to it. You need both scores to get into the flagship PGDM programmes, and the better of your two MICAT attempts is the one that counts.

## Seven programmes, 428 seats in all

MICAT 2027 opens the door to seven programmes and 428 seats. The flagship PGDM-C has 180 seats and the PGDM has 60. The rest are one-year diplomas and a certificate programme in sports management, advertising, digital media and strategic communication, plus an 8-seat Fellow Programme.

For PGDM and PGDM-C, MICA weighs your CAT, XAT or GMAT score with your MICAT score, then calls shortlisted candidates for a group exercise and interview. Other programmes add an interview after MICAT.

## What the test looks like

MICAT is a computer-based test held at centres in 48 cities across India, from Ahmedabad and Guwahati to Jaipur, Kochi and Visakhapatnam. Your centre is printed on the admit card.

The test has three sections. Section A is the Psychometric Test, which checks personality traits. It has no right or wrong answers and no negative marking, but candidates who fail it are not checked further. Section B is the Descriptive Test, a writing test: arguments for and against a statement, your own view, and a story from a picture sequence. Section C is the Aptitude Test: divergent and convergent thinking, verbal ability, quantitative ability and data interpretation.

In past years, the test ran for about 135 minutes, and wrong answers in the aptitude section cost 0.25 marks each. MICA has not published the exact duration or marking scheme for 2027, so treat these as a guide from past papers.

## How to apply in six steps

1. Go to the <a href="https://www.mica.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MICA admissions portal</a> and open the MICAT 2027 registration link.
2. Register with your mobile number, name, email ID, state, city and programme, then enter the captcha.
3. Verify your email ID through the link MICA sends. It is used for all communication and cannot be changed later.
4. Log in and fill the online form with your academic background, experience and other details.
5. Upload the required documents, pay the fee online and submit the form.
6. For PGDM, PGDM-C or PGCM-SM, add your CAT 2026 score, XAT 2027 registration number or GMAT score in the form by 30 January 2027.

The form closes at 11:50 PM on 21 November, so finish it a day early if you can. Payment failures and slow uploads have cost candidates their seats in past years.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the last date to apply for MICAT 2027?**

MICAT I registration closes on 21 November 2026 at 11:50 PM. MICAT II registration opens on 25 November 2026 and runs till 30 January 2027.

**What is the MICAT 2027 application fee?**

Rs 2,500 for one programme, plus Rs 500 for each additional programme. The fee is non-refundable, and you pay separately for MICAT I and MICAT II.

**Is a CAT, XAT or GMAT score compulsory for MICAT 2027?**

Yes, for the PGDM, PGDM-C and PGCM-SM programmes. You need a valid CAT 2026, XAT 2027 or GMAT score along with your MICAT score. The PGCM-AMC, PGCM-DMMC and Strategic Communication programmes need MICAT alone.

**Can I appear for both MICAT I and MICAT II?**

Yes. You register and pay the fee separately for each round, and MICA considers the better of your two MICAT scores for shortlisting.
