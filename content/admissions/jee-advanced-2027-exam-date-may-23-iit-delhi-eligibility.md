**IIT Delhi has fixed the date for JEE Advanced 2027. The exam will be held on May 23, 2027, a Sunday, and the institute has opened the official website for the exam at jeeadv.ac.in. If you are aiming for an IIT seat next year, the countdown has now begun.**

The announcement came on November 13, with the full eligibility criteria. Only the top 2,50,000 candidates in [JEE Main 2027](/admissions/jee-main-2027-nta-digilocker-advisory-nov-26-deadline/) will get a shot at this exam, and the rules on age, attempts and Class 12 year are strict.

## May 23, 2027: the date every JEE aspirant was waiting for

IIT Delhi, the organising institute for JEE Advanced 2027, announced the exam date on November 13 and launched the official website on the same day. The exam will be held in computer-based test mode, and all future announcements will appear on the <a href="https://jeeadv.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JEE Advanced 2027 website</a>.

What IIT Delhi has not announced yet is the registration schedule. There is no application start date, no last date, no fee detail and no admit card date at this point.

> "The exam date is out, but the real test starts now. Your JEE Main 2027 rank decides whether you even get to sit for JEE Advanced."

## Only the top 2.5 lakh in JEE Main 2027 make the cut

You must be among the top 2,50,000 successful candidates in the B.E./B.Tech. paper of JEE Main 2027. The category-wise share is fixed: Open 40.5 percent, GEN-EWS 10 percent, OBC-NCL 27 percent, SC 15 percent and ST 7.5 percent. A 5 percent horizontal reservation applies for PwD candidates within each category.

If two or more candidates secure the same rank at the cut-off, the total can go above 2.5 lakh. That is why the [JEE Main 2027](/admissions/jee-main-2027-nta-digilocker-advisory-nov-26-deadline/) result matters so much. Your rank in that exam is the door to JEE Advanced.

## Age limit, Class 12 year and the two-attempt rule

You must have been born on or after October 1, 2002. SC, ST and PwD candidates get five years of relaxation, so they must have been born on or after October 1, 1997.

You should have appeared for the Class 12 or equivalent exam for the first time in either 2026 or 2027, with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Students who first appeared for Class 12 in 2025 or earlier are not eligible, with one exception. If your board declared the 2024-25 results on or after June 12, 2025, you can still apply if you meet the other conditions.

Attempts are capped at two. A candidate can appear for JEE Advanced a maximum of two times in two consecutive years. More than 12,000 students had signed a petition asking for a third attempt, but the 2027 rules keep the old limit.

## If you already hold an IIT seat, you cannot appear again

If you were admitted to an IIT under any academic programme listed in the JoSAA 2026 Business Rules, you cannot appear for JEE Advanced 2027, even if you left the programme or your admission was cancelled after you joined.

Students admitted to an IIT preparatory programme for the first time in 2026 can appear. So can students who were allotted an IIT seat through JoSAA 2026 but did not report, withdrew before the final round, or had their seat cancelled before the final round.

Foreign nationals and OCI/PIO candidates follow a different route. They do not need to appear for JEE Main 2027 and can register directly. Their seats are supernumerary, capped at 10 percent of the total seats in each IIT programme, and they get no reservation.

## A basic calculator on screen, and what to do next

Candidates will also get an on-screen basic, non-scientific calculator with a 10 to 12-digit display during the exam.

For now, your plan is simple, and it starts with JEE Main 2027:

- Focus on JEE Main 2027 first, because only the top 2.5 lakh ranks qualify for Advanced.
- Check your birth date and Class 12 passing year against the five eligibility rules above.
- Bookmark jeeadv.ac.in and check it every few days for the registration dates.

The date is out, the rules are out, and the next update from IIT Delhi will be the registration schedule. Students who followed [JEE Advanced 2026](/admissions/jee-advanced-2026-branch-preferences-jic-report/) know how fast cut-offs move, so check the [admissions section](/admissions/) regularly and do not miss it.
