**West Bengal's engineering colleges have just had their strongest admission season in years.** Admissions to engineering and pharmacy courses through the WBJEEB counselling rose by 75.4% in 2026, with 27,599 students taking seats compared to 15,729 last year. The figures, shared by state Higher Education Minister Jagannath Chattopadhyay on October 12, show a sharp revival in student interest in the state's technical education.

The jump is not a small one. It means 11,870 more students secured an engineering or pharmacy seat in West Bengal this year than last. Of the 27,599 admissions, 16,409 came through centralised counselling and 11,190 through decentralised counselling. The numbers also show that 2,156 students who first took admission in the centralised round moved to their preferred colleges during the decentralised round, which is why the decentralised figure is counted the way it is.

## Degree colleges also recorded a 49.9% jump in admissions

West Bengal engineering admissions made the headlines, but the state's degree colleges had a strong year too. Admissions through the West Bengal Centralised Admission Portal (WBCAP) and the West Bengal Decentralised Admission Portal (WBDAP) rose by 49.9%, from 2,61,692 in 2025 to 3,92,227 in 2026. That is a rise of 1,30,535 students.

WBCAP alone accounted for 3,78,931 admissions, while WBDAP added 13,296. Students seeking college admission in India often track these [admission alerts](/admissions/) closely, and this year's Bengal numbers stand out. The Times of India reported that around 3.9 lakh students took admission across 460 colleges in the state, with about 3.8 lakh coming through the centralised portal across three phases. Nearly 2.6 lakh of these admissions were in government and government-aided colleges, which is 1.3 lakh more than last year.

## What changed this year

The minister said the surge reflects renewed trust in the state's higher education system, pointing to the new government led by Suvendu Adhikari. He added that in the past 15 years students had lost faith in Bengal's higher education infrastructure, and that this year's response shows a change.

Timing played a part too. The final decentralised phase closed on Sunday, October 11, which the state treats as the likely end of this year's UG admission cycle. Engineering admissions in India have stayed strong overall this year, as [JEE Advanced 2026](/admissions/jee-advanced-2026-branch-preferences-jic-report/) data also showed rising applicant interest at the national level.

Students waiting on counselling rounds elsewhere should note how much [centralised counselling](/admissions/tg-cpget-final-phase-counselling-2026/) shaped the final numbers in Bengal. Two rounds did most of the work: the centralised round filled the bulk of seats, and the decentralised round cleaned up the rest while letting students upgrade.

## What this means if you are applying in 2027

A 75.4% rise tells you competition for Bengal's engineering seats is real again. Government engineering colleges filled fast this year, with all seats taken even in the first counselling round, according to earlier statements by the Chief Minister. Here is what that means for next year's applicants.

- Register for WBJEEB counselling in the first round itself. Waiting for later rounds narrows your options.
- Fill your choice list wide. The 2,156 students who upgraded later did so because the decentralised round existed, but you cannot count on it.
- Keep an eye on the <a href="https://wbjeeb.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official WBJEEB portal</a> for counselling schedules and seat matrices instead of relying on forwards and rumours.
- For degree courses, watch both the centralised and decentralised portals. The centralised portal at <a href="https://wbcap.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">wbcap.in</a> handled nearly 3.8 lakh admissions this year.

> The 75.4% jump is not just a statistic on paper. It means nearly 12,000 more students found an engineering or pharmacy seat in West Bengal this year than last, and that government colleges are full again.

West Bengal has not seen this kind of admission response in a long time. For students in the state, the message is clear: the seats are there, but the crowd is back. Plan early, apply on time, and keep the official portals on your radar.

## FAQ

### How many students got admission through WBJEEB counselling in 2026?

27,599 students took admission through WBJEEB counselling for engineering and pharmacy courses in 2026, up from 15,729 in 2025. That is a 75.4% rise.

### What are WBCAP and WBDAP?

WBCAP is the West Bengal Centralised Admission Portal and WBDAP is the Decentralised Admission Portal for undergraduate degree courses in the state's colleges. Together they recorded 3,92,227 admissions in 2026, up 49.9% from 2025.

### Is the West Bengal UG admission process over for 2026?

Yes, most likely. The final decentralised phase closed on October 11, 2026, which the state data treats as the end of this year's UG admission cycle.

### Where can I check the official WBJEEB counselling details?
