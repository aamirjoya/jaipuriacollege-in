**The Ayush Admissions Central Counselling Committee has put AIAPGET 2026 Round 2 counselling on hold. Registration was to run from November 16 to 19, and the seat allotment result was to come out on November 21. All of it now stands suspended, and no new dates have been announced.**

AACCC issued the notice on November 16, the same day registration was to open. It gave administrative reasons for the hold and said a revised schedule will follow on the <a href="https://aaccc.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AACCC portal</a>. Candidates waiting to register or fill choices have nothing to do right now except watch that portal.

This is the second time AACCC has stopped the process in this cycle. On October 26, it [put the whole counselling on hold](/education-news/aiapget-2026-counselling-put-on-hold-oct-27/) for the same reason. It then released a revised schedule on October 30, and Round 1 ran from October 30 to November 3. The Round 1 result came out on November 5, and reporting ran till November 13. Round 1 allotments are not affected by the new notice.

## The dates that now stand suspended

These were the Round 2 dates in AACCC's revised schedule of October 30. Every one of them is on hold now.

| Round 2 activity | Earlier date | Status now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration and fee payment | November 16 to 19, 2026 | Withheld |
| Choice filling and locking | November 17 to 19, 2026 | Withheld |
| Processing of seat allotment | November 20, 2026 | Withheld |
| Seat allotment result | November 21, 2026 | Withheld |
| Reporting at allotted institute | November 22 to 26, 2026 | Withheld |

## What Round 2 covers

[AIAPGET 2026 counselling](/admissions/aiapget-2026-counselling-round-1-registration-oct-26-schedule/) is the central route to MD and MS seats in Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani and Homoeopathy colleges. It covers 15 percent All India Quota seats, along with seats in deemed universities and central institutions. The exam was held on August 22, 2026, and the result came out on September 18.

Round 2 matters because it opens fresh registration. Candidates who did not register in Round 1 can join now, and candidates who missed a seat in Round 1 can try again with new choices.

> Check aaccc.gov.in every day. No fee is due until the window reopens, and no new date has been fixed yet.

## The fee you will pay when the window reopens

Do not pay anything till the registration window reopens. When it does, the registration fee is the same as in Round 1.

| Institute type | Category | Registration fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government, aided and central universities | UR, EWS, OBC-NCL | Rs 2,000 |
| Government, aided and central universities | SC, ST, PwBD | Rs 1,000 |
| Deemed universities | All categories | Rs 5,000 |

A refundable security deposit is also charged at registration. The amount depends on the type of institute, so check the fresh notice when it comes out.

## What happens to Round 3 and the stray round

Later rounds depend on Round 2 finishing on time, so their dates may also move. As per the earlier schedule, Round 3 registration was to run from November 30 to December 2, with the result on December 4 and reporting from December 5 to 10. The stray vacancy round was set for December 14 to 16, with the result on December 18 and reporting till December 27.

None of those dates is safe to plan around right now. AACCC will confirm them only after it fixes the Round 2 dates. Students counting on Round 3 should keep documents ready and wait for the revised calendar.

## What to do now

- Check aaccc.gov.in daily for the revised Round 2 schedule and the updated seat matrix.
- Keep your AIAPGET 2026 scorecard, admit card, degree certificate and internship completion certificate ready.
- Rework your college preference list using the Round 1 allotment data, so you can fill choices fast when the window opens.
- If you joined a college in Round 1, keep your joining documents safe. Your seat is not affected.
- Do not trust dates shared on WhatsApp groups or coaching sites. Only the AACCC portal counts.

AACCC did the same thing last year too. It withheld the AIAPGET Round 2 allotment result in 2025 and then resumed with a revised schedule. A hold is a delay, not a cancellation. The wait is hard, but the process has come back from this before. Our [admissions page](/admissions/) will carry the revised dates the moment AACCC posts them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is AIAPGET 2026 Round 2 counselling cancelled?**

No. AACCC has put Round 2 on hold until further notice. It has not been cancelled. A revised schedule and seat matrix will be published on the official portal, aaccc.gov.in.

**Do I need to register again for Round 2?**

Yes. Fresh registration is needed for every round of AIAPGET counselling. When the window reopens, register on aaccc.gov.in and pay the fee for your category.

**When will the revised Round 2 schedule come out?**

AACCC has not given a date. After the October 26 hold, the revised schedule came on October 30, so a wait of a few days is normal. Nothing is fixed this time, so check aaccc.gov.in daily.

**Does the hold affect my Round 1 seat?**

No. Round 1 allotments stand. Reporting for Round 1 ran from November 6 to 13. If you joined a college, keep your joining documents safe.
