**If you got a seat in MP NEET PG Round 1, your wait is over. The Directorate of Medical Education (DME), Madhya Pradesh, has restarted admissions, and you have from today, 27 November, to 30 November 2026 to report at your allotted college. The window closes at 6 PM on 30 November, and you must report in person.**

The restart follows a judgement passed on 26 November 2026 by the Madhya Pradesh High Court at Jabalpur, in W.P. 44909/2026 (Dr Sarvjeet Singh and another vs the State of Madhya Pradesh). The DME issued a fresh notice the same day with a revised schedule. Admissions had been on hold since early November, after an interim order in the same case, so students allotted seats have waited more than three weeks for this window.

The [MP NEET PG Round 1 allotment result](/admissions/mp-neet-pg-2026-round-1-allotment-result-reporting-oct-30-nov-10/) came out on 29 October. The first reporting window, 30 October to 10 November, never ran its full course. This new schedule replaces it.

## The new dates, in one table

| What you have to do | Date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Report at your allotted college in person: document check, upgradation choice, admission | 27 to 30 November 2026, up to 6 PM |
| Resign or cancel your admission online | 27 to 30 November 2026, up to 6 PM |
| Change your upgradation choice in your candidate login | Up to 30 November 2026, 11:59 PM |

The schedule covers MD, MS, Post MBBS DNB and PG Diploma seats in government and private medical colleges across Madhya Pradesh. The full revised schedule is on the <a href="https://dme.mponline.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official DME MP portal</a>.

## What to do at the college

Reporting is not an online form. You have to travel to the college, get your papers checked, and pay the fee. Four days sounds like enough time, but the last day is always crowded, and a missing paper on 30 November leaves no room to fix it.

1. Download your provisional allotment letter from your candidate login on the DME MP portal.
2. Carry originals plus two sets of self-attested photocopies of each document below.
3. Go to the allotted college, get your documents verified, and complete admission.
4. Give your upgradation choice, YES or NO, at the time of admission. You can change it online till 11:59 PM on 30 November.

Carry your NEET PG 2026 admit card, scorecard and rank letter, MBBS degree certificate and marksheets, internship completion certificate, registration certificate from the Medical Council or State Medical Council, a government photo ID, and your MP domicile certificate if you claimed a state seat. Caste, EWS or PwD certificates apply only if you claimed that quota. Check the spelling of your name on every paper before you leave home. A mismatch between your ID, your MBBS certificate and your allotment letter is a common reason students face trouble at the reporting desk.

> Reporting closes at 6 PM on 30 November. A seat in Round 1 is only yours once you report and finish admission, so do not leave it to the last evening.

## Upgradation or resignation: pick one before you leave

If the college and course suit you, take the seat and mark your upgradation choice. Choosing YES keeps you in the running for a better option in Round 2. Read the rules on the portal before you tick it, because an upgrade can change your college.

If you do not want the seat at all, use the online resignation window, which runs till 6 PM on 30 November. No seat leaving bond applies if you resign in this window. Do not simply skip reporting. A formal resignation keeps your record clean for later rounds.

Students waiting for the next round should watch the portal too. The [NEET PG Round 3 registration](/admissions/neet-pg-round-3-registration-dec-1-last-date/) for the all-India quota opened on 27 November and closes on 1 December, so the state and national rounds are moving in the same week.

## Before you travel

The four-day window runs Friday to Monday, so it includes the weekend. If you plan to report on Sunday, 29 November, call the college first and confirm it is receiving candidates that day. Book your travel a day early if you can, keep your fee receipt safe, and carry the allotment letter both on your phone and as a printout.
