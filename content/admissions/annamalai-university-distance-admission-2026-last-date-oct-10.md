**October 10 is the last day to apply for Annamalai University's distance education programmes for 2026-27. The university's official portal, audde.in, lists 10-10-2026 as the final date to complete the admission process. If you were planning to enrol, today is the day.**

The Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) of Annamalai University has opened undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma admissions for the 2026-27 academic session. Everything happens online. One thing catches students out, though: you cannot finish the form without a DEB ID from the UGC. More on that below.

## What is closing today and where to apply

The window covers more than 115 UG, PG, and diploma programmes in open and distance learning mode, all UGC-recognised. The disciplines span Tamil, English, History, Sociology, and Economics, along with Commerce, Political Science, Management, and Mathematics. Science students get Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology. Computer and Information Science, Yoga, and Music are on the list too.

Postgraduate options include MA Tamil, MA English, MA History, MA Economics, MA Political Science, MA Sociology, Master of Social Work, and M.Com in Co-Operative Management. Short diplomas include Goods and Services Tax, Tamil Journalism, English Journalism, and Mass Communication.

The application is submitted only through the university's portal. Go to <a href="https://audde.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">audde.in</a> and use the Apply Online option on the homepage.

## The DEB ID: do this step before you start the form

This is the part most students miss. The Distance Education Bureau (DEB) ID is a unique identifier issued by the University Grants Commission. Every student joining an ODL programme at any recognised Indian institution has to generate one. It links you to the national ODL database and validates your admission.

Create your DEB ID on the <a href="https://deb.ugc.ac.in/StudentDEBId" target="_blank" rel="noopener">UGC's DEB ID portal</a> first, then begin the application. Students who run into trouble generating it can reach the CDOE help desk. The university says it has a dedicated support channel for this problem.

> Do not wait till the evening to start your application. The DEB ID step alone can take time, and portals on the last day are always slow.

## Who can apply

Undergraduate programmes ask for Class 12 or equivalent from a recognised board. Postgraduate programmes need a Bachelor's degree. The exact eligibility, including subject requirements for each course, is given in the official prospectus on the portal. Read it before you pay the fee.

## How to complete the application

The whole process is online. Here is the order the university lists:

- Visit the official website audde.in.
- Create your DEB ID at the UGC portal before starting the form.
- Click Apply Online on the CDOE homepage.
- Fill in your personal and academic details.
- Upload scanned copies of the required documents.
- Pay the tuition and admission fee online.
- Submit the form and save the confirmation slip.

Keep scanned copies of your marksheets, a photo ID, and a recent photograph ready before you begin. After submission, watch the portal for the confirmation of your admission slip and your Learner Support Centre allotment. Study material dispatch and assignment schedules come later.

## Why students pick Annamalai's distance programmes

Annamalai University started its distance education centre in 1979. It is among the oldest ODL providers in Tamil Nadu, and the CDOE now runs more than 115 programmes under UGC recognition. For working professionals and students who cannot attend regular classes, it remains one of the most familiar names in distance learning.
