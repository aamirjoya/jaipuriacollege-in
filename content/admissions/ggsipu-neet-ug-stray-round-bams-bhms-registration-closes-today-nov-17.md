**The clock is running out. Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) will shut registration for its NEET UG 2026 stray vacancy round for BAMS and BHMS at 11 pm tonight, 17 November 2026. Students who want a BAMS or BHMS seat in Delhi have until tonight to finish the form, pay the fee, and upload their documents.**

The GGSIPU NEET UG stray round covers admission to Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS, code 153) and Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS, code 154) for the 2026-27 session. The university put out the notice on 13 November, and the window opened on 15 November at 12 noon. A stray vacancy round fills seats left empty after the earlier rounds, so for many students this is the last shot at a BAMS or BHMS seat this year.

## Who can apply, and who cannot

Only students who qualified NEET UG 2026 can take part. Seats go on the basis of the NEET UG All India Rank and merit, and you must meet the required qualifying percentile.

You are out of this round if you already hold or have joined an MBBS, BAMS or BHMS seat through state quota or All India Quota counselling. The same applies if you got a seat in GGSIPU Round 3 but did not complete admission.

Fresh registration and fresh choice filling are compulsory for everyone. Even if you took part in an earlier round, you must register again and fill your choices again. Do not assume your old form carries over. It does not.

## Dates you need to remember

The stray round runs on a tight clock. Here is the full schedule from the university notice:

- **Fresh registration and correction:** 15 November, 12 noon to 17 November, 11 pm
- **Document upload:** same window, till 17 November, 11 pm
- **Withdrawal from counselling:** till 17 November, 11:59 pm

The portal usually slows down in the final hours, so fill the form early in the day.

Other states are running their own last rounds too. The [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray round](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-3-list-103-seats-report-today-nov-16/) reached its reporting stage this week, and the [MP AYUSH stray round](/admissions/mp-ayush-stray-round-2-registration-nov-15-16-2026/) closed registration over the weekend. Stray seats move fast once the list is out.

> This is the last round of the season. If you miss 11 pm tonight, there is no second window for these BAMS and BHMS seats.

## How to register in 5 steps

1. Open the stray round link on the official IPU admissions portal. <a href="https://ipu.admissions.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Apply on the official portal</a>.
2. Register with your NEET UG 2026 details, or log in if you registered earlier and paid the fee.
3. Pay the non-refundable application fee of Rs 2,500 if you are registering fresh. Students who already paid can skip this step.
4. Fill in your details, edit your category and region if needed, then upload your documents.
5. Fill your choices in order of preference, check every entry once, and submit before 11 pm.

Keep a printout or screenshot of the form and fee receipt in case of a mismatch later.

## Documents you will need

Keep these ready before you open the form: NEET UG 2026 scorecard, Class 12 marksheet, other documents asked for in the notice, and a category certificate if it applies to you.

Check the spellings of your name and your NEET roll number twice. A small error can cost you a seat, and the correction window shuts with registration tonight.

## What happens after tonight

Once registration closes, the university will move to seat allotment. The notice is listed on the <a href="https://ipu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official IPU website</a>, and the full schedule is on the admissions portal. Check both pages for the allotment list and reporting dates, and report to your college with originals if you get a seat. This round is part of the wider [NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) for 2026-27.

Only use the official portals, ipu.ac.in and ipu.admissions.nic.in. Fake links and agents promising seats for money have shown up through the 2026-27 counselling season. No agent can get you a seat. Allotment runs on rank and merit alone.

If you are eligible and you want a BAMS or BHMS seat in Delhi, do not wait for the evening. Open the portal now, finish the form, and keep the receipt safe.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the last date to register for the GGSIPU BAMS, BHMS stray round?

17 November 2026, at 11 pm. The registration and correction window opened on 15 November at 12 noon and closes tonight. You can withdraw from the round till 11:59 pm on the same day.

### Who can apply for the GGSIPU NEET UG stray round?

Candidates who qualified NEET UG 2026 and meet the eligibility rules. You cannot apply if you already hold or have joined an MBBS, BAMS or BHMS seat through state or All India Quota counselling, or if you got a GGSIPU Round 3 seat and did not complete admission.

### What is the registration fee for the GGSIPU stray round?

Rs 2,500. The fee is non-refundable. Students who registered earlier and paid the fee can edit their details and upload documents without paying again.

### Where do I register for GGSIPU BAMS and BHMS counselling?

On the official IPU admissions portal, ipu.admissions.nic.in. Check the stray round notice on ipu.ac.in for the full schedule and instructions.
