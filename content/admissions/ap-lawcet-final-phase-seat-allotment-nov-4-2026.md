**The Andhra Pradesh law counselling wait ends today. The second and final phase seat allotment for AP LAWCET and AP PGLCET 2026 is scheduled for release on November 4, and candidates who get a seat have just three days, November 5 to 7, to report at their allotted college.**

This is the last round of centralised counselling for law admissions in Andhra Pradesh this year. If your name appears in the allotment list, the next step is yours to take quickly. Miss the reporting window and the seat can slip away.

## What is being allotted today

The final phase covers seats left after the first round of counselling. It includes the three-year LLB programme, the five-year integrated LLB programme, and LLM seats through AP PGLCET. The counselling is run by the Commissionerate of Higher Education, Government of Andhra Pradesh, for colleges across the state.

Allotment is based on the web options candidates entered earlier. Students ranked their preferred colleges and courses in order, and seats are filled in that order of preference, subject to rank and seat availability. That is why checking the result early matters. It gives you time to plan the visit to your college.

## How to check your allotment

Follow these steps on the official portal:

- Go to <a href="https://cap.apcfss.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official counselling portal cap.apcfss.in</a>.
- Open the AP LAWCET or AP PGLCET 2026 admissions section.
- Log in with your candidate credentials.
- Open the seat allotment section and download your allotment order.

Keep a printout of the allotment order safe. You will need it during reporting, along with your original certificates. If the portal is slow, wait and try again rather than refreshing again and again. Allotment pages see heavy traffic on result day.

## Report at your college between November 5 and 7

Self-reporting at the allotted college runs from November 5 to 7, 2026. This is not an online formality alone. You need to reach the college, submit your documents, and complete the admission steps the college asks for.

Carry your allotment order, hall ticket, rank card, marksheets, and certificates used during verification. If anything in your documents does not match, sort it out with the college office at once. Three days pass fast, and this is the final phase, so there is no later round to fall back on.

> "This is the final round of counselling for 2026 law admissions in Andhra Pradesh. Students who get a seat should report within the given dates and not wait for another chance."

## The road to today's result

The final phase moved fast. Registration and fee payment ran from October 26 to 28, online certificate verification from October 26 to 29, and web option entry from October 28 to 30. Candidates got one day, October 31, to edit their options. Today's allotment is the outcome of those choices.

Students who took part in this round had already completed certificate verification before entering their preferences. Those who skipped verification or did not enter web options are not part of this allotment.

## What if you did not get a seat

Not getting a seat in the final phase is a hard result, but it is not the end of the road for law studies. Private colleges and universities outside the centralised counselling process run their own admissions. Check their admission notices directly, and keep your documents ready.

For students still in the middle of other counselling processes, the same advice applies everywhere: track the official portal, not rumours on social media. The [MCC NEET UG special stray vacancy round](/admissions/mcc-neet-ug-special-stray-vacancy-round-2026-registration-nov-2-nov-4/) also closed its registration on November 4, and fresh [HITSEEE 2027 registration](/admissions/hitseee-2027-registration-opens-november-4-btech-barch-bdes/) opened the same day for engineering aspirants. Deadlines in this season move quickly, and the official notice is always the final word.

## Key dates at a glance

- Registration and fee payment: October 26 to 28, 2026
- Online certificate verification: October 26 to 29, 2026
- Web options entry: October 28 to 30, 2026
- Web options editing: October 31, 2026
- Seat allotment result: November 4, 2026
- Self-reporting at allotted colleges: November 5 to 7, 2026

For the full schedule and any fresh notice, check <a href="https://cets.apsche.ap.gov.in/APSCHEHome.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the APSCHE official website</a> and the counselling portal. If the allotment list shows your name, start your reporting plan today itself. Three days is enough, but only if you move on day one.
