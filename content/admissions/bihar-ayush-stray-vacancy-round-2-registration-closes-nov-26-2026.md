**Registration for Bihar AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2 closes today, 26 November 2026, at 10 PM.** The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) opened the window on 25 November for BAMS, BHMS and BUMS seats in government and private Ayush colleges across the state. The fee payment window stays open till 11:59 PM tonight.

This is the second stray vacancy round of UGMAC(Ayush) 2026, filling seats left vacant after Round 1 on 23 and 24 November. BCECEB issued the notices on 24 November, and no further round has been announced.

## The full schedule, 25 to 30 November

The round runs over six days, and one missed step can cost you the seat.

| Step | Date and time |
| --- | --- |
| Online registration and application opens | 25 November 2026 |
| Last date for online registration | 26 November 2026, 10 PM |
| Last date for payment and form editing | 26 November 2026, 11:59 PM |
| Rank card | 27 November 2026, 11 AM |
| Last date for choice filling, editing and locking | 27 November 2026, 2 PM |
| Provisional seat allotment result | 28 November 2026 |
| Document verification and admission | 29 to 30 November 2026 |

## Who can apply, and who cannot

Any NEET UG 2026 qualified candidate who is not admitted to any Ayush college in India can apply. Fresh candidates who never registered can register now, and candidates who registered earlier but never finished the form or the payment can complete it now.

Candidates who applied earlier and got no seat, filled no choices, or lost a seat after resigning can log in, give consent for this round, and edit the form and the choices.

You cannot take part if you were admitted through UGMAC(Ayush) Round 1 or Round 2, or allotted a seat in Round 3 or Stray Vacancy Round 1. The same applies if you hold an All India Quota seat from Round 1 or Round 2, or were allotted one in AIQ Round 3, Stray Round 1 or Stray Round 2. Candidates on the ineligible list shared by the Ayush Admissions Central Counselling Committee in the national [AYUSH NEET UG counselling](/results/ayush-neet-ug-svr-2-final-result-123-seats-report-nov-25-30-2026/) also cannot apply.

Check the list before you pay: a candidate who already holds a seat gains nothing from the deposit and can face trouble later.

## What you pay: fee and security deposit

| College type | Counselling fee | Security deposit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government Ayush college | Rs 1,200 (UR, EWS, BC, EBC); Rs 600 (SC, ST, DQ) | Rs 10,000 |
| Private Ayush college | Rs 1,200 for all categories | Rs 50,000 |
| Both government and private | Rs 1,200 for all categories | Rs 50,000 |

The counselling fee is not refundable, and a processing charge is extra. The security deposit comes back after all rounds end if you are not admitted or have taken admission. Private colleges charge high tuition, so check the fee before you pick one.

Candidates who are not permanent residents of Bihar cannot take private Ayush seats on reserved-category cut-off marks.

## How to register and lock your choices

1. Open the <a href="https://bceceboard.bihar.gov.in/UGAACIndex.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BCECEB UGMAC(Ayush) notice board</a> and click the Online Portal of UGMAC(Ayush)-2026.
2. Register with your NEET UG 2026 roll number, name and date of birth. An OTP goes to the mobile number and email you used for NEET, and you cannot change them.
3. Fill in your personal, contact and education details, then upload your photo, signature and documents.
4. Preview the form, submit it, and pay the counselling fee and security deposit online.
5. Download the confirmation page and the fee receipt.
6. Fill in your course and college choices in order of preference, save them, and lock them.
7. Download your filled choices. Choices left unsaved are locked automatically after the deadline.

Choice filling is mandatory: a paid form with no locked choices gets no seat. Fill in many choices, put your safest college last, and lock them well before 2 PM on 27 November.

> A paid form with no locked choices gets no seat. Lock your choices well before 2 PM on 27 November.

## Documents to carry on 29 and 30 November

Carry originals to the allotted college; a mismatch can cancel your seat. Our [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) covers the same papers.

- NEET UG 2026 admit card and scorecard
- Class 10 and Class 12 certificates and mark sheets
- Residential and caste certificates, if they apply to you
- Six passport-size photos, the same as on the NEET admit card
- Printed confirmation page of the UGMAC(Ayush) application
- Aadhaar card and biometric check slip
- EWS or DQ documents, if they apply to you, plus any papers the private college asks for

DQ candidates need the original DQ certificate, the UDID card and an affidavit; missing any one means no DQ quota. Certificates in another language need an English version.

## What happens after the allotment

Download your provisional allotment order between 28 and 30 November and report in person with your originals. If you do not report, do not take admission, or your documents do not match, the deposit is forfeited and the seat is cancelled, and you lose the chance to take part in further rounds, if any are held.

Candidates who get no seat can give consent for a later round if the board announces one. West Bengal is running its own stray round too, and its [AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2](/admissions/wb-ayush-stray-vacancy-round-2-registration-opens-nov-21-2026/) allotment result comes on 27 November.
