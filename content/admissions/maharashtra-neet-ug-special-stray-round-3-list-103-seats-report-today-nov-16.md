**If your name is on the Maharashtra NEET UG special stray vacancy round 3 selection list, today is your last chance. The State CET Cell released the list on November 14 for 103 MBBS and BDS seats, and physical reporting at allotted colleges ends today, November 16. The Medical Counselling Committee has fixed today as the last date of joining.**

This round is the final shot at a medical or dental seat in Maharashtra this year. It covers the seats that stayed empty after the regular rounds and two earlier special stray rounds. Students who took part had to file fresh choices between November 13 and 11 AM on November 14. Choices from the first two special stray rounds were cancelled for this round.

## 103 seats on offer: 80 MBBS and 23 BDS

The provisional seat matrix for this round lists 103 vacant seats across private medical and dental colleges and private university institutions in Maharashtra. Of these, 80 are MBBS seats and 23 are BDS seats.

A new college is the biggest addition to the matrix. Ideal Institute of Medical Science at Wada in Palghar has joined the counselling process with 50 MBBS seats, split into 38 seats for the general category and 12 for the women category. The institute had received approval from the National Medical Commission but was still waiting for affiliation from the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences in Nashik. Seats allotted at this college depend on that affiliation coming through.

Ideal Institute was one of two new colleges waiting to join the admission process. The other, Mauli Medical College in Buldhana, has 100 MBBS seats. Together, the two colleges account for 150 seats that were stuck outside the process for months. This third special stray round is, in effect, the ninth round of [Maharashtra NEET UG counselling](/admissions/) this year.

## How to check the Round 3 selection list

The list is published as a PDF on the official portal. Follow these steps:

- Open the <a href="https://medical2026.mahacet.org/NEET-UG-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Maharashtra NEET UG counselling portal</a>.
- Go to the notifications tab and find the link for the special stray vacancy round 3 selection list.
- Open the PDF and search for your name using your NEET roll number or CET application details.
- Note the college name and course, and take a printout of the list.

Check the title of the document before you trust it. The correct file is titled "Provisional Selection List Only for MBBS/BDS Courses - Online Special Stray Vacancy Round 3". More than one selection list can be live on the portal at the same time, so a wrong file can mislead you.

> "Provisional Selection List Only for MBBS/BDS Courses - Online Special Stray Vacancy Round 3"

## Who could take part in Round 3

Notice No. 33, issued on November 3, set clear conditions. Candidates had to be qualified in NEET UG 2026, registered for Maharashtra state counselling, and without a seat in any Group A CAP round.

Some candidates were kept out. Anyone who had already joined an MBBS or BDS Group A seat by October 26, 2026, could not take part. The same applied to candidates who had joined a seat through the All India Quota stray vacancy rounds, and to those who were allotted a seat in an earlier stray round and failed to join.

## What to carry when you report

Reporting is physical, not online. Reach the allotted college in person with your original documents and the fees. Carry the printout of the selection list, your NEET admit card and scorecard, your Class 10 and 12 marksheets, and the fee in the mode your college accepts. Keep photocopies of everything as well.

## Miss today and the seat is gone

Joining is not optional in this round. The CET Cell has warned that candidates who fail to report face disqualification and other penalties under the information brochure. A missed deadline here also shuts the door on later rounds, because candidates allotted a seat in Round 3 who do not join are not eligible for the stray rounds that follow.

The [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray vacancy round 1](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-1-result-nov-8-2026/) list came out on November 8 for 138 seats, and Round 2 followed on November 11. Each round has given only two days to report, and Round 3 is no different.

On the postgraduate side, the [NEET PG Round 2](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-revised-dates-nov-17-result/) final result is due on November 17. Keep the two schedules separate. The dates, portals, and reporting rules are different.
