**The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has released the NEET PG 2026 Round 2 final seat allotment result. A total of 20,707 seats were allotted in this round across the All India Quota, deemed universities, central universities, and DNB seats. If your name is on the list, report to your allotted college by 25 November, 10 PM. Miss that deadline and you lose the seat.**

The final result came out on 20 November, three days after the Madras High Court had put a hold on the round. The court later limited its stay to one college, Sri Venkateshwara Medical College Hospital, and MCC went ahead with the allotment for all other colleges. The [NEET PG Round 2 provisional result](/results/neet-pg-round-2-provisional-allotment-out-nov-20-2026/) had come out earlier the same day, and candidates had till 6 PM to flag any error in it.

## 20,707 seats: 11,057 fresh allotments, 9,650 upgrades

Out of the 20,707 seats allotted in Round 2, 11,057 went to candidates who got a seat for the first time in this counselling. The other 9,650 were upgrades, where candidates moved to a college or course they had ranked higher. The last All India Open rank admitted in this round was 1,33,639, which shows how far down the merit list the allotment reached.

MD seats formed the biggest share, with 9,611 allotments, followed by DNB seats at 4,982. MD General Medicine, MD Anaesthesiology, and MS General Surgery were among the branches with the most seats. The full break-up is in the allotment PDF on the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC portal</a>.

> If your name is on the final list, the clock is already running. You have till 10 PM on 25 November to report. After that, the seat goes to someone else.

## Freeze or Float: pick your option online

This year, candidates do not have to visit the college just to submit their willingness. You can do it online on the MCC portal. Pick Freeze if you are happy with the seat and want to keep it. Pick Float if you want to hold the seat and still try for a better one in Round 3.

Once you submit your willingness, download your allotment letter from the candidate login. You will need it at the time of reporting. No college will complete your admission without it.

## How to check the Round 2 final allotment result

- Go to the official MCC website at <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a>.
- Open the PG Medical section on the homepage.
- Go to Current Events and click the link titled Final Allotment Result for PG 2026 Round 2.
- The result will open as a PDF. Search for your roll number or rank in it.
- Download the allotment letter from your candidate login and keep a printout ready.

Check every detail in the PDF carefully: your name, roll number, allotted institute, course, and category. If anything looks wrong, contact MCC through the Candidates Grievances and Query Redressal System on the portal.

## Documents to carry when you report

- NEET PG seat allotment letter
- NEET PG admit card and scorecard
- Class 10 certificate or birth certificate
- MBBS first, second, and third professional exam mark sheets
- MBBS degree certificate or provisional certificate
- Internship completion certificate
- Permanent or provisional medical registration certificate
- Valid photo ID such as Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, or driving licence

Carry originals along with photocopies. Colleges verify the originals at the counter and keep the copies. Students who report without the allotment letter will be turned away, so do not leave it behind.

## No seat yet? Round 3 starts on 26 November

Candidates who did not get a seat in Round 1 or Round 2 still have a chance. MCC will open Round 3 registration on 26 November, and it will run till 1 December. Choice filling will be open from 27 November to 2 December. The Round 3 allotment result will be declared on 4 December, and reporting will run from 5 to 12 December.

One thing to keep in mind before Round 3: more than [10,105 seats are blocked](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-10105-seats-blocked-float-rule-review/) after Round 2, held by candidates who also have an allotment in state counselling. The government is looking at the float rule after this round, so the rules for Round 3 could change. Check the MCC notice board before you fill your choices.
