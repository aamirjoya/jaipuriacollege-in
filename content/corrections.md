We work hard to get every fact right, and we take corrections seriously. If you spot an error in one of our articles, tell us and we will check it.

## How to report an error

Send us a message through our [contact page](/contact/). Please include:

- The URL of the article with the error
- What is wrong, and what the correct information is
- A link to the official source, if you have one — for example, a university website or a government portal

## What happens next

We check every report against the original source. If the information in our article is wrong, we correct the article as soon as we can.

## How corrections are shown

A correction is never made silently. When we correct an article, we add a visible correction note to the article itself, stating what was wrong and what it now says. Readers can see exactly what changed.

## The original publish date never changes

The original publication date of an article is part of its record and is never changed. A correction adds a note to the article and an updated timestamp showing when the correction was made — the original date stays as it was.
