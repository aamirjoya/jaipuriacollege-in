Jaipuria College is an independent education-news portal for Indian students. We publish practical, easy-to-understand coverage of college admissions, university exam results, admit cards and scholarships.

## What we cover

- **Admissions** - application dates, merit lists and counselling updates
- **Results & Admit Cards** - university result announcements and download guides
- **Scholarships** - government and private scholarship schemes
- **Education News** - entrance exams, policy changes and university updates

## How we work

Our articles are written by the Jaipuria College editorial desk. We check facts against official sources such as university websites and government portals before publishing, and we correct errors promptly when readers point them out.

## Independence

Jaipuria College is an independent publication. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any college, university or educational institution. Any resemblance of our name to an institution's name is coincidental. Please see our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) for the full statement.
