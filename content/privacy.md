This privacy policy explains what information Jaipuria College collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

Jaipuria College is a static website. We do not run user accounts and we do not ask for personal information to read our articles. If you use the contact form, the details you type stay in your own browser until our contact backend is connected; we do not receive them today.

## Cookies and analytics

We may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand which articles readers find useful. We do not sell personal information.

## Third-party links

Our articles link to official sources such as university websites and government portals. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page.
