## Who we are

The Editorial Desk is the team behind Jaipuria College. We cover college admissions, university exam results, admit cards and scholarships for Indian students.

## How we report

- **Two-source rule.** Every fact in a story — dates, deadlines, figures, names — is confirmed against at least two independent sources before we publish. One source is never enough.
- **Official sources first.** We check facts against official sources such as university websites and government portals.
- **No invented material.** We never fabricate quotes, statistics, deadlines or announcements. If a fact cannot be verified, it does not appear in the story.
- **Corrections.** When we get something wrong, we correct it promptly and note the correction on the article. Readers can report an error through our [contact page](/contact/). Our full [corrections policy](/corrections/) explains how this works.

## Independence

Jaipuria College is an independent publication. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any college, university or educational institution. Our full standards are published on the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) page.
