**Eight days are left to claim ₹50,000 a year from the government. Fresh and renewal applications for the AICTE Pragati, Saksham and Swanath scholarships 2026-27 close on November 30, 2026, on the National Scholarship Portal. The date was pushed back from October 31, and this is the final window. If you study in an AICTE-approved technical course and your family earns up to ₹8 lakh a year, this money is meant for you.**

The three schemes are run by the All India Council for Technical Education. Each one pays ₹50,000 a year. Pragati is for girl students, Saksham is for students with a disability of 40% or more, and Swanath is for orphans and other students facing severe family hardship. Together they cover a large share of engineering and diploma students across India.

## Pragati: ₹50,000 a year for girl students in technical courses

Pragati is the biggest of the three. It goes to girl students admitted to the first year of an AICTE-approved technical degree or diploma course, or to the second year through lateral entry. The family income limit is ₹8 lakh a year. Only two girls from one family can receive it.

Around 5,000 Pragati scholarships are given for degree courses and 5,000 for diploma courses every year. Girls from the north-eastern states, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and some other listed Union Territories are covered over and above this number. Selection is based on Class 12 marks, so keep your marksheet ready when you fill the form.

If you are a girl student in engineering or a technical diploma, also look at the [Rolls-Royce Wings4Her scholarship](/scholarships/rolls-royce-wings4her-scholarship-2026-27-women-engineering-75000-nov-23/) for women in engineering, which pays ₹75,000. You cannot hold two government scholarships for the same purpose, so compare the two and pick the one that fits you better.

## Saksham: support for students with 40% or more disability

Saksham is for students with a disability of 40% or more who study in AICTE-approved technical degree or diploma courses. The family income limit is the same, ₹8 lakh a year. Unlike Pragati, Saksham has no fixed number of seats. Every student who meets the conditions can get it.

You will need a disability certificate from the competent medical authority, and it must state the percentage clearly, because the portal checks the 40% mark. Keep a scanned copy ready before you start the form.

## Swanath: for orphans and students who lost a parent

Swanath covers orphans, children who lost one or both parents to Covid-19, and wards of armed forces and central paramilitary personnel killed in action. It also covers children of a parent with a critical illness, or one who died or was severely disabled in an accident.

Up to 1,000 Swanath scholarships are given for degree courses and 1,000 for diploma courses each year. The income limit of ₹8 lakh a year applies here too. Students who already hold another government scholarship cannot apply for Swanath.

> ₹50,000 a year may not cover a full engineering fee, but for many families it pays the hostel, the books and the exam fees for the whole year. The form takes under an hour. The money is worth that hour.

## How to apply on the National Scholarship Portal before November 30

Apply on the <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official National Scholarship Portal</a>. The form is free, and you do not need an agent or a cyber cafe to fill it. Follow these steps:

- Finish your One Time Registration (OTR) with your mobile number and Aadhaar, and complete e-KYC. Then log in with your Aadhaar or OTR number.
- Pick your institute carefully using its AISHE code. Many colleges have similar names, and the wrong pick means your college cannot verify you.
- Select the right scheme, Pragati, Saksham or Swanath, and fill in your course and income details. Your name and Class 12 roll number must match your marksheet.
- Upload your documents and submit before November 30, 2026. Tell your college scholarship desk the same day, because institute verification closes on December 15, 2026.

Keep your Class 12 marksheet, income certificate, caste certificate if it applies, and your disability or orphan documents ready before you start. A form your college does not verify is treated as invalid, so verification matters as much as the application itself.

## Fresh or renewal: check which one you are

This window covers both fresh and renewal applications. If you already received one of these scholarships last year, apply for renewal, not a fresh application. For 2026-27, renewals are open for the 2023-24 batch for degree courses only, and for the 2024-25 and 2025-26 batches for both degree and diploma courses.

One change this year is worth knowing. Under an AICTE corrigendum dated August 18, 2026, Pragati is now classed as merit based, while Saksham and Swanath are welfare based. That means a student can hold one merit based scheme and one welfare based scheme, but not two of the same kind. If you already get the [PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship](/scholarships/pm-usp-csss-scholarship-fresh-application-nov-30-2026/), which also closes on November 30, check the combination rules first.

Eight days is enough, but only if your documents are ready. The income certificate takes the longest, so get it first. Do not wait for the last evening, when the portal slows down under the rush.
