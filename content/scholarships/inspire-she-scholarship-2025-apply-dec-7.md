**The wait is over for science students. The Department of Science and Technology has opened applications for the INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education, or INSPIRE-SHE, for 2025. A total of 12,000 scholarships are on offer, and the last date to apply is December 7, 2026.**

The scheme pays Rs 5,000 a month to students studying basic and natural sciences. Applications opened on November 7, and students can apply only through the official INSPIRE portal. The announcement came after a gap of almost two years, during which no fresh applications were taken for the scheme.

Science and Technology Minister Dr Jitendra Singh shared the news on November 15, calling it a renewed push to support young scientific talent and strengthen India's research system. The scheme, known in full as Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research, has run since 2008.

## Who can apply for INSPIRE-SHE 2025

The call is open only to students who passed Class 12 in 2025. If you passed in 2024 or earlier, or if you are appearing for Class 12 in 2026, this round is not for you.

You must be between 17 and 22 years old and studying an eligible course in basic and natural sciences. Eligible courses include BSc, BS, four-year BSc with Research, and Integrated MSc or MS programmes. Subjects covered include Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Biotechnology, Microbiology, Environmental Science, Statistics and Earth Sciences.

You can qualify through any one of these routes:

- Marks in the top 1% of your Class 12 board, state or central
- A rank within the top 10,000 in JEE Main, JEE Advanced or NEET, while studying an eligible science course
- NTSE scholar, JBNSTS scholar, or International Olympiad medal winner studying an eligible science course

Around 12,000 scholarships are given out each year, divided between boards as per their share of Class 12 candidates. Check the board-wise quota on the official portal before you apply, so you know where you stand.

## Rs 60,000 a year, plus a research grant in later years

Selected students get Rs 5,000 a month, which works out to Rs 60,000 a year. On top of this, a mentorship grant of Rs 20,000 a year is available in the third and fourth years of the course. This grant is linked to a research project done at an institute, university or laboratory in India.

There is one change students must note. The mentorship grant used to be paid every year. Under the revised rules, it will now be paid only in the third and fourth years. The monthly scholarship of Rs 5,000 stays the same.

> "A renewed commitment to nurturing young scientific talent and strengthening India's research ecosystem."

## New rules are stricter: one bad year can end the scholarship

The department has also tightened the rules on keeping the scholarship. This is the part many students may miss, so read it carefully.

Under the revised framework, scholars must pass all subjects in their annual or semester exams on the first attempt, with at least 60% marks or a 7.0 CGPA on a 10-point scale. Students who fail a subject, or score below 60% or 7 CGPA in the first year, will lose the scholarship for the entire programme. Earlier rules allowed some room to recover. That room is now gone.

The message is plain. The scholarship rewards steady performance, not just a strong Class 12 result. If you apply, plan to keep your marks up from the very first year.

## How to apply for INSPIRE-SHE 2025

Applications are accepted only online. Follow these steps to apply on the <a href="https://online-inspire.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official INSPIRE portal</a>:

- Go to online-inspire.gov.in and register with a valid email address
- Log in and open the INSPIRE-SHE application form
- Fill in your personal, academic and course details
- Upload Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, a passport-size photo and the prescribed endorsement form
- Check every detail once, then submit the form
- Save a copy of the submitted form for your records

Incomplete forms, or forms missing documents, can be rejected. The department has asked candidates to read the official guidelines on the portal before submitting.

Students looking at other options can also check the [Rolls-Royce Wings4Her scholarship](/scholarships/rolls-royce-wings4her-scholarship-2026-27-women-engineering-75000-nov-23/), which offers Rs 75,000 a year to women engineering students till November 23, and the [Aditya Birla Capital Scholarship](/scholarships/aditya-birla-capital-scholarship-2026-27-girls-60000-nov-22-last-date/), which closes on November 22. For school-level support, the [National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship](/scholarships/nmmss-scholarship-2026-27-last-date-nov-15-apply-nsp/) pays Rs 12,000 a year to Class 9 to 12 students.

## Key dates to remember

- Application window opens: November 7, 2026
- Last date to apply: December 7, 2026, till 11:59 pm
- Who can apply: Class 12 pass-outs of 2025, aged 17 to 22
- Scholarship amount: Rs 5,000 a month, Rs 60,000 a year
- Mentorship grant: Rs 20,000 a year, third and fourth years only

Do not wait for the last day. The portal can slow down when thousands of students apply together, and a missed deadline means waiting for the next cycle. If you passed Class 12 in 2025 with top marks and are studying science, this is one of the biggest government scholarships open right now.
