**The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMSS) gives Rs 12,000 a year to bright students from poor families, and the last date to apply for 2026-27 is November 15.** The Ministry of Education pushed the deadline back on October 31, so students who had not finished their forms get one more week. If you are in Class 9 in a government school, this is money for your studies that you should not leave on the table.

Applications go through the [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in) (NSP). The portal has been open since June 1, 2026. Finish your One-Time Registration (OTR) on the portal first, then fill the form. As of October 30, over 2.9 lakh students had already applied, with 90,483 fresh applications and 1,99,531 renewals.

## What the NMMSS scholarship gives you

The scheme picks one lakh fresh Class 9 students every year through a selection test run by each state or Union Territory. If you are picked, you get Rs 12,000 a year, sent straight to your bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer. The money does not stop after Class 9. You can renew it in Classes 10, 11, and 12 if you keep meeting the academic rules.

Many bright students drop out after Class 8 because their families cannot afford school costs. This scholarship is meant to keep them in school till Class 12.

> "NMMSS is aimed at supporting meritorious students from economically weaker sections and encouraging them to continue their education beyond Class VIII up to Class XII," the Ministry of Education said in its release.

## Who can apply for NMMSS 2026-27

Not every student can apply, so check the rules before you start the form.

- **School type:** You must study in a state government school, a government-aided school, or a school run by a local body.
- **Class:** The fresh scholarship is for Class 9 students who clear the state selection test.
- **Marks:** You need at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade in Class 7. Students from SC and ST categories get a 5% relaxation.
- **Family income:** Your parents' total income from all sources should not be more than Rs 3.5 lakh a year.

If you are already getting the scholarship and are now in Class 10, 11, or 12, you apply as a renewal student. Renewal also runs on the same portal and the same November 15 date.

## NMMSS 2026-27: new dates to remember

The ministry changed the full schedule. Keep all three dates in mind, because a missed verification can hold up your money even after you apply.

| Step | Last date |
| --- | --- |
| Student application on NSP | November 15, 2026 |
| Institute Nodal Officer (L1) verification | November 30, 2026 |
| District Nodal Officer (L2) verification | December 15, 2026 |

Submitting the form is only step one. Your school has to verify it by November 30, and the district officer by December 15. Talk to your school after you apply and make sure verification is done. Students who applied early should also check that their forms are moving through these two steps.

## How to apply on the National Scholarship Portal

1. Go to <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">scholarships.gov.in</a> and finish your One-Time Registration (OTR) if you have not done it yet.
2. Log in with your OTR details and open the NMMSS application for 2026-27.
3. Fill in your personal, school, and bank details carefully. They must match your documents.
4. Upload the documents the portal asks for, including your income certificate and Class 7 marksheet.
5. Submit the form on or before November 15, 2026, and save the application number.
6. Ask your school to complete its verification before November 30.

Do not wait for the last day. Portals slow down when thousands of students log in at once, and a half-filled form counts for nothing.

The extension is a second chance. The scheme has run since 2008 and kept lakhs of students in school. If you qualify, fill the form this week, get your school to verify it, and keep your application number safe.
