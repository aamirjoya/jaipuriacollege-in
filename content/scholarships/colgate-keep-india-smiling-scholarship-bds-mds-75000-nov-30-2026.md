**Dental students have 12 days left to claim ₹75,000.** Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited is offering the money through its Keep India Smiling Scholarship Program 2026-27. The grant is a one-time payment for students enrolled in BDS or MDS courses. Applications close on 30 November 2026, and the form is short once your documents are ready.

The scholarship is open to students in any year of BDS and to students doing MDS. It covers both government and private dental colleges across India. The money is meant to ease the cost of a dental degree, from fees to books and instruments.

## ₹75,000, paid once, for BDS and MDS students

Selected students receive ₹75,000 in a single payment. It is not a yearly grant, so plan around the full amount arriving once. The programme is run by Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited, and applications are handled on Buddy4Study, the platform that manages the scheme.

A dental degree is costly. A BDS course runs for four years plus an internship year, and the bills add up fast. A grant of this size can cover a large part of one year's fees at many colleges, or the cost of instruments and study material for a full year. For students from families with a tight budget, it can be the difference between staying in the course and dropping out.

## Who can apply for the Colgate scholarship

The rules are simple, and you should check each one before you start the form.

- You must be an Indian national enrolled in BDS (any year) or MDS at a government or private college in India.
- You need at least 65% marks in Class 12.
- If you are in the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of BDS, you need at least 60% marks in your last semester.
- MDS applicants need an overall score above 60% in their BDS course.
- Your parents' total annual income from all sources must not be more than ₹8 lakh.
- Children of employees of Buddy4Study and Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited cannot apply.

Students from Maharashtra, Goa, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, and Andhra Pradesh get preference in selection. That does not mean others cannot apply. It only means applicants from these five states get first preference when scores are close. If you are from another state and meet every other rule, you should still apply.

> One grant, one payment, no renewal form next year. If you clear the marks and income bar, ₹75,000 is yours to use on your dental education.

## Documents you need before you apply

Keep these papers ready so the form does not stall halfway.

- Class 12 marksheet showing 65% or more
- Your latest semester marksheet if you are in the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year of BDS, or your BDS degree marksheet if you are applying for MDS
- Income certificate for your parents, showing total income up to ₹8 lakh a year
- Aadhaar card and a recent photograph
- Your college ID or admission proof
- Bank account details in your name

Scan each document before you sit down to apply. A blurred photo of a marksheet is the most common reason forms get sent back, and fixing it eats into your last days.

## How to apply in 5 steps

1. Open the <a href="https://www.buddy4study.com/page/colgate-keep-india-smiling-scholarship-program" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Colgate Keep India Smiling page on Buddy4Study</a>.
2. Pick the BDS or MDS category that matches your course.
3. Create an account or log in with your existing Buddy4Study account.
4. Fill the form and upload your documents.
5. Check every detail once, then submit before 30 November 2026.

Apply early. Portals slow down in the last two days as thousands of students rush in together, and a form left half done at the deadline does not count. If the page does not load on the first try, wait a few minutes and try again instead of starting over from scratch.
