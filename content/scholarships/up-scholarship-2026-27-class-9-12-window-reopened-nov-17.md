**Uttar Pradesh has reopened the scholarship window for school students. If you study in Class 9, 10, 11 or 12 and missed the first deadline, you now have until Tuesday, 17 November 2026 to apply online. Renewal students get till 20 November.**

A timetable from the Social Welfare Department covers Pre-Matric for Classes 9 and 10 and Post-Matric for Classes 11 and 12, along with fee reimbursement. The earlier last date for fresh applicants was 15 November. It has now moved to 17 November, and applications opened on 12 November.

That leaves fresh applicants less than a week, and the days after the online form matter just as much. A printout has to reach your school and be verified before the application can move ahead.

## The new UP Scholarship dates at a glance

- **17 November 2026:** fresh online applications close (Classes 9 and 11)
- **19 November 2026:** final printout due (fresh applicants)
- **19 to 22 November 2026:** hard copy with documents to school (fresh applicants)
- **12 to 20 November 2026:** renewal and hard-copy submission (Classes 10 and 12)
- **13 to 23 November 2026:** biometric and academic verification by schools
- **24 November 2026:** physical verification by District Inspector of Schools
- **1 to 9 December 2026:** correction of flagged or wrong data
- **25 to 31 January 2027:** money reaches fresh applicants

Renewal students are on a faster track. Reports say their money is planned between 14 and 31 December 2026, while fresh applicants wait till late January.

## Who can apply for the UP Scholarship

This reopened window is for students of Classes 9 to 12 in Uttar Pradesh. Pre-Matric covers Classes 9 and 10, Post-Matric covers Classes 11 and 12. Students from SC, ST, General, OBC and minority categories are covered, with fee reimbursement where it applies.

Fresh applicants are mainly students who took admission in Class 9 or Class 11 this session. Renewal applicants are students in Classes 10 and 12 who got the scholarship last year, and they must use the renewal route with their old registration number. A fresh form from a renewal student creates a duplicate and slows everything down.

Income limits apply and change by category. For the General category, the limit is ₹2 lakh a year. Check the exact figure for your category on the [official UP Scholarship portal](https://scholarship.up.gov.in).

## The form alone is not enough

Filling the online form is only the first step, and this is where many students go wrong. Take the final printout by 19 November and hand it in at school with your documents between 19 and 22 November. Renewal students must hand in their hard copy by 20 November.

Your school then prepares a master database between 12 and 19 November and enters your attendance and marks. Between 13 and 23 November it completes biometric authentication and uploads the same records. Ineligible applications are rejected here, and only verified data moves ahead.

After that, the District Inspector of Schools checks the physical records by 24 November. Entries the National Informatics Centre flags as wrong or doubtful are corrected between 1 and 9 December. An application your school does not forward by 23 November will not move ahead, so hand in the hard copy early.

> The online form takes less than an hour. The printout, the documents and the school visit take longer. Start with the portal, not the photocopy shop.

## How to apply in five steps

1. Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) on the <a href="https://scholarship.up.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UP Scholarship portal</a> with your mobile number and Aadhaar e-KYC. OTR is mandatory since the 2025-26 session, and the form will not open without it.
2. Log in and pick the right scheme: Pre-Matric for Classes 9 and 10, Post-Matric for Classes 11 and 12. Choose Fresh for a first application, or Renewal if you applied last year.
3. Fill in your personal, academic, income, caste, institution and bank details, with your name and date of birth exactly as they appear on your documents.
4. Upload the documents the portal asks for: Aadhaar, income certificate, caste certificate if it applies to you, last year's marksheet and a photo. Keep your bank account active, because the money goes straight to it.
5. Submit the form, take the final printout by 19 November, and hand the hard copy with self-attested documents to your school. Ask for an acknowledgement as proof it was received.

## Three mistakes that can cost you the scholarship

First, treating the online form as the finish line. The hard copy is mandatory, and students who never hand it in drop out.

Second, picking the wrong route. Renewal students who file a fresh form create a duplicate, and fresh students cannot get in through the renewal login.

Third, wrong bank details, or an Aadhaar that is not linked to the bank account. The money moves through direct benefit transfer, so a mismatch stops the payment even after approval. Check the account number twice.
