**Girl students from Class 9 to postgraduate level have nine days left to apply for the Aditya Birla Capital Scholarship 2026-27. The last date is November 22, 2026, and the support goes up to ₹60,000 for one year.**

The scheme is run by the Aditya Birla Capital Foundation. It is built for bright girl students from families that cannot easily pay for studies. Applications opened on September 22, 2026, and the form is online only. This is one of the few [scholarships for girl students](/scholarships/) that covers school, diploma, graduation and post-graduation in a single programme.

## How much money do you get?

The amount changes with your level of study. Students in Classes 9 to 12 and Polytechnic Diploma get ₹25,000 for one year. Undergraduate students get up to ₹45,000. Postgraduate students get ₹60,000.

The money is meant for real study costs. It covers tuition fees, admission charges, examination fees and prescribed books. It also covers living costs such as hostel and mess charges and uniforms. The idea is simple. A girl who scores well should not have to drop out because the family budget is tight.

> A scholarship does not just pay a fee. It keeps a bright student in class when money runs short at home.

## Who can apply?

The rules are clear, so check them before you start the form. The scholarship is open only to girl students. You must be studying in Class 9 to 12, a Polytechnic Diploma, or an undergraduate or postgraduate course at a recognised government school, college or institution in India.

You must have scored at least 60% marks in your most recent academic year. Your family income from all sources must not be more than ₹6 lakh a year. Children of employees of Aditya Birla Capital, its subsidiaries or joint ventures, and Buddy4Study cannot apply.

One point needs care. The rules say recognised government institutions. If you study in a private college, check the official listing before you apply, because the page does not clearly say private students are covered.

## How to apply in 5 steps

- Go to the <a href="https://www.buddy4study.com/page/aditya-birla-capital-scholarship" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Aditya Birla Capital Scholarship page on Buddy4Study</a> and click Apply Now.
- Log in with your Buddy4Study account, or create one with your email address, mobile number or Google account.
- Pick the form for your level of study and click Start Application.
- Fill in your details and upload your documents.
- Read the form once more, accept the terms and submit it.

Do not wait till November 22. Portals slow down near the deadline, and a half-filled form does not count. Aim to finish by November 20 so you have time to fix any document problem.

## Documents you will need

Keep these ready before you open the form. A recent passport-size photograph, your last class or semester marksheet, and your Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets if they apply to you. You will also need a government ID proof such as a masked Aadhaar card, voter ID, driving licence or PAN card.

Proof of admission for the current year is needed too, such as a fee receipt, admission letter, institution identity card or bonafide certificate. Add your bank passbook copy and your current-year income proof. That can be issued by the Gram Panchayat, Ward Counsellor or Sarpanch, or by the SDM, DM, CO or Tehsildar. Salary slips or ITR also work. A disability certificate or a parent's death certificate is needed only if it applies to you.

## A warning about an old date

Some older pages on the internet still show October 30, 2026 as the last date. That was the earlier closing date. The current listing, updated on November 5, 2026, shows November 22, 2026, and the same date is confirmed in fresh reports this week. Treat November 22 as the real deadline, but do not take the extra days lightly. Apply now while the portal is running fine.

If this scheme does not fit you, there are other options open right now. The [HDFC Parivartan ECSS Scholarship](/scholarships/hdfc-parivartan-ecss-scholarship-2026-27-dec-15-deadline/) runs till December 15, 2026, with help from ₹15,000 to ₹75,000. Girls starting BBA, BCA or BMS can also look at the [AICTE Saraswati Scholarship](/scholarships/aicte-saraswati-scholarship-2026-27-girls-bba-bca-bms/), which pays ₹25,000 a year for three years.
