**Forty students in Class 10 and Class 12 will share ₹10 lakh under the ProTeen Young Achievers Scholarship 2026, and the best part is that your board marks do not decide who wins.** ProTeen, a career guidance platform from the UNIDEL Group, opened applications on November 14. Students from any recognised board in India can apply, and the last date is November 30, 2026.

The money can go towards school or college fees and other education costs. Results are expected in the first week of January 2027. If you are in Class 10 or Class 12 and have a clear idea of what you want to do in life, this one is worth your time.

## No marks cut-off, your interests matter more

Most scholarships ask for a percentage first and everything else later. This one works the other way round. ProTeen says the selection looks at a student's interests, career goals, achievements and overall potential, not just exam scores.

That makes it a good fit for students who are strong in other ways. Maybe you built something, won a competition, or have a clear plan for a career in design, medicine, teaching or technology. The process gives you room to show that.

## How the selection works, step by step

The selection runs in three stages. You need to clear each one to move ahead.

First, you complete an interest assessment and write an essay. The essay asks you to describe a career you want to pursue and why it interests you. Keep it honest and specific. Write about a real career, and say what draws you to it in your own words.

Second, shortlisted students submit a video of 60 to 90 seconds. Treat it like a short self-introduction. Say who you are, what you want to do, and why the scholarship would help. Record it in a quiet room with good light, and keep it under 90 seconds.

Third, the students who clear the video round attend a virtual interview with the scholarship team. This is a conversation, not a test. They will likely ask about your essay, your interests and your plans after school.

> Your marks will not decide this scholarship. Your interests, your essay and how clearly you can talk about your future will.

## Who can apply

The rules are simple. You must be studying in Class 10 or Class 12 right now. Students from any recognised board, CBSE, ICSE, state boards and others, can apply. There is no income limit mentioned in the announcement, and no separate quota for any category.

ProTeen runs digital career guidance through psychometric tests, career tools and expert counselling. The platform says it has supported more than 70,000 students so far, and it has added over 20 new school and college partnerships in the last six months.

## How to apply before November 30

Applications are online only, through the official portal. The steps are short, but do not leave them for the last day. Portals slow down when everyone applies at once.

- Go to the <a href="https://scholarship.proteen.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official ProTeen scholarship portal</a> and open the Young Achievers Scholarship 2026 section.
- Register with your details and fill in the application form.
- Complete the interest assessment.
- Write and submit your essay on the career you want to pursue.
- Submit the form before November 30, 2026, and save your application number.

If you are shortlisted, the team will contact you about the video round. Keep an eye on the email and phone number you used to register.
