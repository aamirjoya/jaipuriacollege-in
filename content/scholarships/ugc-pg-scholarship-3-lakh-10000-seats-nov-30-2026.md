**The UGC will pay Rs 15,000 a month to 10,000 first-year postgraduate students, and the form closes on 30 November 2026. The money runs for two years, so a selected student gets Rs 3 lakh in all. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal, and ask your college to verify the form by 15 December.**

Four days are left. The scheme is the National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies, run by the <a href="https://www.ugc.gov.in/Scholarships/stu_Scholarship6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University Grants Commission</a>. It opened on 16 September, and the portal will not take forms after 30 November.

## What the scholarship pays

A selected student gets Rs 15,000 a month for 10 months in each academic year. That is Rs 1.5 lakh a year. The scholarship runs for two years, so the total comes to Rs 3 lakh, if the second year is renewed.

The money does not come for all 12 months. It covers 10 months a year, and it reaches the student through Direct Benefit Transfer into an Aadhaar-linked bank account. The second year depends on your progress. You need satisfactory marks, the required attendance, and promotion to the next year. Changing your course or college during the two years can cost you the award.

> The UGC keeps 10,000 seats a year for this scheme, and the full amount over two years is Rs 3 lakh. No income limit applies, which is rare for a scholarship this size.

## Who can apply

You must be an Indian student in the first year of a regular, full-time postgraduate degree at an eligible university or college. Your age must be below 30 on the date you took admission, not on the date you apply.

This must be your first postgraduate degree. If you already hold a PG degree, you cannot apply for another one under this scheme. Students in distance, correspondence, part-time or private mode cannot apply either. A recognised, regular LL.M. counts too, as long as it is your first PG degree.

There is no family income limit, and selection runs on all-India merit with the government's reservation rules. The UGC reserves 30% of the seats for women, and gives preference to eligible single girl children.

Your college must also qualify. It should be a university or college covered under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, a deemed university, an eligible government-funded degree-awarding institution, or an Institution of National Importance, with a valid AISHE code for portal verification.

## How the 10,000 seats are shared

Half the seats go to Arts, Humanities, Social Sciences, Law and Management. The other half go to Science, Engineering, Technology, Medicine, Agriculture, Forestry and the remaining fields. An MA or MCom student competes inside one half, while an MSc or MTech student competes inside the other.

The 30% reservation for women applies across both halves. One more rule to keep in mind: you cannot hold this scholarship along with another scholarship, stipend, salary or fellowship. If you already get money from another source and this one is offered to you, you have to choose.

## How to apply on the National Scholarship Portal

The form is online only, on <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">scholarships.gov.in</a>. There is no offline form, and no one needs to be paid to submit it.

First, finish the One-Time Registration on the portal if you have not done it already. Log in, find the scheme listed as National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies, and fill in your personal details, your undergraduate qualification, your PG admission details, and your bank account details. Check that the name and details match your college records, because the college checks them during verification.

## What to check before you submit

Check your age against the admission date, not 30 November. If you turned 30 after admission, you are still within the limit.

Your course must be a regular, full-time PG degree, and your college must be eligible under the scheme.

The money goes by Direct Benefit Transfer to an Aadhaar-linked account, so a mismatch in the details can hold up payment. If your account is not linked to Aadhaar, fix that first.

No certificates need uploading at the first stage, but keep your marksheets, admission letter, and bank details ready for when your college verifies the form. After you submit, follow up with your college office. A form the college does not verify by 15 December cannot move ahead, and final verification runs till 31 December.

Do not leave the form for the last evening. The portal slows down when thousands of students apply at once, and a late form is not accepted.
