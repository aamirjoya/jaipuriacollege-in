**A top 1,000 rank in BITSAT 2027 can now mean four years of free tuition at BITS Pilani. The institute has doubled its full fee waiver scheme, and the benefit comes built into the admission offer, so there is no separate form to fill.**

BITS Pilani announced the change on November 1, 2026. Group Vice Chancellor V Ramgopal Rao shared the news with news agency PTI. From the 2027 admission cycle, students who rank among the top 1,000 in BITSAT will get a 100% tuition fee waiver for their full undergraduate course.

## What the BITS Pilani waiver gives you

The waiver covers the entire tuition fee for all four years of the course. It applies to integrated first-degree programmes, which include BE, MSc and BPharma, across BITS Pilani campuses.

There are three conditions. First, your BITSAT rank must be in the top 1,000. Second, your family income must be up to Rs 20 lakh a year. Third, you must keep a CGPA of 7.5 or above at your campus to keep the waiver going each year.

The best part for students is the simple process. The scholarship is part of the admission offer. If you meet the conditions, you do not have to apply for it after joining. That removes one more form from an already busy admission season.

## Why BITS doubled the scheme

This is not a new scheme. BITS Pilani started the full tuition waiver for the top 500 BITSAT rankers in the 2026-27 session. The 2027 change doubles that number to 1,000.

Rao said the number of top-500 rankers who joined BITS Pilani rose by 50% in 2026. The BITSAT score needed to reach that group also went up, from 314 to 324. Students ranked between 501 and 1,000 joined in larger numbers too, even though they got no waiver last year. The total number of students joining from the top 1,000 rose by 50% as well.

> "The scholarship is built into the admission offer, and students do not need to apply for it after joining, so top performers can choose BITS Pilani based on aspiration and opportunity, not affordability," Rao told PTI.

The idea is plain. A good rank should open the door, and fees should not close it. With the income limit set at Rs 20 lakh, the scheme reaches middle-income families as well, not just the poorest students.

## How big is BITS Pilani's aid programme

The waiver sits inside a much larger aid programme. In the 2025-26 academic year, BITS Pilani gave out about Rs 190 crore through scholarships, fee waivers and fellowships across its first-degree, higher-degree and PhD programmes. Of that, Rs 101 crore went to undergraduate students through merit-based, merit-cum-need and international student scholarships.

The institute says one in every four students across its campuses got some form of financial support. That scale matters for aspirants. Even if you miss the top 1,000, other awards exist, including full tuition waivers for the top 2% of performers on each campus.

## How to aim for the waiver

BITSAT is the computer-based entrance test for BITS Pilani's undergraduate programmes. To be in the running for this waiver, you need a top 1,000 rank in BITSAT 2027 and a family income of up to Rs 20 lakh a year.

Registrations for BITSAT 2027 are expected to begin in December 2026. Keep an eye on the <a href="https://admissions.bits-pilani.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BITS admissions portal</a> for the final dates and the complete scholarship terms. The score bar is rising, so early and steady preparation counts. In 2026, a score of 324 was needed to break into the top 500. The bar for the top 1,000 will be lower, but the trend is clear: scores keep climbing each year.

## What to check before you count on it

A few points need care. The one-time admission fee is not covered by the waiver. Read the admission offer letter in full before you accept the seat, so you know what else you will pay.

The CGPA rule is strict too. You must hold a CGPA of 7.5 or above to keep the waiver. A drop below that line can end the support, so plan your first year with that target in mind.

Finally, the income limit uses gross annual family income. Keep your income documents ready, because the institute will check them before it confirms the waiver in your offer.

For a top BITSAT ranker from a middle-income family, this is one of the strongest scholarship offers in India right now. Four years of tuition, no extra form, and a clear rank target to aim at.
