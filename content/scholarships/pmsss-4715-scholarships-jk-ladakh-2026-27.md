**The Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme has allotted 4,715 of its 5,000 scholarships for students of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh in the 2026-27 academic session, hitting 94.3% of its annual target with months to spare.** The current tally has already crossed last year's full-year figure of 3,585, marking a jump of about 31.5% in one year.

Officials credit faster registration and counselling for the sharp rise. The J&K Higher Education Department ran facilitation centres and document-verification centres across the Union Territory this year to help applicants finish the process in time.

## Where the 4,715 scholarships have gone

The split across streams looks like this:

- **General degree courses:** 1,894 allotted against a target of 2,070
- **Professional courses:** 2,741 allotted against a target of 2,830
- **Medical and BDS courses:** 80 allotted against a target of 100

That last line matters. Medical seats under the scheme have always been hard to get, with only 100 scholarships in the kitty for the whole year. The fact that 80 of them found takers in this session tells you how strong the demand was.

> "The current-year allotment is already 1,130 higher than the 2025-26 figure, registering an increase of around 31.5 per cent," an official spokesman said, adding that the 2026-27 achievement is higher than any of the previous three academic sessions.

For context, the scheme recorded 3,627 beneficiaries in 2023-24 and 3,674 in 2024-25. This year's 4,715 has left both of those in the dust.

## What the scheme actually gives you

PMSSS is run through the All India Council for Technical Education and lets eligible students from J&K and Ladakh study at approved colleges outside the two Union Territories. The money covers two parts: the academic fee and a maintenance allowance.

The academic fee ceilings for 2026-27 are set at Rs. 30,000 a year for general degree courses like BA, BCom and BSc, Rs. 1.25 lakh a year for professional courses such as engineering, nursing, pharmacy, architecture and law, and Rs. 3 lakh a year for MBBS, BDS and equivalent medical courses. On top of this, every selected student gets a maintenance allowance of Rs. 1 lakh a year, paid in monthly instalments through the National Scholarship Portal. The <a href="https://www.aicte.gov.in/sites/default/files/pmsss2020/2024-25/Revised Methodology_2024-25.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AICTE methodology document</a> lays out the fee ceilings and disbursement rules in full.

That maintenance money is the part many students underestimate. Living outside home for college is expensive, and Rs. 10,000 a month going straight to the student's bank account makes the difference between managing and dropping out.

## How students got selected this year

The process is straightforward but it has several moving parts. Students register on the official portal, fill in their course and college choices, and go through document verification at a facilitation-cum-document verification centre. Allotment then happens through the AICTE counselling process on merit, with the J&K reservation norms applied for SC, ST and socially and economically backward classes.

This year the J&K Higher Education Department pushed the timeline hard. Registration opened early, counselling started on time, and the field-level facilitation centres handled the crowd. A UT-level review committee headed by the Commissioner Secretary of the Higher Education Department, with the Director of Colleges as member secretary, oversaw verification and review. The numbers suggest the machinery worked.

## Why this matters beyond the numbers

The PMSSS scholarship is not just another item in the [scholarships list](/scholarships/) students scroll through every year. For many families in J&K and Ladakh, it is the single biggest ticket to studying in a big-city college without drowning in fees. A professional-course scholarship of Rs. 1.25 lakh plus Rs. 1 lakh in maintenance covers most of what a middle-class family would otherwise spend on a child's first year outside home.

The 31.5% jump also sends a signal. When the administration fixes the plumbing — early registration, enough verification centres, on-time counselling — eligible students actually show up. The gap between 3,585 last year and 4,715 this year is not luck. It is process.

## What to watch next

Students who missed this session should not waste time. The scheme offers 5,000 fresh scholarships every year: 2,070 for general degrees, 2,830 for professional courses and 100 for medical. The registration window for the next session usually opens a few months before the academic year begins, and the document checklist is the same every year, so keep your Class 12 marksheet, domicile certificate and income proof ready.

If you are comparing options, also look at the [PM Scholarship Scheme for 2026-27](/scholarships/pm-scholarship-scheme-2026-27-last-date-oct-31/) and the [AICTE Saksham scholarship for specially-abled students](/scholarships/aicte-saksham-scholarship-2026-27-specially-abled-students/), both of which have open application windows this year.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who can apply for the PMSSS scholarship?**

Students who have passed Class 12 from a school in Jammu and Kashmir or Ladakh and take admission in an approved college outside these two Union Territories can apply. The scholarship covers general degree, professional, and medical courses.

**How much money does PMSSS give?**

The academic fee support is up to Rs. 30,000 a year for general degree courses, up to Rs. 1.25 lakh for professional courses, and up to Rs. 3 lakh for medical courses. Every selected student also gets a maintenance allowance of Rs. 1 lakh a year.

**How are students selected under PMSSS?**

Selection happens through an AICTE counselling process. Applicants fill in their course and college choices, and allotments are made on merit with reservation norms applied.

**Is the 2026-27 PMSSS process still open for new applicants?**

The 4,715 figure is the count of scholarships already allotted for the current session, so the main rounds are largely done. Students planning for the next session should watch the official AICTE scholarship portal for the fresh registration window.
