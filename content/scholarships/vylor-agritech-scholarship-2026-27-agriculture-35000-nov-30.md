**If you study agriculture, this one is made for you.** The Vylor Agritech Scholarship Program for 2026-27 is open now, and it pays up to ₹35,000 a year. Undergraduate students get ₹25,000 a year. Postgraduate and PhD students get ₹35,000 a year. The last date to apply is **November 30, 2026**, so you have 11 days left. Applications are submitted online through Buddy4Study.

Agriculture students often miss out on big scholarships because most of them are built for engineering or medicine. This programme is different. It is only for students of agriculture and allied fields, from first-year undergraduates to PhD scholars. If your course is in this stream and your family income is under ₹6 lakh a year, you should check it.

## Who can apply for the Vylor Agritech Scholarship

The rules are simple, and the course list is wide. You can apply if you are in any year of a UG, PG or PhD programme in agriculture or an allied discipline. First-year students are as welcome as final-year students. Your college must be a government institution. The list includes IITs, NITs, IISc, ICAR-recognised colleges and State Agricultural Universities.

Your family income from all sources must not be more than ₹6 lakh a year. Girl students get preference during selection. One rule to note: children of Vylor and Buddy4Study employees cannot apply. There is no separate entrance test for this scholarship. Selection is based on your application and the eligibility rules.

## How much money you get, and what it covers

The amount depends on your level of study. Undergraduate students receive ₹25,000 a year. Postgraduate and PhD students receive ₹35,000 a year. The money is meant to cover the real costs of studying. You can use it for tuition fees, hostel charges, food, travel, books, stationery and research work.

For many agriculture students, ₹25,000 or ₹35,000 a year covers a large part of the yearly spend. Hostel and mess bills, field visit costs and books add up fast. This scholarship takes some of that weight off your family.

> "The scholarship is open to students in any year of their course, from first-year undergraduates to PhD scholars, and girl students get preference in selection."

## How to apply before November 30

Applications opened on October 28, 2026. The whole process is online, and it takes only a few steps.

- Open the Vylor Agritech Scholarship page on <a href="https://www.buddy4study.com/article/vylor-agritech-scholarship" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Buddy4Study</a> and register or log in.
- Click Apply Now to open the application form.
- Fill in your personal, academic and family income details.
- Upload the documents asked for on the form.
- Check everything once, then submit before November 30, 2026.

Read the form carefully before you submit. A wrong income figure or a missing document can hold up your application. Keep your ID proof, marksheets, income certificate and admission proof ready before you start, so you do not have to stop halfway.
