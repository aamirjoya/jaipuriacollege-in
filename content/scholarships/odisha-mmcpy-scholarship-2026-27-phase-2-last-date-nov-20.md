**Today, 20 November 2026, is the last day to apply for the Odisha MMCPY Scholarship 2026-27. The second phase closes tonight, and it is open only to fresh students who have not applied yet.**

Odisha's Higher Education Department opened this phase on 5 November for students who missed the first round. That second chance ends today. If you study in Odisha and have not filled the form yet, do it now. The portal gets slow in the last few hours, so do not wait till night.

MMCPY, short for Mukhyamantri Medhabi Chhatra Protsahan Yojana, is Odisha's main merit scholarship for college students. It covers several schemes under one roof, and you apply for all of them through a single portal.

## Five schemes, one portal, and up to Rs 20,000 a year

Five schemes fall under this phase 2 window: e-Medhabruti UG Merit, PG Merit, and Technical and Professional Merit, plus the Vyasakabi Fakir Mohan Bhasabruti Scheme for students of Odia language and the Gopabandhu Sikhya Sahayata Yojana for students from poor families.

Each scheme pays a different amount. UG students under e-Medhabruti get Rs 10,000 a year for three years, and PG students get Rs 15,000 a year for two years. Students in technical and professional courses get Rs 20,000 a year, and the Gopabandhu scheme also pays Rs 20,000 a year. For the Vyasakabi scheme, UG students get a one-time help of Rs 20,000 and PG students get Rs 30,000.

## Who can apply in this second phase

This phase is only for fresh applicants. Students studying UG, PG or technical and professional courses in SAMS and non-SAMS institutions can apply. That covers universities and colleges linked to UGC, AICTE or the Medical Council, along with management, engineering, medical and other technical institutes.

You must live in Odisha, and your family income should stay under Rs 8 lakh a year. Each scheme sets its own marks cut-off, so read the guideline on the portal before you apply. The money goes straight to your bank account, so link your Aadhaar to it first.

Rules differ from state to state. The [UP Scholarship 2026-27](/scholarships/up-scholarship-2026-27-class-9-12-window-reopened-nov-17/) reopened its window for school students in Uttar Pradesh, with its own dates and income limits. Odisha students should stick to the Odisha portal and its own rules. Our [scholarships](/scholarships/) page tracks other open windows across states.

## How to apply before the portal closes

Keep these documents ready before you start: your Aadhaar number, a recent photograph, an income certificate, a caste certificate if it applies to you, a resident certificate, the front page of your bank passbook, your matriculation certificate and your previous exam certificate. Students with a disability should also keep their disability certificate ready.

Follow these steps on the portal:

- Open the <a href="https://scholarship.odisha.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Odisha State Scholarship Portal</a> and register if you are a new user. If you already have an account, log in.
- Enter your Aadhaar number and proceed, then log in with your user ID and password.
- Choose the department name and the scheme you want to apply for.
- Fill in your student, academic, eligibility and bank details.
- Save the form as a draft if you need a break, or click Next to move ahead.
- Check the preview, then submit it. Download the form and take a printout.

The online form is only half the job. Your college or institute must verify your application by 30 November 2026. For the Gopabandhu scheme, verification at the district welfare officer and OSACS level runs till 15 December 2026. Talk to your college office after you submit, and ask them to finish the check on time. An unverified form does not move ahead.

## Stuck on the form? Use the helpline today

If the portal throws an error or your Aadhaar does not match, do not keep retrying the same step. The Higher Education Department runs a helpline on working days from 10:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Call 0674-2396550, 0674-2391126, 0674-2391127 or 0674-2391128. You can also write to scholarshiphed@gmail.com. The guidelines and FAQs on the portal home page answer most common problems, from wrong bank details to a photo that will not upload.

## What to do right now

Apply on the <a href="https://scholarship.odisha.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Odisha State Scholarship Portal</a> before the day ends. Fill the form in one sitting if you can, save a copy, and tell your college office to verify it before 30 November. A missed deadline cannot be fixed later, and this phase was the second chance for students who missed the first one.
