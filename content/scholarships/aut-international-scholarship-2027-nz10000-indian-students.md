**Indian students planning to study in New Zealand in 2027 can still get help with their first-year fees. Auckland University of Technology (AUT) offers NZ$10,000 to undergraduate students and NZ$7,000 to postgraduate students under its International Scholarships for South Asia. The Semester 1 round closed on November 22, 2026, but the Semester 2 round stays open till May 23, 2027.**

The scholarship is open to students from eight South Asian countries, and India is on the list. It is a contribution to tuition fees for the first year only. It does not cover living costs, and it does not pay the full fee. Even so, NZ$10,000 takes a real chunk off the first-year bill, and that is why this scheme draws heavy interest every year.

## What the AUT scholarship pays

Undergraduate students in a bachelor's degree programme get a contribution of NZ$10,000 towards tuition fees in the first year of study. Postgraduate students in a master's degree programme get NZ$7,000. The postgraduate category also covers postgraduate certificates and diplomas that lead to a master's degree.

Doctoral programmes are not covered. The award lasts one year only. AUT credits the money to the student's fees account after the scholarship is accepted, an unconditional offer of place is received, and the tuition fees minus the scholarship amount are paid.

## Who can apply from India

The scheme is open to citizens of India, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan and the Maldives. You must be a new, full-fee-paying international student. You must also hold a current conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible AUT programme that starts in Semester 1 or Semester 2 of 2027.

Your programme must be at bachelor's degree level or above, and it must carry at least 120 points. Previous AUT students cannot apply, and that includes graduates of the university who want to study for a higher qualification. Students who have already started their studies at AUT also cannot apply.

Two more conditions are worth knowing. The scholarship cannot be combined with another AUT International Scholarship that offers a tuition fee contribution. Students whose studies are fully or partly funded by the New Zealand government or their home government are also not eligible.

## What you need before you apply

AUT asks for four things with the application. Keep them ready before you start, because an incomplete application with missing documents may be treated as ineligible.

- A current conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible 2027 programme
- A detailed academic transcript for your most recent qualification, including the grading scale
- A completion certificate, such as a bachelor's degree certificate or senior secondary school results
- Your AUT student ID

Selection is done by a committee with members from the AUT International Office and the relevant faculty. Academic achievement is considered during selection, and awards are made on the committee's recommendation. That means strong marks help, but there is no published cut-off score to chase.

> The money goes straight to your AUT fees account. It is a contribution to first-year tuition, not a full scholarship, and it lasts one year.

## How to apply for the AUT scholarship

Applications go through the AUT scholarships database on the university website. The steps are simple, but the order matters. You need the offer first, and only then does the scholarship application make sense.

- Apply for an eligible AUT programme for 2027 and get your conditional or unconditional offer
- Note down your AUT student ID from the offer
- Collect your transcript with the grading scale and your completion certificate
- Open the AUT scholarships database and submit the International Scholarship application with all documents attached
- Apply early, because the round can close before the last date if all places are filled

You can start the process on the <a href="https://www.aut.ac.nz/scholarships" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AUT scholarships page</a>, which links to the application database and the full regulations for the South Asia round.

## Semester 1 closed today, Semester 2 is the open door

Semester 1 applications for 2027 closed at 11:55 pm New Zealand time on Sunday, November 22, 2026. That is late afternoon in India on the same day, so most Indian students reading this now have missed that round. If that is you, do not wait for next year. The Semester 2 round closes on May 23, 2027, or earlier if all places are filled.

The same award amounts apply. Undergraduate students can get NZ$10,000 and postgraduate students NZ$7,000 towards first-year tuition. The same rules apply too: you need an offer for a programme starting in 2027, and you must enrol full-time and complete 120 points during the scholarship period.
