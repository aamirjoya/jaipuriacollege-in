**First-year college students have nine days left to claim up to ₹12,000 a year from the central government. Fresh applications for the PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship 2026-27 close on November 30, 2026, on the National Scholarship Portal. If you finished Class 12 this year, joined a regular degree course, and your family earns up to ₹4.5 lakh a year, you can apply.**

The scheme is run by the Ministry of Education, and up to 82,000 fresh [scholarships](/scholarships/) are given every year. Half of those seats are kept for girls. The money goes straight into your Aadhaar-seeded bank account.

## How much you get, year by year

You get ₹12,000 a year for the first three years of graduation, and ₹20,000 a year at post-graduation level. BTech and BE students get ₹12,000 for the first three years and ₹20,000 in the fourth year. Students in five-year professional or integrated courses get ₹20,000 in the fourth and fifth years.

The scholarship runs for a maximum of five years, and you must renew it on the portal each year. Renewal needs at least 50% marks in the previous year and 75% attendance.

## Who can apply fresh

Four checks decide whether you can apply. Miss one, and the form will not go through.

- You passed Class 12 in 2026 and joined the first year of a regular degree course. Correspondence, distance learning and diploma courses are not covered.
- You scored above the 80th percentile of students who passed in your stream from your board. That means you are in the top 20% of your board. It does not mean 80% marks, and many students get this wrong.
- Your family income from all sources is up to ₹4.5 lakh a year. You will need an income certificate for a fresh application.
- You are not taking another scholarship or fee reimbursement for the same purpose.

Your board sends a merit list of top 20% students to the portal. If your name is not on it, the scheme may not show up when you log in. Drop-year students and older students who never held this scholarship cannot apply fresh.

> The 80th percentile rule is about your rank in your board, not your marks. A student with 78% can qualify if that puts her in the top 20% of her board stream, while a student with 85% may miss out if her board's cut-off sits higher.

The 82,000 seats are split by board, stream, gender and category, and the final list is made on merit within these slots.

## How to apply before November 30

Apply on the <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official National Scholarship Portal</a>. The form is free, and you do not need an agent. Follow these steps:

- Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) with your mobile number and Aadhaar, and finish e-KYC. Then log in with your Aadhaar or OTR number, select your domicile state and the post-matric option. This creates your Application ID.
- Fill in your details. Your name, father's name and Class 12 roll number must match your marksheet exactly.
- Pick your institute carefully using its AISHE code. Many colleges have similar names, and picking the wrong one means your college cannot verify you.
- Select the PM-USP Central Sector Scheme, upload your documents and submit before November 30, 2026.
- Tell your college scholarship desk the same day. Institute verification closes on December 15, 2026, and the state nodal officer finishes by December 31. A form your college does not verify is treated as invalid.

Keep your Class 12 marksheet, income certificate, category certificate if it applies, and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account in your own name ready before you start.

## Fresh or renewal: do not mix them up

This window is only for first-time applicants. If you already received this scholarship last year, you needed the renewal window, and that closed on October 31, 2026. Our guide to the [PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship renewal](/scholarships/pm-usp-csss-scholarship-renewal-2026-deadline-oct-31/) explains the renewal rules in full.

If your state already pays your full college fee, compare the two schemes first, because you cannot take both. For students with no fee support, this is straightforward money for doing well in Class 12. Nine days is enough, but only if your income certificate and Aadhaar-seeded bank account are ready.

## FAQ

### What is the last date to apply for the PM-USP Central Sector Scholarship 2026-27?

November 30, 2026. Fresh applications close on that date on the National Scholarship Portal. Your college must verify the form by December 15, 2026.

### How much money does the Central Sector Scholarship pay?

₹12,000 a year for the first three years of graduation, and ₹20,000 a year at post-graduation. BTech and BE students get ₹20,000 in the fourth year, and students in five-year professional or integrated courses get ₹20,000 in the fourth and fifth years.

### Who can apply for a fresh Central Sector Scholarship?

First-year students in a regular degree course who scored above the 80th percentile in their Class 12 board stream and whose family income is up to ₹4.5 lakh a year. Distance, diploma and drop-year students cannot apply fresh.

### I already got this scholarship last year. Do I apply fresh?

No. Students who already hold the scholarship must renew it, not apply fresh. The renewal window for 2026-27 closed on October 31, 2026.
