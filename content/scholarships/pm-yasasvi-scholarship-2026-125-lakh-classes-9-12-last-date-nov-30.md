**Students in Classes 9 to 12 from OBC, EBC and DNT families can get up to Rs 1.25 lakh a year under the PM YASASVI scholarship. The form closes on 30 November 2026, five days from today, and it goes only through the <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Scholarship Portal</a>.**

The scheme is run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Its full name is the PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India. This round covers the Top Class Education in Schools part of the scheme. Forms reopened on 22 October, and the portal stops taking them on 30 November.

| Class | Money a year |
|---|---|
| Class 9 and 10 | Up to Rs 75,000 |
| Class 11 and 12 | Up to Rs 1,25,000 |

The money covers tuition fees, hostel charges, books and stationery, and other school charges. For college students under the same scheme, the support also covers living costs and a one-time computer or laptop allowance, within the limits the scheme sets.

## Who can apply

Three conditions decide it. The student must belong to an OBC, EBC or denotified, nomadic or semi-nomadic tribe family. The family income from all sources must stay under Rs 2.5 lakh a year. And the student must study in Classes 9, 10, 11 or 12 in a school listed under the Top Class Schools scheme.

That last point matters. A student in a school that is not on the Top Class Schools list cannot use this part of the scheme, so ask your school office whether your school is on the list before you fill the form. Selection runs on merit, and seats are limited. 30% of the scholarships are kept for girl students.

## How to apply before 30 November

The form is online only. Keep these papers ready before you start: Aadhaar card, caste certificate, income certificate, last year's marksheet, school bonafide certificate, bank account details linked with Aadhaar, and a recent photo. A domicile certificate helps if the form asks for it, and a disability certificate if it applies to you.

- Open <a href="https://scholarships.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official portal</a> and finish the One-Time Registration if you have not done it before.
- Log in and search for "PM YASASVI Central Sector Scheme of Top Class Education in Schools for OBC, EBC and DNT Students".
- Fill in personal, school and family income details.
- Upload the documents the form asks for.
- Check the form once more and submit it. Save the reference number.

Do not wait for the last day. The portal slows down when thousands of students submit at once, and a form stuck halfway on 30 November will not count.

> Rs 75,000 a year for Classes 9 and 10, Rs 1.25 lakh for Classes 11 and 12. The form closes on 30 November.

## Five parts, one scheme

PM YASASVI has five parts: pre-matric scholarship, post-matric scholarship, Top Class Education in Schools, Top Class Education in College, and hostel facilities for OBC boys and girls. This article covers the school part only, because that is the one whose form closes on 30 November.

The same deadline applies to more schemes. [Five other scholarships close on 30 November](/scholarships/five-scholarships-closing-nov-30-2026/), with awards from Rs 10,000 to Rs 2 lakh a year. College students should also check the [Central Sector Scholarship for college students](/scholarships/pm-usp-csss-scholarship-fresh-application-nov-30-2026/), which runs on the same portal and closes on the same day. More schemes with deadlines this month are listed in our [scholarships section](/scholarships/).

## One more date to remember

If the portal marks your form as defective, you get time to fix it. Corrections stay open till 15 December 2026. Check the status of your form after you submit it, so a small mistake does not cost you the scholarship.
