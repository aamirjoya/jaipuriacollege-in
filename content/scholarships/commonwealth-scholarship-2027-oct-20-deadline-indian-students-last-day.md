**The clock is almost at zero. The UK's most generous postgraduate scholarship closes in less than 24 hours, and Indian students who have not submitted their forms yet must act tonight or tomorrow morning.**

The Commonwealth Master's Scholarship 2027-28 shuts its portal on Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at 8:30 PM Indian time. What you get if you win it: full tuition at a UK university, a monthly living stipend of £1,712 (roughly Rs 2.15 lakh), £2,000 a month if you study in London, a free return flight from India, and a travel grant for your studies. There are almost no postgraduate awards this complete, and this is your final day to apply.

This scholarship sits under the Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan, which the UK government has funded since 1960. Over 34,000 scholars have gone through it. The programme runs on one clear expectation: you study in the UK, then you come back to India and use what you learned here. Your application is read through that lens from the first line to the last.

## Who can apply: the eligibility bar is clear

You must be an Indian citizen and resident. You need a first degree of at least upper second-class (2:1) standard, which you must hold by September 2027. You must be unable to afford UK study without this award. You should be available to start your course in September or October 2027.

Two things students often misunderstand. First, there is no age limit. Second, the CSC asks for no IELTS score from Indian applicants. What it does demand is a strong development angle: your personal statement must explain how your degree will help India after you return. Applications that skip that part rarely move forward.

The scholarship covers a full-time, one-year taught Master's in the UK. MBAs are not covered. Courses must fit one of the CSC's development themes, such as science and technology, health systems, or global prosperity.

## The catch most students miss: you must apply twice

India follows a dual-application path. You fill the CSC online form on the Commission's portal, and you also apply to India's national nominating agency. For India, that route runs through the Education Ministry's External Scholarships Division (applicants usually know it through the SAKSHAT portal).

Here is the part that matters tonight: the CSC deadline stands on its own. Even if the nominating agency's notice looks delayed or the portal shows old cycle dates, the CSC form still closes at 8:30 PM IST on October 20. Submit the CSC form first to lock in a valid application ID, then sort the India-side paperwork in parallel. Nominating agencies send their lists by December 2026, and CSC results typically reach winners by July 2027.

To start, go to the <a href="https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Commonwealth Scholarship Commission website</a> and open the application system from there. Use only the official site. The CSC itself has warned students about copycat organisations running lookalike "scholarship council" sites.

## Tonight and tomorrow: the last-day playbook

Treat 8:30 PM on October 20 as the hard wall, not the target. Portals slow down when thousands of students log in at once, so set your own cutoff at least six hours earlier.

- **Finish the CSC form first.** Save every section, generate the final review, and submit. Keep a PDF of your submitted summary.
- **Chase your referees tonight.** You need two references, and they must be uploaded inside the CSC system before the deadline. The Commission does not accept references by email or after the cutoff. Confirm that your referees use their institutional email IDs and are free tomorrow.
- **Merge your transcripts into one clean PDF.** Open each file after compression to make sure it is readable. Blurry scans are a silent rejection.
- **Match details across documents.** Your passport name, application name, and transcript name should all agree. Small mismatches create doubt.
- **Keep your development statement tight.** Tie your course choice to one of the CSC themes and to a problem back home in India. Short and specific beats long and generic.

One more warning: never pay anyone who claims they can get you nominated. Applications are free, and guarantees are scams. Never share your login credentials with agents either.

> "The Commonwealth Scholarship is one of the few awards that covers everything: tuition, living costs, and the flight home. But it is not a lottery ticket. The winners are the students who can clearly explain what their one year in the UK will change back in India. The way the CSC reads applications has not changed in decades: development impact first, grades second."

## What happens after you submit

National shortlisting runs through December. CSC assessment follows into early 2027. Outcome emails usually arrive by July 2027, and the course begins in September or October 2027.
