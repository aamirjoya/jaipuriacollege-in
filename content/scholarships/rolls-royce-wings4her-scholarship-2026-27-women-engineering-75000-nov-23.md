**Rolls-Royce India will pay ₹75,000 a year to women studying engineering, and the last date to apply is November 23, 2026. The Wings4Her Scholarship 2026-27 is open to girls in the first and second year of a BTech or BE course at AICTE-recognised colleges across India.**

The scholarship is meant for students who do well in class and need money to keep studying. Rolls-Royce runs the programme with Buddy4Study, the platform that handles the applications. If you are in your first or second year of engineering and your family income is within the limit, this is one of the biggest scholarships open right now.

## What you get: ₹75,000 every year

Selected students receive ₹75,000 per year. The money is meant to help with academic expenses, so fees, books and other study costs become easier to manage.

It is one of several [scholarships](/scholarships/) that support girl students in technical courses. A similar one is the [WINGS Scholarship at IIT Bombay](/scholarships/wings-scholarship-iit-bombay-2-lakh-girls-nov-30/), which pays ₹2 lakh a year in tuition support, but only for students joining IIT Bombay. Wings4Her is different. It is open to students at any AICTE-recognised engineering college in India.

## Who can apply

The rules are clear, and you should check each one before you fill the form:

- You must be a female student in the first or second year of an undergraduate engineering degree.
- Your college must be recognised by the AICTE.
- You need at least 60% marks, or an equivalent CGPA or grade, in Class 10, Class 12 and your latest available semester.
- Your family's yearly income from all sources should not be more than ₹4 lakh.
- Children of employees of Buddy4Study or Rolls-Royce India cannot apply.

One point many students miss: students who received this scholarship in an earlier year can apply again. If you got Wings4Her last year, you are not blocked from trying again for 2026-27. And if your latest semester marksheet is not out yet, the most recent available odd or even semester marksheet will be considered.

> First and second year only. If you are in third year or later, this door is already shut for this cycle, so do not wait for the last day to check your eligibility.

## Who gets preference

Meeting the basic rules gets you in the race. Some students get preference in selection:

- Students with disabilities (PwD).
- Students from single-parent households and orphans.
- Students who live in aspirational districts.
- Students in six branches: Aerospace, Mechanical, Electronics, Electrical, Computer Science and Marine Engineering.

If you fall in one of these groups, say so clearly in your form. It can matter when many students apply.

## How to apply before November 23

Applications are online only. There is no offline form and no college counter to visit. Follow these steps:

- Open the <a href="https://www.buddy4study.com/page/rolls-royce-wings4her-scholarship-for-women-engineering-students" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Wings4Her page on Buddy4Study</a> and read the full list of rules.
- Register on Buddy4Study, or log in if you already have an account.
- Fill the application form with your personal, academic and family income details.
- Upload the documents the form asks for. Keep your Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, your latest semester marksheet and your family income proof ready before you start.
- Check every detail once, then submit the form before November 23, 2026.

Use a laptop if you can. A bigger screen makes it easier to check your entries and upload clear scans. Save a copy of the submitted form and any reference number you get.

## Two more scholarships closing this month

November is a busy month for scholarship deadlines, and two more are worth a look. The [Aditya Birla Capital Scholarship](/scholarships/aditya-birla-capital-scholarship-2026-27-girls-60000-nov-22-last-date/) supports girl students from Class 9 to postgraduate level with up to ₹60,000, and its last date is November 22, 2026. The Colgate Keep India Smiling Scholarship gives a one-time ₹75,000 to BDS and MDS students, with the last date on November 30, 2026.

Check each one against your own course and year before you apply. The Wings4Her deadline on November 23 is the nearest of the three, so start with it if engineering is your field.
