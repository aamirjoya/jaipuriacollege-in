**Five scholarships for Indian students close on the same day, 30 November 2026, and the amounts run from Rs 10,000 to Rs 2 lakh a year. Two of them are for daughters of truck and bus drivers, one pays full tuition for women joining IIT Bombay, one supports first-year professional course students, and one is for agriculture students. All five take applications online through Buddy4Study.**

Seven days are left. The deadline is the same for every scheme in this list, so a student who fits more than one can apply to each of them before 30 November. The table below gives the short version. The sections after it carry the details that decide whether you qualify.

| Scholarship | Who it is for | Amount | Last date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mahindra Saarthi Abhiyaan | Daughters of truck drivers, Class 11 to PG | Rs 10,000 | 30 November 2026 |
| SDEF India Scholarship | First-year BTech, MBBS, BDS or BArch students | Up to Rs 50,000 a year | 30 November 2026 |
| WINGS, IIT Bombay | Women joining UG STEM at IIT Bombay in 2027-28 | Rs 2 lakh a year | 30 November 2026 |
| Vylor Agritech Scholarship | Agriculture students, UG, PG and PhD | Rs 25,000 to Rs 35,000 a year | 30 November 2026 |
| SML Mahindra Scholarship | Daughters of commercial vehicle drivers | Up to Rs 30,000 | 30 November 2026 |

## Two schemes for daughters of drivers

Two of the five schemes are for girls whose fathers drive for a living. They look alike at first glance, so check which one fits you.

Mahindra Saarthi Abhiyaan is open to girls in Class 11, Class 12, graduation or postgraduation whose father or guardian drives a truck. The award is a fixed Rs 10,000 for each selected student. Children of Buddy4Study or Mahindra and Mahindra employees cannot apply.

SML Mahindra works in two parts. SML Saarthi gives Rs 10,000 to girls in Class 11 and 12. SML Unnati gives Rs 30,000 to girls doing an undergraduate degree, diploma or polytechnic course in STEM or a core engineering subject. The family income must not be more than Rs 6 lakh a year, and the student must have scored at least 60% marks. The scheme covers daughters of drivers of heavy and light goods vehicles and of large passenger vehicles such as buses.

A girl who fits both schemes can apply to both. The income rule and the 60% rule apply only to SML Mahindra.

## The other three schemes

The biggest award in this list is WINGS, the Women INspiring Growth in STEM scholarship at IIT Bombay. It gives Rs 2 lakh a year in tuition support to women planning an undergraduate STEM course at the institute in the 2027-28 session. The scheme was started by IIT Bombay alumnus Bharat Desai and his wife Neerja Sethi. The money continues only if the student keeps a CPI of 6.0 or above, and she must not hold another government tuition grant at the same time. We have covered the [WINGS scholarship](/scholarships/wings-scholarship-iit-bombay-2-lakh-girls-nov-30/) in detail here.

The SDEF India Scholarship comes from the Swami Dayanand Education Foundation. It is for students joining the first year of a full-time professional undergraduate course in 2026-27, in BTech or BE, MBBS, BDS or BArch. The family income must not be more than Rs 12 lakh a year, and the student must not have taken a drop or gap year after Class 12. Selected students get up to Rs 50,000 a year, paid to the college, and the support can run for up to four years.

For agriculture students, the Vylor Agritech Scholarship covers undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD courses in agriculture and allied fields. The college must be a government institution such as an IIT, NIT, IISc, ICAR-recognised college or State Agricultural University. Family income must not be more than Rs 6 lakh a year. Undergraduates get Rs 25,000 a year, while postgraduates and PhD students get Rs 35,000 a year. We have covered the [Vylor Agritech scholarship](/scholarships/vylor-agritech-scholarship-2026-27-agriculture-35000-nov-30/) in detail here.

## How to apply before 30 November

Every scheme in this list takes applications on the Buddy4Study portal. There is no offline form. The steps are the same for all five:

- Open the scholarship page on <a href="https://www.buddy4study.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Buddy4Study</a> and search for the scheme by name.
- Log in with your Buddy4Study account, or create a free one with your email ID and mobile number.
- Fill the form with personal and academic details, and answer the eligibility questions honestly.
- Complete the eKYC step and upload the documents the form asks for, such as marksheets, income proof and bank account details.
- Check every detail once more and submit the form before 30 November 2026. Save a copy of the confirmation.

Do not leave it for the last day. The portal slows down when thousands of students apply at once, and a form submitted after the deadline is not accepted.

## What to check before you submit

A wrong detail in the form can cost the scholarship. Check that the name on the form matches the name on your marksheet and Aadhaar. Check the income figure against the scheme's limit, because the limits differ: Rs 12 lakh for SDEF, Rs 6 lakh for Vylor and SML Mahindra. Check the marks rule too. SML Mahindra asks for 60%, while the other schemes carry their own academic conditions.

If you are applying to WINGS, remember that the scholarship is confirmed only after admission to IIT Bombay. You can register interest now and complete the process when the admission comes through.
