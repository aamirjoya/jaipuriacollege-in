Jaipuria College is an independent education-news portal. This page explains how we work, so readers can judge our coverage for themselves.

## How stories are sourced

We cover college admissions, university results, admit cards, scholarships and education news for Indian students. Our starting points are official sources: university websites, government portals, examination bodies and official notices. We also follow announcements from recognised education authorities.

## Two independent sources for every fact

Every fact in an article — a date, a deadline, a fee, a seat count, a result — must be confirmed against at least two independent sources before we publish. One source is never enough. If a fact cannot be verified, it does not appear in the article.

## No fabrication

We never invent quotes, statistics, deadlines or announcements. We do not present estimates as facts, and we do not publish a figure we cannot trace to a source.

## Human review before publishing

Every article is reviewed by a member of the editorial desk before it is published. Nothing goes live without that check.

## How corrections work

When we get something wrong, we correct the article promptly and add a visible correction note to it, stating what changed. The original publication date of the article is never changed — a correction adds a note and an updated timestamp. Our full [corrections policy](/corrections/) explains how readers can report an error.

## AI is never presented as a human author

Software may help with routine tasks, but it is never presented as a person. Every article on this site is published under the byline of the [Editorial Desk](/author/editorial-desk/) — a real editorial team — and is reviewed by a human before publication. No article claims to be written by a named person who does not exist.

## Independence

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any college, university or educational institution. No institution pays for coverage, and coverage never influences what we publish.
