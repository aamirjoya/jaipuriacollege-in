The National Testing Agency has released the advance city intimation slip for the UGC NET December 2026 exam today, November 23. Candidates can log in to the <a href="https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UGC NET portal</a> with their application number and password to see the city where their exam centre will be located.

**The exam runs from December 14 to 18 and on December 21, and the admit card will follow on December 7.**

More than 9.75 lakh candidates have paid the exam fee for this session, according to an NTA update on November 2. The test will run in computer-based mode over six days, in two shifts each day, across 87 subjects.

## The slip names the city, not the centre

The city intimation slip tells you only the city. It does not carry the centre address, the exam date, the shift, or the reporting time. Those details will appear on the admit card, which NTA will release on December 7.

That gap is by design. The slip exists so candidates can plan travel and stay in advance, while the exact centre is confirmed closer to the exam. Anyone waiting for the centre address will have to wait for the admit card.

## Dates to remember

| Event | Date |

| Exam | December 14 to 18 and December 21, 2026 |
| Morning shift | 9 am to 12 noon |

NTA announced the subject-wise schedule in a notice dated November 19. In that notice, the agency also said the city intimation would appear on its website 21 days before the exam. The [revised exam dates](/education-news/ugc-net-december-2026-revised-bulletin-new-exam-dates-rules/) for the December session were set earlier in the cycle, and the [correction window](/education-news/ugc-net-december-2026-correction-window-open-nov-4-deadline/) for applications opened earlier this month.

## How to download the city slip

1. Go to ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
2. Open the link for the UGC NET December 2026 advance city intimation slip on the homepage.
3. Enter your application number and password, or date of birth, and complete the captcha.
4. Your allotted exam city will appear on the screen. Download the slip and keep a copy saved.

If the link does not open at first, check again after some time. NTA has not given a fixed release time for the slip. For help with the download, candidates can call the NTA helpline at 011-40759000 or write to ugcnet@nta.ac.in.

## New exam-day rules this time

NTA has changed a few rules for this session, and they are worth reading before exam day.

Centre gates will close 30 minutes before the exam begins, so reaching early matters more than usual. Long boots are not allowed inside the centre. Normal shoes, slippers and sandals with low heels are fine.

Wrist watches are now on the barred list, and candidates should not carry a ball point pen. NTA will provide a pen at the test centre.

## What the exam decides

UGC NET is held in two papers taken in a single sitting of three hours, with no break between Paper 1 and Paper 2. The exam decides eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship, Assistant Professor posts and PhD admission.

With 9,75,505 candidates having paid the fee, the December session is one of the largest NTA exams of the year. The city slip is the first document in the final stretch. Our [education news](/education-news/) section will carry the admit card update when it goes live on December 7.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?**

No. The slip only tells you the city where your exam centre is located. The admit card, coming on December 7, will carry the centre address, exam date, shift and reporting time.

**What do I need to download the UGC NET city slip?**

Your application number and password or date of birth, plus the security captcha on the official portal, ugcnet.nta.nic.in.

**When will the UGC NET December 2026 admit card be released?**

NTA will release the admit card on December 7, 2026, a week before the exam starts.

**How many candidates are appearing for UGC NET December 2026?**

NTA said 9,75,505 candidates had paid the exam fee as of its November 2 update. In all, 10,17,544 candidates had registered by October 31.
