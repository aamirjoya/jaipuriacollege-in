**Seventy-five postgraduate medical students could lose the seats they won in NEET PG Round 1. Their colleges never finished the admission reporting on the MCC portal. The Medical Counselling Committee has now given those colleges one last day, today, November 13, to finish the job.**

MCC issued the notice on Thursday, November 12. It named 72 institutes that had failed to complete the mandatory reporting, even after repeated emails and phone calls. As a one-time measure, the Round 1 reporting portal has been reopened for November 13 only, from 9:30 AM to 11 PM.

The affected students hold confirmed allotments. Their seats are real. What is missing is the online paperwork. Until the college reports the admission on the portal, MCC does not count the student as joined.

## What happens if the college misses today

MCC has spelled out the result in plain words. Any admission not submitted on the portal by the college will be treated as null and void. The candidate will be marked as Not Reported. The seat will then go back into the pool and become available for allotment in the next round.

That is why today matters so much. A student who did everything right can still lose a seat because the institute did not finish its part of the process. MCC has also warned that any fallout from non-compliance will be the sole responsibility of the institute or the candidate.

Students in this group should contact their college at once. Ask the admission office to confirm that your online admission letter has been generated on the portal. Do not wait for the college to call you. The window closes at 11 PM tonight.

## Why 72 colleges could not finish in time

The delay came from two sides. Some candidates uploaded certificates that were unclear or hard to read. The colleges sent those documents back for correction, but the revised copies never came in. Other institutes simply ran out of time on verification and could not clear the pending cases before the portal closed.

A senior health ministry official told reporters that smaller DNB institutes had the hardest time. Many of them do not have dedicated administrative staff for online counselling work. In some places, doctors who spend the day treating patients were also handling document verification, while the data entry work sat with clerical staff.

The numbers put the problem in perspective. About 72 institutions out of roughly 1,600 to 1,700 participating institutes are affected. The list includes big names too, such as Grant Medical College in Mumbai, ESIC Medical College and Hospital in Ahmedabad, Santosh Medical College in Ghaziabad, Mahatma Gandhi Medical College and Research Institute in Puducherry, Shroff Eye Centre in Delhi, Medanta Super Speciality Hospital in Indore, and the Ranchi Institute of Neuro-Psychiatry and Allied Sciences.

> "Any admission not submitted on the portal by the college authorities would be treated as null and void."

## Round 2 choice filling paused for two hours today

MCC also paused [NEET PG Round 2](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-revised-dates-nov-17-result/) choice filling for two hours today, from 12 noon to 2 PM. The pause, announced in Notice No. 9 on November 13, gave institutes a clear slot to finish the pending Round 1 reporting without clashing with live choice filling.

Choice filling resumed after 2 PM. Candidates should not have filled or changed choices during the two-hour pause. The Round 2 deadlines have not changed. Registration closes at 3 PM on November 14. Choice filling closes at 11:55 PM on November 15. The provisional result comes on November 16 and the final Round 2 result on November 17, with reporting and joining from November 18 to 24.

The reporting mess also explains part of the Round 2 churn. MCC revised the Round 2 schedule after the National Medical Commission approved [9,783 new PG seats](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-9783-new-seats-total-68305/), taking the total to 68,305 this year. New seats, new matrices, and pending Round 1 cases have all landed in the same week.

## What students should do today

If you are one of the 75 affected candidates, act now. The steps are simple, and each one takes only a few minutes.

1. Call or visit your college admission office and ask whether your Round 1 admission has been reported on the MCC portal.
2. Ask the college to confirm that your online admission letter has been generated. That letter is the proof that the reporting is done.
3. If the college says documents are pending, upload clear, readable copies at once and ask for confirmation that they were accepted.
4. If you are also taking part in Round 2, fill your choices only after 2 PM today, and keep an eye on the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/pg-medical-counselling/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC PG counselling portal</a> for fresh notices.
5. Keep checking the [education news section](/education-news/) for the next MCC notice, including the Round 3 dates, which have not been announced yet.

MCC has called this a one-time measure. There is no sign of another extension. For the 75 students waiting today, the next few hours decide whether their Round 1 seat stays theirs.
