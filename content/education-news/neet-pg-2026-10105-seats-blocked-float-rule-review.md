**More than 10,000 postgraduate medical seats are stuck in the "blocked" category after Round 2 of [NEET PG 2026](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-9783-new-seats-total-68305/). The government is now looking hard at the float rule that made this possible. For thousands of candidates still waiting for a seat, what happens next could decide whether those seats open up in Round 3.**

The number comes from the SARTHA app, a medical admission and counselling platform. Its data, filtered for Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) counselling, shows 10,105 seats listed under "Blocking" after Round 2. The Times of India first reported the figures, and they have since been picked up by other news outlets. The data was shared on social media by Dr Kapil Dev, and medico-socio activist Dr Nachiket Bhatia has also flagged the issue.

## What a blocked seat means for waiting candidates

A blocked seat is not a vacant seat. It is a seat held by a candidate who also has an allotment somewhere else. In most cases, the candidate holds one seat through All India Quota counselling and another through a state counselling process. While the seat stays blocked, no other candidate can claim it.

This hurts the candidates waiting in line. A seat that looks filled on paper may actually come back into the pool later, but only after the candidate holding it gives it up. Till then, it sits out of reach for everyone else. With more than 10,000 seats in this state, the scale of the problem is hard to ignore.

## MD General Medicine tops the list with 1,026 blocked seats

The branch-wise split shows where the pressure is highest:

- MD General Medicine: 1,026 blocked seats
- MS Obstetrics and Gynaecology: 861 blocked seats
- MS General Surgery: 786 blocked seats

These are among the most sought-after branches in postgraduate medicine. General Medicine alone accounts for one in ten of all blocked seats. For candidates hoping for a General Medicine seat in Round 3, these numbers explain why the competition feels so tight even when the total seat count looks large.

## The float option is at the centre of the problem

The float option lets a candidate keep an allotted seat while trying for a better one in the next round. It was brought in to help students, and for many it does. But when All India and state counselling run side by side on separate portals, a candidate can float in both at the same time.

A senior official familiar with the counselling process put it plainly: "A candidate may opt for float in both processes and end up blocking two seats." That single line explains most of the 10,105.

There is a second problem behind it. India still has no common platform that links All India and state counselling. The two processes run on separate portals, so neither side can easily see that a candidate already holds a seat in the other. The official flagged this gap as well, and it is one reason the double holding went unnoticed till the SARTHA data made it visible.

> "This is the first time we have introduced the float system in this manner. We will analyse the situation after the third round."

## What the government may change

The government is weighing two changes for future counselling cycles. The first is to allow the float option only in Round 1. The second is to make physical joining compulsory from Round 2. Either change would force candidates to pick one seat and free the other much earlier.

Nothing is final yet. The official said the government will study the position after Round 3 before taking a call. That means the rules for this year's remaining rounds are unlikely to change. Candidates in Round 3 and the stray vacancy round will play by the current rules.

## What Round 2 candidates must do right now

The [Round 2 final result](/results/neet-pg-round-2-provisional-allotment-out-nov-20-2026/) was declared on November 20, after a delay caused by a Madras High Court interim stay on November 17. The court later clarified that the stay applied only to one deemed university, and MCC resumed the process.

Candidates who got a seat in Round 2 must finish reporting and admission formalities by November 25, 2026, by 10 pm. MCC has said clearly that no extension will be given. The allotment list covers 36,824 candidates, with fresh allotments, upgrades and retained seats across MD, MS and diploma courses.

If you have a seat, do not wait for the last day. Carry your original documents, complete the verification at the allotted college, and download your allotment letter from the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MCC portal</a> after logging in with your registration details.

Round 3 counselling is scheduled from November 26 to December 4, as per the MCC schedule published on November 16. The stray vacancy round for All India Quota seats is scheduled from December 16 to 24. Both dates can change if the court matter throws up fresh directions, so check the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC website</a> before you plan travel or choice filling.
