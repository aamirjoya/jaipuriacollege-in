**The Maharashtra State CET Cell has released the tentative schedule for MHT CET 2027, and it is the biggest change to the exam in years. Twenty-one entrance exams will run from January 28 to June 10, 2027, and eleven courses will now get two attempts instead of one.**

The schedule came out in a press note dated November 16, 2026. It covers undergraduate and postgraduate professional courses for the academic year 2027-28. The MBA, MMS, PGDM and PGCM exams open the season on January 28, and the BEd exams close it on June 10.

## Two attempts for 11 courses, and the first one is a must

This is the headline change. Till last year, only three courses had two attempts. From 2027, eleven courses get the same benefit. The list covers MBA/MMS, PGDM, PGCM, the five-year LLB, the PCM group, the PCB group, BHMCT, BCA, BBA, BMS and BBM.

The rule is simple. The first attempt is compulsory for every student. The second attempt is optional. If you sit for both, the CET Cell will count the better percentile of the two for admission. A rough paper in April no longer has to decide your whole year.

The numbers explain the change. In 2025-26, 18,14,456 students registered for 20 CETs and 16,31,335 appeared. With that many students in the fray, one bad day was costing too many of them a seat.

> "Starting this year, CETs will be conducted for the first time for three courses: PGDM, PGCM and GNM," said Higher and Technical Education Minister Chandrakant Dada Patil.

One detail matters for Class 12 students. Both attempts for the big [entrance exams](/education-news/) sit after the board exams, so the CET dates will not clash with your school papers.

## Three new CETs, and one course dropped

This year brings three CETs that never existed before. PGDM, PGCM and GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) get their own entrance tests for the first time. The GNM test is a big one for nursing aspirants in Maharashtra, since [BSc Nursing](/education-news/bsc-nursing-zero-percentile-maharashtra-nov-15-deadline-2026/) seats in the state have drawn heavy competition this year.

At the same time, one CET is gone. The three-year integrated BEd-MEd course will not have a CET in 2027: only one institute offers it, with 55 seats, and only 12 students took admission in 2025-26.

The June dates for MPEd, MEd and BEd also came from a request. Students and institutes had asked for these exams to be held in June, and the CET Cell agreed. That is why the teaching courses close the calendar instead of sitting in the middle of it.

## What students should do now

First, do not wait for the application form to start preparing. The dates are out, and the gap between the two attempts is short. For PCM, the two windows are barely a month apart. Plan your revision so the first attempt is your real attempt, not a trial run.

Second, download the timetable PDF from the <a href="https://cetcell.mahacet.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CET Cell website</a>. That PDF is the only source that counts: dates on WhatsApp groups and coaching pages can be old or wrong.

Third, watch for the application notice. Registration dates are not out yet. The notice will carry the fee, the documents needed, and the last date to apply, so check the site every few days and do not miss a short window.

The schedule is tentative, but the shape of the year is now clear: 21 exams, 11 second chances, and a season that runs from late January to early June.
