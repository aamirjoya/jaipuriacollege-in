**Students still seeking admission in a college affiliated with Lucknow University have ten extra days to breathe.** The university has extended the deadline for obtaining the Lucknow University Registration Number, or LURN, till October 10. Without this number, an admission is not considered complete, so the extension matters to every late applicant still standing in admission queues.

The LURN is mandatory for anyone seeking admission to a college affiliated with Lucknow University. Colleges run the registration through the Samarth portal, and the link for registration is on the university's official website, <a href="https://www.lkouniv.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">www.lkouniv.ac.in</a>. Missing the number means missing records, and missing records mean trouble later at exam form and degree time.

## Classes already delayed by two months, admissions still open

The extended deadline is really a symptom of a longer story. The academic session in several affiliated colleges has already been delayed by nearly two months. The university's own admission process for first-year undergraduate and postgraduate courses continued till August, and now the affiliated colleges are still taking students.

Many colleges still have empty seats. Students who could not get into the university's own departments are moving to affiliated colleges, and that flow is still going on. This is why the deadline for LURN had to move.

> "Several students turn to affiliated colleges after failing to secure admission in the university. As a result, many colleges still have vacant seats and are continuing their admission process." — Manoj Pandey, president, LUACTA

## Why the extension was needed

According to Manoj Pandey, president of the Lucknow University Affiliated Colleges Teachers' Association (LUACTA), the sequence is simple. The university kept its own admission window open till August. Every year, a large number of students who do not make it to the university's departments apply to affiliated colleges instead. When the university's process ends late, the colleges' process starts late too. Fixing a registration deadline in the middle of that chain would have cut off genuine students.

LURN sits at the heart of this chain. It is the single number that connects a student to the university's records system. If a student takes admission but never gets the number, the admission stays incomplete in the university's eyes.

## What students should do right now

If you are taking or have taken admission in an affiliated college of Lucknow University for 2026-27, treat October 10 as a hard finish line, not a target. Check with your college office whether your registration is done.

- Confirm with your college that your name has been entered for LURN registration.
- Complete the registration on the Samarth portal through the link on the university website if you have to do it yourself.
- Keep a screenshot or printout of the registration confirmation with your admission papers.
- Ask the college office for a written receipt of your admission fee and documents, so nothing depends on memory.
- Do not wait for October 10. Portals slow down on the last two days, and that is when mistakes happen.
