Chhattisgarh will turn 30 of its colleges into Centres of Excellence and give around 1.4 lakh undergraduate students a mandatory 60-hour internship worth two credits. The decisions came out of a Higher Education Department review meeting chaired by Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai at Mahanadi Bhawan in Nava Raipur on Wednesday, 25 November.

**Thirty colleges at Rs 3 crore each adds up to Rs 90 crore in this budget year alone, and the internship will now count toward the degree instead of sitting outside it.**

## What the 30 colleges will get

The 30 colleges picked for the upgrade will get modern laboratories, advanced libraries and smart classrooms. The government said the colleges will also focus on skill development, preparation for competitive exams and campus placements. The names of the 30 colleges have not been announced yet.

Each college gets Rs 3 crore in the 2026-27 budget for modern equipment, materials and conferences. Another Rs 10 crore per college has been proposed for digitisation and construction over the next two years. Rs 50 crore more has been set aside for the Chhattisgarh Higher Education Mission. The mission will carry out the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and push the state's gross enrolment ratio in higher education up from 24.5%, against a national average of 30%, toward 50% by 2035.

## The internship: 60 hours and two credits

The Internship Implementation Policy-2026 has received the Governor's approval. Under it, around 1.4 lakh undergraduate students will do a mandatory 60-hour internship. The internship carries two academic credits, so it becomes part of the degree.

Students can intern with industries and corporate organisations, through academic projects, or with local industries and community organisations. Internship cells are being set up in universities and colleges to run the programme. The state government shared the announcement through <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DgPRsjREjbe/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">its official MyGov Chhattisgarh post</a>, and <a href="https://news.careers360.com/chhattisgarh-30-colleges-become-centres-of-excellence-14-lakh-ug-students-get-internships" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Careers360 reported the details of the meeting</a>.

> Around 1.4 lakh students. 60 hours of work experience. Two credits on the degree. The internship is now part of the course, not something extra.

## Teachers: 1,295 posts in the pipeline

The meeting also reviewed recruitment. Recruitment to 595 professor posts in government colleges is already underway through the Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission. A proposal has gone to the commission for 700 more posts: 625 assistant professors in 15 subjects, 50 librarians and 25 sports officers. Together, that is 1,295 posts.

In addition, 362 assistant professors have been promoted to professor, and a proposal to promote 143 more eligible assistant professors is set to go in December. To keep classes running in the 2026-27 session, the department has arranged 3,236 guest lecturers.

New courses are in the works too. A proposal has been prepared for 280 academic and non-academic posts to start 23 new departments and courses at Atal Bihari Vajpayee University in Bilaspur, at a cost of Rs 38.50 crore. Shaheed Nandkumar Patel University in Raigarh and Sant Gahira Guru University in Surguja will each get 49 new academic posts to start BPharma and MPharma courses, and 17 more posts have been proposed for Pandit Ravishankar Shukla University in Raipur.

## English, AI and one admission portal

A 90-hour communicative English course is running in 148 government colleges in tribal areas, with around 10,000 students enrolled.

The Higher Education Department has also signed an agreement with Google for Education to bring artificial intelligence into higher education. In the first phase, more than 500 faculty members from 22 colleges in Raipur district and over 50 officials of the Higher Education Directorate have received practical AI training. Ten more free offline training sessions are planned for faculty in private colleges.

Admissions should get simpler too: the department is building one integrated portal for the state, so students will not have to apply on each university's portal separately. Study material is being made available through the E-Gyanarjan mobile app, along with DigiLocker and Aadhaar-based verification. NEP 2020 has been in place in major undergraduate courses in the state since July 2024, and the fifth semester of the four-year undergraduate programme began in the 2026-27 session. Other states are moving the same way: [IITs are adding 6,000 new seats in robotics, physical AI and space tech from 2027](/education-news/iit-6000-new-seats-robotics-physical-ai-space-tech-2027/), and the [IIT Madras finishing school for biotech students](/education-news/iit-madras-udyog-finishing-school-biotech-jan-2027/) is built around the same idea, that classroom learning alone is no longer enough.

## What this means for students in Chhattisgarh

If you study in a government college in the state, the internship is the part that touches you first. It is compulsory, it carries credits, and it has to be done outside the classroom, in a workplace or on a real project. The details that matter most, the list of 30 colleges and the date internships begin, have not been announced yet. The practical step for now is to check your college notice board and ask whether an internship cell has been set up. Our [education news](/education-news/) section tracks these state announcements as they come in.

The state has 348 government colleges, 329 private colleges, nine state universities and 20 private universities, so the 30-college upgrade covers a small share of the total. Most students will feel the internship rule long before they see a new lab or smart classroom.
