**The National Testing Agency has opened the correction window for UGC NET December 2026, and the clock is short. If you applied for the exam, you have till November 4, 11:50 PM, to check every detail on your form and fix mistakes on the official portal ugcnet.nta.nic.in.**

NTA shared the update on November 2 through its official X account. Along with the correction window, the agency gave the first firm applicant number for this session: 9,75,505 application fees have been paid. That figure is subject to bank reconciliation, so the final count may shift slightly once payment records are checked.

This matters because UGC NET decides three things at once: eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship, eligibility for appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to PhD programmes. A small error in your name, subject or centre choice can turn into a big problem on exam day, when the admit card and your ID are matched at the gate.

## What you can change in the next three days

NTA has listed four groups of fields that are open for editing in this window:

- Personal details
- Name change
- Exam centre preferences
- Subject and qualification details

Only candidates who completed the application and paid the fee can make corrections. The fee window closed on November 1, so no new applications can be filed through this window. If you have not paid, this correction link will not help you.

Start with the fields that affect your exam day directly. Your name must match your photo ID, because a mismatch can block entry at the centre. Your subject choice decides which paper you sit for, and your centre preferences decide where you travel in December. Check the spelling of your name character by character, not just at a glance.

## How to correct your UGC NET form, step by step

Go to the <a href="https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UGC NET portal</a> and log in with your application number and password. Open the correction link, which is active from November 2 to November 4.

- Log in on ugcnet.nta.nic.in and open the correction link
- Go through each section of the form slowly, starting with personal details and name
- Change only the fields that are actually wrong, and save each section
- Check your exam centre preferences and subject details once more before you submit
- Download the updated confirmation page and keep it safe

NTA has asked candidates to download and retain the confirmation page through the login link. Do that after every change, not just at the end. If the portal slows down near the deadline, a saved copy is your proof of what you submitted.

> "Ensure your details are accurate and verified."

That line from NTA's own announcement sums up the whole window. The agency is not asking you to reapply. It is asking you to verify what is already on record, while there is still time to change it.

## Dates you should not miss after the correction window

The schedule after November 4 moves fast. The city intimation slip, which tells you the city where your centre will be, releases on November 23. The admit card, which carries the exact centre address and shift, downloads from December 7. The exam runs on December 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 21 in computer-based test mode. A subject-wise schedule with the date and shift for each paper is still to come.

One practical tip: do not leave corrections for the last hour. The window closes at 11:50 PM on November 4, and portals get slow when thousands of candidates log in together. A correction made on November 2 or 3 costs you the same effort and carries far less risk. If you hit a problem, NTA's helpline is 011-40759000 and the email is ugcnet@nta.ac.in.
