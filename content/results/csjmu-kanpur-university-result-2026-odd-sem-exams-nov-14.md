**Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University (CSJMU), Kanpur, starts its odd semester exams for 2026-27 on 14 November 2026.** The university issued fresh exam instructions on 11 November, three days before the first paper. Students of BA, B.Sc, B.Com and other UG and PG courses should check their date sheet and exam centre now, before the rush begins.

At the same time, the 2025-26 result cycle is complete. The even semester results came out between 16 May and 9 June 2026, and the special back paper result followed on 18 September 2026. If you are still waiting to see your marks, the right portal depends on your course and session. Picking the wrong one is the most common reason students see nothing.

## Exams from 14 November: what the schedule says

The final odd semester schedule came out on 26 October 2026. BA first semester papers run from 14 November to 1 December 2026. Other courses follow their own date sheets, so do not go by a friend's timetable. The university has also published the final list of nodal and examination centres for the 2026-27 odd semester exams.

> "Check the date sheet for your own course on the university website. A wrong date sheet is the easiest mistake to make in exam week, and it costs a full year."

## CSJMU result 2026: which results are out

The 2025-26 even semester results were declared course by course. The first set came on 16 May 2026, and the last major set on 9 June 2026 covered BA (Hons) sixth semester, BCA fourth semester, B.Ed fourth semester, M.Ed fourth semester and M.Sc (Ag) fourth semester, among others.

The latest result on record is the special back paper result of 18 September 2026. It covers UG fifth and sixth semester and PG third and fourth semester students who sat the exam on 11, 12 and 13 August 2026. An Environmental Studies result also came out on 2 September 2026.

Students checking [university results](/results/) across India will find the same pattern everywhere: each university runs its own portal, and the link changes with the session. CSJMU is no different. Use the portal that matches your session, or you will see an old result list.

## How to check your CSJMU result step by step

CSJMU runs more than one result portal. The steps below follow the official result notices.

- Open the official university website at <a href="https://csjmu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">csjmu.ac.in</a> and go to the Results section.
- For 2025-26 UG and PG semester results, log in to the Samarth portal at <a href="https://csjmu.samarth.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">csjmu.samarth.edu.in</a> with your student ID and password.
- For NEP results of sessions up to 2024-25, use admission.csjmu.ac.in with your roll number and date of birth.
- For medical, annual and semester results such as MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BUMS and Nursing, use results.csjmu.ac.in with your roll number.
- Pick your course and semester, enter the details asked, and click View Result.
- Check your name, roll number, paper-wise marks and SGPA. Download the marksheet and keep a printout. The online marksheet is provisional, and it works for reference until your college issues the original.

## Why some students still cannot see their result

A missing result usually has one of three causes, and each has a different fix.

Some colleges did not deposit the 2025-26 exam fee. The university held back their students' results and asked the colleges to pay by 6 October 2026 through a notice dated 5 October 2026. If your whole class sees the same blank page, ask your college office first. Colleges that still do not pay cannot fill the 2026-27 odd semester exam forms for their students either.

The second cause is the wrong portal. The old NEP portal at admission.csjmu.ac.in lists sessions only up to 2024-25. A 2025-26 result opens only through Samarth. Medical results open only on results.csjmu.ac.in.

## Scrutiny and challenge evaluation after the result

CSJMU gives two steps to students who are unhappy with their marks. Scrutiny comes first. Challenge evaluation is the second step.

Log in to your Samarth student account and open the Scrutiny option. Pay the fee online and submit the form. The university emails a copy of your answer book. Apply for challenge evaluation within 7 days of getting that copy. Only online applications count. Manual forms are rejected.

The same process runs on other campuses too. The [JNTUH BPharmacy result](/results/jntuh-bpharmacy-result-2026-nov-10-rc-rv-deadline-nov-17/) declared on 10 November 2026, for example, gives students until 17 November to apply for recounting or revaluation. Deadlines are short everywhere, so do not wait.
