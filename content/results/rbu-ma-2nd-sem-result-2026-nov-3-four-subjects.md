**Rabindra Bharati University (RBU), Kolkata, has published the MA 2nd semester result 2026 for four subjects.** The result notices are dated November 3, 2026, and they cover Economics and Bengali under the Faculty of Arts, plus Vocal Music and Drama under the Faculty of Fine Arts. All four are CBCS results, and each one is posted as a PDF list on the university's official examinations page.

If you sat for one of these papers, your result is already live. Open the list for your subject, find your roll number, and save a copy of the PDF for your records.

## The 4 subjects in the November 3 RBU result

The November 3 update is split across the university's two faculties. Under the Faculty of Arts, the lists cover MA 2nd Semester in Economics (CBCS) and MA 2nd Semester in Bengali (CBCS). Under the Faculty of Fine Arts, the lists cover MA 2nd Semester in Vocal Music (CBCS) and MA 2nd Semester in Drama (CBCS).

This is how RBU releases results. There is no single result day for the whole university. Each subject comes out as its own PDF list, and the lists appear on the examinations page as soon as the department clears them. Students in other MA subjects should keep checking the same page, because more lists follow in the same pattern.

Recent [university results](/results/) on this site show the same subject-by-subject pattern elsewhere too. The [Calicut University result](/results/calicut-university-result-oct-30-2026-pg-diploma-ma-sanskrit/) from October 30, for example, covered two separate courses on one day. RBU has already released earlier MA 2nd semester lists in recent weeks, including Philosophy and History on October 13, so the November 3 lists are the latest batch in an ongoing series.

## How to check your RBU MA 2nd sem result

Checking the result takes a few minutes.

- Go to the <a href="https://rbu.ac.in/home/examinations" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official RBU examinations page</a>.
- Find the notice with your subject name and the date November 03, 2026.
- Open the PDF for your subject.
- Search the PDF for your roll number and check the marks shown against it.
- Download or print the PDF and keep it safe until your college issues the formal marksheet.

One common mistake is opening the wrong subject list. Economics and Bengali sit under the Faculty of Arts, while Vocal Music and Drama sit under the Faculty of Fine Arts. If your roll number does not show up, check the faculty heading on the notice before you assume something is wrong.

> Open the PDF for your own subject, search for your roll number, and save a copy at once. A saved PDF is your proof of the result until the formal marksheet reaches you.

## What to check in your result, and what to do next

Once you find your entry, read it line by line. Check that your name and roll number match your admit card. Then check the marks and the result status shown for each paper. A small spelling difference in a name is worth flagging with your department early, because corrections take longer once marksheets are printed.

Students who cleared all papers can move on to the next semester's formalities as their department directs. Students with a paper still pending should watch the examinations page for revaluation or supplementary notices. RBU posts those notices on the same page, so there is no need to rely on forwarded messages or unofficial groups for the next step.

For students still waiting on other subjects, the wait is normal. RBU releases each subject list separately, and a subject that is missing today can appear on the page in the next batch. Check the <a href="https://rbu.ac.in/home/examinations" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official RBU examinations page</a> rather than a third-party result site, because the university page is the first place the PDF appears.
