**The Karnataka State Law University (KSLU), Hubballi, has declared the KSLU result 2026 for its undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes.** Students who appeared for the semester examinations can now check their scorecards online through the university's official website, kslu.karnataka.gov.in. The results cover the three-year LLB, the five-year integrated BA LLB, BBA LLB and BCom LLB courses, as well as the LLM programme.

You will need your university registration number to access the result. The marksheet shows subject-wise internal and external marks, so keep a printed copy ready for admission and academic paperwork.

## Which KSLU results were declared today

The announcement covers semester examinations for both UG and PG law programmes at the university. This includes the traditional three-year LLB, the five-year integrated programmes that combine undergraduate studies with law, and the postgraduate LLM.

KSLU is the state's dedicated university for legal education, and its semester results are usually checked by thousands of students across affiliated law colleges in Karnataka. If you wrote your papers this session, your marksheet should now be live on the portal.

## How to check the KSLU result 2026 online

The process takes only a few minutes. Keep your registration number ready before you start. Results for other law programmes are also announced from time to time, like the recent [SPPU BA LLB LLM result](/results/sppu-ba-llb-llm-result-oct-12-2026/) announced earlier this month.

- Visit the official website, kslu.karnataka.gov.in.
- Look for the Examination section on the homepage.
- Click on the KSLU Results link.
- Select the course you appeared for in the semester examination.
- Enter your university registration number and the CAPTCHA code shown.
- Click on Get Result.
- Check the result on the screen, then download the marksheet and save a copy.

> All you need is your university registration number. The portal does not ask for a separate password or login ID.

Use the university's official portal to check your result and download the marksheet. If a course-wise direct link is shown on the website, use it. Avoid third-party result sites that ask for personal details. The full list of declared examinations is also tracked on our [results section](/results/), updated as new announcements come in.

## What your KSLU marksheet shows

The online marksheet carries your subject-wise marks, split into internal assessment and external examination scores. Internal marks come from your college's evaluation during the semester. External marks come from the university's written examination.

Download the marksheet and check these details right away:

- Your name, registration number, and course name are correct.
- Marks for every subject you appeared in are listed.
- The pass or fail status matches your expectations.

Keep a digital copy saved on your phone and a printed copy in your records file. You will need it for scholarships, further admissions, and verification at your college. The same habit helps with every exam — students who checked the [Mumbai University BE result](/results/mumbai-university-be-result-oct-2026-sem-8-out-check-mumresults/) earlier this month followed the same steps.

## What to do after checking your result

If everything looks right, save your marksheet and wait for your college's instructions on collecting the physical copy. Colleges usually issue the official marksheets a few weeks after the online declaration.

If you spot an error in your name or marks, contact your college office immediately. For corrections, revaluation, or answer-script scrutiny, the university will issue separate notices. Apply only through the <a href="https://kslu.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KSLU portal</a> when those notices are announced, and follow the deadlines the university sets.

Students who did not clear one or more papers should watch the university website for supplementary examination schedules. KSLU announces these separately, and the application window is usually short. Keep checking the official site so you do not miss it.
