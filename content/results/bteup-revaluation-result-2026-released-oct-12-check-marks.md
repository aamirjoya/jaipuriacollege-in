**The Board of Technical Education Uttar Pradesh has declared the BTEUP revaluation result 2026 for the June examinations. Students who applied for a review of their answer sheets can now check their revised marks on the board's official website. The result went live on October 12.**

This release is for polytechnic students who were not satisfied with their May-June 2026 scores and asked the board to check their answer sheets again. Your revised marks and your updated qualifying status both appear on the new scorecard.

## Your June 2026 papers were rechecked: here is what this means

The BTEUP revaluation result 2026 is the second version of your June exam scorecard. If the re-checking raised your marks in any subject, the new scorecard shows the higher total. If the review found no change, your original marks stay as they were. Either way, this revised result is the final word on those papers.

Thousands of diploma students across Uttar Pradesh appear in BTEUP's even semester and annual examinations every year. A re-evaluation gives those who felt their copies were not marked fairly a second chance at the correct total.

> Keep your enrolment number ready before you open the result page. The portal will not show your result without it.

## Who this result is for

This result is only for students of the even semester, annual, and special back paper examinations held in May-June 2026 who applied for re-evaluation. If you did not submit a review request, this release is not for you. Your regular result stands exactly as it was declared.

## How to check your revised marks in 6 steps

Follow these steps on the <a href="https://bteup.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">BTEUP official website</a>:

1. Open the official website at bteup.ac.in.
2. On the homepage, find the link titled "View Result of re-evaluation June – 2026".
3. Click that link. A student login window will open.
4. Enter your enrolment number and date of birth in the required fields.
5. Submit the details. Your revised result will appear on the screen.
6. Check your revised marks and qualifying status, then download or save a copy.

The portal is busiest in the first few hours after a result goes live. If the page does not load, wait a while and try again instead of entering your details on any third-party site. Your result is only on the official board website.

## What to do after you see your result

First, compare the revised marks with your original scorecard. Note which subjects changed and by how much. This matters if you are applying for lateral entry into a degree course or a job that asks for semester-wise marks.

Second, check the qualifying status printed on the new scorecard. If you had a back paper in June, confirm that it now shows as cleared. Your college will go by this revised status, not the old one.

Third, save a printed or PDF copy of the revised result. Colleges, employers, and future admission rounds may ask for proof of the updated marks. Keep it with your other academic documents.

Fourth, look closely at every detail. If your name, enrolment number, or any mark looks wrong, contact your institute for clarification and further help. Do not wait, since corrections are easier to make early.

Finally, keep watching the <a href="https://bteup.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">board's official portal</a> for any notice about the final marksheet or further rounds. Re-evaluation is over for this cycle, but the board may issue instructions about collecting the updated marksheet through your college.
