The wait is over for thousands of MP government job aspirants. The Madhya Pradesh Agency for Promotion of Information Technology (MAP IT) has released the **CPCT admit card 2026** on the official portal, cpct.mp.gov.in. The Computer Proficiency Certification Test (CPCT) will be held on **November 13, 14 and 15, 2026**, at centres across Madhya Pradesh. If you registered for the November session, download your hall ticket now and check every detail on it before exam day.

## Download your CPCT hall ticket in 4 steps

Getting the admit card takes only a few minutes. Keep your login details ready before you start.

- Go to the official portal at <a href="https://www.cpct.mp.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cpct.mp.gov.in</a>.
- Look for the admit card notice on the homepage and open the candidate login.
- Enter your User ID and password, along with the captcha shown on the screen.
- Download the hall ticket and take a clear printout. Check your name, photo, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address on the printout.

MAP IT has changed the reporting time, gate closing time and exam start and end time for this session. Do not go by the old timings. The timings printed on your admit card are the ones that count, so read them carefully and plan to reach the centre early.

## 120 minutes, two sections: how the CPCT paper works

The CPCT is a computer-based test that runs for 120 minutes in total. It has two parts, and both are compulsory.

Section A is a multiple-choice test on computer proficiency. It runs for 75 minutes and checks your knowledge of computers and IT. Section B is the typing test. You get 15 minutes for English typing and 15 minutes for Hindi typing.

> "Both sections are mandatory. A candidate who skips either part cannot clear the test, no matter how well the other part goes."

The CPCT score matters because it is the basic screening test for several Madhya Pradesh government posts, including Data Entry Operator, Clerk, Assistant Grade 3 and Stenographer. Many departments ask for a valid CPCT score before they shortlist candidates for these posts. A good score here opens the door to the next stage of recruitment.

## What to carry on exam day

Two things are non-negotiable at the centre gate. First, a printed copy of your admit card. Second, a valid photo ID card, the one mentioned on your admit card. The photo and signature on both documents must be clear and easy to read.

Candidates who arrive without either document will not be allowed to take the test. Keep both ready the night before, along with a pen if the centre instructions ask for one. Do not carry anything the admit card forbids, such as a mobile phone or smart watch, into the exam hall.

## Registration closed on October 25: what happens next

Registration for this session ran from October 12 to October 25, 2026, and the window is now closed. No fresh applications can be made for the November 13 to 15 exam. If you missed this round, the next CPCT session is tentatively planned for January 8 to 10, 2027, so watch the official portal for the fresh notification.

After the exam, the scorecard will appear in your candidate login on the same portal. MAP IT releases it after the objection review is complete, so the final score may differ from the provisional marks shown right after the test. Keep checking cpct.mp.gov.in for the scorecard link and the objection schedule.
