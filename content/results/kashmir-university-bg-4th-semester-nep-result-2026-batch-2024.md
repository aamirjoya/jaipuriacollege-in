**The University of Kashmir has released the BG 4th Semester (NEP) result for Batch 2024 today, 12 November 2026.** Students who appeared in the June 2026 session under the UG Professional category can now check their marks on the official results portal. The release is listed as Notification No. 06 on the university website.

If you are in Batch 2024 and sat for the BG 4th Semester papers in June, this is the result you have been waiting for. Open the official portal, find the notification, and check your marks. The whole process takes a few minutes.

## What today's Kashmir University BG result covers

Today's release is specific. It covers the BG 4th Semester (NEP) examination for Batch 2024, held in the June 2026 session. The university has placed it under the UG Professional category.

Here is what the official listing shows.

- Result: BG 4th Semester (NEP), Batch 2024
- Exam session: June 2026
- Notification number: 06
- Category: UG Professional
- Release date: 12 November 2026
- Official portal: egov.uok.edu.in/results

Do not mix this up with the earlier BG 4th Semester release. The university had already declared the BG 4th Semester (NEP) result for the UG Non Professional stream in September and October 2026, under Notification No. 2.BG. That was a different stream. Today's Notification No. 06 is the UG Professional release, and it is the one Batch 2024 professional-course students should open.

## How to check your Kashmir University BG 4th Semester result

Go to the <a href="https://egov.uok.edu.in/results/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Kashmir University results portal</a> and follow these steps.

1. Open egov.uok.edu.in/results in your browser.
2. Find the entry dated 12 November 2026 that reads "Notification No. 06. BG 4th Semester (NEP) Batch-2024 Session June, 2026".
3. Open the result link shown against that notification.
4. Enter the details the page asks for, such as your roll number or registration number.
5. Submit and view your result on the screen.
6. Download or print the result and save a copy for your records.

The portal can slow down when thousands of students check at the same time. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Use only the official website. Links shared on social media or messaging groups may lead to fake pages, so do not enter your roll number anywhere else.

> The BG 4th Semester (NEP) result for Batch 2024 is live now under Notification No. 06. Check it on the official portal and save a copy before you log out.

## What to do after you see your result

Read every line of your result with care. Check your name, roll number, subject names and marks. Small errors do happen, and it is easier to get them fixed early.

The result you see online is the provisional copy. Your college will tell you when the original marksheet is ready for collection. Keep a printout of the online result with you until then.

The university has not yet announced the dates, fees or process for revaluation or rechecking for this release. A separate notice is expected on the official website. Keep checking the <a href="https://www.kashmiruniversity.net" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University of Kashmir website</a> for that notice. Do not pay anyone who claims they can change your marks for a fee.

Students who cleared all their 4th Semester papers can now move ahead with the next semester. Those who still have a backlog should watch for the next supplementary exam notice from the university.
