**The wait ends today for medical aspirants in West Bengal.** The West Bengal Medical Counselling Committee (WBMCC) is set to publish the seat allotment result for the NEET UG 2026 stray vacancy round today, October 23, 2026, by 12 noon. Candidates who registered, verified their documents, and locked their choices can check their allotment on the <a href="https://wbmcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official WBMCC portal</a>.

This is the last round of the state's [NEET UG counselling](/results/) for 2026. Seats that stayed vacant after the first three rounds are now being allotted. MBBS and BDS seats under the 85 per cent state quota, the management quota, and the NRI quota are all covered in this round. For students who still do not hold a seat, this is the final chance to enter a West Bengal medical or dental college this year.

## Today, 12 noon: when and where the result appears

WBMCC will release the provisional seat allotment as a PDF on wbmcc.nic.in. You do not need to log in and guess. Open the PDF, search for your roll number, and your allotted college and course will be listed against it. Students who also tracked the [national stray vacancy result](/results/neet-ug-stray-vacancy-result-2026-oct-20/) released by MCC earlier this week will recognise the same format.

Keep these timings in mind for today:

- Allotment result: October 23, 2026, by 12 noon
- Reporting at allotted college: October 23 and 24 (2 pm to 6 pm), October 26 (10 am to 4 pm)

If you miss your reporting window, you lose the seat. There is no second chance after this round.

## How to check the WB NEET UG stray vacancy result

1. Go to wbmcc.nic.in.
2. Open the UG Medical and Dental Counselling section.
3. Find the link for the NEET UG 2026 stray vacancy seat allotment result.
4. Download the allotment PDF.
5. Press Ctrl+F, type your roll number, and check your allotment.
6. Download your provisional allotment letter and keep a printout.

The allotment letter is not optional. You need it on reporting day along with your documents.

> "This is the final round of the year. Every seat allotted today must be reported to on time, because WBMCC will not hold another round after this."

## Reporting: dates, timings and documents

If the PDF shows your name, report in person at the allotted college. Reporting runs on three days: October 23 and 24 from 2 pm to 6 pm, and October 26 from 10 am to 4 pm. Carry originals plus photocopies of everything.

Documents you should keep ready:

- NEET UG 2026 admit card and scorecard
- Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates
- Birth certificate
- West Bengal domicile certificate
- Caste or category certificate, if applicable
- Provisional allotment letter downloaded from the <a href="https://wbmcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WBMCC website</a>
- Eight recent passport-size photographs
- Aadhaar card or another photo ID

Admission fees must be paid at the college counter during reporting. Confirm the exact fee with your allotted college before you leave home.

## Who was eligible for this round

Only candidates who completed the full process could get a seat in this round. [Registration](/admissions/wb-neet-ug-2026-stray-vacancy-round-registration-closes-today/) closed on October 16, 2026. Document verification happened on October 17, and choice filling and locking ran from October 17 to 18. Seats were allotted on merit, the choices each candidate locked, and the seats actually available in each college.

## If your name is not in the PDF

Do not panic right away. First, double-check that you searched the right PDF for your course and quota. If you locked your choices correctly and still got no seat, this counselling cycle has ended for you. The national stray vacancy round also closed this week, so there is no central round left either. Your options now are to prepare for NEET UG 2027 or look at allied courses whose admissions are still open.

## What happens after reporting

Once you report and pay the fee, your admission is confirmed. WBMCC does not run another round after the stray vacancy round, so this admission is final. Read the schedule notice on the official portal once more before you travel, and reach the college well within the reporting hours for your day.
