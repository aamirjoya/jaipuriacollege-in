**The Chhattisgarh NEET PG 2026 Round 1 provisional seat allotment result is out today, November 3.** The Directorate of Medical Education (DME) Chhattisgarh has published the list on the official counselling portal, and candidates who registered between October 22 and October 27 and locked their choices can now see which college and course they have got. If your name is on the list, do not wait. Reporting at the allotted college closes on November 11, 2026, and a seat left unjoined by that date is lost.

This round covers state quota seats in MD, MS, and Postgraduate Diploma courses in government and private medical colleges across Chhattisgarh. Seats were allotted on the basis of NEET PG 2026 rank, the state merit list, reservation rules, and the choices each candidate filled. The result is provisional, so read every line of your allotment letter before you travel to the college.

## How to check your allotment in the PDF

You can <a href="https://cgmcc.admissions.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">check your allotment on the official Chhattisgarh counselling portal</a>. The list is published as a PDF, and no login is needed to open it.

- Open the portal and look for the Round 1 provisional allotment result link on the homepage.
- Open the PDF and search for your roll number or NEET PG rank.
- Note the allotted college, course, quota, and category carefully.
- Download the PDF and keep a printout for the reporting desk.

Only candidates who registered on the portal in the October 22 to October 27 window and locked their choices were considered for this round. State quota seats also need Chhattisgarh domicile proof, so keep that document ready with the rest of your file.

## Reporting runs in person, and November 11 is the final cutoff

Reporting is physical. You have to go to the allotted medical college, get your documents checked, and finish the admission formalities there. The counselling schedule sets out two scrutiny and admission windows, and the second one, from November 10 to November 11, is the last chance. Scrutiny in that window runs till 4 pm on November 11, and admission till 5 pm the same day.

> A seat in Round 1 is only yours once the college has verified your documents. The PDF alone does not complete your admission.

Plan your travel with a day to spare. Colleges can ask for a missing paper, and a last-day rush leaves no room to fix it. If you cannot reach the college in time, you risk losing both the seat and the security deposit, as per the counselling rules.

## What to carry to the college

Take originals plus self-attested photocopies of everything below. The college will verify the originals and keep copies in its records.

- NEET PG 2026 scorecard and admit card
- MBBS degree and internship completion certificate
- Permanent registration certificate from the medical council
- Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates
- Caste or category certificate from the Chhattisgarh government, if it applies to you
- Domicile certificate of Chhattisgarh
- Valid photo identity proof
- Printout of the provisional allotment letter

Check the fee payment step with your allotted college before you leave home. Some colleges collect fees at the reporting desk, and the amount and payment mode can differ from one college to another.

## What comes after Round 1

DME Chhattisgarh is expected to announce the Round 2 registration schedule and a revised seat matrix once Round 1 reporting closes. Candidates who did not get a seat in Round 1, or who want to try for a better option in the next round, should watch the official portal for those dates and the vacant seat list.

## FAQ

### Where can I check the Chhattisgarh NEET PG 2026 Round 1 allotment result?

### What is the last date to report at the allotted college?

Allotted candidates must report at their designated medical college on or before November 11, 2026. Admission is confirmed only after document verification at the college.

### Which courses does Chhattisgarh NEET PG Round 1 counselling cover?

State quota seats in MD, MS, and Postgraduate Diploma courses in government and private medical colleges across Chhattisgarh.

### What happens if I do not report by November 11?

A candidate who does not report within the deadline loses the allotted seat and may also forfeit the security deposit, as per the counselling rules.
