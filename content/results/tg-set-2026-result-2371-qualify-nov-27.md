**Osmania University declared the [TG-SET 2026 results](/results/) on Friday, 27 November 2026. A total of 2,371 candidates qualified for Assistant Professor and Lecturer eligibility, out of 35,003 who appeared for the exam. Scorecards are live on the official TG-SET portal.**

The test ran as a computer-based exam across 29 subjects on 28, 29 and 30 September 2026. A total of 40,863 candidates had registered, and 35,003 sat for the papers. About 1 in 15 of those who appeared made the cut.

## How to check your TG-SET 2026 scorecard

1. Open the official TG-SET portal at <a href="https://www.telanganaset.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">telanganaset.org</a>.
2. Look for the TG-SET 2026 result link on the home page.
3. Enter your hall ticket number and date of birth.
4. Submit the details to see your scorecard, then download and save a copy.

The result is also listed on the Osmania University website, <a href="https://www.osmania.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">osmania.ac.in</a>. Keep a printout of the scorecard handy. You will need it at the certificate verification stage.

## What qualified candidates must do next

Certificate verification runs from 10 to 12 December 2026, as per the official press note issued by the TG-SET office. Only candidates whose result shows them as qualified will be called for it. The eligibility certificate is issued after the documents are checked, so qualified candidates should keep their original certificates and identity proof ready.

For TG-SET 2027, the next notification will come out in January 2027, the TG-SET office has said. Candidates who did not qualify this time can plan for that cycle instead of waiting around for a fresh announcement.

## What qualifying in TG-SET means

TG-SET is an eligibility test. Clearing it means you meet the minimum standard to apply for Assistant Professor and Lecturer posts in universities and colleges across Telangana. It is not a job offer, and it does not place you in a college by itself.

When a university or college advertises a teaching vacancy, qualified candidates can apply against it. The eligibility certificate, issued after verification, is the document that proves you cleared the test. That is why the December verification matters. Without it, the certificate does not come through.

State eligibility tests like this one run on the same idea across India. Karnataka's [KSET 2026 result](/results/kset-2026-provisional-result-8171-qualify-cutoff-nov-20/) saw 8,171 candidates qualify, and candidates aiming for the national-level test can check the [UGC NET December 2026](/education-news/ugc-net-december-2026-city-intimation-slip-out-nov-23/) city intimation slip, which is already out.

## How the result was announced

The results were released at Osmania University on Friday. Vice-Chancellor Prof. Kumar Molugaram and Registrar Prof. G. Naresh Reddy were present at the release, along with the TG-SET team. The Vice-Chancellor praised the SET administration for running the exam in a transparent way and for declaring the results on time. Member Secretary Prof. B. Srinivas, who led the exam team, congratulated the candidates who qualified.
