**The West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education declared the HS 3rd semester result 2026 on Friday, 27 November. Out of 7,02,997 students, 6,51,129 passed. That works out to a pass rate of 92.62 percent. The result link went live at 3 pm on the official portal.**

The council announced the result at a press conference at 2:30 pm at Vidyasagar Bhavan in Salt Lake, Kolkata. Students who sat the Class 12 third semester exams can now download their marksheets from <a href="https://result.wb.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">result.wb.gov.in</a> or wbchse.wb.gov.in. You need your roll number and registration number to log in.

The exams ran from 21 September to 14 October 2026 across 825 centres and 2,105 venues. The answer key came out on 15 October, and the result arrived within 44 days of the last exam.

## Girls edge past boys by a small margin

Girls did slightly better than boys this time. The gap is narrow, but it held across the full count of more than seven lakh students.

| Students | Appeared | Passed | Pass rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Girls | 3,93,179 | 3,64,312 | 92.66% |
| Boys | 3,09,818 | 2,86,817 | 92.58% |
| Total | 7,02,997 | 6,51,129 | 92.62% |

More girls sat the exam than boys, and more girls passed too. The difference between the two pass rates is less than a tenth of a percentage point.

## Commerce leads, Arts trails behind

The gap between streams is much wider than the gap between boys and girls. Commerce students recorded the best result of the three streams, while Arts students had the lowest pass rate by a clear distance.

| Stream | Pass rate |
| --- | --- |
| Commerce | 98.11% |
| Science | 98% |
| Arts | 91% |

> Commerce students recorded the highest pass rate at 98.11 percent, while Arts stood at 91 percent. Science was close behind Commerce at 98 percent.

The overall pass rate of 92.62 percent is 1.10 points lower than last year's 93.72 percent. The council also shared the toppers list at the press conference.

Nadia district recorded the highest pass rate among districts at 95.58 percent. East Midnapore, Howrah, South 24 Parganas and Hooghly followed it in the district list. Around 1.48 percent of students scored above 90 percent marks.

## How to check your marksheet

The steps are short, but keep your admit card ready before you start. The portal asks for the roll number and registration number printed on it.

1. Go to <a href="https://result.wb.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">result.wb.gov.in</a> or wbchse.wb.gov.in.
2. Click the link for the WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026.
3. Enter your roll number and registration number.
4. Submit the form and your marksheet will appear on the screen.
5. Download it and take a printout for your records.

Students without internet access can get the result by SMS. Type WB12 followed by your roll number and send it to 5676750 or 58888. The result will come back as a text message.

If the portal is slow, wait a few minutes and try again. Result sites often struggle in the first hour after the link goes live, and the link stays up, so there is no need to keep refreshing in a panic.

## What to do after you check

The marksheet you download now is provisional. It shows your subject-wise marks, but it is not the final document. Your school will hand out the original marksheet and certificate in the coming days, so stay in touch with your school office and collect it when it arrives.

Read every line of the online marksheet before you close the page. Check the spelling of your name, your roll number, and the marks in each subject. If anything looks wrong, tell your school at once. Schools raise such errors with the council, and the sooner you report one, the sooner it gets fixed.

Keep your roll number and registration number safe. You will need them again for the next semester and for any correction request.
