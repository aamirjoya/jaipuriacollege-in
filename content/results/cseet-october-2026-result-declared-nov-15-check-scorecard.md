The wait is over. The Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI) has declared the **CSEET October 2026 result** today, November 15, at 2 pm. Candidates who took the exam can now check their scorecards on the official website, icsi.edu.

The result link went live at the time ICSI had announced in its notice on November 11. Along with the result, ICSI has also released the subject-wise break-up of marks for every candidate. The formal e-Result-cum-Marks Statement is available for download right after the declaration.

## How to check your CSEET October 2026 scorecard

Keep your 17-digit application number and date of birth ready before you start. The result is available only online, and you will need both details to log in.

1. Go to the official ICSI website at <a href="https://www.icsi.edu" target="_blank" rel="noopener">icsi.edu</a>.
2. Find the CSEET October 2026 result link on the homepage.
3. Enter your application number and date of birth.
4. Click on login. Your scorecard will appear on the screen.
5. Check your marks and qualifying status, then download the e-Result-cum-Marks Statement and take a printout.

Check every detail on the statement carefully. If you spot an error in your name or marks, contact ICSI through the official channels and get it fixed early. Do not trust third-party links or messages that promise early access to your result. The scorecard is available only through icsi.edu.

## What you need to pass CSEET

CSEET is the entry test for the Company Secretary course. To pass, you need at least 40% marks in each subject and 50% marks in total. Both rules apply together. A high total will not help if you fall short in even one subject.

The October session was held in offline mode on October 1, 3, 5 and 6, 2026, at exam centres across the country. The result stays valid for one year from the date of declaration, so candidates who pass can move ahead with the next stage of the CS course within that window.

> "Passing CSEET is the first door into the Company Secretary course. The scorecard you download today is the document you will need for the next step, so save it safely."

## Not happy with your marks? Verification opens November 16

ICSI has also opened three options for candidates who want their answer books checked again. All three start on November 16 through the SMASH portal at <a href="https://smash.icsi.edu" target="_blank" rel="noopener">smash.icsi.edu</a>.

Verification of marks costs Rs 250 per subject, and applications close on December 6, 2026. You can apply within 21 days of the result declaration. Inspection of answer books costs Rs 450 per subject, and certified copies cost Rs 500 per subject. Applications for both of these will be accepted from November 16 to December 15, 2026.

Think before you apply. Verification only rechecks the totalling and unmarked answers. It does not mean a re-evaluation of your answers. If you were close to the cut-off in one subject, it may be worth it. If the gap is large, your time is better spent preparing for the next session.

## February 2027 session: applications open tomorrow

ICSI has already confirmed the next CSEET cycle. The February 2027 examination will be held from February 1 to 4, 2027. Online enrolment applications for that session open on November 16, 2026.

Candidates who did not clear this time do not have to wait long. The February session is less than three months away, and the syllabus stays the same. Use the subject-wise marks from today's result to see where you lost marks, and focus your preparation there.

Results like this one draw heavy traffic in the first few hours. If the ICSI website loads slowly, wait a few minutes and try again. The link will stay live, and your scorecard is not going anywhere.
