**The University of Calicut has declared eight exam results on October 1, 2026. The list covers MBA, B.Tech, BA, BSc, BCA and integrated postgraduate courses, and every score is available on the university's official results portal.**

Students who appeared in these examinations can open <a href="https://results.uoc.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">results.uoc.ac.in</a> and look for their exact course, semester and exam session. Keep your register number ready before you start.

Calicut University is one of the largest universities in Kerala, with hundreds of affiliated colleges. Its result portal handles dozens of declarations through the year, so the page can feel crowded. The trick is simple: match the full name of your examination, not just the course. A second-semester B.Tech student under the 2024 scheme and one under the 2019 scheme have separate result links.

## Which 8 results came out on October 1

The university released results for the following examinations:

- Fourth Semester MBA (Regular) Examination 11/2019 (2002 admission)
- Second Semester MBA in Health Care Management and International Finance, Examination 7/2026 (2022, 2024, 2025 admissions)
- Second Semester B.Tech Supplementary, April 2026 Examination (2019 scheme, 2020 to 2023 admissions)
- Second Semester B.Tech Regular, Improvement and Supplementary, April 2026 Examination (2024 scheme, 2024 to 2025 admissions)
- I Semester SDE CUCBCSS BA, BA Afsal-Ul-Ulama and BSc One Time Regular Supplementary Examination, September 2022 (2014 to 2016 admissions)
- First Semester CUCBCSS-UG BSc and BCA One Time Regular Supplementary Examinations, September 2022
- Second Semester Integrated M.A Development Studies Supplementary and Improvement Examination, April 2026 (CCSS, 2021 to 2023 admissions)
- First Semester Integrated MSc Chemistry and Physics Supplementary and Improvement Examination, November 2024 (CCSS, 2021 to 2023 admissions)

The mix includes both regular exams and one-time regular supplementary exams. If you missed a paper earlier and sat for the supplementary round, your result is in this batch too.

> Treat the online result as a first look. The printed marksheet your college gives you is the document that counts for jobs, admissions and certificates.

## How to check your Calicut University result

1. Go to the official results portal at <a href="https://results.uoc.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">results.uoc.ac.in</a>.
2. Find the entry that matches your course, semester and exam session exactly.
3. Open the result link and enter your register number and the other details the page asks for.
4. Submit and view your score on the screen.
5. Download or print the page and keep a copy until you get the original marksheet.

The portal sometimes slows down in the first few hours after a declaration because so many students check at once. If the page does not load, wait and try again instead of guessing your details on another site.

## What to verify once your score appears

Read the whole result before you celebrate. Check that your name is spelled the way it appears on your hall ticket, that the register number matches, and that the subject codes match the papers you wrote. Mistakes are rare, but catching one early saves weeks of paperwork later.

If something looks wrong, or if your name does not appear at all, contact your college office first. They deal with the university's Pareeksha Bhavan directly and can tell you whether it is a data issue or something that needs a formal application.

Also remember that this is one of many [university results](/results/) declared in the October session. SPPU released its B.Ed, B.P.Ed and M.Ed [SPPU results](/results/sppu-bed-bped-med-result-2026/) on the same day, and the MAT September 2026 [scorecards](/results/mat-september-2026-result-scorecard-download/) also came out this week. If you are waiting on another university, keep an eye on its own official portal rather than forwarding rumours in student groups.

## A note on supplementary and improvement exams

Several of the October 1 results are supplementary or improvement examinations. These are second chances, and they count the same as a regular pass once declared. Employers and other universities accept them without distinction, so there is no reason to hesitate before downloading your scorecard.

That said, improvement exams can change your division or CGPA. If your marksheet shows a new grade, update it everywhere it matters: your college records, any ongoing application forms, and your own files. Small mismatches between old and new marks are a common reason applications get held up.

## Where to go for official updates

The university posts all result notices, revaluation circulars and marksheet distribution schedules on its main website at <a href="https://www.uoc.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">uoc.ac.in</a>. The results portal only shows scores. For anything about revaluation, photocopy of answer scripts, or grievances, the circulars from Pareeksha Bhavan are the final word.

Students should avoid unofficial websites and social media forwards for marks or dates. Every October, fake result links circulate in student groups, and the only safe address remains the university's own portal.
