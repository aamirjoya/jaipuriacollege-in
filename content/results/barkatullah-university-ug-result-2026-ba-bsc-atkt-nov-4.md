**Barkatullah University has declared the BA and BSc ATKT results.** The Bhopal university released the scores on 4 November 2026, and students can now check them on the official result portal. If you sat for the ATKT exam, your result is live.

Two first-year courses are covered in this release. The B.A Plain Regular Year I result is for the April 2026 exam, while the B.Sc NEP Year I result is for the December 2025 exam. Both are ATKT results, which means they are for students who were clearing subjects left over from an earlier attempt.

## Which results came out on 4 November

- **B.A Plain Regular Year I (April 2026):** ATKT result declared 4 November 2026
- **B.Sc NEP Year I (December 2025):** ATKT result declared 4 November 2026

The university puts up its results course by course on its Online Result page. Open the page, find your course in the list, and click the link. You will need your roll number to see your marks.

> The ATKT result is for students clearing subjects left over from an earlier attempt. If you have passed, you can move ahead with your next year of study.

## How to check your Barkatullah University result

1. Go to the <a href="https://www.bubhopal.ac.in/1594/Online-Result" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Online Result page</a> on bubhopal.ac.in.
2. Find your course in the result list: B.A Plain Regular Year I or B.Sc NEP Year I.
3. Click the result link for your course.
4. Enter your roll number and the other details asked on the page.
5. Submit and check your marks on the screen.
6. Download or save a copy of the result for your records.

Keep your roll number ready before you open the page. The portal asks for it first, and a wrong entry will show no result. If the page loads slowly, wait and try again. Result days bring heavy traffic, and the university site can take time to respond.

The marksheet you see online is provisional. Colleges treat it as proof for the next step, but the original marksheet comes later from your college. Save a printout or a PDF copy now so you have it when you need it.

## What ATKT means for your next year

ATKT stands for Allowed To Keep Terms. In plain words, it is a second chance. A student who could not clear one or more subjects in the main exam gets to sit for those subjects again. The ATKT result tells you whether you have cleared them now.

Passed all your subjects? You can move ahead with your next year of study. If a subject is still pending, talk to your college office about the next ATKT round. Do not wait for the last day. Your college can tell you the exam form dates and the fee for the next attempt.

Students who checked the [JNVU M.Com results](/results/jnvu-m-com-result-2026-nov-4-sem-2-4-check/) this week will know the drill. The process is the same everywhere: open the official portal, pick your course, enter your roll number.

## Not happy with your marks? Revaluation is an option

If your marks look wrong, you can ask for a recheck. Barkatullah University takes revaluation requests through MPOnline. The fee is Rs 200 per subject, and you can apply for up to two subjects. A Rs 25 portal fee is added on top.

Revaluation forms for many Barkatullah University programmes are listed with 6 November as the last date, so do not sit on this. Check the notice for your course on the university website and apply before the window closes. Once it shuts, the portal stops taking forms.

A revaluation can raise or lower your marks. The university changes the result only when the difference crosses its rule, so apply only if you have a real reason to think the checking went wrong. Keep the payment receipt safe. You will need it if you have to follow up.

To see how revaluation results look when they arrive, the [RRBMU revaluation results](/results/rrbmu-revaluation-result-2026-nov-3-ba-bsc-bcom/) for BA, BSc and BCom came out on 3 November. That gives you a sense of the timeline.

## Keep your documents ready

Once the result is out, colleges move fast. Keep your admit card, roll number and a printout of the online result in one place. You will need them for admission to the next year, for scholarship forms, and for any revaluation request.
