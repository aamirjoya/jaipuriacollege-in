**The [OSSTET 2026 result](/results/) is out. The Board of Secondary Education (BSE), Odisha declared it on Monday, 23 November, and the result went live at 5:30 PM on the board's official websites. Out of 55,968 candidates who took the exam, 44,298 have qualified. That is a pass rate of 79.15%. If you appeared for the test, check your scorecard on the <a href="https://www.bseodisha.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BSE Odisha website</a> now.**

The exam was held on 30 September 2026 at 261 centres in 30 districts of the state. A total of 59,357 candidates had registered for the two papers. Of them, 3,389 stayed away on exam day.

## Paper I saw the bigger crowd, Paper II the tighter pass rate

Paper I drew almost all the candidates. Out of 55,193 who had registered for it, 52,019 appeared and 41,192 qualified. The pass rate for Paper I is 79.19%.

Paper II was smaller. Of the 4,164 registered candidates, 3,949 appeared and 3,106 qualified. The pass rate here is 78.65%, a little below Paper I.

| Paper | Registered | Appeared | Qualified | Pass rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper I | 55,193 | 52,019 | 41,192 | 79.19% |
| Paper II | 4,164 | 3,949 | 3,106 | 78.65% |
| Total | 59,357 | 55,968 | 44,298 | 79.15% |

> Nearly 8 in 10 candidates cleared the test. Paper I carried the numbers, with 41,192 passes, while Paper II stayed close behind at 78.65%.

OSSTET is the eligibility test for secondary school teaching posts in Odisha. Clearing it is the first step toward applying for those posts. The certificate that follows is the proof candidates will need when recruitment opens.

## How to check your OSSTET result

Keep your roll number and date of birth ready before you open the site. The scorecard opens with these details.

- Open the <a href="https://www.bseodisha.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official BSE Odisha website</a> or bseodisha.nic.in.
- Find the OSSTET 2026 result link on the home page.
- Enter your roll number and date of birth.
- Submit the details to see your scorecard.
- Check your name, roll number, marks, and qualifying status.
- Download the scorecard PDF and save a printout.

Check the spelling of your name and your roll number on the scorecard. If anything looks wrong, note it down and contact the board through the details on its website. Do not pay anyone who claims they can change your result. The board publishes results only through its own websites.

## What qualified candidates should do next

Qualified candidates can download their digitised certificate from the BSE Odisha website from 10 AM on 25 November 2026. The download needs a fee payment, and it stays open until the board issues the physical hard copies. Keep a safe copy of the certificate. You will need it when you apply for secondary school teaching posts in the state.

Teachers in other states have faced the same wait this month. Uttar Pradesh declared its [Special TET result for in-service teachers](/results/up-special-tet-result-2026-38902-qualified/) on 20 November, and Uttarakhand put out its [UTET result for Paper 1 and Paper 2](/results/utet-result-2026-paper-1-paper-2-out-check-scorecard/) earlier in the month. In both cases the certificate followed the result by a few days, much like Odisha has planned here.

## If you did not qualify, your answer sheet is open till 2 December

Candidates who did not make it can download their OMR answer sheet from the BSE Odisha website. The window stays open until 5 PM on 2 December 2026. The sheet shows how you marked each answer, so it is worth a look before you plan your next attempt.

A fail in this test does not close the door. The test is held every year, and the board will issue a fresh notification for the next round. Keep checking the official website for it, and do not trust dates shared on social media or messaging groups. The board website is the only place where the next schedule will appear first.
