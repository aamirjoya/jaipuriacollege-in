**Andhra University has declared the semester results for thousands of students. The scorecards for BA, BBA, BCom, BSc, MA, MSc and MCom are live on the official results portal, and students who sat the July 2026 exams can check their marks right now with a hall ticket number.**

The university released the results on November 12, 2026. The batch covers the second semester regular examinations and the odd semester main examinations held in July 2026. More course-wise results have followed since, including the M.Sc Geo-Physics second semester result on November 13.

## Andhra University result 2026: what is out

The results cover both undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Students from BA, BBA, BCom, BSc, MA, MSc and MCom can now see their subject-wise marks, total marks and result status online.

Andhra University is based in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. It was set up in 1926 and is recognised by the University Grants Commission. Its results portal is the only place to check the official scorecard. Students should not trust random links shared on social media or messaging apps.

The release came just a day after the [Osmania University UG semester 6 result](/results/osmania-university-ug-sem-6-result-2026-nov-11-bsc-bcom-bba-ba/) and close on the heels of the [AKNU UG second semester result](/results/aknu-ug-2nd-semester-result-2026-ba-bsc-bcom-out/), which shows how busy this week has been for university results in the two Telugu states.

> The scorecard is live on the official results portal. Keep your hall ticket number ready before you open it, and save a copy the moment it loads.

## How to check your Andhra University result

Follow these steps to download your scorecard from the <a href="https://results.andhrauniversity.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Andhra University results portal</a>:

- Open results.andhrauniversity.edu.in in your browser.
- Find the Examination Results section on the page.
- Pick the result link for your course and semester.
- Enter your hall ticket number and submit.
- Your scorecard will appear on the screen.
- Check every detail, then download it and take a printout.

If the page does not load at the first try, wait a few minutes and try again. Result portals slow down when lakhs of students check at the same time. A stable connection and a fresh browser tab usually fix most loading problems.

## What to check on your scorecard

Your online marksheet carries your name, registration number, course name, subject codes and names, marks in each subject, total marks, maximum marks and your result status. It also shows the date the result was declared.

Read every line before you close the page. A wrong spelling in your name, a missing subject or a wrong total should be reported to the university examination department at once. Do not wait for the original marksheet to arrive before raising the issue, because corrections take time.

Keep the downloaded scorecard safe. The university will issue the original marksheet later, and the printout works as your record till then. Students who need the marksheet for the next semester admission or a scholarship form should keep both a digital copy and a printout.

## Use the scorecard for your next step

The scorecard is more than a result slip. Colleges ask for it at the time of next semester admission, and scholarship forms often ask for the latest marks. Students who plan to apply for a scholarship should keep the scorecard, the hall ticket and the fee receipt in one folder, so nothing is missing when the form opens.

If you are in your final year, the same scorecard goes into your placement file and your higher studies applications. A clean printout with no pen marks works best for scanning. Take a photo of the printout as a backup, and store the PDF on your phone as well as on a drive. A lost scorecard is easy to download again while the portal link stays live, but it is simpler to keep your own copy from day one.

## What comes next after the result

Students who cleared all papers can move on to the next semester as per their college schedule. Those who did not clear a paper should watch the official portal for the supplementary exam notice. The university has not shared revaluation dates or supplementary exam dates in this notice, so do not believe any date doing the rounds on social media.

The university keeps adding course-wise results to the portal through the week. If your course link is not live yet, check the portal again the next day instead of paying anyone who claims to fetch the result early. The result is free, and the official portal is the only source that counts.
