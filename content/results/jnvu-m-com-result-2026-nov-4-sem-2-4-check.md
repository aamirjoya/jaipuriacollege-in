**Jai Narain Vyas University (JNVU), Jodhpur, has released the M.Com result 2026 for two semesters.** The university published the M.Com (Business Finance and Economics) 2nd semester and 4th semester results on November 4, 2026, for the 2025-26 academic session. Students who appeared for these exams can now check their marks on the official results portal.

The results follow the NEP 2020 semester pattern. The university also declared nine postgraduate revaluation results a day earlier, on November 3, 2026.

## What came out on November 4

The [JNVU result 2026](/results/) release covers one course across two semesters. The university has activated both result links on its portal, and each one opens with the student's roll number.

- **M.Com (Business Finance and Economics), Semester II:** result declared November 4, 2026
- **M.Com (Business Finance and Economics), Semester IV:** result declared November 4, 2026

Both results belong to the even semester examinations of the 2025-26 session. The 4th semester result matters most for final-year students, because it completes their postgraduate degree. The 2nd semester result moves first-year M.Com students into their next semester.

## 9 revaluation results from November 3

A day before the M.Com results, JNVU published nine postgraduate revaluation results on November 3, 2026, for students who had asked the university to recheck their answer scripts.

- M.Sc 4th semester, Botany
- M.Sc 4th semester, Chemistry
- M.Sc 2nd semester, Chemistry
- M.Sc 2nd semester, Botany
- M.Sc 4th semester, Mathematics
- M.Sc 2nd semester, Mathematics
- M.Sc 2nd semester, Zoology
- MA 2nd semester, Sociology
- MA 4th semester, Journalism and Mass Communication

Students who applied for revaluation should check the updated result on the same portal. The revised marks replace the earlier scores, so download the new marksheet even if you saved the old one.

> The two M.Com results sit at the top of the university's postgraduate results list for 2025-26. Open the link for your semester, enter your roll number, and your marksheet appears on the screen.

## How to check your JNVU M.Com result

Checking the result takes only a few minutes. Keep your roll number ready before you open the portal.

- Open the official JNVU results portal at <a href="https://results.jnvuiums.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">results.jnvuiums.in</a>.
- Go to the results section and pick the Post Graduation Result (2025-2026) list.
- Find the link for your course, M.Com (Business Finance and Economics), and your semester.
- Enter your roll number and the other details the page asks for.
- Click on the Get Result option and wait for your marksheet to load.
- Download the provisional marksheet and save a copy on your phone and computer.

The portal can slow down when many students check at once, right after a result goes live. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. The result links stay active on the portal, so there is no rush to check in the first hour.

## What to check on your marksheet

Once your marksheet opens, read every detail before you close the page. Check your name, roll number, and course name first, then the subject-wise marks and the total. If any detail looks wrong, note it down and contact your college office or the university examination section with your admit card ready.

Save the downloaded marksheet in at least two places. You will need it for the next semester's admission, for scholarship forms, and for any verification your college asks for.

## What comes next for M.Com students

For 2nd semester students, the result clears the way to the 3rd semester. Colleges will use these marks for promotion, so keep the marksheet safe until your college confirms the next steps.

Final semester students should watch for the degree and convocation notices from the university. If you plan to apply for jobs or higher studies, the provisional marksheet works for most applications until the original arrives.

Students who are not happy with their marks can look for the revaluation or rechecking window on the university portal. Do not pay anyone who promises to change your marks outside the official process. Only the university can revise a result, and it does so only through the revaluation route.
