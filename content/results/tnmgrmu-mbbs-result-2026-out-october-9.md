**The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University released three MBBS results on October 9, 2026.** MBBS Final (Part II), MBBS Second Year and MBBS Second Professional marks are now live on the university's official result portal.

If you sat for any of these examinations, your result is ready. Here is exactly what came out and how to check it.

## Three MBBS results, all on the same day

TNMGRMU published the results together on October 9. The three examinations covered are:

- **MBBS Final (Part II)** — declared October 9, 2026
- **MBBS Second Year** — declared October 9, 2026
- **MBBS Second Professional** — declared October 9, 2026

The three results cover different stages of the MBBS course. Final-year students, second-year students and second professional students all have something to check today.

Each result appears as a separate entry on the portal under its own exam term. So pick the exact name that matches your course before you enter your details.

> "Pick the exact exam term before you enter your register number. Second Year and Second Professional are listed as separate entries on the portal, and selecting the wrong one will show no result."

The Final (Part II) result matters the most for outgoing students, because it decides completion of the MBBS programme. For Second Year and Second Professional students, the result decides movement into the next phase of the course.

## How to check your TNMGRMU MBBS result 2026

The university has kept the process simple. Keep your registration number ready — that is the main detail the portal asks for.

1. Open the <a href="https://cms2results.tnmgrmuexam.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official result portal</a>. You can also reach it through the Results link on <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnmgrmu.ac.in</a>.
2. Select the exam term for your course — MBBS Final (Part II), MBBS Second Year or MBBS Second Professional.
3. Enter your registration number.
4. Complete the security check shown on the page.
5. Submit and view your result on the screen.
6. Save or print a copy for your records.

The portal does not ask for your date of birth. You only need the registration number, the correct exam term and the security check.

## Result page not loading? Try this

Result days bring heavy traffic, and the university's own site can slow down or throw errors. This is normal, not a problem with your result.

- Confirm you picked the right exam term. MBBS Second Year and MBBS Second Professional are two separate entries.
- Recheck your registration number for typing errors.
- Complete the security check exactly as shown on the page.
- Try again after some time, or switch browsers. Thousands of students checking at once can slow the portal down.

Never pay anyone to "unlock" or "correct" your result. The official TNMGRMU portal is the only place to check your marks.

## What to do after checking your result

Download or print the result page and keep it safe. Match your name, registration number and subject-wise marks on the screen. If anything looks wrong, report it to your college office right away.

Your marksheet will come through your college once the university releases it. For the next steps — revaluation or retotaling, supplementary examinations and the next professional examination — keep watching <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnmgrmu.ac.in</a> for official notices.
