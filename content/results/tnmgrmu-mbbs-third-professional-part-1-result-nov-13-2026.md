**The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University has declared the TNMGRMU MBBS result 2026 for the Third Professional Part 1 examination on November 13, 2026.** Students who appeared for the exam can now check their marks on the university's official result portal with their registration number.

It is the latest in a busy week for the university. Pharm D fifth year results came out on November 12. A day before that, on November 11, the university released results for MPharm, MSc, BAMS and BUMS courses. Third professional MBBS students now have their turn.

## MBBS Third Professional Part 1 leads a busy results week

The university has been releasing results course by course through the week. Here is what came out over the last three days:

- **November 13, 2026:** MBBS Third Professional Part 1
- **November 12, 2026:** Pharm D Fifth Year, Pharm D (Post Baccalaureate) Fifth Year
- **November 11, 2026:** MPharm Third Semester, MPharm Fourth Semester, MSc Biostatistics First and Second Year, MSc (Public Health) First and Second Year, MSc Epidemiology First and Second Year, BUMS Second Professional, BAMS Second Professional, BAMS Third Professional

Each result sits as a separate entry on the portal under its own exam term. Students should pick the exact name that matches their course before they enter any details.

> "Pick the exact exam term on the portal before you enter your register number. Third Professional Part 1 is listed as its own entry, and the wrong term will show no result."

The Third Professional Part 1 result decides whether students move into the next phase of the MBBS course, so the result page sees heavy traffic on the day it goes live.

## How to check your TNMGRMU MBBS result 2026

The university has kept the process simple. Keep your registration number ready. That is the main detail the portal asks for.

1. Open the <a href="https://cms2results.tnmgrmuexam.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official result portal</a>. You can also reach it through the Results link on <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnmgrmu.ac.in</a>.
2. Select the exam term for your course: MBBS Third Professional Part 1.
3. Enter your registration number.
4. Complete the security check shown on the page.
5. Submit and view your result on the screen.
6. Save or print a copy for your records.

The portal does not ask for your date of birth. You only need the registration number, the correct exam term and the security check.

## Result page not loading? Try this

Result days bring heavy traffic, and the university's site can slow down or throw errors. This is normal, and it is not a problem with your result.

- Confirm you picked the right exam term. Third Professional Part 1 is a separate entry from the other MBBS results.
- Recheck your registration number for typing errors.
- Complete the security check exactly as shown on the page.
- Try again after some time, or switch browsers. Thousands of students checking at once can slow the portal down.

Never pay anyone to "fix" or "correct" your result. The official TNMGRMU portal is the only place to check your marks.

## The online result is provisional, and revaluation is not allowed

The marks you see on the portal are provisional. The official marksheet will come through your college once the university releases it. Treat the online copy as a quick check, not the final record.

One rule students should know: the university does not allow revaluation of these results. If the marks on screen do not match what you expected, watch for the university's official notices on supplementary examinations and the next professional examination. Keep watching <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnmgrmu.ac.in</a> for those updates.

## What to do after checking your result

Download or print the result page and keep it safe. Match your name, registration number and subject-wise marks on the screen. If anything looks wrong, report it to your college office right away.
