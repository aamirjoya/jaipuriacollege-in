**Savitribai Phule Pune University has declared its semester results for five courses.** Students of second and third year BA, third year LLB, and first and second year LLM can now check their marksheets online. The university published the results on 12 October 2026, and the provisional marksheets are available on the official result portal.

## Which courses are covered in this result

The October 12 release covers both undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. BA students who appeared for the Summer Session 2026 are included, while LLM students are from the Winter Session 2025. The university releases results in phases, so this is one batch of the 2025-26 cycle.

- **Second Year BA** (2019 Pattern, N+2+1), Summer Session 2026
- **Third Year BA** (2019 Pattern, N+2+1), Summer Session 2026
- **Third Year LLB** (2017-2023 Mixed Pattern, N+2+1), Summer Session 2026
- **First Year LLM** (2014 Pattern, N+2+1), Winter Session 2025
- **Second Year LLM** (2014 Pattern, N+2+1), Winter Session 2025

Make sure you open the entry that matches your exact course, pattern and session. SPPU publishes separate links for each combination, and picking the wrong one will show no records.

> "Always check results only on the official university portal. Third-party sites may show outdated links, and your login details stay safest on SPPU's own server."

## How to check your SPPU marksheet online

The process takes just a few minutes if you have your hall ticket ready. The portal you need is the <a href="https://onlineresults.unipune.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official SPPU online result dashboard</a>, which is run by the university's examination department.

1. Open onlineresults.unipune.ac.in in your browser.
2. Look for the result dashboard or the active result links for your session.
3. Select your course name, matching the syllabus pattern and the exam session (Summer 2026 for BA and LLB, Winter 2025 for LLM).
4. Enter your seat number and any other details the page asks for.
5. Submit and wait for your statement of marks to appear on the screen.
6. Check the marks carefully, then download the marksheet and take a printout for your records.

The portal can slow down on result day when thousands of students log in together. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again, preferably during non-peak hours. Your <a href="/results/how-to-check-university-result-online/">university result</a> will still be there.

## What to verify on your marksheet

Once the marksheet opens, do not just look at the final percentage. Go through every line before you close the page.

- Your name, roll number and registration number should match your hall ticket exactly.
- Subject codes, internal assessment marks and theory scores should all be present.
- Your passing status, aggregate percentage and any division or class remark should make sense for your course.

If anything looks wrong, whether a missing subject, a blank column, or a wrongly spelled name, take the matter to your college's examination section or the university's examination department without delay. The online marksheet is provisional, and the original physical marksheet comes later from the university. Students planning to apply for revaluation should watch for the examination department's next circulars, which carry the deadline and fee details.

Students from other programmes are still waiting. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which SPPU courses had their results declared on 12 October 2026?

Second Year BA, Third Year BA, Third Year LLB, First Year LLM and Second Year LLM. The BA and LLB results are for the Summer Session 2026, while the LLM results are for the Winter Session 2025.

### Where can I check my SPPU result 2026?

On the university's official result portal at onlineresults.unipune.ac.in. Pick your course and exam session, then enter your seat number to see the marksheet.

### What details do I need to check my SPPU result?

Your seat number, as printed on your exam hall ticket, plus the correct course name, pattern and exam session. Keep your hall ticket handy before you open the portal.

### What if there is a mistake in my SPPU marksheet?

Report it to your college's examination section or the university's examination department right away. The online marksheet is provisional, and corrections follow the university's official process.
