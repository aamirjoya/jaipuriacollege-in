**University of Calicut has released the revaluation results for its Fourth Year Bachelor of Hotel Management (BHM) examinations. Both results are listed on the official portal with the date 25 November 2026, and students who applied for revaluation can check their updated marks on results.uoc.ac.in.**

The two entries cover different examinations, so students need to open the one that matches their own exam. One is for the 9/2025 examination of the 2019 admission batch. The other is for the 4/2026 examination. The [Calicut University results](/results/) are published online only, and the portal is the only place to check them.

## Two BHM revaluation results listed on 25 November

| Result | Examination | Admission batch | Listed on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Revaluation result, Fourth Year BHM | 9/2025 | 2019 Admission | 25 November 2026 |
| Revaluation result, Fourth Year BHM | 4/2026 | Not stated in the listing | 25 November 2026 |

The 9/2025 entry names the 2019 admission batch in its title. The 4/2026 entry carries no admission year in the listing, so students should go by the examination they appeared in and applied for revaluation for.

The two codes mark two separate examination sessions, one from 2025 and one from 2026. Students from the 2019 admission batch are in the final year of the course, so this revaluation result is the last score update many of them were waiting on before they apply for jobs or higher studies.

> Both revaluation results for Fourth Year BHM are listed on the official portal with the date 25 November 2026.

## How to check your BHM revaluation result

Keep your register number ready before you start. The portal asks for it on the results page.

- Open <a href="https://results.uoc.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official Calicut University results portal</a>.
- Find the entry titled "Revaluation Result of Fourth Year Bachelor of Hotel Management" for your examination, 9/2025 or 4/2026.
- Open the result link and enter your register number and the other details the portal asks for.
- Check your result on screen. Save a copy or take a printout for your records.

The portal lists many courses with similar names, so read the full entry title before you open it. Picking the wrong examination is the most common reason a result does not show up.

## What a revaluation result tells you

A revaluation result shows the outcome after the university checks an answer script again at a student's request. Marks can stay the same or change. The listing itself does not say how many scripts changed or by how much, so each student needs to open their own result to see what happened to their marks.

The online result is for quick reference. Collect your updated marksheet or grade card from your college when it is ready. If the name, register number or marks shown online do not match your records, contact the university examination office or your college examination section and get it checked.

## Why this result matters for final-year students

Fourth year is the last year of the BHM course. Students leaving college this year need their final marks for job applications, campus placement rounds and forms for higher studies. A revaluation can change the marks on the marksheet, so the updated result is the one to use from here on. Students who applied for revaluation should download the new result now instead of relying on the old score, since the two can differ.

For students whose marks stayed the same, the revaluation request is settled for that paper. There is nothing more to do on that paper, and the focus shifts to the next step, whether that is a job, an internship or a higher study form.
