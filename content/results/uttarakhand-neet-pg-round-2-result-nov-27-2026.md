**HNBUMU declared the [provisional seat allotment result](/results/) for Uttarakhand NEET PG 2026 round 2 on Friday, 27 November. The main broad sheet lists 956 candidates. If your name is on it, check every detail now. Errors must reach the Counselling Board by 11 AM today, 28 November, and allotment letters open for download after 2 PM today.**

The result covers MD, MS, PG Diploma and DNB seats under state, all India and NRI quotas. A separate sheet covers 10 NRI candidates. Allotment is based on each candidate's merit and the choices they filled. The board has said the result is provisional and can still change.

| Result declared | 27 November 2026, after 8 PM |
| Candidates in main sheet | 956 |
| Highest NEET score | 562 (state rank 1, all India rank 1,456) |
| NRI sheet | 10 candidates |

## What the broad sheet tells you

The broad sheet lists every candidate with their state rank, NEET roll number, name, NEET rank, category, NEET marks, previous round allotment, choices filled, and the college and course allotted in this round. The remarks column sums up where each candidate stands.

| Remark in the broad sheet | Candidates |
| --- | --- |
| Seat not available as per merit and choices | 557 |
| No choices filled by the candidate | 123 |
| Previous admitted seat retained | 118 |
| Previous admitted college or seat upgraded | 35 |
| No remark, a seat is listed against the candidate | 123 |

More than half the candidates in the sheet, 557 of 956, got no seat in this round because no seat matched their merit and choices. Another 123 filled no choices at all. On the other side, 118 candidates kept the seat they got in round 1, and 35 moved to a better college or seat. The remaining 123 have a seat listed against their name with no remark.

## Check your result, then act before 11 AM

The window to fix a mistake is short. It closes at 11 AM today, so do the check first thing.

1. Go to the <a href="https://hnbumu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official HNBUMU website</a> and open the NEET PG 2026 Uttarakhand State Centralised Counselling section.
2. Open the Round 2 seat allotment result PDF and press Ctrl+F to search for your NEET PG roll number or name.
3. Check the allotted college, course, quota and preference number against the choices you filled.
4. If anything is wrong, email the Counselling Board at ukneetcounselling@gmail.com before 11 AM today, 28 November.
5. Download your provisional allotment letter from the <a href="https://meta-secure.com/HNBUMU_MDMS" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official counselling portal</a> after 2 PM today.
6. Report at the allotted college with your original documents by 30 November 2026.

> The Counselling Board says candidates should check their result and report any error by 11:00 AM on 28 November 2026.

The [UP NEET PG round 2 allotment](/results/up-neet-pg-round-2-seat-allotment-result-nov-26-2026/) came out a day earlier, on 26 November. The [NEET PG 2026 round 2](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-final-allotment-20707-seats-report-nov-25/) national picture has also moved fast this week, with state rounds following the all India allotment. Uttarakhand is one of the last state rounds to declare.

## NRI candidates have a separate sheet

Ten candidates appear on the NRI broad sheet, which is based on the Uttarakhand State NEET PG NRI merit list and the choices filled for round 2. Four of the ten filled no choices. The NRI sheet covers two colleges, both in Dehradun: Shri Guru Ram Rai Institute of Medical and Health Sciences and Gautam Buddha Chikitsa Mahavidyalaya. The same deadlines apply to NRI candidates, so the 11 AM error window and the 2 PM allotment letter timing hold for them too.

## Carry these documents when you report

Admission is not complete until the college checks your original documents. If you cannot produce the originals that prove your eligibility or reservation, the admission stands cancelled. The board has also said that if any submitted document is found forged or fraudulent at any stage, the candidate loses the right to admission and the allotment is cancelled.

In-service PMHS candidates must carry the original NOC or weightage certificate from the competent authority. Every candidate must also produce a notarised affidavit on non-judicial stamp paper of Rs 100, in the format enclosed with the notice, at the time of admission in the allotted college.

The result is based on the information candidates gave online. The college will verify the originals at admission, so keep the documents ready before you travel. Reporting closes on 30 November, and the board has given no extension in the notice.
