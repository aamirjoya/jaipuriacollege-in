**Eight new result notices from Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences are now live, and they cover BNYS and BSc MLT students who sat the July 2026 examinations. If your course is on the list, your result PDF is waiting on the official KNRUHS notifications page.**

The notices are all dated 27 October 2026. That date matters because the university released several lists on the same day, and students searching for the [KNRUHS result 2026](/results/) need to open the exact notice for their course, year and category. Opening the wrong PDF will not show your number, even if your own result is already out.

## Which 8 KNRUHS notices came out on 27 October

Three notices cover BNYS students. The Third BNYS Part-II result is for regular candidates, while the Third BNYS Part-I results are for backlog candidates and referred candidates, listed as two separate notices.

Five notices cover BSc MLT students. The BSc MLT Final Year results are out for backlog and referred candidates. The Second Year results are also out for backlog and referred candidates. For First Year students, the notice on the list covers backlog candidates.

One point needs care. A First Year referred notice is not confirmed in the list used for this report, so First Year referred students should not assume their result is part of this batch. Check the notifications page again for your exact category before drawing any conclusion about your result.

Jagran Josh's table for the same date also lists First MBBS examinations from August 2026, for regular and backlog candidates. Students in that group should check the same official page, since the MBBS lists sit alongside the BNYS and BSc MLT notices.

## How to check your KNRUHS result in the PDF

The results are published as PDFs, not as a login-based scorecard page. That changes how you check, so follow the steps in order:

- Open the <a href="https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/all-notifications" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KNRUHS notifications page</a>.
- Go to the Results section or scroll through the notification list.
- Find the title that matches your course, year, exam month and category. Regular, backlog and referred are separate notices.
- Click View Document to open the result PDF.
- Search the PDF for your hall ticket or registration number.
- Save or print a copy for your records.

Before you rely on a PDF, check three details against your admit card: your number, your course name and your year. A small mismatch in any of these three means you may have opened the wrong notice. If your number is missing or a detail looks wrong, contact your college or the KNRUHS examinations wing through the contact details on the official site.

> Check the course, year and category first. A result PDF only helps when it is the right list for you.

## If the official page does not load

Heavy traffic can slow the official site right after results come out. Jagran Josh reported that the KNRUHS website was down for a time because of high traffic, and advised students to wait and try again. That is still the safest advice. Close the browser tab, wait a short while, and retry on the official page.

Do not download your result from a random third-party link that claims to host the same PDF. Unofficial copies can be old, incomplete or edited. The <a href="https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">KNRUHS official website</a> is the only source you should trust for the final list, and the notifications page is where each notice is posted.

Students who cleared their papers can now move to the next step for their course. Students with a backlog or referred status should speak to their college office early about the next examination chance, since the notices do not announce a supplementary exam date.

## What KNRUHS is

Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences is a state university in Warangal, Telangana. It was established in 2014 and is recognised by the University Grants Commission. It conducts examinations for health science courses across its affiliated colleges, and it publishes results course by course through its notifications page.

That phased pattern is normal for this university. Earlier in October, it also published BDS Final Year and Final MBBS Part-II lists dated 15 and 19 October, so a course missing from this batch may still appear in a later notice.
