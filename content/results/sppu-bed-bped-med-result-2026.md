**Savitribai Phule Pune University has declared the SPPU result 2026 for six examinations on October 1. First and Second Year results are out for B.Ed (General), B.P.Ed and M.Ed (General) students of the Summer Session 2026.** The results are live on the university's official result dashboard, and students can check them right now with their seat number and mother's name.

This is a fresh declaration straight from the university portal. The <a href="https://onlineresults.unipune.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official SPPU result dashboard</a> lists all six entries with the result date of 01 October 2026, so there is no confusion about which papers are out.

## Which courses are covered in today's SPPU result 2026

Six result entries went live on the dashboard today:

- First Year Bachelor of Education (General), Rev. 2015
- Second Year Bachelor of Education (General), Rev. 2015
- First Year Bachelor of Physical Education, 2015 Pattern
- Second Year Bachelor of Physical Education, 2015 Pattern
- First Year Master of Education (General), Rev. 2016
- Second Year Master of Education (General), Rev. 2016

All six are for the Summer Session 2026 examinations under the N+2+1 scheme. Note that these are the 2015/2016 pattern papers, so make sure you open the entry that matches your own course pattern and year on the dashboard.

## How to check your SPPU result in 5 steps

1. Open the <a href="https://onlineresults.unipune.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SPPU online result dashboard</a>.
2. Scroll down to find your course and year entry for Summer Session 2026, then click it.
3. Enter your seat number and mother's name in the fields shown.
4. Click "Check Result" and wait for your scorecard to appear on screen.
5. Download the PDF or take a printout and keep it safe.

> The online marksheet is provisional. It works for your own reference, but colleges and employers will ask for the original marksheet later, so collect it from your college when it is issued.

Double-check the entries on the dashboard before you start. SPPU publishes results for many courses in batches, and two entries can look similar. Picking the wrong pattern or year is the most common reason students panic over a "result not found" message.

## What to verify on your scorecard

Your name, seat number and course name come first. Then check the subject-wise marks, the total and the final result status. If anything looks wrong, like a misspelled name or a missing subject, contact your college office or the university examination section without delay.

Do not enter your seat number or personal details on any third-party website that claims to show your result. The university's own dashboard is the only safe place. You can also reach the result portal through <a href="https://www.unipune.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the SPPU official website</a> by going to the Student Corner section and clicking on Online Result.

Unhappy with your marks? SPPU usually opens a revaluation window on its portal within a few days of the result declaration. The window is short, and the university announces it separately, so keep watching the portal if you plan to apply.

## Your paper is not in today's list? Do this

SPPU does not release all results on one day. It publishes them in batches, course by course, through the year. The dashboard shows a result date against every entry, so the fastest way to know whether your paper is out is to scroll the list and look at those dates.

If your entry has appeared but shows no data, check that you picked the right year and pattern. A Second Year student clicking the First Year link will get nothing, and so will a 2015 Pattern student who opens a 2019 Pattern entry. When the portal throws an error, clear your browser cache or try again after some time. Result day traffic can be heavy in the first few hours.

Keep your seat number and mother's name exactly as printed on your admit card. A single spelling difference can block the login. If you have lost the admit card, contact your college office for the seat number before you start.

## What to do after you download the scorecard

Final-year students should ask their college when the original marksheet will arrive. Many job applications and PG admissions accept the provisional online scorecard for the first round, but they will ask for the original later. Do not miss that step.

Keep a printed copy of the scorecard in a file with your other documents. Phone screenshots get deleted or lost. A printout also helps at the college counter when you apply for revaluation or collect the marksheet.
