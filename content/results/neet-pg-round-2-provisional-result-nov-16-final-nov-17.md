The wait ends today. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) will release the **NEET PG 2026 Round 2 provisional seat allotment result** on Monday, November 16. Candidates who registered for the second round can check whether they have been allotted a seat on the official website, <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a>.

The result covers MD, MS, DNB and postgraduate diploma seats under the All India Quota, along with seats in deemed universities, central universities and other participating medical colleges. Like other [exam results](/results/) announced this month, the NEET PG Round 2 result will be published as a PDF list that candidates can search with their roll number.

## Provisional today, final tomorrow

Today's list is provisional, and that word matters. MCC will publish the final Round 2 allotment result tomorrow, November 17. A seat shown in today's provisional list can still change in the final list, so do not rush to the college or pay any fee based on the provisional allotment alone.

The schedule comes from MCC's revised notice of November 7, issued after the National Medical Commission approved new postgraduate medical seats for the Round 2 seat matrix. Registration for Round 2 ran from November 7 to November 14, and choice filling closed on November 15. Seat processing took place over the last two days, and the provisional result is the next step in that revised plan.

## Round 2 dates at a glance

- **Provisional result:** November 16, 2026
- **Final result:** November 17, 2026
- **Reporting to allotted colleges:** November 18 to 24, 2026
- **Verification of joined candidates:** November 25, 2026

Keep these dates written down. The reporting window is seven days, and colleges will not extend it for individual candidates. If your name appears in the final list, plan your travel and documents now so you are not caught short on November 18.

## How to check your NEET PG Round 2 provisional result

The result link will appear on the MCC website during the day. Follow these steps:

1. Go to the official MCC website at <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a>.
2. Open the PG Medical Counselling section.
3. Find the link for the Round 2 provisional seat allotment result.
4. Open the PDF and search for your roll number with Ctrl+F.
5. Note the allotted college and course, then wait for the final result tomorrow.

The MCC website slows down when a big result goes live. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Do not use links shared on Telegram or WhatsApp groups. The only safe copy is the one on mcc.nic.in.

> "A provisional allotment is a strong hint, not a confirmed seat. Check the final list on November 17 before you book travel or pay anything."

## Reporting starts November 18: keep these ready

Candidates who get a seat in the final list must report to their allotted college between November 18 and 24. Carry your original documents, because colleges verify them at the time of joining. MCC has already published a list of 99 candidates who were provisionally converted from the Indian to the NRI category for Round 2, and those candidates must produce the same original documents at reporting. If the documents do not match, the admission can be cancelled.

## Why this round matters more than usual

Round 2 carries extra seats this year. The National Medical Commission approved [9,783 new PG seats](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-9783-new-seats-total-68305/), taking the total to 68,305, and these seats were added to the Round 2 matrix. That is why MCC revised the schedule and pushed registration to November 7. New seats also mean new options in choice filling, and some colleges have opened fresh MD seats for this round, including [IIT Kharagpur's MD programmes](/admissions/iit-kharagpur-md-programmes-2026-27-12-seats-neet-pg-round-2/).

If you get a seat tomorrow, report on time and keep a copy of every document you submit. If you do not get a seat, watch mcc.nic.in for the next round. The counselling is not over yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I check the NEET PG Round 2 provisional result?**

On the official MCC website, mcc.nic.in. Open the PG Medical Counselling section and look for the Round 2 provisional result link. The result is published as a PDF that you can search with your roll number.

**Is the provisional result the final allotment?**

No. The provisional result released today, November 16, can still change. The final Round 2 allotment result will be declared on November 17, and only the final list counts for reporting to the allotted college.

**When do I have to report to the allotted college?**

Reporting to the allotted institute runs from November 18 to November 24, 2026. Verification of candidates who join will be done on November 25.

**What if I do not get a seat in Round 2?**

Do not lose hope. Keep checking mcc.nic.in for the next round notice. Your registration and choices from this round do not carry forward automatically, so read the next notice carefully when it comes out.
