**The Maharashtra ANM and GNM CAP Round 2 selection list is due today, 17 November 2026.** The State CET Cell will put up the Round 2 seat matrix and the selection list on the ANM/GNM admission portal, and students who get a seat must join their college between 18 and 20 November. The list is for the 2026-27 admission to Auxiliary Nursing and Midwifery (ANM) and General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) diploma courses across Maharashtra.

The schedule comes from Notice No. 06, issued by the State CET Cell on 4 November 2026. The CAP Round 1 selection list came out on 14 November, and joining for that round ended at 5.30 pm on 16 November.

## The dates that matter now

The Round 2 seat matrix and the selection list are both due on 17 November. The matrix will show how many seats are actually vacant for this round. Joining runs from 18 to 20 November, and the last day ends at 5.30 pm. You have to report in person at the allotted college with your original documents and the fee, paid by demand draft or cheque. Cheques are accepted even on bank holidays.

One rule from Round 1 still applies. If you got a seat in Round 1 and joined the college, you stay in the running for Round 2. If you got a seat and did not join, you drop out of all later CAP rounds, though you can still take part in the institute-level round from 21 to 30 November. Students who joined a college in the CAP rounds cannot take part in that round.

If you are happy with your Round 1 college, you can hand in a status retention form there. After that, you are not considered in later rounds, and the form cannot be withdrawn. If you do not submit it and a higher choice comes up in Round 2, you must take the new seat.

## Close to 44,000 seats were in the CAP

The CAP covers government colleges and the 85% state quota in private colleges. Admission runs on Class 12 (HSC) marks, and the merit list is drawn up on HSC percentage. Science-stream candidates come first, then vocational science, then arts, and finally commerce.

The provisional seat matrices published on 10 November show the scale. GNM has 400 seats in government and municipal institutes, and 32,434 CAP seats in private institutes out of an intake of 38,800. ANM has 1,400 government seats and 11,562 CAP seats in private institutes out of an intake of 13,685. That is close to 44,000 CAP seats in all. How many are still vacant for Round 2 will be known only from the seat matrix due today.

> "Check the seat matrix first. It tells you which colleges still have seats before you look for your name in the list."

## How to check the Round 2 list

1. Open the CET Cell's ANM/GNM admission portal at <a href="https://medical2026.mahacet.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">medical2026.mahacet.org</a>.
2. Look for the CAP Round 2 seat matrix and the selection list links.
3. Download the selection list PDF and search for your application number or name.
4. Note the allotted college, course, and category. If your name is not in the list, wait for the institute-level round from 21 to 30 November.

Keep the mobile number and email you registered with active till the process ends, and keep your application number, login ID, and password safe.

## Who is eligible for these courses

You must be at least 17 years old on or before 31 December 2026, with no upper age limit. You need Class 12 (10+2) passed with English and at least 40% aggregate marks, or 35% for reserved categories other than EWS. Any stream qualifies, but science is preferred, followed by arts and then commerce.

ANM is open only to female candidates. In GNM, up to 30% of seats are reserved for male candidates, and up to 5% for registered ANMs with pass marks. You must be an Indian national and domiciled in Maharashtra. Candidates from outside the state and NRIs can get seats only against the 15% institutional quota in private colleges.

## The scholarship rule you should not miss

Taking part in the CAP is mandatory if you want the benefit of freeship and scholarship schemes. The Maharashtra State Board of Nursing and Paramedical Education has said so in a release dated 15 November. Under an order of the Aurangabad bench of the Bombay High Court dated 19 October 2026, students who take part in the CAP remain eligible for these schemes.

The application fee was Rs 1,000 for open and EWS candidates and Rs 800 for others. It is paid online and is not refundable. One warning from the brochure is worth reading before you join. Giving up a seat after the admission cut-off date carries a penalty of Rs 25,000 for ANM and Rs 50,000 for GNM, including first-year tuition fees.

Registration opened on 21 September 2026 and closed on 9 November. The provisional merit lists came out on 10 November, choice filling for Round 1 ran from 10 to 12 November, and the Round 1 list was published on 14 November. The schedule may change again on directions from the Indian Nursing Council, the state or central government, or a court.
