**The wait is over for law aspirants. The Consortium of National Law Universities has released the [CLAT 2027 admit card](/results/clat-2027-admit-card-nov-20-release-date-exam-dec-6/) for undergraduate and postgraduate candidates today, November 21, 2026.** The hall ticket went live at 10 am on the official portal. Candidates can now log in with their registered mobile number and password and download it.

The hall ticket was first expected on November 20 and went live a day later, on the morning of November 21. What matters now is simple: download the card, check every detail on it, and print at least two copies. The exam is only 15 days away.

CLAT 2027 will be held on Sunday, December 6, 2026, in offline pen-and-paper mode. The paper runs from 2 pm to 4 pm. Candidates with disabilities get extra time till 4.40 pm. The download window for the admit card stays open till 1.30 pm on December 6, but no one should wait till the last day.

## How to download the CLAT 2027 admit card

The download takes less than two minutes if your login details are ready.

- Go to the <a href="https://consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official CLAT 2027 portal</a> at consortiumofnlus.ac.in.
- Click on the CLAT 2027 tab at the top of the homepage.
- Log in with your registered mobile number and password.
- Click on the "Download Admit Card" button.
- Check the details on the screen, then download the file and save a copy.

Take two printouts. Carry one to the exam centre on December 6 and keep the second copy safe at home. You may need it again during NLU counselling after the exam. A photo of the admit card on your phone will not work at the gate. The printed copy is your entry ticket.

## What to check on your hall ticket

The admit card carries your name, roll number, photograph, and signature. It also shows your exam centre address, the exam timings, and the instructions for exam day. Read every line the moment you download it.

A wrong centre address or a spelling error in your name can cause real trouble on exam day. Flag such errors early, while there is still time to fix them. If any detail looks wrong, write to the Consortium at clat@consortiumofnlus.ac.in or call the helpline on 080-47162020. Do not wait till December 5.

> "Download the admit card today, print two copies, and visit your exam centre a day before if you can. On exam day, the portal slows down and the queue outside the centre does not."

## Exam day: December 6, 2 pm to 4 pm

CLAT is a national-level entrance test for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes at the National Law Universities across India. The 2027 exam follows the same pattern as past years: one paper, two hours, offline mode, with OMR sheets.

Reach your centre early. The admit card lists your reporting time along with the exam timings. Carry the printed admit card and a valid photo ID. Read the instructions printed on the card before you leave home. They cover what you can and cannot carry into the hall, and the rules for the OMR sheet.

There is no second date for anyone who misses the exam. That is why the download window stays open till 1.30 pm on exam day itself, but a last-minute download only adds stress. Get it done today.

## Beware of fake question paper sellers

The Consortium has put out a clear warning along with the admit card release. It has received reports of people claiming to offer CLAT question papers in exchange for money. These claims are fraud. No one outside the Consortium has the paper.

Do not pay anyone who promises the question paper, a leaked answer key, or a guaranteed seat. Do not share your personal or bank details with such people. If you come across such a post or message, save a screenshot and report it to the Consortium at clat@consortiumofnlus.ac.in, along with any links or payment requests.

## What happens after the exam

The answer key usually follows within a few days, and the result comes after that. The [latest results](/results/) page will carry updates as they come. For now, the only task is the admit card. Download it, print it, check it, and get back to your revision.
