**The Karnataka Management Aptitude Test (KMAT) 2026 session 1 result is out.** The Karnataka Private Post Graduate Colleges Association (KPPGCA) declared the result on October 8, and scorecards are now live for download on the official portal, kmatindia.com. If you took the exam on September 20, you can log in right now and see your score.

KMAT is the gateway to MBA, PGDM, and MCA seats in 228 colleges across Karnataka — 159 MBA institutes and 69 MCA institutes under the KPPGCA network accept the score. That is a big pool, and your scorecard decides where you stand in it.

## Scorecard live on kmatindia.com: log in with application number and DOB

Only two details open the result window: your application number (or roll number) and your date of birth. The scorecard downloads as a PDF, so keep a saved copy and take a printout. You will need it during admissions.

Here is the exact sequence:

- Go to the <a href="https://kmatindia.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KMAT website</a> and click on the KMAT 2026 Result link.
- Enter your application number or roll number in the first field.
- Enter your date of birth in the required format.
- Press Login. Your scorecard appears on the screen.
- Download the PDF and save a printout for admission use.

Hit a busy server? The site gets crowded during the day. Try early morning or late evening, and keep your credentials ready so the login takes seconds.

## What your KMAT scorecard actually shows

Open it and check every field before you do anything else. The scorecard carries your name and photograph, your application and roll numbers, the date and time of your exam, and then the part that matters: section-wise scores, the total score, your percentile, your rank, and your category.

Your KMAT paper had three sections — Verbal Ability, Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Ability. Each section score is printed separately, and the total decides your rank. Read the percentile line twice. It tells you how many candidates scored below you, and colleges use it to shortlist for the next rounds.

> Check your scorecard early in the day. Portals get crowded in the evening, and login errors are common when traffic spikes.

If something on the scorecard looks wrong — a misspelt name, a wrong date of birth — write to the exam authority through the contact on the official portal, using the same application number and password you registered with. Get it fixed now. A mismatch at the verification desk can delay your admission later.

## 228 institutes accept the score: what to do next

Now that the result is out, the next round begins. Institutes that accept KMAT shortlist candidates for Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) on the basis of the exam score. There is no single central counselling for KMAT — each college runs its own admission process, so you need to apply to the colleges you want individually.

Start with the colleges you are serious about. Check their websites for the application form, their cutoff from last year, and their GD-PI schedule. Most colleges announce these within days of the result. A strong score opens the door, but a good GD-PI performance often decides the final seat. For the group discussion, read up on current business and economy news — colleges pick topics that are in the headlines. For the personal interview, prepare a clear answer for "why MBA" and know your own profile well: your graduation subjects, your marks, and anything you did outside the classroom.

Compare colleges before you apply. Fees, placement records, and location matter as much as the brand name. A college that fits your budget and your target industry is worth more than a bigger name with weak placements.
