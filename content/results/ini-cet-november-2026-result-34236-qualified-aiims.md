**The INI CET November 2026 result is out. AIIMS New Delhi declared it on Saturday, November 7, and 34,236 candidates have qualified for postgraduate medical seats across the country.** If you sat for the exam on November 1, you can check your name in the merit list PDF on the official website, aiimsexams.ac.in. Your scorecard is available through the candidate login on the same portal.

This is one of the biggest postgraduate medical exams in India. The INI CET November 2026 result decides who can apply for MD, MS, DM (six years), MCh (six years) and MDS seats at the Institutes of National Importance. These include AIIMS campuses, JIPMER, NIMHANS, PGIMER and SCTIMST. Thousands of doctors waited six days for this list after the exam, and the wait ended today.

## INI CET November 2026 result: what the merit list shows

AIIMS has published the result as a merit list PDF, not as a simple pass or fail notice. Open the PDF and search for your roll number with Ctrl+F. The list shows your overall rank, your percentile score, your category and your qualifying status. Check all four details before you close the file. A wrong roll number or a missed detail now can cause trouble at the counselling stage.

Save a copy of the PDF on your phone and your laptop. You will need your rank and percentile when you fill choices during counselling, and the PDF is the proof of your result. Take a screenshot of your entry too, as a backup.

> AIIMS has released the INI CET November 2026 result as a merit list PDF on aiimsexams.ac.in. Search the list by roll number, then download your scorecard from the candidate login.

## How to check the INI CET November 2026 result

Follow these steps to check your result on the <a href="https://aiimsexams.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AIIMS examination website</a>:

- Go to aiimsexams.ac.in.
- Open the Results and Announcements section on the homepage.
- Click on the link for the INI CET November 2026 session result.
- The result PDF will open on your screen.
- Press Ctrl+F, type your roll number, and check your qualifying status.
- Download the PDF and keep it saved for counselling.

For your scorecard, log in to the candidate portal with your registration ID and password. The scorecard carries your marks and rank in full, so download it and keep a printed copy ready. If the site loads slowly, wait and try again instead of clicking on links shared on WhatsApp or Telegram. Fake result links spread fast on result day, and a wrong link can steal your login details.

## What comes next: counselling and seat allotment

Qualifying in the exam is the first step. The next step is counselling, and AIIMS will announce its schedule separately. There is no counselling date in the result notice, so do not trust dates shared on social media. Watch the official website for the registration window.

Once counselling opens, qualified candidates will have to register and fill in their preferred institutes and courses. Seat allotment will follow the counselling schedule and the admission rules of each institute. Your rank decides your turn in the choice-filling order, so keep your documents ready before the window opens. You will need your MBBS degree and mark sheets, internship completion certificate, registration certificate, category certificate if it applies, and a photo ID.

## A short recap of the INI CET November 2026 exam

AIIMS released the admit cards for this session on October 26, and the exam was held on November 1 at centres across India. The result has come out within a week, which is quick for an exam of this size. Candidates who qualified should now focus on counselling, while those who did not make the list can plan for the next session. The January session of INI CET is the next chance, and its schedule will be announced on the AIIMS exams website in due course.

Check your result today, save your scorecard, and keep an eye on aiimsexams.ac.in for the counselling dates. The merit list is only the start of the admission process.
