**The Chhattisgarh Employees Selection Board has released the [CG Vyapam Lecturer admit card 2026](/results/) for the LSL26 exam, and registered candidates can download their hall tickets now.** The written test for 854 lecturer posts is on Sunday, November 29, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 12:15 PM.

This is a state-level teaching recruitment, and the exam is less than a week away. If you applied, download the hall ticket today and check every detail on it. A wrong centre or a missing printout on exam morning is hard to fix at the gate.

## The key dates and numbers in one place

| Detail | What is confirmed |
| --- | --- |
| Conducting body | Chhattisgarh Employees Selection Board (CGSSB), also called CG Vyapam |
| Exam | Lecturer Recruitment Examination (LSL26) |
| Admit card released | November 23, 2026 |
| Exam date | November 29, 2026 (Sunday) |
| Exam timing | 10:00 AM to 12:15 PM |
| Total posts | 854 lecturer posts |
| Cadre split | E Cadre 424, T Cadre 430 |
| Exam mode | Offline, OMR-based |
| Exam centres | Five divisional headquarters in Chhattisgarh |
| Official website | cgssb.cgstate.gov.in |

The 854 posts are in the School Education Department, under the Directorate of Public Instruction, Chhattisgarh. The board released the hall tickets on November 23, which gives candidates six days between the release and the exam.

## How to download the admit card

The hall ticket is available only through the board's candidate portal. It is not sent by post or email.

1. Open the candidate portal at <a href="https://vyapamprofile.cgstate.gov.in/online/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">vyapamprofile.cgstate.gov.in/online/</a>.
2. Log in with your registered mobile number, password and the captcha shown on the screen.
3. Find the link for the Lecturer Written Recruitment Examination (LSL26).
4. Open the admit card and download all of its pages.
5. Take a clear printout and keep a digital copy as a backup.

If the portal is slow, try again after some time. A large number of candidates log in at once in the first hours after the release, and the site can take longer to respond.

## What the LSL26 exam looks like

The test is a single-shift written exam in offline OMR mode. Candidates will mark answers on an OMR sheet, so carry a blue or black ballpoint pen. The paper covers the subject the candidate applied for. The subjects include Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Physics, Political Science, History, Geography and Economics.

The exam runs at five divisional headquarters across Chhattisgarh. Your own centre, reporting time and roll number are printed on your admit card. Only the card tells you where you have to report, so do not go by another candidate's centre.

> Download the admit card early and read every line on it. The centre, the shift and the reporting time printed on your card are the final word, not what any website says.

## Check these details the moment you download it

Your name and photograph should match your application. The exam date should read November 29, 2026, and the timing should read 10:00 AM to 12:15 PM. Check the centre address and plan how you will reach it. If you spot a mistake in your name or photo, contact the board through its official website at <a href="https://cgssb.cgstate.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cgssb.cgstate.gov.in</a> instead of waiting until exam day.

This is one of several admit cards out this week. The [SSC CPO admit card 2026](/results/ssc-cpo-admit-card-2026-paper-1-out-nov-23-exam/) is also live for the Paper 1 exam, and the [CLAT 2027 admit card](/results/clat-2027-admit-card-out-nov-21-download-exam-dec-6/) is out for the December 6 law entrance test. Each exam has its own portal and its own download link, so use the link printed in your own notification.
