**The wait is finally over for thousands of nursing, paramedical, and medical students in Uttar Pradesh. Atal Bihari Vajpayee Medical University, Lucknow has declared the ABVMU result 2026 scorecards, and you can download yours right now from the university's official website.**

The release covers the Common Nursing Entrance Test (CNET), the Common Allied and Healthcare Entrance Test (CAHET), and semester examinations across nursing, paramedical, and medical courses. The declaration was reported on 23 October 2026, and students who appeared for these examinations should check their scorecards without delay. More declared scorecards are listed on our [results](/results/) page.

## Your scorecard is one login away

ABVMU has made the result available only through its official portal. Keep your enrollment number and password ready before you begin. Follow these steps:

- Open the <a href="https://abvmuup.edu.in/exam-result.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official ABVMU Examination Result page</a>.
- Click the "Click to View Result" button to reach the student result portal.
- Enter your enrollment number and password on the login page.
- Submit the details and wait for your scorecard to appear on the screen.
- Download the scorecard and save a copy for future reference.

If the login fails, type the credentials again slowly. A single wrong digit in the enrollment number is the most common reason students get locked out of the page.

> "Check your scorecard on the official website only. No third-party site can give you the real result, and sharing your login on one can put your personal details at risk."

## What your scorecard shows

Once you log in, the scorecard will display your name, roll number, enrollment number, course and college name, subject-wise marks, total marks, and the final result status. Read every line carefully. A spelling error in your name or a wrong mark today can turn into a long correction process later.

Spot a mistake? Report it to the university's examination department or the concerned college administration as soon as possible. Do not wait for the last date of any correction window. Also keep a digital copy and a printed copy of the scorecard with you, since you will need it for admissions, registrations, and any verification that follows.

Candidates who cleared CNET or CAHET should keep watching the university's counselling schedule. The entrance score is only the first gate; seat allotment and college reporting follow the counselling rounds, and each round carries its own deadlines. Missing a reporting date can cost you the seat even with a good rank. Check the counselling section of the university website daily until your admission is complete.

This is a busy week for medical education news. One more word of caution. When a big result drops, fake websites and social media messages start circulating with "direct links" that ask for your login details. ABVMU's result lives on its own official domain, reached through the link on <a href="https://abvmuup.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ABVMU's official website</a>. If a message or site asks you to pay to view your result, it is a scam.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I check my ABVMU result 2026?**
On the official ABVMU Examination Result page at abvmuup.edu.in/exam-result.php. The "Click to View Result" button takes you to the student result portal, where you log in with your enrollment number and password.

**Which results has ABVMU declared in October 2026?**
Scorecards for the Common Nursing Entrance Test (CNET), the Common Allied and Healthcare Entrance Test (CAHET), and various semester examinations across nursing, paramedical, and medical courses.

**What details will I see on my ABVMU scorecard?**
Your name, roll number, enrollment number, course and college name, subject-wise marks, total marks, and result status. Check every detail for errors and report any mistake to the university's examination department.

**What should I do if I cannot log in to the result portal?**
Re-check your enrollment number and password for typing errors. If the problem continues, contact the university through the official channels on its website and never share your login details on any third-party site.
