**Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) has declared its 2026 annual and semester examination results.** Students of MBBS, BDS, BHMS, B.Pharm, BSc Nursing and other medical and paramedical courses can now check their marks on the university's Examination Management System (EMS) portal using just their registration number.

The results cover the exams held in June-July 2026. Both sources that reported the declaration this morning confirm the results are live on <a href="https://rguhs.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official RGUHS portal</a>, though the site has been slow at times because of heavy traffic from students checking their marks at once.

## How to check your RGUHS result 2026

The process is simple. You only need your registration number.

- Open the RGUHS EMS result portal at <a href="https://rguhs.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rguhs.karnataka.gov.in</a>.
- Select your course and the relevant examination from the list.
- Enter your registration number.
- Click View or View Result.
- Your subject-wise marks and pass status will appear. Download and save the digital marksheet.

Jagran Josh notes that an alternate link is also available through the Gnanasangama Karnataka portal if the main site does not load. If the page keeps failing, close your browser, check your internet connection, clear your cache, and try again in a little while. Thousands of students are hitting the portal at the same time, so patience is part of the process today.

## Courses covered this time

The declared results span undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma programmes:

- **UG:** MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BUMS, BHMS, BSc Nursing (Basic), Post Basic BSc Nursing, BPharm
- **PG:** MD, MS, PG Diplomas, MPharm

As with other [university result declarations this month](/results/), the digital marksheet is for reference only. Collect the original marks card from your college as per its notice. Before you do anything else, check your name, registration number and subject marks line by line. If anything looks wrong, take it up with your college or the university examination section right away, since correction windows can be short.

> RGUHS does not allow revaluation of EMS results. If you are unhappy with your marks, you can apply for re-totalling and a photocopy of your answer sheets, as per the university's rules.

Students who have passed can move on to the next semester or year, or complete their programme formalities as their college directs. For those who could not clear a paper, the university is expected to release separate notifications about supplementary examinations and the re-totalling process after this announcement.

## About RGUHS

Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and was set up in 1996. It is recognised by the University Grants Commission and is among the largest health-science universities in India, covering modern medicine, homoeopathy, Ayurveda, Siddha, Yoga, naturopathy, Unani and allied health courses.
