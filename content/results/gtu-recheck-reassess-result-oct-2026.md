**Good news for GTU students who had applied for re-checking or re-assessment.** Gujarat Technological University has released the re-check/re-assess results for the May 2026 exams, and the update went live on **3 October 2026**. Four exams are covered this time: DIPL Semester 3 (Remedial), Pharm.D Year 1 (Regular), Pharm.D Year 1 (Remedial) and Pharm.D Year 2 (Regular). If you were waiting on one of these, you can check your updated scorecard right now on the official portal.

## Which GTU re-check/re-assess results were released on 3 October 2026

The [GTU result](/results/) list for this update covers four exams from the May 2026 session, all under the re-check/re-assess category:

- **DIPL Sem 3 – Remedial (May 2026)**
- **Pharm.D Year 1 – Regular (May 2026)**
- **Pharm.D Year 1 – Remedial (May 2026)**
- **Pharm.D Year 2 – Regular (May 2026)**

Make sure you open the entry that matches your exact course, year and exam type. Regular and remedial entries are listed separately, and picking the wrong one will not show your result.

## What a re-check/re-assess result actually means

A re-check/re-assess result is an **updated** result, not a fresh one. After the original result came out, some students applied to have their answer scripts rechecked or reassessed. GTU has now finished that process and published the revised outcomes.

For you, there are two possibilities. Either the new result confirms your original marks, or it shows a revised score after re-assessment. Either way, this updated result is the one that counts.

## How to check your GTU re-check/re-assess result online

1. Go to the official GTU result portal: <a href="https://gturesults.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gturesults.in</a>.
2. Find your exact exam in the list, for example "DIPL SEM 3 – Remedial (MAY 2026)" or "Pharm.D Year 1 – Regular (MAY 2026)".
3. Enter your enrollment number, exactly as printed on your admit card or fee receipt.
4. Fill in any other detail the page asks for, such as the security code shown on screen.
5. Submit the form and view your updated result on screen.
6. Download or print the result page and keep a copy with you.

If you have never done this before, this guide on [how to check your university result online](/results/how-to-check-university-result-online/) walks you through the same kind of process step by step.

> "Enter search criteria and hit Search. This is a computer generated provisional result, please consider the hard-copy gradesheet as final result." (GTU result portal notice)

## Three things to remember before you check

**The online result is provisional.** GTU states this clearly on the portal. The computer-generated page you see on screen is not your final marksheet. Collect your official hard-copy gradesheet through your institute when it is issued.

**Result queries have a 15-day window.** Institutes can raise result-related queries with GTU only within 15 days of the result declaration date. If something on your scorecard looks wrong, speak to your institute office quickly instead of waiting.

**Use only official GTU websites.** Stick to gturesults.in and the official <a href="https://www.gtu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GTU website</a> for checking results. Random blogs and social media posts may carry wrong dates or links.

GTU has been declaring semester results for the May 2026 cycle on a rolling basis, and more re-check/re-assess updates for other courses may follow. Keep an eye on the portal if your course is not in this round yet.
