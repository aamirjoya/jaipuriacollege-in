**Dr NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada, released the provisional list of successful candidates for the PG Medical Degree and Diploma examinations held in September 2026 on Monday, November 23. A total of 2,603 candidates passed the degree exams across 24 specialities, and five more qualified in the diploma courses. The [results](https://jaipuriacollege.in/results/) are live on the university portal, and students who want their answer scripts retotalled must apply by December 3.**

## General medicine tops the pass list

General Medicine recorded the highest number of successful candidates at 343. General Surgery followed with 313, and Obstetrics and Gynaecology with 278. Anaesthesiology had 226 passes, Paediatrics 200, Orthopaedics 189, and Radio-diagnosis 175.

## How to check your result

Open the official results portal at <a href="http://results.uhsap.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">results.uhsap.in</a>. Find the PG Medical Degree and Diploma September 2026 result link, then enter your hall ticket number to see your result. Keep the hall ticket number from your admit card ready before you open the portal, since the result page asks for it.

If the page loads slowly, try again after a few minutes. Result portals often get heavy traffic in the first hours after a declaration.

## Retotalling costs Rs 2,000 and closes on December 3

Students who want their answer scripts retotalled can apply online at <a href="https://drntr.uhsap.in/epay/retotal" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the university's retotalling portal</a> by paying a fee of Rs 2,000. The last date to apply is December 3, 2026. The notification was issued by Controller of Examinations Dr Ch. Sreekanth.

A university-appointed committee carries out the retotalling, and students do not need to appear in person for it.

## Retotalling is not revaluation

Many students mix the two up, and the difference matters here. The university has said there is no provision for revaluation of digitally evaluated answer scripts, in line with Supreme Court orders. Retotalling only checks whether the marks were added up correctly. It does not mean a fresh evaluation of the answers.

So if your total looks wrong, apply for retotalling before December 3. If you are unhappy with the marks themselves, revaluation is not an option for this exam.
