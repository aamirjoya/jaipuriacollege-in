**The Kerala NEET UG special stray vacancy allotment result is out, and the clock is ticking. Allotted students must join their medical or dental college between November 9 and 12, and anyone who misses the 2 PM deadline on November 12 will lose the seat.**

The Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE) Kerala published the final allotment for the special stray vacancy round of Kerala NEET UG counselling 2026. The round covers MBBS and BDS seats in government and self-financing medical and dental colleges across the state. Students who registered options for this round can now check whether they have got a seat.

This round moved on a tight schedule. Option entry ran from November 3 to 6. CEE Kerala released the provisional allotment on November 7 and the final allotment on November 8. Reporting starts today, November 9. That leaves allotted students just four days to pay the fee and finish admission at the college.

## What the allotment memo shows

Your allotment memo is the document that matters now. It carries your name and NEET UG roll number, the course and college allotted to you, the category of allotment, and the fee you must pay. The same details also appear on your candidate home page on the CEE Kerala portal.

You can also download a data sheet from the home page of the candidate portal. It holds your basic details in one place. Take a printout of the allotment memo and carry it with you when you report. The college will ask for it at the time of admission.

## How to check the Kerala NEET UG special stray allotment result

Checking the result takes only a few minutes. Keep your application number and password ready before you start.

- Go to the official portal, <a href="https://cee.kerala.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CEE Kerala official website</a>.
- Open the KEAM 2026 section on the home page.
- Log in to the candidate portal with your application number and password.
- Open your allotment memo and download it for your records.
- Also download the data sheet from the candidate home page.

If the portal loads slowly, wait and try again. Result days bring heavy traffic, and the site often takes a few tries. Do not trust allotment lists shared on social media or messaging groups. The candidate portal is the only place that shows your own allotment.

## Join by November 12, 2 PM, or lose the seat

The joining window runs from November 9 to 12, 2026. You must pay the fee shown in your allotment memo to the allotted college and complete the admission formalities within this period. The cut-off is sharp: candidates must join before 2 PM on November 12.

Students who do not join within the given time will lose their allotment. The registration fee paid for this round will not be refunded in that case. So if you have a seat, do not wait for the last day. Fee payment and document checks can take longer than expected, and a delay on November 12 could cost you the seat.

> "A seat in a special stray round is hard to get, because it comes from the last few vacancies of the season. Students who have one should treat the November 12 deadline as final and report early."

The [Maharashtra NEET UG special stray round result](/admissions/maharashtra-neet-ug-special-stray-round-1-result-nov-8-2026/) came out on the same weekend, and the [Assam NEET UG special stray round result](/results/assam-neet-ug-special-stray-round-result-nov-8-2026/) was also released on November 8. States are closing their final rounds one after another, so this week is the last stretch of MBBS and BDS admissions for 2026 in several states.

## What happens to seats that stay vacant

Some seats may still be left after this round. CEE Kerala has said those vacancies will be filled at once through a second phase of special stray vacancy filling. The options students registered in this phase will be used for that filling, so there is no fresh option entry mentioned for the second phase.

That is good news for students who did not get a seat this time. Their registered options still count. But it also means the second phase will move fast, and candidates should keep checking the CEE Kerala website for the next notice instead of assuming the process is over.

## Why this round matters for Kerala's medical aspirants

The special stray vacancy round is the last stage of Kerala NEET UG counselling for 2026. It picks up the seats that stayed empty after the regular rounds and the first stray vacancy round. For many students, it is the final chance to get an MBBS or BDS seat in Kerala this year without waiting for the next academic session.

The [results](/results/) section will carry updates on the second phase of special stray vacancy filling as soon as CEE Kerala announces it. Allotted students should finish their admission first, and the rest should keep their login details ready and watch the official portal.
