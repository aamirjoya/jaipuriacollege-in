**The [UP Special TET result](/results/) is out. The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) declared it late on Friday night, 20 November, only 17 days after the exam. Out of 93,681 in-service teachers who took the test, 38,902 have qualified. If you appeared for it, check your scorecard on the official UPESSC portal now. Qualified teachers can also download their eligibility certificate with their roll number and date of birth.**

## Only 3 in 10 primary teachers cleared the test

The primary level result is the big talking point. Just 15,159 of the 50,666 teachers who took the primary level paper passed. That is a pass rate of 29.92%. In plain words, 7 out of 10 primary teachers did not make it.

The upper primary level went much better. There, 23,743 of 43,015 teachers qualified, a pass rate of 55.20%. More than half the candidates cleared this paper.

Put together, 38,902 of 93,681 teachers passed. The overall pass rate is 41.53%. The gap between the two levels is wide, and it is the first thing most teachers will notice when they open the result.

> Only 3 in 10 primary teachers passed the test. At the upper primary level, more than half made it through.

## Why this exam was held for working teachers

This was not a fresh recruitment exam. The Special TET was held for teachers who already work in the Basic Education Department of Uttar Pradesh. The commission ran it under Advertisement No. 03/2026.

The test follows a Supreme Court direction that serving teachers must clear a teacher eligibility test. The exam took place on 3 November 2026 in two shifts. It was held at six divisional headquarters across the state: Prayagraj, Lucknow, Varanasi, Gorakhpur, Meerut and Agra.

The paper had 150 multiple-choice questions for 150 marks. There was no negative marking, so every attempt counted. To pass, general category candidates needed 90 marks out of 150, which is 60%. Reserved category candidates needed 82 marks, or 55%.

Other states run their own eligibility tests for working teachers. Tamil Nadu, for one, declared its [TET result for Paper 1 and Paper 2](/results/tn-tet-result-2026-out-paper-1-paper-2-scorecard/) in October.

## How to check your result and download the certificate

Keep your roll number and date of birth ready before you start. The scorecard and the certificate both open with these two details.

- Open the <a href="https://upessc.up.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UPESSC portal</a>.
- Find the link for the Special TET Result 2026 on the home page.
- Enter your 10-digit roll number and your date of birth.
- Fill in the captcha code shown on the screen and submit.
- Your result and scorecard will appear. Check your marks and your qualifying status.
- Download the scorecard PDF and save a printout.

Qualified teachers should download the eligibility certificate too. It is available on the same portal. You can also reach it through the <a href="https://www.upessc.net/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">candidate login portal</a>. The certificate shows your name, roll number, date of birth, the level you appeared for, your result status, and the certificate number with its date of issue. The commission has said the certificate will also come to DigiLocker soon. It stays valid for life, so keep a safe copy. The certificate is the proof that you meet the eligibility rule for your teaching post. Your school or the department can ask to see it during service checks, so keep both a printout and a digital copy where you can find them fast.

## What to do if you did not qualify

A fail in this test is not the end of the road. The result shows your marks, so check where you fell short. Teachers who missed the cut-off by a few marks should look at the paper again and note the weak areas. Also check the small details on your scorecard, like the spelling of your name and your roll number, and keep a copy saved.

UPESSC has not announced the date of the next Special TET yet. Keep checking the official portal for the next notification. Do not trust dates shared on social media or messaging groups. The portal is the only place where the next schedule will appear first.

One more thing: do not pay anyone who promises to change your result or issue a certificate. The commission issues certificates only through its own portal, and no agent can speed that up.
