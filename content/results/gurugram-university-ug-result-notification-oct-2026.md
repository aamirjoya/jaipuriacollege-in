**Gurugram University has put up two new result notifications for its undergraduate examinations.** Result Notification 10 and Result Notification 9, both dated October 1, 2026, are now live as PDF files on the university's official website. Students who sat in these UG examinations can open the notice that matches their exam session and look for their programme and semester inside it.

The university posts result notices in a running series, and the last one before this was Notification 8, which came on September 28, 2026. Two more notices arriving within three days tells you the examination branch is clearing results quickly this session. If your exams were held in December 2025 or May 2026, this [Gurugram University result](/results/) update is the one to read closely.

## What the two October 1 notifications cover

According to the notices board on the university's site, Result Notification 10 is for UG examinations held in December 2025 and May 2026. Result Notification 9 is listed for UG examinations held in December 2025 and May 2025. Both entries carry the date 2026-10-01, and both open as downloadable PDFs.

A word of caution here. The session labels on the two notices are not identical, and it is easy to open the wrong file and read someone else's session by mistake. Always confirm the exam session printed inside the PDF before you look for your own entry. The notice is the record that counts, not a forwarded message in a student group.

Separate listings of the university's results page also show UG semester 7 and 8 results and a PG diploma clinical art second-semester result dated October 1, 2026. If your course is one of these, check the same Result Notices page rather than waiting for your college to circulate the PDF.

## How to find and read your result

The process is simple, and it takes a few minutes. Follow these steps.

1. Go to the <a href="https://gurugramuniversity.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Gurugram University website</a> and open the Notices or Result Notices section.
2. Look for "Result notification 10 for UG Examinations held in Dec-2025 & May 2026" and "Result notification 9 for UG Examinations held in Dec-2025 & May 2025", both dated 2026-10-01.
3. Click the PDF link to open the notification for your exam session.
4. Inside the document, find your programme and semester, then look for your own entry with the details the PDF lists.
5. Download or save the PDF. Keep a copy until your college hands you the original marksheet.

> Do not trust forwarded screenshots in student groups. Open the notification PDF on the official website, match your programme, semester and exam session, and only then read your result.

Students can also check detailed marks on the university's <a href="https://gurugramuniversity.samarth.edu.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Samarth result portal</a>, which is where Gurugram University publishes individual scores for its courses. If the portal asks for a login, keep your registration or roll number ready.

## What to do if something looks wrong

If your name is missing from the PDF, or a mark does not match what you expected, do not panic. The first step is to confirm you opened the right notification and the right session inside it. A December 2025 entry read inside a May 2026 file will only confuse you.

Next, compare what you see with the Samarth portal. If the two do not match, contact your college examination cell right away and ask how the university handles result corrections. The university also publishes separate re-evaluation notifications on the same notices page, so keep an eye on that list if you plan to ask for a recheck. The online result is for information. The original marksheet from your college is the final document.
