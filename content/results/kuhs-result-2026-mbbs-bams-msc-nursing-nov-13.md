**Kerala University of Health Sciences has declared six results in two days, and the biggest one is the Second Professional MBBS result.** Students who sat for the September 2026 MBBS exams can now check their marks on the official KUHS results page. The university also released the First Professional BAMS supplementary result, the Second Year MSc Nursing result and the BUMS rank list.

KUHS, based in Thrissur, runs exams for affiliated medical, dental, nursing, pharmacy, AYUSH and allied health colleges across Kerala. The university publishes results course by course, so each result has its own notice and its own link.

## BAMS supplementary result leads the November 13 list

The newest notice went up on Friday, November 13. It covers the First Professional BAMS Degree supplementary examinations held in September 2026, under the 2012, 2016 and 2021 schemes. Students from Ayurveda colleges affiliated to KUHS can check this result now.

The same day, the university also published a revised result for two students of the Third Professional BHMS Degree regular examinations held in July 2026. Revised results like this one apply only to the students named in the notice.

## MBBS and MSc Nursing results came out on November 12

Thursday, November 12, brought four notices. The main one is the Second Professional MBBS Degree result for the regular and supplementary examinations held in September 2026. It covers students under the 2010, 2019 and 2024 schemes, so check the notice that matches your scheme.

The same day also saw the Second Year MSc Nursing Degree result for the regular and supplementary examinations held in October 2026. Nursing students can check it the same way, with their register number.

Two more notices went up on November 12. One is the fresh evaluation result of the Seventh Semester BSc Nursing Degree supplementary examinations held in May 2026. The other is the rank list for the Third (Final) Professional BUMS Degree regular examinations of July 2026, for the 2022 scheme and 2021 admission batch.

Students waiting on other courses should keep checking the page. Earlier in November, the university released fresh evaluation results for BSc Nursing and BDS papers, a fresh valuation result for Second Professional BAMS, and the Third Year B Pharm supplementary result.

## How to check your KUHS result 2026

The result is a PDF marksheet on the university website. Follow these steps:

- Go to the <a href="https://kuhs.ac.in/kuhs2k06/index.php?view=article&id=6623" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KUHS results page</a>.
- Find your course in the list of latest results.
- Open the notice for your exam and scheme.
- Enter your register number. Some scorecards also ask for your date of birth.
- Download the PDF and save a copy on your phone.

> Download the result the same day you check it. The university updates its results page often, and the link for your course can move down the list fast.

The marksheet shows your name, register number, course, subject-wise marks, total marks and result status. Read every line before you close the file. If your name is spelt wrong or a subject mark looks off, tell your college office first. The college can guide you on the correction process. You can also reach the KUHS helpdesk at 0487 2207650 or 0487 2206770, or write to helpdesk@kuhs.ac.in.

## What the provisional result means for you

The copy you download is provisional. Your college will issue the signed marksheet later, and that is the document that counts for official work. So treat the online result as a quick check, not the final paper.Students who cleared all papers can move ahead with the next semester without waiting for the printed marksheet. Those who did not clear a paper should watch for the supplementary exam notice. KUHS usually opens supplementary exams within a few weeks of the result, and the notice will carry the fee and last date. Check the results page yourself, because dates for supplementary exams are easy to miss.
