**Karnataka State Open University (KSOU) has declared three provisional postgraduate results on 23 October 2026.** Students who took the May/June 2026 examinations can now check their marksheets on the university's official result portal. The three results cover first semester MA English, second semester MBA (OS), and first and second year MSc Psychology.

If you are waiting on one of these results, this is the update you need. Read the list below carefully, because KSOU has released several batches of results this month, and each one covers a different set of students.

## The 3 results declared on 23 October 2026

The October 23 batch has one re-valuation result and two regular provisional results. Here is exactly what was released:

- **MA English, first semester:** Provisional re-valuation result for students of the 2025-26 July Cycle and repeaters.
- **MBA (OS), second semester:** Provisional result for repeater students.
- **MSc Psychology, first and second year:** Provisional result for repeater students.

All three belong to the May/June 2026 examination session. The MA English result is the one to watch if you had applied for re-valuation, since it carries the revised marks. The other two are for students who were repeating the semester or year.

> Check only the official result page, and match your programme, semester and category before you open any result link. Picking the wrong entry is the most common reason students think their result is missing.

## How to check your KSOU PG result 2026

The result is available online only. There is no offline list to collect from a study centre for this batch, so keep your registration number ready and follow these steps:

1. Open the official KSOU result page on the <a href="https://ksouportal.com/results/ksouresult.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">KSOU result portal</a>.
2. Look for your programme in the list of announced results.
3. Match the semester or year and your category, such as repeater or re-valuation, before you open the entry.
4. Follow the instructions on the page and enter the details it asks for.
5. Download the marksheet and save a copy. Take a printout too, so you have one even if the portal slows down later.

One detail matters here. The October 23 results are provisional, not final documents. That is normal for KSOU's online releases. Use the online marksheet to plan your next semester or to apply for further study, and collect the final documents from the university when they are issued.

## What to do if something looks wrong in your marksheet

Start by checking the basics: your name, registration number, programme, semester, and the marks in each subject. If a subject shows a wrong entry or a missing mark, do not wait for it to fix itself. For internal assessment problems in the MBA (OS) and MSc Psychology results, the university directs students to the Registrar Evaluation.

Students who are unhappy with a re-valuation outcome should check the university's rules for the next step before the window closes. KSOU announces these windows separately, so watch the official website rather than relying on social media posts. You can also reach the university through its <a href="https://www.ksoumysuru.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official website</a> for notices and contact details.

## KSOU has been releasing May/June 2026 results in batches

The October 23 announcement is part of a longer run. KSOU has been putting out May/June 2026 results in batches through October, including MBA and MA Economics results between October 9 and 16. That pattern is useful to know if your own result is still pending: a missing result today does not mean it has been skipped, since later batches may still cover your programme.

KSOU is a Mysuru-based open university, recognised by the University Grants Commission, and it runs undergraduate, postgraduate, certificate, and diploma courses through open and distance learning. Its students are spread across Karnataka and beyond, which is why the online result portal is the main way most of them get their marks.

## What this result means for your next step

For MA English students, the re-valuation result settles the revised marks, so you can move ahead with the next semester or with applications that need the updated score. MBA (OS) repeaters who have cleared the second semester can plan for the next stage of the programme, and MSc Psychology repeaters in the first and second year now know where they stand.

Save the downloaded marksheet in two places, on your phone and on a computer or email. Result portals get heavy traffic in the first few days after a release, and a saved copy means you are not stuck refreshing the page when you need the marks for an application.
