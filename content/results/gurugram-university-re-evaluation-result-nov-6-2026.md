**Gurugram University has declared the re-evaluation result for examinations held in May-June 2026.** The result came out under Notification 2606-004, dated November 5, 2026, and published on the university website on November 6, 2026. Students who had applied for re-evaluation of their answer scripts can now see their revised marks.

The notification covers a wide set of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. BA, BCom, BSc, BBA and BTech students are all in the list, along with MA students under the NEP pattern. If you applied for re-evaluation after the May-June 2026 exams, this [Gurugram University re-evaluation result](/results/) is the notice to open first.

## Which courses and subjects are covered

The result is not limited to one or two courses. The notification lists BA(EC), BA(PAS), BCom, BSc, BBA, BA(E), BCom(Hons.), BTech(ME), BTech(CSEAIML), BTechCSE, BTech(CSEIOTCT) and MA(G) NEP, across different semesters. Each entry in the PDF shows the student name, exam roll number, university roll number, course, semester, subject, paper ID and the marks after re-evaluation.

Sixth-semester students form a large part of the list. BA(EC) sixth-semester results include Economics of Public Finance and Economics of Industry. BCom sixth-semester students can check Cost Accounting-II, Financial Management, and Goods and Services Tax and Customs Law. BA(PAS) sixth-semester entries cover History (Modern World), English and Hindi (Compulsory).

The technical courses are in there too. BTech(ME) sixth-semester students have Fluid Machines, BTech(CSEAIML) sixth-semester students have Statistical Computing, and BTechCSE fourth-semester students have Discrete Mathematics and Microprocessor and Microcontroller. BBA sixth-semester results include Income Tax, BCom(Hons.) sixth-semester results include Income Tax Law and Administration, and MA(G) NEP fourth-semester results include Industrial Geography.

Second-semester results are part of the same notification as well. BA entries cover History of India (c.1200 AD to 1707 AD), BCom entries cover Financial Accounting-II, Business Mathematics-II and Business Economics-II, and BSc entries cover subjects such as Electro-magnetic Induction and Zoology (Genetics). BSc fourth-semester results include Chemistry (Inorganic, Physical and Organic) and Physics.

## Four more result notices came on November 3

The re-evaluation result for May-June 2026 is not the only fresh notice on the university's board. On November 3, 2026, Gurugram University listed four more result notices on its official result page. Students from older exam sessions should check these too.

The November 3 list has a re-checking result for May-2025 examinations, a retotal result under Notification 261-007 for December 2025 and January 2026 examinations, and two re-evaluation results under Notifications 2601-23 and 2601-24 for the same December 2025 and January 2026 session. The two re-evaluation notices cover BTech, BSc, BSc BEd and MSc courses, among others.

This is a busy week for the university's examination branch. Regular result notifications for UG examinations were already coming through in October, and the re-evaluation and retotal lists are now following close behind. If you applied for a recheck in any of these sessions, check the notice that matches your exam session before you read anything else.

## How to check your re-evaluation result

The result is published as a PDF notification, not as a login-based scorecard. Follow these steps.

1. Go to the <a href="https://gurugramuniversity.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Gurugram University website</a> and open the Result Notices section.
2. Find "Re-Evaluation Result of Exams, Notification No. 2606-004", dated November 5, 2026, for May-June 2026 examinations.
3. Click the PDF link to open the notification.
4. Press Ctrl+F on a computer, or use the search option in your PDF viewer on a phone, and type your name or roll number.
5. Check your course, semester, subject and paper ID, then read the marks after re-evaluation.
6. Download and save the PDF. Keep a copy until your college gives you the updated marksheet.

Students who want detailed marks can also use the university's <a href="https://gurugramuniversity.samarth.edu.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Samarth result portal</a>, where individual scores are published for its courses. Keep your registration number or roll number ready if the portal asks for a login.

> Do not trust forwarded screenshots in student groups. Open the notification PDF on the official website, match your programme, semester and exam session, and only then read your result.

## What to do if your marks changed, or did not

If your marks went up after re-evaluation, the PDF entry is the record for now. Your college will issue the corrected marksheet, so ask your college examination cell when it will be ready. Keep the PDF saved, since you may need it for admission or scholarship forms in the meantime.

If your marks stayed the same, the re-checking found no change in that paper. Do not pay anyone who promises to change a result. The university publishes these lists itself, and no agent can alter a PDF on its website.

One warning helps here. The university has published several notices with similar names in the last few weeks, for different sessions and notification numbers. Notification 2606-004 is for May-June 2026. Notifications 2601-23 and 2601-24 are for December 2025 and January 2026. Opening the wrong file and reading the wrong session is the most common mistake students make with these PDFs.
