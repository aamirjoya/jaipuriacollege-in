**The Medical Counselling Committee will declare the NEET PG 2026 Round 1 seat allotment result tomorrow, October 24.** Candidates who registered for the round can check whether they have been allotted a seat on the official MCC website at mcc.nic.in. Those who get a seat will need to report to their allotted college between October 26 and November 2, 2026.

The result comes at the end of a packed counselling calendar. [NEET PG 2026 Round 1 registration](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-1-registration-oct-12/) opened on October 12 and closed on October 21, while choice filling ran from October 13 to October 22. MCC processed the allotments on October 22 and 23, and the outcome is now due on October 24.

## How to check the NEET PG 2026 Round 1 result

Keep your login details ready before the result goes live. Follow these steps:

- Go to the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC portal</a> at mcc.nic.in.
- Look for the NEET PG 2026 Round 1 seat allotment result link on the homepage.
- Enter your login credentials on the page that opens.
- Submit the details and check your allotment status.
- Download the allotment letter and take a printout for the reporting day.

Check the result only on mcc.nic.in. MCC does not authorise any other website to publish the allotment list, so be careful of lookalike sites and forwarded links on social media.

## Before you check: a few practical tips

MCC's result pages often slow down in the first hour as lakhs of candidates log in at once. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again instead of hitting refresh repeatedly. Have your roll number and password written down where you can see them, so you do not get locked out.

Once the result is out, compare your allotment against the [published seat matrix](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-counselling-seat-matrix/) and the choices you locked in. If the allotted college and course match what you expected, download the provisional allotment letter the same day. You will need it at the reporting desk, along with your original documents and photocopies.

## Got a seat? Report between October 26 and November 2

An allotment is only the first step. Candidates who receive a seat in Round 1 must complete the reporting and joining formalities at the allotted college or institute between October 26 and November 2, 2026. Missing this window means losing the seat, so plan your travel and documents now.

Institutes will verify the data of joined candidates and share it with MCC on November 3. Before you leave for reporting, go through the document list in MCC's information bulletin carefully and carry the originals plus photocopies. Name mismatches between your marksheets and ID proof are one of the most common reasons students are sent back from verification desks.

> "Do not wait for the last day to report. Colleges are strict about the reporting window, and a missed deadline cannot be undone."

| Choice filling and locking | October 13 to 22 |
| Seat allotment processing | October 22 and 23 |
| **Round 1 result** | **October 24** |

MCC has called this schedule tentative, so keep an eye on the portal for any changes. Students who filled their choices before the [choice filling deadline of October 22](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-1-choice-filling-oct-22-deadline/) are the ones in the running for this round.

## What comes after Round 1: Round 2 and stray vacancy

If you do not get a seat tomorrow, or you decide not to join, the counselling continues. Round 2 registration and payment will run from November 6 to 11, with choice filling from November 7 to 12 and the Round 2 result on November 14. Round 2 reporting is scheduled for November 16 to 23.

Round 3 will follow with its result on December 4, and the stray vacancy round result on December 24. Keep track of the fresh registration windows for each round, because you need to register again to stay in the process.

MCC's counselling covers 50% All India Quota seats for MD, MS, DNB and postgraduate diploma courses, plus seats in central and deemed universities and ESIC institutes. State quota seats follow the separate schedules of the [state counselling authorities](/admissions/).
