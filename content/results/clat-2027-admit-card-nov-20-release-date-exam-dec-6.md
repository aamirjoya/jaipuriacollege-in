**The wait is almost over for law aspirants. The Consortium of National Law Universities is expected to release the [CLAT 2027 admit card](/admissions/clat-2027-registration-last-date-october-31/) on November 20, 2026, just 16 days before the exam.** Candidates will be able to download their hall tickets from the official portal using their registered mobile number and password. The Common Law Admission Test will be held on December 6 in offline pen-and-paper mode, from 2 pm to 4 pm.

The admit card is the one document no candidate can enter the exam hall without. It carries the roll number, the exam centre address, and the test-day instructions. With the release date now reported by leading news outlets, aspirants should keep their login details ready and check the portal on November 20 instead of waiting till the last day.

## What the CLAT 2027 admit card will carry

The hall ticket will show the candidate's roll number, the name and address of the allotted exam centre, and the instructions for exam day. Students should read every line on it the moment they download it. A wrong centre address or a spelling error in the name can cause trouble on exam day, and such errors are best flagged early.

The Consortium has not sent physical copies of the admit card in past years, and the same applies this year. The download is online only. Candidates are advised to take at least two printouts. One copy goes to the exam centre on December 6, and the second is useful later during the NLU counselling process.

> "Download the admit card early, print two copies, and visit your exam centre a day before if you can. On exam day, the portal slows down and the queue outside the centre does not."

## How to download the CLAT 2027 admit card

Once the link goes live on November 20, follow these steps:

- Go to the official website of the Consortium of NLUs at <a href="https://consortiumofnlus.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">consortiumofnlus.ac.in</a>.
- Click on the CLAT 2027 portal link on the homepage.
- Log in with your registered mobile number and password.
- Open your candidate dashboard and click on the admit card download link.
- Save the PDF and take at least two printouts.

If the portal loads slowly on the first day, wait and try again after some time. The link stays active till the exam date, so there is no need to panic. Still, downloading early is the safer choice. A digital copy on a mobile phone will not be accepted at the gate, so a printed copy is a must.

## CLAT 2027 exam: key dates and pattern

CLAT 2027 will be held on Sunday, December 6, 2026, from 2 pm to 4 pm. The test runs for two hours and will be conducted at around 131 centres across India. The paper covers English language, current affairs and general knowledge, legal reasoning, logical reasoning, and quantitative techniques.

Last year, the Consortium released the CLAT 2026 admit card on November 22, about 15 days before the exam. This year's reported date of November 20 follows the same pattern. The exact release time has not been announced yet, so candidates should check the portal through the day.

Students who completed the [CLAT 2027 registration](/admissions/clat-2027-registration-last-date-october-31/) can download the admit card. Those who also applied for other law entrances, such as [AILET 2027](/admissions/ailet-2027-registration-closes-today-nov-10-nlu-delhi/) for NLU Delhi, should keep the two exam schedules separate and not mix up the admit cards.

## What to carry on exam day

Along with the printed admit card, candidates must carry a valid original photo ID, such as Aadhaar, PAN card, voter ID, or passport. The name on the ID should match the name on the admit card. Reach the centre well before the reporting time printed on the hall ticket, because late entry is not allowed.

Candidates should also check the centre location in advance. Many centres are in schools or colleges in unfamiliar parts of a city, and a trial visit a day before the exam removes one worry from the morning. Keep a pen, a transparent water bottle, and the admit card ready the night before.
