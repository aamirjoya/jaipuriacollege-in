**IBPS has declared the Specialist Officer prelims result 2026. Candidates who wrote the exam on August 29 can now check their qualifying status and download the scorecard from the official portal.**

The result came out on October 15, 2026, under the CRP SPL-XVI recruitment cycle. This drive plans to fill 1,043 Specialist Officer posts in public sector banks. The scorecard download link stays live only till October 22, 2026, so candidates should not leave it for the last day.

## 1,043 posts across six specialist streams

IBPS hires Specialist Officers for banks that need professionals in specific fields, not generalist clerks. This cycle covers six streams: IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Law Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer. Each stream carries its own eligibility conditions, and the mains paper later will also differ from stream to stream. Posts are spread across participating public sector banks, and the official CRP SPL-XVI notification lists the post-wise eligibility and vacancy break-up for each one.

Clearing the prelims only decides who gets to write the mains on November 1. It does not by itself guarantee a job, so treat it as a gate and not the finish line.

The prelims was held on August 29, 2026, at exam centres across the country. As always, it worked only as a screening test: its marks decide who moves ahead, while the final selection rests on the mains and the interview. Candidates who missed the cut-off should not lose heart. The scorecard still shows section-wise marks, and those numbers point to exactly which sections need more work before the next recruitment cycle.

## How to download the scorecard before October 22

Follow these steps on the official site. The link closes on October 22, 2026.

1. Open <a href="https://ibps.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ibps.in</a> and look for the CRP Specialist Officers section on the homepage.
2. Click the link titled "Result Status of Online Preliminary Examination for CRP-SPL-XVI".
3. A login window opens. Enter your registration number or roll number, and your password or date of birth.
4. Type the captcha code exactly as shown on the screen and submit.
5. Your result appears. Download the scorecard and take a printout for your records. A screenshot also helps as a backup, since the portal slows down near the deadline when everyone rushes in.

Keep a printed copy safe. You will need the scorecard and your registration details again at the mains and interview stages.

## Check these details on your scorecard

The scorecard shows your name, roll number and category, along with your overall and section-wise marks and the exam date. Read every field once before you close the tab. If your name is misspelled or a number looks off, contact IBPS through the contact details given on its website. Small errors now can cause trouble later during document verification.

> "Qualifying in the prelims only decides who moves ahead. The real contest is the mains, and candidates should begin preparing for it right away."

## What comes next: mains on November 1, then interview

Candidates who cleared the prelims will sit for the IBPS SO mains on November 1, 2026. The mains paper pattern changes from post to post, so read the official CRP SPL-XVI notification for the syllabus that applies to your stream. Do not prepare from a friend's stream by mistake.

After the mains comes the interview round. The final merit list is built on mains and interview scores, and successful candidates get provisional allotment in participating banks, subject to document checks.

IBPS has not announced the mains admit card date yet. Keep checking the IBPS website for the admit card, reporting time and exam-day instructions as November 1 gets closer.

## Keep these ready for the mains stage

- A printed copy of your prelims scorecard, downloaded before October 22.
- Your registration number, roll number and login password, kept somewhere safe.
- The official CRP SPL-XVI notification PDF for your stream, so you know the exact mains syllabus and pattern.
- A habit of checking <a href="https://ibps.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the IBPS website</a> for the mains admit card and reporting instructions.
