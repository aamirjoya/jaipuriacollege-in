**The Bihar Staff Selection Commission has released the [BSSC Stenographer admit card 2026](/results/) for 531 Stenographer and Steno Typist Grade-III posts. The hall tickets went live on 27 November, and the computer-based test is on 1 December 2026 in two shifts.** Candidates who applied under Advertisement No. 07/25 can download the admit card from the official BSSC portal.

The exam runs at centres across Bihar. You need your registration number and date of birth to log in and get the hall ticket. Keep both ready before you open the site, because the download window is short. The test is three days away.

## Key dates for the BSSC Stenographer exam

| What happens | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Exam city slip released | 24 November 2026 |
| Admit card released | 27 November 2026 |
| Written exam (CBT) | 1 December 2026, two shifts |
| Posts on offer | 531, Stenographer and Steno Typist Grade-III |

The commission issued the exam notice (Memo No. 5178) on 23 November. The city slip came first, on 24 November, so candidates already knew their exam city before the admit card link went live.

## How to download the BSSC Stenographer admit card

Open the official BSSC website at <a href="https://bssc.bihar.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">bssc.bihar.gov.in</a> and go to the Notice Board on the homepage. Look for the link that says "Click Here to Download Admit Card for Adv. No. 07/25, Post-Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III". Enter your registration number and date of birth in the login window and submit the details. Your hall ticket will appear on the screen. Download it and take a clear printout for exam day.

Do not wait until the night before the exam. The portal gets heavy traffic close to the test date, and a slow login at the last minute is the last thing you need. Download the card now and check every detail on it while there is still time to get a mistake fixed.

## What to check on your hall ticket

Your admit card carries your name, roll number, registration number, father's name, date of birth, photograph and signature. It also shows your category, the exam name and post, the exam date and shift timing, reporting time, and the full name and address of your exam centre. Read the exam-day instructions printed on it as well.

If any detail looks wrong, report it to the commission at the earliest. A spelling error in your name or a wrong date of birth can cause trouble at the centre gate, and the board is the only body that can correct it. Do not try to edit the PDF yourself.

> Candidates must carry a printed copy of their admit card to the examination centre. Entry will not be allowed without a valid hall ticket.

That rule is stated plainly in the commission's notice. A soft copy on your phone will not work as a substitute, so print the card and keep it with a valid photo ID. Candidates appearing for other exams this week faced the same rule. The [Rajasthan CET admit card](/results/rajasthan-cet-12th-level-admit-card-2026-out-nov-26/) came out on 26 November, and the [SSC CPO admit card](/results/ssc-cpo-admit-card-2026-paper-1-out-nov-23-exam/) was released for the Paper 1 exam that ran from 23 November.

## What the exam looks like on December 1

The written test is a computer-based test, and it runs in two shifts on the same day. Your shift and reporting time are printed on the admit card, so check them before you plan your travel. Reach the centre early. The reporting time on the card is the time you must be there, not the time the paper starts, and late entry is usually not allowed.

This is a state-level recruitment, and the 531 posts cover both Stenographer and Steno Typist Grade-III roles. The admit card is only the first step. Candidates who clear the written test move to the next stages of the selection process, which the commission will announce after the result.
