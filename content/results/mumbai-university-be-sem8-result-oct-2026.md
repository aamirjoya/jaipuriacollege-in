**Mumbai University has declared the Semester 8 results for seven B.E. branches. EXTC and Electrical Engineering results went live on 5 October 2026, while five more branches were released on 3 October.** Final-year students can now check their marks on the university's official result portal and download a copy for their records.

This is the result that matters most for a B.E. student. Semester 8 closes your degree, decides your final CGPA, and feeds into placement records and higher-studies applications. If you are in the final year, check it today and save a copy before the rush begins.

## EXTC and Electrical results released on 5 October

The two newest results on the [Mumbai University results](/results/) portal are for B.E. Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (Sem VIII, program code 1T01028) and B.E. Electrical Engineering (Sem VIII, program code 1T00828). Both are dated 5 October 2026 and follow the CBCGS to C scheme.

Match the program code before you open your result. The portal lists several schemes side by side, and opening the wrong one can give you the wrong PDF. Keep your exam seat number handy, since that is the detail the portal uses to identify you.

## Five more branches were declared on 3 October

Two days earlier, the university released Semester 8 results for five more branches: B.E. Data Engineering (1T01858), B.E. Electronics Engineering (1T01138), B.E. Electronics and Computer Science (1T00938), B.E. Chemical Engineering (1T00538), and B.E. Bio-Medical Engineering (1T00338). All five carry the 3 October 2026 date.

> If your branch is in this list, your Semester 8 result is already waiting on the portal. This is the final result of your B.E., and the marksheet that follows is what you will submit for jobs and admissions.

## How to check your MU B.E Sem 8 result

Follow these steps on the <a href="https://www.mumresults.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official result portal</a>:

- Open the result portal and look for the online results list.
- Find your branch by its full name and program code, and confirm the semester and scheme.
- Open the result link for your course.
- Enter your seat number or the other details the page asks for.
- Submit and view your result on the screen.
- Download or print the PDF and keep it with your other marksheets.

New to this? Our guide on [how to check your university result online](/results/how-to-check-university-result-online/) walks through the process step by step, including what to do when a portal is slow on result day.

## After you check: three things worth doing

First, save a clean copy of the result PDF. Phones get lost and college emails get changed, but a backup copy on a drive or in cloud storage stays. You will need this result for placement documentation, transcript applications, and anything your college asks for during the final-year exit process.

Second, read every line of your result carefully. Name, seat number, subject codes, and marks should all match what you expect. Small errors are easier to fix when you report them early, so contact your college office or the university exam section if something looks wrong. The university's revaluation and answer-book verification rules are published on the <a href="https://mu.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Mumbai University website</a>, so check that notice rather than a forwarded message.

Third, if you are an M.Ed student, recheck your Semester II result. It was first listed on 3 October and updated on 5 October, so the entry you saw earlier may have changed.

## What the result day pattern tells you

Mumbai University releases results in batches rather than all at once. Earlier in the month, it declared the [M.Com and MA Semester 4 results on 2 October](/results/mumbai-university-mcom-ma-sem4-result-oct-2026/), and branch after branch of Semester 8 has followed. If your branch is not on the portal yet, keep checking the official portal instead of relying on rumours. New entries appear there first, and the dates beside each entry tell you exactly when a result was declared or updated.
