**The Staff Selection Commission has released the [SSC GD PET/PST admit card 2026](/results/) for the physical stage of the Constable (GD) exam.** The call letters went live on November 12 on the official website, <a href="https://ssc.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ssc.gov.in</a>. If you cleared the written exam, you can download your admit card now and check your test date, time and venue.

A total of **1,97,408 candidates** have been shortlisted for this stage. They are competing for **25,487 posts** of Constable (General Duty) in the Central Armed Police Forces and the Secretariat Security Force, and Rifleman (General Duty) in Assam Rifles. The physical tests run from **November 26 to December 11, 2026**, and the Central Reserve Police Force will run them along with the other forces.

## Download your SSC GD PET/PST admit card in 7 steps

Keep your registration number and password ready before you start.

- Go to the official SSC website at <a href="https://ssc.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ssc.gov.in</a>, or open your regional SSC website.
- Look for the "Admit Card" or "Latest News" section on the homepage.
- Click the link for the SSC GD Constable PET/PST Admit Card 2026.
- Pick your region from the list shown.
- Enter your registration number or roll number, along with your date of birth or password.
- Type the captcha code on the screen and click on submit.
- Check every detail on the admit card, then download it as a PDF and take a clear printout.

If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" option to reset it. The admit card will not come by post or email. If the site feels slow, try again early in the morning or late at night.

## 25,487 posts, 1,97,408 candidates: what the numbers say

This recruitment started with a notification on December 1, 2025. The online application window stayed open till December 31, 2025. The computer-based written test was held from April 27 to May 30, 2026, and its result came out on October 12, 2026.

Only the candidates who cleared that written test have reached the physical stage. About 1.97 lakh candidates now face the physical test for 25,487 posts, close to eight candidates for each post.

> "The admit card is a must for the physical test. No candidate will be allowed to take the test without it, so download it early and check every detail on it."

The physical stage has two parts. The Physical Efficiency Test checks how fit you are through running and other tasks. The Physical Standard Test checks your height and chest against the standards in the recruitment notice.

## Check every detail on your admit card today

Your call letter carries your name and roll number, your registration number, the date and reporting time of your physical test, and the name and address of your test centre. It also has your photograph and signature, plus the instructions for test day.

Read all of it the day you download it. The date, time and venue differ from candidate to candidate, so do not go by what a friend's admit card says. If any detail looks wrong, contact the concerned authority right away.

On test day, carry a printed copy of the admit card and a valid photo ID card. Reach the centre early, since the reporting time on your card is strict.

Other exams have their own call letters. The [IBPS RRB PO admit card 2026](/results/ibps-rrb-po-admit-card-2026-out-nov-21-22-prelims/) and the [IGNOU December TEE admit card](/results/ignou-december-tee-2026-admit-card-out-exams-nov-25/) are separate downloads, so do not mix up the links.

## What happens after the physical test

Candidates who clear the PET and PST will move to the next stage, which is a detailed medical exam. Clearing the physical test does not mean final selection. You still have to pass the medical exam and meet all the other standards before the final merit list is made.

For now, the job is simple. Download the admit card, check the date and venue, and start preparing for the run. The tests begin on November 26. Keep checking <a href="https://ssc.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ssc.gov.in</a> and <a href="https://rect.crpf.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rect.crpf.gov.in</a> for any fresh notice, and do not trust any other site for your admit card link.

## FAQs

### Where can I download the SSC GD PET/PST admit card 2026?

On the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in. Open the admit card section, pick your region, and log in with your registration number or roll number and password or date of birth. You can also check rect.crpf.gov.in, since the CRPF is running the physical tests.

### When will the SSC GD physical test 2026 be held?

The PET and PST are tentatively scheduled from November 26 to December 11, 2026. Your admit card shows your exact date, time and venue, which can differ from other candidates.

### How many candidates are shortlisted for the SSC GD PET/PST 2026?

A total of 1,97,408 candidates who cleared the written exam have been shortlisted for the physical stage. The recruitment fills 25,487 posts of Constable (GD) and Rifleman (GD).

### What should I carry for the SSC GD physical test?

Carry a printed copy of your admit card and a valid photo ID. Without the admit card, you will not be allowed to take the physical test.
