**The Directorate General of Medical Education and Training (DGME), Uttar Pradesh, has released the UP NEET PG 2026 Round 2 seat allotment result today, 26 November 2026. Candidates who took part in this round can check the college and course allotted to them on the official portal, <a href="https://upneet.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">upneet.gov.in</a>. If you got a seat, download your allotment letter and finish admission at the college between 27 November and 2 December 2026.**

Round 2 covers MD, MS, Diploma and DNB seats in government and private medical colleges, institutes and universities across Uttar Pradesh. These are state quota seats. The allotment was prepared from each candidate's state merit rank, NEET PG score, reservation category, the choices they filled, and seat availability.

The UP NEET PG Round 2 registration ran from 16 to 19 November. The merit list came out on 19 November, and choice filling ran from 20 to 25 November.

| Round 2 event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | 16 to 19 November 2026 |
| Merit list | 19 November 2026 |
| Choice filling | 20 to 25 November 2026 |
| Seat allotment result | 26 November 2026 |
| Allotment letter download and admission | 27 November to 2 December 2026 |

## How to check the UP NEET PG round 2 allotment result

Go to the official portal, upneet.gov.in, and open the PG Medical counselling section. Click the link for the Round 2 seat allotment result. Log in with your registered credentials if the page asks for them. Your allotted college and course will show on the screen. Download the result and save a copy.

The portal can run slow on result day because many candidates check at the same time. If the page does not open, wait a few minutes and try again. Keep your login details ready before you start so you do not waste time hunting for them.

## Got a seat? Finish admission before 2 December

An allotment is not the same as admission. You still have to report to the college and complete the formalities. The window for that is 27 November to 2 December 2026. Your allotment letter tells you which documents to carry and what fee to pay at the college. Read it fully before you travel.

Candidates who miss the 2 December deadline risk losing the seat. The college cannot hold it past the date DGME has fixed.

Start with the allotment letter. Download it from the portal and check the college name, the course, and the reporting dates printed on it. Then keep the documents it asks for ready, along with the fee it mentions. Reach the college inside the window, not on the last day, so a missing paper still leaves you time to fix it.

> The allotment letter download and admission window runs from 27 November to 2 December 2026. Candidates who miss it risk losing the seat.

## Round 2 follows the round 1 allotment

The [UP NEET PG Round 1 allotment](/results/up-neet-pg-2026-round-1-seat-allotment-result-nov-2-reporting/) came out on 2 November, and those candidates reported to their colleges between 3 and 7 November. Round 2 is the second round of the same counselling, run by the same authority on the same portal. If you took a seat in Round 1 and finished admission, this result does not change anything for you.

## No seat in this round? Watch for the next notice

If your name is not on the Round 2 list, the counselling is not over for you yet. DGME will publish the notice for the next round on upneet.gov.in. Check the portal itself instead of trusting forwarded messages.

The all-India process runs on a separate track. The [NEET PG Round 2 final allotment](/admissions/neet-pg-2026-round-2-final-allotment-20707-seats-report-nov-25/) from MCC covered 20,707 seats, and MCC Round 3 registration runs from 26 November to 1 December, with the Round 3 result due on 4 December. That applies to All India Quota, deemed and central university seats, not to UP state quota seats. Keep the two tracks separate when you check dates.
