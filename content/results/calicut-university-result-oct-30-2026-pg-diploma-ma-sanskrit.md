**The University of Calicut has declared two results dated October 30, 2026.** One is the second semester result for the Post Graduate Diploma in Commercial Tissue Culture of Agri-Horticultural Crops. The other is the revaluation result for the fourth semester MA Sanskrit Language and Literature (General) course. Both are live on the university's official result portal, and students need only their register number to check them.

This is a small, focused release, not a full batch. That is exactly why the details matter. The two announcements cover different courses, semesters and admission years, so read the examination name in full before you enter any detail.

## The two results declared on October 30

The first result is the Second Semester Post Graduate Diploma in Commercial Tissue Culture of Agri-Horticultural Crops Examination 4/2026. It applies to students admitted in 2025. If you joined this diploma course in 2025 and sat the second semester examination, this is your result.

Next, the revaluation result is the Revaluation Result of the Fourth Semester MA Sanskrit Language and Literature (General) CBCSS Examination 4/2026. It covers the 2022, 2023 and 2024 admission batches. Only students who applied for revaluation of that fourth semester examination will see a changed outcome here. If you did not apply for revaluation, this release does not change your earlier result.

Both results were published by Pareeksha Bhavan, the university's examination wing, so its result portal is the place to trust. A forwarded screenshot is not a substitute for the official page.

> Two results, two different batches: match the full examination name and your admission year before you type your register number. A wrong link can show you someone else's course page, or no result at all.

## How to check your Calicut University result

Follow these steps on the official portal:

- Open the <a href="https://results.uoc.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Calicut University result portal</a>.
- Find the result that matches your course, semester and examination session.
- Confirm the admission year shown with the result. The PG Diploma result is for 2025 admission, while the MA Sanskrit revaluation result covers 2022, 2023 and 2024 admissions.
- Enter your register number when the form asks for it.
- Check the result on screen, then save or print a copy.

Students who get stuck usually miss one small step: they search by course name alone. Calicut University publishes many results under similar names, with separate links for different schemes and admission years. The full examination title, such as Examination 4/2026, tells you that you have the right page.

## What to check once your result opens

Read every line on the result page before you close it. Your name, register number, course name, semester and subject-wise marks or grades should all match your records. For MA Sanskrit students, compare the revaluation outcome with your earlier result so you can see exactly which papers changed.

If a detail looks wrong, do not rely on a screenshot alone. Keep the printed copy, note the examination name and register number exactly as shown, and raise the issue through your college or the university's examination office. Small errors are far easier to fix when you report them early with the full details in hand.

The announcements do not give dates for revised mark statements or further result-related requests. That part will come through a university or department notice, so check that channel instead of guessing a date from another course's notice. Students waiting on other Calicut papers should keep an eye on the portal, since the university releases results in phases. An earlier round of [Calicut University results](/results/calicut-university-result-oct-2026/) came out on October 1 for eight examinations.

## After the result: what comes next

For PG Diploma students, the next step is to follow department instructions on academic records and further examination requirements. A second semester result usually feeds into the next stage of the course, so confirm whether anything else is needed from you.

MA Sanskrit students, by contrast, get a settled outcome for the papers they asked to be checked again. If your marks changed, keep both the old and the new copies until your updated record is confirmed. If nothing changed, your earlier result stands, and the revaluation process for those papers is closed.
