**The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) has opened the final selective edit window for the FMGE October 2026 session.** Candidates who were flagged for wrong or poor-quality photographs, signatures, or thumb impressions can now fix them. The window became active on October 3, 2026, and it stays open only till October 19, 2026. Once it shuts, no more corrections will be allowed before the exam.

This is not for everyone. The facility is meant only for candidates who have received a notice from NBEMS about an image deficiency in their application form. If your uploaded images were fine, there is nothing for you to change. But if you got a deficiency notice, treat this as urgent. Waiting till the last day is risky, because a failed upload or a slow website could leave you with a rejected application and no exam to write.

## What exactly you can change in this window

The selective edit window is narrow on purpose. You cannot rewrite your personal details or pick a new exam centre here. Only three image fields can be corrected:

- Photograph
- Signature
- Thumb impression

Your new uploads must match the size and format NBEMS has specified in the information bulletin. Check the full form once before you hit submit, and download the confirmation page after editing. Keep that confirmation saved. It is your proof that you made the correction on time.

## Exam pattern: two papers, no negative marking, 50% to qualify

FMGE is an objective-type test of multiple choice questions covering subjects from the MBBS curriculum. This session is split across two papers, given in two sessions of two hours thirty minutes each on the same day. Each correct answer earns one mark, and there is no negative marking for wrong answers.

The passing rule is simple and strict. A candidate must score at least 50 percent of the total marks to qualify. There is no separate paper-wise cut-off mentioned in the official pattern, but you should read the information bulletin for the exact scheme. ## Why FMGE exists and who has to take it

FMGE is the screening examination for eligible foreign medical graduates. If you are an Indian citizen or an Overseas Citizen of India who completed your MBBS or an equivalent medical degree from a college outside India, this is your gateway to practice here.

One detail many candidates get wrong: anyone who earned their primary medical qualification abroad on or after March 15, 2002, must clear FMGE before registering with the National Medical Commission and practising as a doctor in India. The application also asked for proof of your degree, internship details, and citizenship documents. The fee for this session was set at Rs 5,250 plus 18 percent GST.

## How to fix your images, step by step

The correction process runs through your existing application login. Here is exactly what to do:

- Go to the official NBEMS website, <a href="https://nbe.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">nbe.edu.in</a>.
- Open the FMGE October 2026 application link on the homepage.
- Log in using your registered credentials.
- Find and open the selective edit window for your application.
- Check the image deficiency that NBEMS has highlighted for your form.
- Upload the corrected photograph, signature, or thumb impression, as applicable.
- Review the uploaded image carefully on screen before submitting.
- Submit the correction and save or download the confirmation page.

Do this on a computer, not a phone, so you can see the image preview clearly. A blurry signature that the board already rejected once will not pass the second time either. And if your login is not showing any edit option, it means NBEMS did not flag your form. You are fine.
