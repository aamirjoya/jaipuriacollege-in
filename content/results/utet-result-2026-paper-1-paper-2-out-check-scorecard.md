**The wait is over for thousands of teacher aspirants in Uttarakhand. The Uttarakhand Board of School Education (UBSE) has declared the UTET Result 2026 for Paper 1 and Paper 2 today, November 13. Candidates who took the exam on September 27 can now download their scorecards from the official board portal.**

The result covers both papers of the Uttarakhand Teacher Eligibility Test. Paper 1 is for teaching primary classes, from Class 1 to Class 5. Paper 2 is for teaching upper primary classes, from Class 6 to Class 8. The board has also released the final answer key for both papers, so candidates can match their answers with the final key and check where they lost marks.

The board had issued the result notice on November 2. The scorecard link will stay live only for a short window, so candidates should not leave the download for the last day. The portal can slow down when too many people log in at once, and a last-minute rush is common with [teacher eligibility test results](/results/) like this one.

## UTET Result 2026: scorecard link closes on November 25

The result link will remain active till November 25, 2026, at 11:59 pm. After that, the link closes. Candidates should download the scorecard now and keep a saved copy on their phone and computer. The scorecard is the proof of eligibility that teacher recruitment drives ask for, and getting a copy later can take time.

A qualifying UTET scorecard opens the door to primary and upper primary teacher posts in Uttarakhand government schools. The Uttarakhand Education Department and local school bodies announce these posts from time to time, and only candidates with a valid UTET scorecard can apply. Passing the test does not give a job by itself. It makes a candidate eligible to apply when vacancies open.

> Download your scorecard today and save a copy. The link closes on November 25, and the scorecard is the document every teacher recruitment in Uttarakhand will ask for first.

## How to download the UTET 2026 scorecard

Follow these steps on the <a href="https://ubse.uk.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UBSE website</a>:

- Open ubse.uk.gov.in and look for the Departmental Exam/UTET section on the homepage.
- Click on the UTET 2026 Result link for Paper 1 and Paper 2.
- Enter your registration number and password, or your roll number and date of birth.
- Fill in the captcha code shown on the screen and submit.
- Your scorecard will appear. Check your name, roll number, and marks carefully.
- Download the scorecard and save a PDF copy for future use.

If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Heavy traffic on result day is normal. Do not share your registration number or password with anyone, and do not use unofficial websites that ask for your login details. The board has advised candidates to use only the official portal.

## One warning: do not use uktet.com right now

Several news reports still list uktet.com as an official result website. That domain no longer belongs to the board. When checked today, it opens a gambling website. Candidates should use only ubse.uk.gov.in to check their result. Any site that asks for money or personal details in the name of the UTET result is a fraud.

## What marks do you need to qualify?

The qualifying marks for UTET 2026 depend on the category. General category candidates need 60 per cent. OBC, PwD, and ex-servicemen candidates need 50 per cent. SC and ST candidates need 40 per cent. Candidates who score at or above the mark for their category are declared qualified.

The final answer key is now final. The board released the provisional answer key on October 5 and invited objections. After checking those objections, it prepared the final key. The result is based on this final key, and the board will not accept fresh objections against it.

Candidates who did not qualify this time can look at the answer key, find the sections where they lost marks, and plan for the next UTET. The test is held every year, and the syllabus and pattern stay broadly the same. Those who qualified should keep their documents ready, because teacher recruitment notices in Uttarakhand often give short windows to apply. A valid scorecard, a degree or diploma in teacher training, and the usual identity and caste documents are what the application asks for.
