**The wait is over for Rajasthan's computer course students. The [RSCIT Result 2026](/results/) for the exams held on October 4 and 11 is out, and candidates can check their scorecards on the official portal.**

Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota, declared the result on November 17, 2026. The Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology (RSCIT) exam is run by the Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited (RKCL) together with VMOU. Lakhs of students, job seekers and first-time computer learners across Rajasthan take this course every year to build basic digital skills.

The result covers both exam dates from October. Candidates who sat for the paper on October 4 or October 11 can now see their marks online. The scorecard is available on the same portal where the exam was announced, and the certificate download has also opened for those who passed.

## Both October exam dates covered in one result

RKCL held the RSCIT exam on two days in October, the 4th and the 11th. The result for both dates came out together on November 17. There is no separate list for each date. Students only need to pick the right exam date on the portal before they search.

The exam was held offline. The paper carries 75 marks in total, and candidates need at least 28 marks to qualify. Those who score 28 or more will be able to download their certificate. Those who fall short will need to appear again in a later exam cycle, and the dates for that will be announced on the official portal.

> "Candidates who appeared for the examinations can now access their results through the official examination portal, rkcl.vmou.ac.in." (The Times of India, on the RSCIT result declaration)

## How to check your RSCIT result 2026

Checking the result takes less than two minutes if you have your roll number ready. Follow these steps:

- Open the official exam portal, <a href="https://rkcl.vmou.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rkcl.vmou.ac.in</a>.
- Look for the list of declared results on the home page.
- Pick the result for the October 4 and 11 exams and click on "View Result".
- Select your district from the list.
- Search by roll number, or by name and date of birth.
- Your scorecard will appear on the screen. Download the PDF and save it.

Students who do not remember their roll number can use the name-wise search. You will need your name exactly as it appears on the admit card, along with your date of birth. Keep a copy of the scorecard on your phone as well as on paper, because the certificate download uses the same details.

## What your scorecard shows

The scorecard lists your name, registration number, course name, marks and qualifying status. Read every line before you close the page. If your name is spelt wrong or the marks look off, note it down and contact the exam centre or the university help desk through the official portal. Do not trust screenshots or result links shared on WhatsApp groups. Only the scorecard on rkcl.vmou.ac.in is the real one.

Students waiting on other recent exams can also check the [UTET result 2026](/results/utet-result-2026-paper-1-paper-2-out-check-scorecard/) and the [CSEET result for October 2026](/results/cseet-october-2026-result-declared-nov-15-check-scorecard/), both of which are live now.

## Download your certificate from DigiLocker

Passing the exam is only half the job. The certificate is what you will show at job applications and government offices. Qualified candidates can download it from the National Academic Depository (NAD) portal on <a href="https://www.digilocker.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DigiLocker</a>.

Sign in to DigiLocker with the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar, then look for the RSCIT certificate under issued documents. The digital certificate carries the same value as a printed one, so you can use it wherever a copy is asked for. If the certificate does not show up right away, wait a day and check again, since certificates are added in batches after the result.

RSCIT is one of the most popular computer courses in Rajasthan. It teaches the basics that government jobs and office work now expect: using a computer, working with documents, sending emails and handling online forms. For many students in small towns, it is the first formal proof of computer skills they hold. That is why the result day matters so much, and why it is worth checking the scorecard on the official portal instead of waiting for someone else to send you a link.
