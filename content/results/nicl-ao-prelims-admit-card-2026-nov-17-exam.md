**The wait is over for insurance job aspirants. National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) has released the NICL AO prelims admit card 2026 today, November 10, for 321 Administrative Officer Scale I posts. The prelims exam will be held on November 17, and the call letter can be downloaded till the exam day.**

This is one of the biggest insurance officer recruitments this year. The 321 posts cover Generalist and Specialist streams. Candidates who applied can now log in to the official portal and get their hall ticket. Check the exam city, centre, shift and reporting time on it as soon as you download it.

## 321 posts across 8 streams

Generalist Officers take the biggest share, with 200 posts. The rest are specialist roles. Risk Engineers get 28 posts, Information Technology gets 21, and Finance, Legal and Agriculture get 20 each. Automobile Engineers have 10 posts, and Company Secretaries have 2. Add them up and the total comes to 321.

The prelims is the first step. Only candidates who clear it move to the mains. The mains exam is set for December 22, 2026. After that comes a personal interview, document verification and a medical check. Banking aspirants tracking other [exam results](/results/) will know the pattern well. The [IBPS RRB PO admit card 2026](/results/ibps-rrb-po-admit-card-2026-out-nov-21-22-prelims/) also went live this week for 4,256 Officer Scale I posts, with prelims on November 21 and 22.

> Download the call letter today, not on November 17. A last-day rush, a slow server or a wrong detail on the card can all cost you the exam.

## How to download the NICL AO admit card 2026

Keep your registration number and password ready before you start. The login also accepts your roll number and date of birth.

- Go to the official NICL website at <a href="https://nationalinsurance.nic.co.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">nationalinsurance.nic.co.in</a>.
- Open the Recruitment section and find the Administrative Officer admit card link.
- Pick English or Hindi as your language.
- Enter your registration number or roll number.
- Enter your password or date of birth in DD-MM-YY format.
- Type the captcha and click Login.
- Check your name, roll number, exam date, reporting time and centre address on the screen.
- Download the call letter and take a printout.

If the site feels slow, try again after a few minutes. Many candidates will log in on the first day, so the portal may run slow at peak hours. Do not refresh the page again and again while the login is loading.

## What the prelims paper looks like

You get 60 minutes for the prelims paper, which carries 100 marks. It has three sections: English Language, Reasoning Ability and Quantitative Aptitude. The paper is objective, and the marks decide who moves to the mains.

The mains is a bigger test. It has a 250-mark objective paper and a 30-mark descriptive paper. Candidates who clear the mains are called for the interview, then document verification and a medical exam. Start mains preparation now if you feel good about the prelims. Waiting for the result wastes a month.

## Check these details before exam day

Carry a printed copy of the admit card and an original photo ID to the centre. Without them, you may not be allowed inside. The [CAT 2026 admit card](/results/cat-2026-admit-card-november-4-download/) rules are the same, and they apply here too. Match the name and photo on your ID with the admit card.

Read the instructions on the call letter once more. Note the reporting time, not just the exam time. Reach the centre early, because late entry is not allowed in most online exams. Keep a second printout at home as backup.

NICL will post all updates on its own website. Do not trust links shared on Telegram or WhatsApp groups. Fake sites ask for money or personal details, and they look real at first glance. If a link does not come from the official NICL portal, do not open it.

The link is live now at <a href="https://nationalinsurance.nic.co.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">nationalinsurance.nic.co.in</a>. Download the card, check every detail, and use the six days left to revise. The exam is on November 17, and there is no second chance to sit for it this year.
