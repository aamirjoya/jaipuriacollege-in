**If you sat for the ANUCDE MBA or MCA entrance test on September 20, your wait is over.** Acharya Nagarjuna University's Centre for Distance Education has declared the entrance exam results for the 2026-27 academic session. The rank cards are live on the official portal, and qualified candidates have just four days left to complete admission. The last date to submit the admission form and pay the fee is October 10, 2026.

## The rank card is live: download it before anything else

Your first job is to download the rank card from the official ANUCDE portal. Keep your Hall Ticket Number or Application Number ready before you start.

- Open the official ANUCDE portal at <a href="https://www.anucde.info/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">anucde.info</a>.
- Look for the MBA/MCA Entrance Test Rank Card link for the 2026-27 session.
- Enter your Hall Ticket Number or Application Number.
- Submit the details and view your rank card on screen.
- Download the card and keep a printed copy for your records.

Check your name, qualifying status, and personal details the moment the card opens. Any mismatch should be reported to the university right away, since the card becomes part of your admission file.

## October 10 is the hard stop: how to finish your admission

The result alone does not book your seat. After the rank card, you must complete the admission process on the Samarth eGov portal. The deadline covers both the online form and the fee payment.

- Visit the Samarth admission portal at <a href="https://anucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">anucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in</a> and open the A26 Batch admissions link.
- Register as a new candidate and complete your profile.
- Fill in your academic qualifications and upload the required documents.
- Pay the admission fee online through net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI.
- Submit the form before October 10, 2026, and save the final receipt.

Late forms will not be accepted, so do not leave the payment for the last evening. Bank servers slow down on deadline days every year.

> "Download your rank card today, not tomorrow. With the admission window closing on October 10, every day you wait is a day you could have used to fix a document error."

## Who can apply: eligibility and the AP ICET route

A bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks (45% for BC, SC, and ST candidates) is the basic requirement for both programmes. You also need mathematics at the Intermediate or degree level. These rules match what the university has published for the current admission cycle.

You do not strictly need the ANUCDE entrance test to get in. A valid AP ICET score works for both the MBA and the MCA. Candidates with 10 or more years of relevant work experience can ask for an exemption from the entrance test. Other <a href="/results/mat-september-2026-result-scorecard-download/">MBA entrance result</a> announcements this season have followed a similar pattern of accepting multiple test scores.

## What ANUCDE is: a UGC-recognised distance option at Guntur

ANUCDE is the distance education arm of Acharya Nagarjuna University, a state university based in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. The parent university carries NAAC's "A" grade, and the distance programmes are recognised by the UGC Distance Education Bureau. For working professionals and students who cannot attend a regular campus, it remains one of the established state-university options for an MBA or MCA through distance mode.

Keep an eye on the ANUCDE website after you submit your form. The university is expected to publish the admission confirmation list and the study material dispatch schedule for the A26 batch next.
