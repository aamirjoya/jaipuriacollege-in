**The wait ends today for Assam's medical aspirants. The Directorate of Medical Education (DME), Assam, will publish the NEET UG 2026 special stray round seat allotment result today, November 8, 2026.** Students who took part in this final round of counselling can check the allotment list on the official portal, dme.assam.gov.in. Those who get a seat must complete their admission at the allotted college by November 16.

The date changed once before it was fixed. The result was first planned for November 7, but DME Assam revised the schedule and moved it to November 8. That is why some news reports still show the old date. The latest update from the directorate is clear: the list comes out today.

## What the special stray round is about

The round moved fast. Registration opened on November 4. Choice filling and locking closed on November 5. DME processed the allotments on November 6, and the result now follows on November 8. The short gap between the result and the reporting date means allotted students have little time to waste.

## How to check the Assam NEET UG special stray round result

You do not need a login for this one. DME Assam will put the allotment list up as a public PDF on its website. Here is how to check it:

- Go to the official portal, <a href="https://dme.assam.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dme.assam.gov.in</a>.
- Look for the latest notification about the NEET UG 2026 special stray round.
- Open the seat allotment result link once it is live.
- Download the PDF and search for your NEET UG roll number or application number.

The PDF will list the candidates who have been allotted seats. For each name, it will show the registration serial number, DME application number, NEET UG 2026 roll number, rank and score, revised state rank, category and sub-category, and the college code, quota, and name of the institution allotted. Read every detail against your own records. A wrong college code or quota entry should be raised with DME Assam right away.

> "This is the final round of counselling, so the result matters more than usual. A seat in this round is often the last chance for students who waited through every earlier phase."

## November 16 is the reporting date

Getting your name on the list is only half the job. Every provisionally allotted candidate must report to the allotted institute on November 16, 2026, to complete the admission process. That means physical reporting at the college, with documents verified at the principal's office.

Students who miss the date risk losing the seat. The directorate has been clear that the allotment can be cancelled if a candidate does not report in time. The seat then goes to another waiting candidate. If your allotted college is far from home, plan the travel now instead of waiting for the PDF to appear.

Keep your documents ready before you travel. Carry your original certificates, identity proof, category documents if any, and the allotment letter from the DME portal. Colleges verify originals at the time of admission, and missing papers can delay or block the process. The [college admission documents checklist](/admissions/college-admission-documents-checklist-india/) covers what most colleges ask for, so go through it once before you leave.

## What to do after the result is out

If you are allotted a seat, download the allotment letter from the portal and note the reporting time given by your college. Some colleges ask students to report in the morning, so check the notice on the DME website or the college website instead of guessing.

If your name is not on the list, this round has ended for you. There is no further round in the state counselling process after a special stray round. Do not fall for agents or websites that promise a seat after the counselling has closed. The official portal is the only source that counts.

For the latest [results](/results/) across exams, keep checking this section. The [INI CET November 2026 result](/results/ini-cet-november-2026-result-34236-qualified-aiims/) was also declared this week, with 34,236 candidates qualifying.

## Keep checking the official portal

DME Assam may put up fresh notices about document verification, fee payment, or changes to the reporting schedule. The directorate's website is the only place to trust for such updates. Check it once in the morning and once in the evening until your admission is complete.
