**The wait is over for NEET PG aspirants. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has released the [NEET PG Round 2 provisional result](/results/) today, 20 November 2026, on its official portal mcc.nic.in. If you took part in this round, check the PDF now. You have only till 6 PM today to point out any mistake in your allotment.**

## The result was due on 16 November. A court stay held it back

This result was first due on 16 November, but the Madras High Court passed an interim stay on 17 November in the case of Sri Venkateshwaraa Medical College Hospital and Research Centre vs the Government of India. The stay put Round 2 seat processing on hold, and thousands of PG aspirants were left waiting with no fresh date.

The court has now made it clear that the stay was never meant for the full counselling process. The interim order applies only to Sri Venkateshwaraa Deemed University in Puducherry. It does not touch the counselling or admission process for any other college run by MCC or by the Centralised Admission Committee (CENTAC).

> "It is hereby clarified that the interim stay granted earlier operates strictly in respect of Sri Venkateshwaraa Deemed University alone, and does not apply to the counselling or admission processes for other colleges conducted by either CENTAC or MCC."

With that confusion cleared, MCC has resumed the counselling and published the provisional allotment. The result leaves out that college's seats, and a separate schedule will come for it once the court gives its final word.

## Provisional does not mean final. Read this before you celebrate

A provisional allotment is a draft list, not a confirmed seat. MCC has said in its advisory that the list is indicative in nature and may change before the final result is declared. That is the most important line in the whole notice, and students should not miss it.

The provisional allotment gives you no claim or right over the seat, and it cannot be challenged in any court of law. Do not travel to the allotted college on the basis of this list. Wait for the final allotment result and your allotment letter, and only then report with your documents.

Read every line of your allotment with care. Check the college name, the course, the quota, and your own details. A small error spotted now can be fixed. After the final result, it is much harder to correct.

## You have till 6 PM today to raise an objection

If you find any discrepancy in the provisional result, report it through the Candidates Grievances and Query Redressal System on the candidate login portal. The window closes at 6 PM today, 20 November 2026.

Do not send your complaint by email or social media. MCC will only act on grievances filed through the portal before the deadline. Once the window closes, MCC will prepare the final allotment result. The reporting schedule will be announced along with it, so keep checking the portal for the dates.

## How to check the NEET PG Round 2 provisional result

The result comes as a PDF, so keep your roll number ready.

- Open the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC portal</a>.
- Go to the PG Medical section on the home page.
- Look for the Round 2 provisional allotment link under Current Events.
- Open the result PDF and search for your roll number with Ctrl+F.
- If you have a seat, note the college and course, but do not report yet.
- Save a copy of the result PDF before you close the page.

Do not trust a result link shared on social media or on any other website. The MCC portal is the only safe place to check.

## What to keep ready for reporting

Keep your documents in one folder now, because reporting will start quickly once the final result is out. You will need your NEET PG admit card and scorecard or rank letter, the seat allotment letter, your MBBS degree certificate or provisional certificate, MBBS marksheets, and your internship completion certificate. Carry your medical registration certificate, Class 10 certificate or birth certificate, a valid government photo ID, and recent passport-size photographs. If you are claiming a reserved seat, keep your category or caste certificate ready. PwBD candidates need their disability certificate, and NRI or OCI candidates need their supporting documents.

## What happens next: final result and Round 3

After the 6 PM deadline, MCC will release the final allotment result. You can then download your allotment letter from the candidate login, and the reporting schedule will be announced with the final result.

State rounds run on their own dates alongside the all-India counselling. [UP NEET PG Round 2](/admissions/up-neet-pg-round-2-registration-closes-nov-19-2026/) registration closed on 19 November, and the state merit list was due the same day. [Karnataka NEET PG Round 2](/admissions/karnataka-neet-pg-round-2-option-entry-closes-nov-19-2026/) option entry also closed on 19 November. If you are part of a state round, track your state portal too, because the all-India result does not cover state seats.

Candidates who do not get a seat in Round 2 still have another chance. Round 3 registration and choice filling are set to begin on 26 November 2026. If your name is not on the final list, get ready for Round 3.
