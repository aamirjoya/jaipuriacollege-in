**The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection has released the IBPS SO Mains admit card 2026.** Candidates who cleared the prelims can now download their call letters from the official IBPS website. The mains exam for Specialist Officer posts under CRP SPL-XVI is scheduled for November 1, 2026, which leaves just seven days to sort out the download, check every detail, and plan the trip to the centre.

This stage matters more than the prelims did. The prelims only screened candidates. The mains score, along with the interview that follows, decides the final merit list for these 1,043 Specialist Officer posts in participating public sector banks. So a small mistake now, like turning up with the wrong ID or at the wrong centre, can cost a candidate a seat they have already worked hard to reach.

## Who can download the IBPS SO Mains admit card

Only candidates who qualified in the prelims can download this call letter. If you did not clear the prelims, no mains admit card will be generated for your registration number. The [IBPS SO prelims result](/results/ibps-so-prelims-result-2026-out-scorecard-download-nov-1-mains/) was declared on October 15, 2026, after the prelims exam on August 29. Candidates who made the cut should download the mains call letter now instead of waiting till the last day, when the website tends to slow down under heavy traffic.

Six streams are covered in this recruitment: IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Law Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer, all at Scale I level. Each stream has its own mains paper, so read the instructions that apply to your own post. Do not prepare from another stream's paper pattern by mistake.

## How to download the admit card from ibps.in

Keep your registration number and password or date of birth ready before you start. Then follow these steps:

- Go to <a href="https://ibps.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official IBPS portal</a> at ibps.in.
- Open the CRP Specialist Officers section on the homepage.
- Click the link for the CRP SPL-XVI Mains call letter.
- Enter your registration number or roll number and your password or date of birth.
- Enter the captcha code if one appears on the screen.
- Submit the details and check that your call letter has loaded fully.
- Download the PDF and take a clear printout on A4 paper.

IBPS does not send the call letter by post or by email. The online download is the only way to get it.

## Check these details the moment you download it

A quick check now can save a stressful morning on November 1. Go through the call letter line by line and confirm your name, photograph, signature, roll number and registration number. Then check the exam date, your reporting time, the shift, and the full address of your centre.

> "Download the call letter early, check every detail on it, and reach the centre before the reporting time. Most exam-day problems start with a detail nobody checked."

If any detail looks wrong, contact IBPS through its official helpdesk channels right away. Do not wait till the exam day to raise a mismatch in your name, photograph or centre. Also check how to reach the centre in advance. Look up the route, work out the travel time, and keep a buffer for traffic. Candidates who arrive after the gates close are not allowed in, whatever the reason for the delay.

## What to carry on November 1

Pack your exam-day folder the night before. You will need a printed copy of the IBPS SO Mains admit card, a valid original photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN card, passport or voter ID, along with a photocopy of that ID, and a recent passport-size photograph that matches the one in your application form. Some centres also ask candidates to paste the photograph on the call letter, so read the instructions printed on your own admit card and follow them exactly.

Leave banned items at home. Calculators, watches, mobile phones and other electronic gadgets are not allowed inside the hall.

## If the download link does not work

A link that refuses to load is usually a small problem, not a missing admit card. Refresh the page and try again after a few minutes. Recheck your registration number and password, and make sure you typed your date of birth in the format asked for. Try a different browser or device if the problem continues, and check that your internet connection is stable. Stay away from unofficial websites that ask for payment or extra personal details to "fetch" your admit card. IBPS never charges for the download, and entering your login details on a fake site puts your application at risk.

## What comes after the mains

Candidates who clear the mains will be called for the interview round. The final selection rests on mains and interview scores, and successful candidates get provisional allotment in participating banks after document checks. That is why the next seven days are worth using well. Revise your own stream's syllabus, practise with the time limit your paper sets, and keep the admit card and ID ready so the exam morning holds no surprises.
