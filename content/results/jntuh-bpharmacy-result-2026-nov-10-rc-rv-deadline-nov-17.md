**Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH) has declared the BPharmacy II-II semester regular and supplementary results on November 10, 2026. Students who wrote the exams in September 2026 can now check their scorecards on the university's official results portal. You only need your hall ticket number to see your marks.**

The result covers the BPharmacy II-II semester regular and supplementary exams, along with the II-I semester supplementary exams. The [JNTUH BPharmacy result](/results/) is live on the official portal, and students can download the scorecard for their records. The university has also put out results for several postgraduate programmes and other exams held in July 2026, so students from other courses should check the portal too.

## What was declared on November 10

The main result in this round is for BPharmacy students in their second year, second semester. Both regular students and those who appeared for supplementary exams are covered. The September 2026 exams are now fully accounted for in this declaration, and the result is available as a PDF on the portal.

JNTUH is one of the biggest technical universities in Telangana. It was set up in 1972 and is based in Hyderabad. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds NAAC accreditation. Its results matter to thousands of pharmacy students across its affiliated colleges, many of whom need the marksheet for the next semester, for internships, or for higher studies.

## More JNTUH results came out on November 9

The BPharmacy result was not the only one. A day earlier, on November 9, the university declared a set of results for the July 2026 exams. These include the PhD coursework MTech I-II semester (R25) results, MTech I semester supplementary and II semester regular RC/RV results, MCA results across the first four semesters, MBA results across the first four semesters, and the BPharmacy IV-II semester supplementary RC/RV results.

> "Check the result as soon as you can, and read every detail on the scorecard. If anything looks wrong, raise it with your college or apply for recounting before the deadline passes."

The last date to apply for recounting (RC) or revaluation (RV) for these November 9 results is November 16, 2026. That is one day earlier than the BPharmacy II-II deadline, so students in these courses should not mix up the two dates.

## How to check your JNTUH BPharmacy result

The steps are simple, and the whole process takes a few minutes. Keep your hall ticket number ready before you start.

- Go to the official results portal at <a href="https://results.jntuh.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">results.jntuh.ac.in</a>.
- On the JNTUH website, open the "View Results" section and pick a result server.
- Select your exam from the list of declared results.
- Enter your hall ticket number where asked and submit.
- Your result will appear on the screen. Check it and save a copy for your records.

If the portal is slow, wait a few minutes and try again. Result days bring heavy traffic, and the page can take time to load. Do not trust result links shared on random websites or social media. Use only the official portal, or the link from the university's own website, <a href="https://jntuh.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">jntuh.ac.in</a>.

## Recounting and revaluation: last date is November 17

Students who are not happy with their marks can apply for recounting or revaluation. For the BPharmacy II-II semester result declared on November 10, the last date to submit RC/RV applications is November 17, 2026.

Do not wait for the last day. Read the fee details and the procedure on the official university website first, then apply through the proper channel. The university has asked students to check the official notice for the exact fee and instructions before they submit a request. Like the [Gurugram University re-evaluation results](/results/gurugram-university-re-evaluation-result-nov-6-2026/) declared earlier this month, the revised marks after revaluation will be the final ones for that paper.

## What to check on your scorecard

Once your result opens, go through it line by line. Check your name and hall ticket number first, then your course and semester. Look at the subject-wise marks and the total. If any subject shows "absent" when you did appear for the exam, or if your name is spelt wrong, contact your college exam cell at once.

Keep a printed copy of the scorecard with you. The online result works as a grade card for most purposes, and the university later issues a printed Memorandum of Grades through the college. Students who have cleared all papers can move ahead with the next semester without delay. Those with backlogs should note the supplementary exam schedule when it is announced.
