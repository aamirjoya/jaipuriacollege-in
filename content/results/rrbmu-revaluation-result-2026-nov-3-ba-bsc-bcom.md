**Four RRBMU revaluation results are out. Raj Rishi Bhartrihari Matsya University (RRBMU), Alwar, released the revaluation results for BA Part III, BSc Part III, BCom Part III and BCom Part II (Exam 2026) on 3 November 2026. If you applied for revaluation, your updated marks are now live on the university result portal.**

This is the result many students were waiting for. The main annual results for these courses came out in September 2026, and students who felt their marks did not match their answers applied for a re-check. That re-check is now complete for these four exams.

## Which RRBMU revaluation results were released on Nov 3

All four results carry the same release date of 3 November 2026:

- BA Part III Exam 2026, revaluation
- BSc Part III Exam 2026, revaluation
- BCom Part III Exam 2026, revaluation
- BCom Part II Exam 2026, revaluation

Check the result for your own course and year. A Part II result will not show Part III marks, and the other way round. If you applied for more than one paper, go through each paper's marks one by one.

> "A revaluation result can change your total, so do not rely on your old marksheet once the new result is out. Download the updated copy and keep it safe."

## How to check your RRBMU revaluation result 2026

The steps below are general guidance. The portal's layout may look a little different on your screen, so follow what it shows you.

- Open the <a href="https://www.rrbmuniv.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RRBMU official website</a> and find the result section.
- Look for your exam in the result list, such as BA Part III Exam 2026 (Revaluation).
- Open the result link for your exam on the university result portal.
- Enter the details the portal asks for, such as your roll number.
- Submit the form and view your result on the screen.
- Save a copy or take a printout for your records.

## What revaluation means for your marks

Revaluation is not the same as simply adding up the marks again. In a re-check of this kind, the answer script is assessed afresh. That is why the outcome can go either way. Your marks in a paper can rise, fall, or stay exactly where they were.

This matters most for final-year students. A change in even one paper can shift your total and your division. The revaluation result is treated as the final word for that paper, so check it carefully while you have it open.

## What to check after you open your result

Small errors cause big trouble later, so spend two minutes on these checks before you close the page:

- Your name is spelt the same way as on your admit card.
- The roll number on the result matches yours.
- The course, year and paper names are the ones you applied for.
- The subject-wise marks add up to the total shown.
- You have saved or printed a copy of the page.

If you spot a mismatch, do not edit the printout yourself. Contact your college or the university examination office and ask what the official correction process is. Keep your admit card and your earlier marksheet ready when you ask, because the office will usually ask for both.

Students should also keep an eye on the official website for what comes next. The university puts out separate notices for revised marksheets and further procedures, and those notices are the only reliable source for dates. Do not trust a date you saw on a random social media post. When in doubt, apply on the <a href="https://www.rrbmuniv.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official RRBMU portal</a> or through the links given on the university website, and ignore any third-party page that asks for money to show your result.

## FAQ

### When was the RRBMU revaluation result 2026 released?

The revaluation results for all four exams were listed on 3 November 2026: BA Part III, BSc Part III, BCom Part III and BCom Part II (Exam 2026).

### Where can I check my RRBMU revaluation result?

Check it through the result links on the university's official website, rrbmuniv.ac.in, which lead to the RRBMU result portal. Keep your roll number ready and match the result with your course and year.

### Can my marks go down after revaluation?

Yes. In revaluation, the answer script is checked again, so the marks can go up, go down, or stay the same. The revaluation result is treated as final for that paper.

### What should I do if my revaluation result shows an error?

First, save a copy of the result and check your name, roll number and subject-wise marks. If anything looks wrong, contact your college or the university examination office and follow the official notice for that exam.
