**The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) has released the INI CET January 2027 admit card on October 26, just six days before the exam.** Candidates who registered for the Institute of National Importance Combined Entrance Test can now download their hall ticket from MyPage on the official website, aiimsexams.ac.in. The exam is on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and it will be held in a single session in computer-based test mode.

INI CET is the entrance test for postgraduate medical courses such as MD, MS, MCh, and DM at AIIMS and other Institutes of National Importance. Registration for the January 2027 session ran from September 16 to October 13, and the exam city intimation slip came out on October 20. That slip told you only your exam city. The admit card is the document that carries your exact centre address, shift, and reporting time, and it is the one you must carry on exam day.

## Download your admit card from MyPage today

The admit card is available online only. There is no offline copy, and AIIMS will not send it by post. Log in to MyPage on the official portal and open the link for the INI CET January 2027 admit card. You will need three things to sign in: your Registration ID, your password, and your Examination Unique Code (EUC).

Follow these steps:

- Go to aiimsexams.ac.in and open the Academic Courses section
- Select INI CET for the January 2027 session
- Log in to MyPage with your Registration ID, password, and EUC
- Open the admit card link and check every detail on it
- Download it and take a clear printout on A4 paper

The link may appear for candidates in batches, so if you do not see it at first, wait a while and try again. Clear your browser cache or switch browsers before you panic. What you should not do is trust a third-party site that claims to have a direct download link. The admit card lives only on the official AIIMS portal.

For the full list of declared scores and hall tickets, visit the [results section](/results/).

## Check your centre, shift, and reporting time before you travel

Once the admit card opens, read it line by line. Check your name, photograph, exam centre address, shift, and reporting time. The centre and shift printed on the admit card are final, so plan your travel around them now rather than on October 31. If you have to travel to another city, book your tickets early. Exam-week trains and buses fill up fast, and reaching the city a day early is far better than rushing in on exam morning.

If any detail on the admit card looks wrong, raise it from the Help or Query section of MyPage right away, with your application number and a screenshot. Do not leave it till the last day. A name mismatch between your admit card and your photo ID can cause trouble at the gate, and the day before the exam is the worst time to get it fixed.

> "The city slip told you where to go. The admit card tells you exactly where to stand. Candidates who mix up the two reach the wrong building on exam morning."

## What to carry on November 1, and what the gate rules mean

Reach your centre well before the reporting time printed on your admit card. You will need time for verification and frisking, and the reporting time on the card is the time you must be there, not the time the exam starts.

Carry these items:

- A printed copy of your INI CET January 2027 admit card
- One original government photo ID that matches the name on your application
- A spare printout of the admit card, in case the first copy gets smudged or torn

No entry is allowed without a valid admit card, even if you have your registration details on your phone. Read every instruction printed on the card itself, since those instructions are the final word on what is allowed inside the hall. Keep your phone and other barred items out of the exam hall as per those instructions.

## Six days left: what to do now

With the exam on November 1, the next six days are about revision and logistics, not new topics. Download the admit card today, check your centre, and sort your travel. In the last two days, revise your short notes and sleep properly. A tired mind on exam morning costs more marks than any single topic you could cram.

Download your admit card only through the <a href="https://aiimsexams.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AIIMS examinations portal</a>. That is the only place where the genuine link appears, and it is the only source you should trust for your centre, shift, and reporting time.
