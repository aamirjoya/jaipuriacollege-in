**The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University has declared the MDS Part II and BPharm Eighth Semester results today, October 23, 2026.** Students who appeared in these examinations can check their results on the university's official exam result portal using their registration number.

Both results are part of a busy October for the university. The MDS Part I and BOT Final Year results came out on October 22, and the MBBS First Professional results were released on October 21. The BPT Final Year result was declared on October 14, and MD Siddha Third Year and BDS Third Year results came out on October 16.

The university publishes its results online only. The marksheet for each course is available on the official portal, and the same link is listed under the Results section of <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official website tnmgrmu.ac.in</a>. Students should use only the official link and avoid entering their registration number on any other website.

## How to check your TNMGRMU result in 5 steps

The process is simple. Keep your registration number ready before you start.

- Visit the official exam result portal of the university, linked from <a href="https://tnmgrmu.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">tnmgrmu.ac.in</a>.
- Open the Results section and find your course in the list of declared results.
- Enter your registration number and select the correct exam term.
- Complete the simple security check and click on View Result.
- Your result will appear on the screen. Download it and save a copy.

Double-check your course name and term before submitting. The university lists many similar-sounding courses, and a wrong term selection can show you someone else's schedule instead of your result.

## Site may feel slow today

> "If the official portal does not load, wait a while and try again later. Never share your registration number on lookalike websites."

Many students check their results in the first few hours after a declaration, and the portal can feel slow under the rush. Students reported the same on previous result days this month, when the [MGR Medical University declared its MBBS results on October 9](/results/tnmgrmu-mbbs-result-2026-out-october-9/). Give it some time, use a stable internet connection, and clear your browser cache if the page fails to load.

The online marksheet is what matters right now. It carries your name, register number, course name, subject-wise marks, total marks, and result status. Go through every line carefully. If your name spelling or registration number looks wrong, contact your college office or the university's examination department at once, so the correction can be recorded.

## What happens after the result

Students who have cleared the exam can move to the next semester or the next stage of their course. Those who are not happy with their marks have limited options here. As per the university's guidelines, revaluation is not allowed at TNMGRMU. Students can apply for re-totalling and request a photocopy of their answer sheets instead. The university is expected to issue separate notifications with the procedure and fee for these requests.

The pattern of result declarations this month suggests more courses could follow. Results for MD Siddha Third Year and BDS Third Year were declared on October 16, BPT Final Year on October 14, and MBBS Second Year and Second Professional on October 9. Students still waiting should keep checking the official results page rather than relying on forwards on social media.

## About the university

The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University, located in Guindy, Chennai, was established in 1987 and is named after the former Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, M. G. Ramachandran. It is recognised by the University Grants Commission and is NAAC accredited. The university conducts examinations and declares [results](/results/) for medical, dental, paramedical, AYUSH, nursing, and allied health science colleges across Tamil Nadu.
