**Rayalaseema University released two semester results on October 8, 2026.** The BPEd and MPEd second-semester results for the August 2026 session are now live on the university's official portal.

If you are a physical education student at this university, your wait is over. The Regular and Supplementary results for both programmes were published together. Here is everything you need to check your score.

## Two physical education results in one day

The university declared the BPEd (Bachelor of Physical Education) and MPEd (Master of Physical Education) second-semester results on the same day. Both cover the examinations held in August 2026:

- **BPEd II Semester (Regular and Supplementary)**, declared October 8, 2026
- **MPEd II Semester (Regular and Supplementary)**, declared October 8, 2026

Students from both batches can now download their provisional marksheets. The Regular results are for students who appeared in the current session, while the Supplementary results are for those who cleared their backlogs this time. Physical education courses follow the same semester pattern as the rest of the university, and the second semester is a key checkpoint in these two-year and one-year programmes.

Rayalaseema University is a state public university in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, established in 2008 and recognised by the UGC. Its results portal also carries the latest announcements for other UG and PG programmes.

## How to check your RUK result in 5 steps

The process is simple. Keep your registration number ready before you start, because the portal will not show your result without it. Like we explained in our <a href="https://ruk.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Rayalaseema University official website</a> walkthrough for other exams, follow the official link only and avoid third-party mirrors.

1. Go to the official website (ruk.ac.in) and open the Results section.
2. Find your course and semester in the list of declared results.
3. Click the result link for your programme (BPEd or MPEd, second semester).
4. Enter your registration number and click to get the result.
5. Your result will appear on the screen. Download the PDF and keep a printout.

## What your marksheet will show

Open the PDF and read it slowly. Your marksheet will carry your name, registration number, course name, and subject-wise marks with codes, along with your total marks and overall result status. Read every line before you close the file.

Names are where mistakes hide most often. A single wrong letter in your name or registration number can cause trouble later, especially when the same records feed into your degree certificate. Match the printed details against your admit card. If a subject you actually attempted shows as absent, note it down and prepare to raise it.

Also check the result date printed on the marksheet. It should match the declared date of October 8, 2026 for these two examinations. The same lesson applies whenever you pull a result. ## If a mark looks wrong, here is what to do

Universities usually open a short revaluation or recounting window after results. If you feel a particular paper was marked unfairly, you can apply for a recheck through the university within the announced window. We explained the general flow in our <a href="/results/gtu-recheck-reassess-result-oct-2026/">GTU recheck and reassessment result</a> explainer, and Rayalaseema University follows a similar process for its programmes.

Do not wait. Correction windows close fast, and the university will not reopen them once the dates pass. Before you apply, confirm the exact last date, fee, and procedure from the official notice on the university website, because these details change from one examination session to the next.

For students who cleared a backlog in the Supplementary attempt, the updated status should reflect in your cumulative record. Keep both the old and new marksheets together so you can show a clean academic record if any college or employer asks.

## What comes next for BPEd and MPEd students

With the second semester done, students now move into the next phase of their programme. Second-semester marks matter when colleges prepare internal records for teaching practice and practical evaluations that carry serious weight in physical education degrees.

Students planning for teaching jobs should know that these results feed directly into their final degree percentage. Keep your downloaded PDFs safe in at least two places: your phone and an email draft to yourself. When the original marksheet arrives at your college, collect it on time and check it against the online copy.
