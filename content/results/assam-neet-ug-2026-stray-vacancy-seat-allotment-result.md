**The last door into an Assam medical college this year is now open.** The Directorate of Medical Education (DME), Assam, has published the provisional seat allotment result for the NEET UG 2026 stray vacancy round on <a href="https://dme.assam.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dme.assam.gov.in</a>. If your name is in the PDF, you have two days — October 25 and 26, 2026 — to walk into your allotted college with your original documents. Miss it, and the seat is gone.

This is the final round of the state's [NEET UG counselling](/results/) for 2026. Only the seats that stayed vacant after the earlier rounds are being allotted now, and the PDF covers MBBS and BDS admissions in government medical and dental colleges of Assam. Candidates who registered for the stray round and locked their choices before the October 19 deadline have been considered for allotment.

## Two days only: the October 25-26 reporting window

The schedule is strict, and the message from DME is clear. Allotted candidates must report in person to the office of the Principal of the allotted college on October 25 or 26, 2026, during office hours. This is where your original documents are verified and your admission is formalised. Students who went through the [WB NEET UG stray vacancy allotment](/results/wb-neet-ug-2026-stray-vacancy-seat-allotment-result/) this week will recognise the same pattern: no online reporting, no extensions. Absence during the two-day period results in seat cancellation.

> A stray round seat is use-it-or-lose-it. Keep your originals ready, reach the college on day one, and pay the admission fee at the counter the same day.

## How to check the Assam NEET UG stray round result

The result is published as a public PDF, so you do not need to log in to find out your allotment. Here is the process:

1. Go to the official portal <a href="https://dme.assam.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">dme.assam.gov.in</a>.
2. Scroll to the Latest section on the homepage.
3. Find the notification for the NEET UG 2026 stray round seat allotment.
4. Click the result or allotment PDF link and download it.
5. Press Ctrl+F and search for your NEET UG roll number, name, or state rank.
6. Note your allotted college, course, category quota, and merit position.

Students with a seat allotted can also log in to the candidate portal to download their individual allotment letter, which you will need at the college.

## Documents you must carry to the college

Arrive with both originals and self-attested photocopies of each of these:

- NEET UG 2026 scorecard
- Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates
- Assam Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC)
- Category certificate, if applicable
- Stray round allotment letter
- Passport-size photographs
- Admission fee, payable at the college counter

Keep the originals in a folder you will not hand over casually, and ask for a receipt for every payment you make at the counter. Reporting days at medical colleges are crowded, so reach early and confirm the exact reporting room with the college office before October 25.

## What comes after this round

Nothing. That is the point of a stray vacancy round. Like the [Rajasthan NEET UG stray vacancy allotment](/results/rajasthan-neet-ug-2026-stray-vacancy-allotment-result-oct-22/) announced earlier this week, this round ends the state counselling for 2026. If you are not allotted a seat, your options for MBBS or BDS this academic year are effectively closed in Assam's government colleges. If you are, the next two days decide everything.

## FAQs

**Q: When was the Assam NEET UG 2026 stray round seat allotment result declared?**
A: The Directorate of Medical Education (DME), Assam, declared the provisional stray vacancy seat allotment result on October 22, 2026, on the official portal dme.assam.gov.in.

**Q: How do I check my Assam NEET UG stray round allotment?**
A: Go to dme.assam.gov.in, open the Latest section on the homepage, and download the NEET UG 2026 stray round seat allotment PDF. Press Ctrl+F and type your NEET UG roll number, name, or state rank to find your allotted college and course.

**Q: What are the reporting dates for the Assam NEET UG stray vacancy round?**
A: Allotted candidates must report in person to the Principal's office of their allotted medical or dental college on October 25 or 26, 2026, during office hours, with original documents and the admission fee. Missing this window leads to seat cancellation.

**Q: Is the stray vacancy round the last round of Assam NEET UG counselling 2026?**
A: Yes. The stray vacancy round is the final stage of the state's NEET UG counselling process. It fills the remaining vacant MBBS and BDS seats in government medical and dental colleges of Assam, and DME holds no further rounds after this.
