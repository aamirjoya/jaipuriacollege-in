**The Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) has released the JTET admit card 2026 today, 15 October 2026.** Candidates who applied for the Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test can now download their hall tickets from the <a href="https://jacexamportal.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JAC exam portal</a>. The exam is scheduled for 1 November 2026.

Your hall ticket is your entry pass to the exam hall. The invigilator will not let you in without a printed copy. If you applied for either the Primary level (Classes 1 to 5) or the Upper Primary level (Classes 6 to 8), download your admit card now. Portal traffic always spikes in the last few days before the exam, so do not leave this for the final evening.

## How to download the JTET admit card 2026

The process takes only a few minutes. Keep your registration or application number ready before you start.

1. Open the <a href="https://jacexamportal.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JAC exam portal</a> on a laptop or phone.
2. Look for the "JHARKHAND TET EXAM - 2026" link in the announcements section.
3. Enter the registration or application details the login page asks for.
4. Submit the form and wait for your hall ticket to appear.
5. Check your name, exam level, exam centre and shift timing with care.
6. Download the PDF and take a clear printout.

If the page does not load, try again after some time or use a different browser. It is the same advice given to students downloading the [MH SET admit card 2026](/results/mh-set-admit-card-2026-out-oct-15/), which also went live today for the October 25 exam.

## JTET 2026 exam date and the two shifts

The Jharkhand Teacher Eligibility Test 2026 will be held on Sunday, 1 November 2026. JAC is running the exam in two shifts. One shift is for Primary-level candidates (Classes 1 to 5) and the other for Upper Primary-level candidates (Classes 6 to 8).

Your admit card is the final word on your shift. It shows your reporting time, your gate number and the exact address of your centre. Read it once on the day you download it and once again the night before the exam.

> Print two copies of your admit card. Keep one with your exam-day documents and one at home as backup.

## What is printed on your hall ticket

Every detail on the admit card matters. Check each line the moment you download it, because corrections take time.

- Your name, application or roll number, photograph and signature
- The exam level you applied for: Primary or Upper Primary
- The exam date, your shift and the reporting time
- Your exam centre name and full address
- The rules you must follow inside the hall

If anything looks wrong, a misspelt name or a wrong exam level, contact the exam authority right away. Do not wait until exam day. For other hall ticket news this week, the [CBSE Class 12 special exam admit card](/results/cbse-class-12-special-exam-west-asia-admit-card-2026/) release followed the same pattern: check early, fix early.

## Exam-day rules every candidate should know

Reach your centre well before the reporting time on your admit card. Gates close before the exam starts, and latecomers are usually not allowed in. Carry your printed hall ticket and an original photo ID, such as Aadhaar or voter ID. A photocopy of your ID will not do.

Leave your mobile phone, smartwatch and any other electronic device at home. Most centres do not have a safe place to keep them. Carry only what the instructions allow: your admit card, your ID and a pen, if permitted. Keep checking the <a href="https://jac.jharkhand.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JAC official website</a> for any last-minute notice about the exam.
