**The UP NEET PG 2026 Round 1 seat allotment result is due today, November 2.** If you filled choices for a state quota postgraduate medical seat in Uttar Pradesh, this is the list that decides which college and course you get in the first round. The Directorate General of Medical Education and Training (DGME), Uttar Pradesh, will publish it on the official counselling portal, and the next step for allotted candidates starts tomorrow.

One point needs to be clear at the start. DGME has not announced a fixed time for the result. The schedule says November 2, and nothing more. So if the link is not live when you first check, it does not mean you missed it. Check again later in the day, and use only the official portal. Fake result links spread fast on result days, and a wrong link can cost you time and personal details.

## UP NEET PG Round 1 result: the dates that matter now

This round covers state quota seats in MD, MS, diploma and DNB courses at government and private medical colleges, institutes and universities across Uttar Pradesh. The [UP NEET PG Round 1 seat allotment result](/results/) follows a short, tight schedule. DGME published the merit list on October 26, and choice filling closed at 5 PM on October 31. Allotment is worked out from your state merit rank, your category, your filled choices, and the seats left in each college and course.

The dates after today matter just as much as the result itself:

- Seat allotment result: November 2, 2026
- Allotment letter download and admission: November 3 to 7, 2026
- Second admission window: November 12 and 13, 2026

Treat November 3 as your working deadline, even though a second window exists. Colleges fill seats round by round, and late reporting only adds risk. Download the letter the moment your allotment shows, and keep a printed copy with you.

## How to check your UP NEET PG Round 1 allotment

Keep your NEET PG roll number and login details ready before you open the portal.

- Go to the <a href="https://upneet.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UP NEET PG counselling portal</a> and look for the Round 1 seat allotment result link.
- Log in with your NEET PG roll number and the details you registered with.
- Open your allotment status and read it fully: college name, course, category, and any reporting instruction shown on screen.
- Download the allotment letter and save a copy on your phone as well as on paper.
- Note the reporting dates on the letter itself. The letter, not a news report, is the final word on where and when you must report.

If your name does not appear in this round, do not panic and do not pay anyone who promises a seat. Wait for the next round notice on the portal. Seats open up when allotted candidates do not join, and DGME will publish the next steps on the same site.

> Download the allotment letter first, then plan travel. A screenshot of the result page is not a substitute for the letter at the college counter.

## Reporting from November 3: what to carry

Allotted candidates must report to the designated medical college or nodal centre and finish admission formalities in the given window. The official counselling brochure lists the documents you must produce in original. Carry them in a folder, in the order the college asks for them, and keep photocopies in a separate folder.

- NEET PG admit card and rank letter
- Allotment letter downloaded from the portal
- MBBS degree and marksheet documents
- Internship completion certificate
- Category certificate, if it applies to you
- Photo ID and recent photographs, as asked in the college notice

Fee payment and document verification also have to be completed within the notified admission period. Pay only through the route given in the official notice, and save every receipt. If a document is missing or a name does not match across papers, the college can hold up admission, so check spellings and dates tonight, not at the counter.

Students in other states are going through the same process this week. The [Jharkhand NEET PG Round 1 allotment](/admissions/jharkhand-neet-pg-2026-round-1-allotment-result-nov-2-reporting-nov-10/) also came out with reporting till November 10, and the [MP NEET PG Round 1 allotment](/admissions/mp-neet-pg-2026-round-1-allotment-result-reporting-oct-30-nov-10/) followed a similar reporting pattern. The rules differ by state, so do not copy another state's dates onto your UP admission.

## If you do not join the seat you get

Some candidates get a college they do not want to join. The brochure says a candidate who does not take admission to the allotted seat in Round 1 may take part in the next round, subject to the counselling rules. That choice has a cost, though. You give up a confirmed seat for a chance in the next round, and there is no promise of a better one.

Read the full notice on the <a href="https://dgme.up.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DGME Uttar Pradesh website</a> before you decide, and check what happens to your security deposit in each case. Talk to a senior or a counsellor if you are unsure.

For now, the job is simple. Check the portal, download the letter if you are allotted, and report between November 3 and 7 with your documents ready. The rest of the round follows from that one step.
