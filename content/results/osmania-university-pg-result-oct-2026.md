**Osmania University has declared its postgraduate results for the 2026 session, and students across four faculties can now check their marks memos online.** The results cover the PG examinations held between July 23 and September 5, and were released by Vice-Chancellor Prof. Kumar Molugaram on Saturday, October 17. If you wrote papers under the Science, Commerce, Arts or Social Sciences faculties, your Osmania University result should now be available through the university's official portal.

OU says the results were processed and announced within 42 days of the last examination. That makes it one of the quicker PG result declarations this season, since many universities take two months or more to finish evaluation.

## What the latest Osmania University result covers

The October 17 announcement is for postgraduate programmes under four of the university's 12 faculties: Science, Commerce, Arts and Social Sciences. So students of courses like M.A., M.Com and M.Sc. are the ones who should check first.

OU releases results faculty by faculty and course by course. If your course notification is not showing yet, keep checking the official portal over the next few days.

The university is one of the oldest in the country. Established in 1918 in Hyderabad, Osmania is a state public university with its own colleges, constituent colleges and a large network of affiliated colleges across Telangana. Its PG results usually draw lakhs of searches every semester.

## How to check your Osmania University PG result online

Keep your hall ticket number ready before you start. The whole process takes a couple of minutes, and there is no separate login or password involved.

- Open the <a href="https://www.osmania.ac.in/examination-results.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Osmania University result portal</a> and look for the examination results list.
- Find your faculty, course and semester in the result notifications.
- Click the link for your course and enter your hall ticket number.
- Submit and view your marks memo on the screen.
- Download the marks memo and save a copy on your phone.

The marks memo shows subject-wise marks, grade points, your SGPA and the overall result status. Check your name, hall ticket number and the subject list for errors right away. If anything looks wrong, report it to your college office or the university's examination branch without delay.

> Keep your hall ticket number handy before you open the portal. Results are listed faculty-wise, so pick the correct course and semester before you search.

## How OU turns your CGPA into a percentage

Osmania University follows the Choice Based Credit System with a 10-point grading scale. The conversion formula students use is:

**Percentage = (CGPA x 10) − 7.5**

So a CGPA of 8.0 works out to 72.5%. Most colleges and employers accept this formula, but the university issues an official conversion certificate if you need one for applications.

To pass, you need at least Grade E, which means 40%. Anything below that counts as a backlog, and you will have to reappear for the paper in a supplementary examination.

## What to do if you want a recheck or revaluation

Not happy with a theory paper score? OU offers a three-step remedy, and the order matters. First comes recounting of marks, then a certified photocopy of the answer script, and finally revaluation.

The indicative fees are Rs. 1,000 per paper for the photocopy and Rs. 500 per paper for revaluation. The university issues a separate notice for each of these, with the application window and the last date. Apply only within that window, and apply only through the official channels the notice mentions.

## What comes next for PG students

Final-year students should download the marks memo right now and keep both a digital and a printed copy. You will need it for Ph.D. applications, NET and JRF forms, and government job applications that ask for PG marks. Most colleges issue the physical marksheet a few weeks after the online declaration, so follow your college's instructions on collection.

If you have a backlog in any paper, watch the examination page for supplementary examination schedules. OU announces those separately, and the application window usually stays open for a short time. Missing the window means waiting for the next cycle.
