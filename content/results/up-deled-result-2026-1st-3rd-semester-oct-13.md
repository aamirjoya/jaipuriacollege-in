**The wait is over for thousands of D.El.Ed trainees in Uttar Pradesh.** The Examination Regulatory Authority, Prayagraj, declared the UP D.El.Ed result 2026 on 13 October. Students who sat for the 1st and 3rd semester exams can now check their subject-wise scorecards online. The results cover five batches, so trainees from several admission years will find their marks in this round.

D.El.Ed stands for Diploma in Elementary Education. In Uttar Pradesh it is still widely known by its older name, BTC or Basic Training Certificate. The two-year course trains teachers for primary schools across the state. A semester result matters a lot here, because trainees need clear semesters to move ahead to teaching practice, final exams, and later teacher recruitment tests.

## Which batches are covered in this result

This round is a big one, because it covers more than one admission year. The 1st and 3rd semester results for the 2022 and 2019 batches are live on the updeledinfo.in portal. Results across the 2023, 2021, and 2018 batches are available on btcresult.in. Clearance marksheets for BTC 2015 candidates are also part of this release.

That mix can confuse students, so read your batch line with care. Open the link that matches your own batch and semester. A 2022-batch trainee and a 2019-batch trainee may be on different portals. If your result does not show up on one portal, try the other official portal before you panic. You can also track other [university result updates](/results/) on this site while you wait for your next semester.

## How to check your UP D.El.Ed result 2026

Keep your roll number ready before you open the portal. The site gets heavy traffic in the first few hours after a result, so a slow page is normal. Try again after a short gap if the page does not load.

- Open the official portal <a href="https://btcresult.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">btcresult.in</a> or <a href="https://updeledinfo.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">updeledinfo.in</a>.
- Pick the link for your batch and semester, such as D.El.Ed 2022 1st and 3rd Semester.
- Enter your roll number. On btcresult.in, you also need your date of birth and the security code shown on the screen.
- Press the search or submit button and wait for your scorecard to load.
- Save the scorecard as a PDF and take a printout for your records.

The steps are the same ones you would follow to [check a university result online](/results/how-to-check-university-result-online/), with one extra detail. On btcresult.in, the date of birth and security code fields are required. A wrong date of birth format is the most common reason the login fails, so type it exactly as it appears in your exam records.

## What to check on your scorecard

Do not close the page after one glance at the pass or fail line. Your scorecard carries details that you will need again, and a small error now can cause real trouble later.

> "A small spelling error in your name can cause big trouble later. Check every line of your scorecard today, not next week."

First, match your name, roll number, and enrollment number with your admit card. Then look at the subject-wise marks, total marks, and pass status. Check the back paper status too, if it applies to you. If anything looks wrong, speak to your college office or the Examination Regulatory Authority at the earliest. Keep a copy of your admit card and your downloaded scorecard with you when you raise the issue.

If you cleared your papers, save a clean printout. You will need it for the next semester, for teaching practice records, and for future verification.

## Second semester exams ended, next steps

While these results came out, the authority was also finishing the 2nd semester exams across the state. The 2nd semester examinations concluded on 14 October. Students who appeared for those papers should watch the same two portals for their results in the coming weeks.

For now, the job is simple. Check your result, save your scorecard, and report any error without delay. This round follows close on the heels of the [BTEUP revaluation result](/results/bteup-revaluation-result-2026-released-oct-12-check-marks/) declared a day earlier. Trainees with back papers should speak to their college about the next exam cycle. Clearing pending papers on time keeps your course on track.
