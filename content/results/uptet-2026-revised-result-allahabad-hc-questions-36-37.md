**Candidates who missed the UPTET 2026 cut-off by one or two marks may still qualify. The Allahabad High Court has ordered the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission to add one mark each for two disputed questions and publish a revised result within a month. The order covers candidates who attempted Questions 36 and 37 in the July 3 second shift paper and were not declared successful. Candidates who already qualified keep their results.**

More than 19 lakh candidates appeared for UPTET 2026. The test decides teacher eligibility for primary classes (1 to 5) and upper primary classes (6 to 8) in Uttar Pradesh. For many of them, two marks is the gap between a pass and a fail.

## What the court ordered

A Division Bench of Justice Saumitra Dayal Singh and Justice Swarupama Chaturvedi passed the order in the commission's appeal, U.P. Education Service Selection Commissioner, Prayagraj v. Vijay Sonkar and Others. The bench partly allowed the appeal and changed the relief given by the single judge.

Questions 36 and 37 of the master set, asked in the July 3 second shift paper, each had two correct answers. The commission had already accepted two options as correct for each question and given full marks to candidates who marked either accepted option. The court said that benefit must now reach every candidate in the same position, not only the ones who went to court.

One mark for each of the two questions goes to every unsuccessful candidate who attempted it, whatever option they marked. A question left blank earns no mark. Results of candidates declared successful on 26 August 2026 stay untouched. The commission must prepare the supplementary result preferably within one month of the order, and it can ask the court for more time if it needs it.

> "Question number 36 and 37 of the Master Set shall be excluded from evaluation, or dealt with in such manner as is consistent with the binding judicial precedent discussed hereinabove."

## Who gains from the order, and who does not

The order helps one group clearly: candidates who sat the July 3 second shift paper, attempted either or both disputed questions, and fell short of the qualifying marks. Two extra marks can push a borderline candidate over the line, and the court said the benefit applies to all such candidates alike.

Two groups get nothing from it. Candidates who left a disputed question blank receive no mark for that question, because the court tied the relief to attempted questions. Candidates who already qualified are also outside it, since the court protected their results after the final result was already declared.

The court also made clear that the relief is not limited to the four writ petitioners who brought the case. It extends uniformly to all candidates in the same position, following earlier rulings in the Ashok Yadav, Ran Vijay Singh and Shimla Singh cases.

## How two questions reached the High Court

The final result and the final answer key came out on 26 August 2026. When candidates studied the key, they found that Questions 36 and 37 each admitted two correct answers. The commission reviewed the objections on its own and gave full marks to candidates who had marked either accepted option.

That did not settle the matter. Four candidates who had marked one of the two correct responses, but not the one the key first showed as correct, went to court. A single judge directed that the two questions be excluded from evaluation or dealt with under binding precedent, and that the benefit reach all similarly placed candidates. The commission appealed. The Division Bench kept the core of that relief, protected the results already declared, and set the one-month window for the supplementary result.

## What to do now

There is no fresh form to fill for the revised result. The commission will prepare it from the existing answer sheets, so candidates only need to watch for the notice. Check the <a href="https://upessc.up.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official UPESSC portal</a> for the supplementary result, and keep your registration number and roll number ready.

If you are preparing for the next cycle instead, the dates are already out. Registration for UPTET 2026-27 closes on 23 December 2026, and the exam is scheduled for 26 and 27 February 2027.
