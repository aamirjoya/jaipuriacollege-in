**Tripura University has declared results for four examinations in its latest batch, and students of nursing, veterinary science, commerce and integrated courses can now check their scores.** The fresh listings on the university's official result portal include the MSc Nursing 4th semester examination 2026, the BVSc and AH 1st year compartmental examination 2026, the M.Com special supplementary examination 2026, and the IMDC 3rd and 5th special examinations 2026.

The [Tripura University result 2026](https://tripurauniv.ac.in/result/university-results) listings sit at the top of the university's result page, with the MSc Nursing 4th semester result dated 16 October 2026. Like most [university results](https://jaipuriacollege.in/results/) released in phases, these are provisional, so students should verify every detail and collect the official marksheet from their department.

## Which exams are covered in this round

The four new results are:

- **Provisional Result of Master of Science in Nursing 4th Semester Examination-2026** (session 2025) — listed with a direct View option, dated 16/10/2026.
- **Provisional Result of Bachelor of Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry 1st Year Compartmental Examination-2026** (session 2026) — also with a direct View link.
- **Provisional Result of M.Com Special Supplementary Examination-2026** (session 2026) — direct View link on the portal.
- **Provisional Result of IMDC 3rd and 5th Special Examinations-2026** — marked Samarth, so it opens through an external link to the university's Samarth portal instead of a direct PDF.

Tripura University, a central university at Suryamaninagar in Agartala, releases its results course-wise in phases. The same page already lists a long line of earlier declarations, from MBBS and BDS professional exams to B.Tech, B.Pharma, MCA and M.Sc supplementary results. If your course is not in this batch, keep checking the portal, because new rows are added regularly.

## How to check your Tripura University result

You do not need a roll number for the first step. The university publishes the full result sheet as a PDF, so anyone can open it and find their name.

1. Open the <a href="https://tripurauniv.ac.in/result/university-results" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Tripura University result portal</a>.
2. Scroll through the examination list and find your exam name and session.
3. Click **View** next to it. The result PDF opens in a new tab.
4. Use Ctrl+F (or find-in-page on your phone) to search for your name or roll number.
5. Download or print the PDF for your records, and confirm that your name, roll number and marks are correct.

For the IMDC 3rd and 5th special examinations, click the **External Link** instead. That takes you to the Samarth portal, where you sign in with your own credentials before viewing your result. If you have never used the Samarth portal, the login is the same one tied to your university registration.

## What to do after you see your marks

First, check the basics. Students often spot spelling mistakes in names or wrong roll numbers only when it is too late. If anything looks off, contact your department or the university's examination controller right away, since the online sheet is provisional.

Compartmental and supplementary candidates should read the whole notification PDF, not just their row of marks. It usually carries the pass criteria and the dates for the next step. Students who were waiting on these marks for further study or a job application can now download the PDF and attach it as proof while the original marksheet is issued.

Students of the integrated IMDC course under the NEP batches should also keep their Samarth login details handy. Results for newer batches are hosted there, and you will need the same account for future semesters.

Tripura University is one of several universities pushing out results this week. The [KSOU result 2026 for MBA and MA courses](/results/ksou-result-2026-mba-ma-out-oct-16/) also went live on the same day, so it is a good time to clear your backlog of pending scorecards.

## Keep the official page as your single source

Fake result PDFs circulate on messaging apps every exam season. Download your scorecard only from tripurauniv.ac.in and trust only the rows the university itself lists. When the next batch of results comes out, it will appear on the same result page at the top, dated and marked provisional, just like this one.
