**If you applied for the Bihar School Librarian Eligibility Test, open your dummy admit card today. The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the Bihar SLET 2026 dummy admit card on November 2, and you have only until November 5 to check every detail on it and fix mistakes.** A small spelling error left uncorrected now can follow you into the final hall ticket and even your result records later.

SLET is the eligibility test for school librarian posts in Bihar. The board puts out a dummy admit card first so that candidates can spot errors in their own application data before the final document is prepared. That is the whole point of this step. It is not a formality to skip, and it is not the card you will take into the exam hall.

## Download it from the official SLET portal only

Go to the official portal, <a href="https://sletregistration.cbrt.co.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sletregistration.cbrt.co.in</a>, and sign in with your user ID and password. Look for the Bihar SLET dummy admit card 2026 link on your dashboard, download the card, and save a copy on your phone as well as on a computer. Keep the confirmation page too after you submit any change, so you have proof of what you corrected and when.

Do not trust a link forwarded in a WhatsApp group or a social media post. If a page asks for money to download the card, close it. The dummy admit card comes through the board portal after login, and no payment is involved in this step.

## Check these details line by line

Your dummy admit card carries your name, roll number, registration number, parents' names, photograph, signature, and reserved category. Read each one slowly, and match it against the details you gave in your application form.

> "A dummy admit card is your last easy chance to fix a mistake. Once the final card is printed, a correction takes far longer, and in some cases it may not be possible at all."

Photographs and signatures cause the most trouble. A blurred photo, a photo of someone else, or a signature that does not match can create a problem at the centre gate. Category details matter just as much. If your reserved category is printed wrong, your eligibility claim in later stages can run into questions. Check the spelling of your name and your parents' names letter by letter. One swapped letter is easy to miss on a quick glance and hard to explain later.

If everything is correct, you do not need to change anything. Just keep the downloaded copy safe and wait for the final admit card. If something is wrong, correct it online through the same login before November 5. Do not wait for the last evening. Portals slow down when thousands of candidates log in at once, and a failed submission on the final day leaves you with no time to try again.

## How to download and correct in 5 steps

- Visit <a href="https://sletregistration.cbrt.co.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official SLET portal</a> and log in with your user ID and password.
- Open the Bihar SLET dummy admit card 2026 link on your dashboard.
- Download the card and check your name, roll number, registration number, parents' names, photograph, signature, and category.
- If you find an error, correct it online and submit the change.
- Download and save the confirmation page for future reference.

## What the exam itself looks like

BSEB will hold SLET 2026 in computer-based test mode. The paper will have 150 multiple-choice questions for 150 marks, one mark per question, with no negative marking. A wrong answer will not cut into your score, so you can attempt every question, but read each one carefully, because careless answers still cost marks.

Careers360's listing puts the test window from November 22 onwards. Treat that as a guide for planning, not as your personal exam date. Your final admit card will carry your exact date, shift, and centre, so do not book travel on the basis of a news listing alone.

## Why this week matters more than it looks

Many candidates treat the dummy card as a simple download. The board treats it differently. It is the stage where your record gets locked in for the next steps. A candidate who corrects a name spelling now saves weeks of follow-up later. A candidate who ignores a wrong photograph may face a long argument at the centre on exam day, when there is no time to fix anything.

Bihar candidates have seen this pattern before. In the recent [Bihar STET 2026 update](/results/bihar-stet-2026-november-4-exam-moved-november-21/), a date change meant revised admit cards, and candidates who checked early caught the change in time. The [results section](/results/) on this site tracks fresh admit card and result updates, so check back for the final SLET hall ticket notice when it comes.

## What to do next

Download the dummy card today, not on November 5. Correct any error in the same sitting, save the confirmation page, and then get back to revision. The correction window is short by design, and the candidates who use the first day of it are the ones who never have to worry about it again.
