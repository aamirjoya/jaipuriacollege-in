**The merit list for MP AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2 is due today, November 19. The Directorate of AYUSH, Madhya Pradesh, will publish it with the vacant seat list and the list of eligible candidates on the official portal. Choice filling starts tomorrow, November 20, and you get only two days.**

After three regular rounds, 1,561 undergraduate AYUSH seats were still vacant in Madhya Pradesh, which is why the state is running stray vacancy rounds. Stray Round 1 finished this week, with admissions on November 18.

## What comes out today

Three lists are due on the MP AYUSH portal today, as per the official time schedule published on November 10:

- The merit list for Stray Vacancy Round 2
- The vacant seat position in government, autonomous and private AYUSH colleges
- The list of eligible candidates

All three lists matter. The merit list shows your rank. The vacant seat list shows which colleges still have seats. Read both before you fill your choices, or you may waste your best options.

## The full Stray Round 2 schedule

| --- | --- |

| Registration closes | November 16, 11:59 PM |
| Document verification at help centres | November 16 to 17 |
| Merit list, vacant seats and eligible candidates | November 19, 2026 |

> "Merit list on November 19. Choice filling on November 20 and 21. College-wise merit list on November 25. Reporting on November 26 and 27."

## How to check the merit list in 5 steps

1. Go to the <a href="https://ayush.mponline.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MP AYUSH portal</a> and open the Stray Vacancy Round 2 section.
2. Look for the merit list, vacant seats and eligible candidates links under the UG courses tab.
3. Open the merit list PDF and search for your name or NEET UG 2026 roll number.
4. Download the vacant seat list and note the colleges where seats are open in your course.
5. Save both PDFs on your phone.

The portal can load slowly on result day because thousands of students check it at once. If the page does not open, wait a few minutes and try again.

## Who can take part in this round

Stray Round 2 is for NEET UG 2026 qualified candidates who want a seat in an undergraduate AYUSH course in Madhya Pradesh. It covers Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS), Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS), Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery (BUMS) and Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences (BNYS).

Only candidates who registered on November 15 and 16 and finished document verification on November 16 or 17 will appear in the merit list. If you missed verification, your name will not be on it.

MP AYUSH counselling fills state quota seats in these courses on NEET scores, in government, autonomous and private colleges. Candidates who applied in the [MP NEET UG special stray vacancy round](/admissions/mp-neet-ug-2026-special-stray-vacancy-round-choice-filling-nov-11/) will know the pattern: short windows, no second chance, and reporting dates that cannot move.

## What to do before choice filling starts

Choice filling opens on November 20 and closes on November 21. Two days is a short window, so prepare your list today.

First, match your merit rank with the vacant seats. A college with no vacant seat in your course cannot give you a seat. Second, put the colleges you want most at the top, and fill only choices you are ready to accept. Third, keep your login details ready for the portal.

Students who registered for [MP AYUSH Stray Round 2](/admissions/mp-ayush-stray-round-2-registration-nov-15-16-2026/) know the drill: the portal gets heavy traffic in the last hours. Fill your choices early on November 20 instead of waiting for November 21.

## What happens after choice filling

The college-wise merit list comes out on November 25. That list shows which college each candidate has been allotted. If you get a seat, report to the college on November 26 or 27 with your original documents and the admission fee. An unclaimed seat can go to the next candidate, so do not miss the reporting dates. Keep checking the [results section](/results/) for the college-wise merit list and the next notice.
