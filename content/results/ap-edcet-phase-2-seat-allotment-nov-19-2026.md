**The wait ends today for thousands of BEd aspirants in Andhra Pradesh. The AP EDCET Phase 2 seat allotment will be released on November 19, 2026, and candidates who get a seat will have only four days, November 20 to 23, to report at their allotted college.**

The Andhra Pradesh State Council for Higher Education (APSCHE) has not announced an exact time for the release. The download link will go live on the Common Admission Portal during the day, and candidates can check it by logging in with their username and password. This is the last and final phase of counselling for BEd admission under the convenor quota, so the stakes are high for everyone still waiting for a seat.

## What Phase 2 covers

Phase 2 covers BEd and BEd Special Education seats for the academic year 2026-27. AP EDCET is the state-level entrance test for BEd admission in Andhra Pradesh, conducted by Acharya Nagarjuna University, Guntur, for APSCHE. Candidates qualify through this test and then take part in web counselling, where they fill college preferences in order and seats are allotted on merit. The Commissionerate of Higher Education issued the Phase 2 notification on November 11, and the whole round moved fast after that. Registration ran from November 12 to 14, certificate verification at helpline centres from November 13 to 15, and web options were filled from November 13 to 15, with one day, November 16, to change them.

Seats are allotted on the basis of AP EDCET rank, category reservation, and the web options each candidate filled. The first-year BEd classes have already started. They began on October 24, right after the [AP EDCET Phase 1 seat allotment](/results/ap-edcet-2026-phase-1-seat-allotment-reporting-oct-24-27/), so students allotted in Phase 2 will join classes already in progress.

> "Phase 2 is the last and final phase of counselling for BEd admission under the convenor quota."

## How to check your allotment

Keep your login details ready before you open the portal. The steps are simple, and the whole check takes a few minutes.

- Go to the Common Admission Portal at <a href="https://cap.apcfss.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cap.apcfss.in</a>.
- Log in with your username and password.
- Click on the AP EDCET Phase 2 seat allotment link.
- Your allotment order will show the college and course allotted to you.
- Download the allotment order and save a copy. You will need it at the time of reporting.

If the link does not open on the first try, wait and try again. The portal gets heavy traffic on result days, and the link may appear later in the day since no release time has been fixed.

## What to do after you get a seat

Getting a seat is only the first step. You must complete both parts of reporting between November 20 and 23, or the seat can slip away.

First, complete self-reporting online on the same portal. Then report in person at the allotted college with your documents. Carry your allotment order, AP EDCET hall ticket and rank card, marksheets and certificates, and the documents you uploaded during certificate verification. APSCHE has said it will not extend the reporting deadline unless it issues a fresh notice, so do not leave it for the last day.

Students who do not get a seat in Phase 2 should not assume the process is over. APSCHE has not said whether a spot round will be held. Keep an eye on the portal and on the [results](/results/) page for any fresh notice.

The Phase 2 round has moved on a tight schedule from start to finish. That leaves little room for delay, but it also means candidates will know where they stand before the week is out.
