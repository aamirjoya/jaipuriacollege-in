**The final result of the [AYUSH NEET UG Stray Vacancy Round 2](/results/) is out. AACCC has put the list on its UG counselling page, and candidates who got a seat must report to their allotted college between 25 and 30 November 2026.**

This round, called SVR-II, is the last step of the central AYUSH undergraduate counselling for 2026-27. It covers BAMS, BHMS, BUMS, BSMS and BPharm seats under the all-India quota. AACCC allotted 123 seats in this round. The provisional list came out on 23 November, and the final list followed on the official portal.

No fresh registration took place for this round. Candidates who had filled choices in Stray Vacancy Round 1 were considered again, and the same choices were used for allotment. That is why there was nothing new to fill. If you did not take part in SVR-I, this round had no opening for you.

## Who this result is for

This result is only for candidates who took part in the central AYUSH UG counselling through AACCC. It covers seats in government, government-aided, central university, national institute and deemed university colleges. State quota seats are handled by state authorities, so this list says nothing about them.

If your name is on the final list, the seat is yours to take, subject to document checks at the college. The provisional list was only a draft. Seats could change between the provisional and final lists, so check the final PDF, not the one from 23 November.

## Dates to keep in mind

| Step | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Provisional result | 23 November 2026 |
| Final result | 24 November 2026 |
| Report to allotted college | 25 to 30 November 2026 |
| Colleges verify joined data | 1 to 2 December 2026 |

Six days is a short window, and the last day will be crowded. Colleges will check your documents in person, so plan the trip now instead of waiting for 30 November.

> Check the final list before you travel. A seat in the provisional list is not a confirmed admission, and the final list is the only one the college will accept.

## How to check the final result

Open the <a href="https://aaccc.gov.in/ug-counselling/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AACCC UG counselling page</a> and look for "Final Result of AACCC UG SVR – 2 2026-27" under Current Events. The list is a PDF. Download it and search for your NEET UG roll number with Ctrl+F.

- Go to the <a href="https://aaccc.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AACCC portal</a>.
- Open the UG Counselling section.
- Click the link for the final result of AACCC UG SVR-2 2026-27.
- Search the PDF for your roll number and note the institute and course.
- Download your allotment letter from the candidate login and keep a copy.

The PDF shows your rank, roll number, allotted institute, course, quota and category. Read each line before you travel. If a detail looks wrong, contact AACCC through the candidate portal instead of going to the college with a wrong letter.

## What to carry when you report

Your college will ask for originals and photocopies. Keep them in one folder before you leave home.

- NEET UG 2026 admit card and scorecard
- Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets and certificates
- Allotment letter downloaded from the candidate login
- A photo ID such as Aadhaar
- Category certificate, if your seat came through a reserved category
- Passport-size photographs

Colleges follow their own reporting rules, and AACCC has published a separate reporting-rules notice for SVR rounds 1 and 2. Read the notice for your college and follow the instructions it gives.

## If your name is missing from the list

A missing name in SVR-II means the central rounds are over for you this year. SVR-II was the last round in the published schedule, and there is no fresh registration after it. State AYUSH counselling rounds may still be running in some states, so check your state portal if you applied there. Keep your documents ready, because state stray rounds also move fast.
