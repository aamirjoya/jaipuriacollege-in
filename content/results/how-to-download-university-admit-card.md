The admit card is your entry pass to the examination hall. Without it, you do not get in, no matter how well you have prepared. Every university has its own portal and its own process, but the broad steps are the same almost everywhere. This guide walks you through them.

## Where the admit card appears

Universities publish admit cards on their official website, usually under an Examination or Student section. Look for a link labelled Admit Card, Hall Ticket, or Download Admit Card. It becomes active only after the university releases it, which is typically one to three weeks before the exams begin.

Always use the university's official website. The address is printed in your admission receipt, your college notice board, or the university's official notifications. Avoid third-party links that claim to offer a direct download, since they can be outdated or fake.

## What you need to log in

The download page will ask for login details. Most university portals ask for a combination of the following:

- Your registration number, enrollment number, or application number
- Your date of birth, or the password you created during registration
- A captcha or verification code shown on the page

Your registration number is usually printed on your fee receipt, your previous marksheet, or the email or SMS the university sent you at admission. If you cannot find it, your college office can give it to you.

## If you forgot your login details

Most portals have a "Forgot Password" or "Forgot Registration Number" option on the login page. It usually sends a reset link or OTP to your registered mobile number or email address. Make sure the phone number you registered with is still active. If the reset does not work, your college office or the university's student helpdesk can look up your credentials after verifying your identity.

## Common problems and quick fixes

A blank download page usually means the link is not live yet or your browser cache is stale. Try a different browser or an incognito window before panicking. If the PDF downloads but looks garbled, download it again; interrupted downloads are common on slow connections during the first few days of release.

If the portal says your record is not found, double-check that you selected the right course and semester, and confirm with your college that your exam form was actually submitted and the fee was paid. A surprising number of "missing admit cards" trace back to an exam form that was never submitted.

## Step-by-step: downloading the admit card

1. Go to your university's official website and open the Examination or Admit Card section.
2. Click the admit card link for your course and semester.
3. Enter your registration or enrollment number and date of birth or password.
4. Fill in the captcha code and submit.
5. Your admit card will open on the screen as a PDF.
6. Download the file and print it on A4 paper. Take at least two clear printouts and keep one as a spare.

If the page does not load on the first day, it may simply be heavy traffic. Wait a few hours and try again before assuming something is wrong with your record.

## What to check on the card

Read every line of the admit card as soon as you download it. Check your name, roll number, registration number, and enrollment number. Make sure your photograph and signature are visible and correct. Then check the exam details: subject names and codes, the date and timing of each paper, and the name and address of your exam centre.

A wrong subject code or an old photograph is easy to miss and hard to fix on exam day. Students who spot errors early get them corrected without stress. Students who spot them at the gate do not get in.

## What to do if something is wrong

If any detail is wrong, if your photograph is missing, or if the admit card does not download at all, act the same day. Start with your college office, since most universities correct student records through the college. For direct correction, contact the university's examination section or the Controller of Examinations through the contact details on the official website.

Keep a written record of your complaint, such as an email or an acknowledgement slip. Do not wait until the day before the exam, when the office is flooded with last-minute requests.

## On exam day: what to carry

Carry the printed admit card and one original government photo ID, such as Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, driving licence, or voter ID. Most exam bodies do not accept a digital copy of the admit card on a phone. Leave mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, and bags at home or in the designated area, and follow the invigilator's instructions inside the hall.

Reach the centre early, at least 45 to 60 minutes before the reporting time. A calm entry is worth more than five extra minutes of revision in the auto-rickshaw.
