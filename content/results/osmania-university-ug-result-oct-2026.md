**Osmania University has declared the results of five degree courses on 7 October 2026. If you appeared for the BA, BBA, BCom, BSc or BSc (Hons) examinations held in July 2026, your score is now available on the university's official website.**

The announcement covers the UG (CBCS) Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog) examinations. Osmania put up a separate result listing for each course, so students should pick the exact listing for their own programme before entering their details.

## Which Osmania University results are out?

The [Osmania University results](/results/) declared on October 7, 2026 cover five undergraduate programmes under the Choice Based Credit System:

- **B.A (CBCS)** — Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog)
- **BBA (CBCS)** — Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog)
- **B.Com (CBCS)** — Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog)
- **B.Sc (CBCS)** — Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog)
- **B.Sc (Hons) (CBCS)** — Sem-II (Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog)

All of these are for the examinations conducted in July 2026. Both regular Sem-II students and Sem-I backlog students check the same listing for their course.

Backlog students should read the result as two separate outcomes. The Sem-II (Regular) columns show this year's marks, while the Sem-I (Backlog) columns show whether the older backlog papers are finally cleared. Students who clear all backlog papers move ahead with their regular batch as per their college's schedule, so confirming the backlog status matters as much as the new marks.

Osmania follows the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) with a 10-point grading scale for these programmes. Your marks memo will show subject-wise marks along with the grade and grade point for each paper, and the semester grade point average. Students moving into higher semesters should keep these memos together, because colleges often ask for the full set during final-year verification.

## How to check your Osmania University result 2026

The university has published the results on its official examination results page. Follow these steps:

1. Go to the official Osmania University website at <a href="https://www.osmania.ac.in/examination-results.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">osmania.ac.in/examination-results.php</a>.
2. Find the examination results section and look for your course listing, for example "UG(CBCS) B.Com Sem-II(Regular) and Sem-I (Backlog) Examinations July-2026 Results".
3. Click the result link next to your course.
4. Enter your hall ticket number and other details the page asks for.
5. Submit the form and view your result on the screen.
6. Download the result and take a printout for your records.

## What to check on your scorecard

Once the result opens, look at every detail with care. Your name, hall ticket number, course name, semester and subject-wise marks should all be correct. [University results](/results/calicut-university-result-oct-2026/) from the last few days show how common small name or mark errors can be, so do not skip this step.

If anything looks wrong — a spelling mistake in your name, a missing subject, or marks that do not match what you expected — contact your college examination office or the university's examination branch at once. Do not wait for the college to notice it.

## Revaluation, memos and next steps

The university has not announced revaluation, recounting or memo distribution dates along with this result. If you want your answer scripts rechecked, keep watching the official website and your college notice board for a separate notification. Revaluation applications at most universities come with a short window and a per-paper fee, so act quickly once the notice appears rather than waiting for a reminder.

The online result is a provisional scorecard. The university will send the original marks memos to colleges later, and your college will tell you when to collect them. Until then, save the downloaded copy safely.

One warning that students should take seriously: trust only the official portal and your college for result-related information. Messages on social media and WhatsApp about result corrections, fees or revaluation dates are often fake.
