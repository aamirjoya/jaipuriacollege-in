**Savitribai Phule Pune University has released three results today, 23 November 2026. BHMCT third year, BHMCT fourth year and second year M.Sc Mathematics students can now check their marks on the official results portal.**

All three entries sit at the top of the university's live results list, each dated 23 November 2026. The [SPPU result](/results/) for these courses is available online only, so students do not need to visit the university office or wait for a printed list at the college.

## Three results declared on 23 November

| Course | Pattern | Session | Result date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Third Year BHMCT | 2019 Pattern | Summer Session 2026 (N+2+1) | 23 November 2026 |
| Fourth Year BHMCT | 2019 Pattern | Summer Session 2026 (N+2+1) | 23 November 2026 |
| Second Year M.Sc Mathematics | 2019 Pattern | Winter Session 2025 (N+2+1) | 23 November 2026 |

The two BHMCT results cover the Summer Session 2026 exams. The M.Sc Mathematics result is for the Winter Session 2025 exams, which is the session label the university portal uses for that course.

> The three new results are the first entries on the university's results dashboard today, dated 23 November 2026.

## How to check your SPPU result

The university puts the results on its online dashboard. Keep your seat number or PRN ready before you start.

- Open <a href="https://onlineresults.unipune.ac.in/Result/Dashboard/Default" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the official SPPU results dashboard</a>.
- Find your course in the list of released results. Look for the exact year and pattern, since the portal lists many courses with similar names.
- Select the matching pattern and session, then enter the details the portal asks for.
- Submit and check your result on screen. Save a copy or take a printout for your records.

The portal lists results by course, pattern and session. Picking the wrong session is the most common reason a result does not show up, so match all three fields with your exam before you search.

## What the pattern and session labels mean

The 2019 Pattern tag refers to the syllabus and exam pattern under which the course runs. Students who joined under a newer pattern will see a different label, such as a 2023 or 2025 Pattern, for their own course.

Summer Session 2026 means the exams held in the summer session of this year. Winter Session 2025 means the exams held in the winter session that started in late 2025. The portal also prints an (N+2+1) tag next to these three results. The listing itself does not explain that tag, so students who need its exact meaning should check the university circulars.

## What this batch means for final-year students

The fourth year BHMCT result is the last one for final-year hotel management students. Many of them need it for job applications, campus placement rounds and higher study forms. With the result now online, they can download their marks and move ahead instead of waiting for the college to put up a list.

Second year M.Sc Mathematics students are in the same spot. The result closes the Winter Session 2025 exams for them and clears the way for the next semester.

This is how SPPU works through the year. Results come out in batches, course by course, rather than all at once. The university released 11 results in one batch on 4 October 2026, covering BHMCT second and third year, B.Pharmacy, LL.B. and other courses. Today's batch is smaller, with three courses. Students in other courses should keep checking the same dashboard, since new entries appear there first.

The earlier [SPPU B.Ed, B.P.Ed and M.Ed results](/results/sppu-bed-bped-med-result-2026/) came out in a similar batch on 1 October 2026. The [Mumbai University result](/results/mumbai-university-result-nov-2026-tybcom-mcom-bsc-it-out/) followed the same pattern last week, with seven result PDFs across two days.

## If your result is not showing

First, confirm you selected the right course, pattern and session. The portal holds hundreds of entries, and two courses can share a name while differing in year or pattern.

Second, refresh the page or try again later. The portal gets heavy traffic in the first hours after a release, and a slow page does not mean your result is missing.

Third, if the result still does not appear, contact your college examination section or the SPPU examination department. They can confirm whether your result was held back or whether the entry sits under a different session label.

The online result is for quick reference. Collect your original marksheet or grade card from your college when it is ready, and report any error in the online result to your college examination section.
