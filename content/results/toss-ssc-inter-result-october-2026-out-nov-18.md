**The Telangana Open School Society (TOSS) has declared the [TOSS SSC and Inter results 2026](/results/) for the October session, and students can check their marks online from today, November 18.**

The results cover the SSC (Class 10) and Intermediate (Class 12) supplementary exams held from October 11 to 17, 2026. Students who sat for either exam can now see their subject-wise marks and qualifying status on the <a href="https://telanganaopenschool.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official TOSS website</a>. All you need is your hall ticket number or admission number.

The result came a day after the [RSCIT result 2026](/results/rscit-result-2026-october-exams-out-nov-17/) for the October 4 and 11 exams, which was declared on November 17. Open school students in Telangana had been waiting for this date since the exams ended in mid-October, and the society released both class results together.

## Girls lead in both classes

Girls did better than boys in both exams, and the gap was wider in Intermediate. In the SSC exam, girls recorded a pass rate of 52.57%, while boys stood at 42.65%. Out of 8,095 candidates who appeared for SSC, 3,775 cleared the exam. The overall pass rate for Class 10 was 46.63%.

At the Intermediate level, the overall pass rate was 60.76%. Girls passed at 67.17%, while boys passed at 56.60%. A total of 6,482 candidates passed out of 10,669 who appeared for the Intermediate exam.

> Girls did better than boys in both exams, and the gap was wider in Intermediate, where girls passed at 67.17% against 56.60% for boys.

These numbers come from the result data released with the declaration. If your result shows "pass" against every subject, you have cleared the exam. If it shows a compartment or fail in any subject, you will need to appear for that subject in the next session, as per the society's rules.

## How to check your TOSS result in 5 steps

Keep your hall ticket number ready before you start. The result page asks for it first, and a wrong number will show an error.

- Go to telanganaopenschool.org.
- Open the Results section on the homepage.
- Click the link for TOSS SSC Results 2026 or TOSS Intermediate Results 2026.
- Enter your hall ticket number or admission number and submit.
- Check your subject-wise marks and qualifying status, then download and save the result.

You can also go straight to the <a href="https://portal.telanganaopenschool.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TOSS result portal</a> and pick your class from there. Take a printout of the result page and keep it safe. You will need it for admission to the next class or course until the formal marks memo arrives.

## Marks memo from November 21, recounting till November 28

The web memoranda of marks will be available for download from November 21. That is the document most colleges and study centres will ask for, so download it once it is live and check every detail on it.

If you spot a mistake in your name, marks, or any other detail, report it through your District Educational Officer, headmaster, principal, or AI coordinator by November 30. Do not wait till the last day, because corrections take time to process.

Students who are not happy with their marks can apply for recounting or reverification of their answer scripts between November 21 and November 28. Applications go through the official website or MeeSeva centres. The fees are:

- Recounting: Rs 350 per subject for SSC, Rs 400 per subject for Intermediate.
- Reverification with a photocopy of the evaluated answer script: Rs 1,200 per subject for both classes.

## What to do after you check your result

Passing the SSC exam opens the door to Intermediate admission through the open school system or a regular college. Students who cleared Intermediate can now apply for degree courses, diploma programmes, or entrance exams for the next academic year.

If you did not clear one or more subjects, read your result carefully first. It will show which subjects you need to reappear for. The society announces the next exam session on its website, so keep checking telanganaopenschool.org for the dates instead of relying on forwarded messages.

Students waiting on a university scorecard can also check the [RGUHS result 2026](/results/rguhs-result-2026-mbbs-bsc-nursing-bpharm-ems-out/) for MBBS, BSc Nursing, and BPharm students, which is out this week too. For now, the TOSS result is the one to act on: check it, save it, and note November 21 for your marks memo.
