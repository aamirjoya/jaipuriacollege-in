**Osmania University has released the UG (CBCS) Semester 6 results for the September 2026 exams.** Students of B.Sc, B.Com, BBA and B.A. can now check their marks on the official portal. The results went live on 11 November 2026, and the links are active on the university website.

If you sat for the instant or makeup papers in September, this is the result you have been waiting for. Open the official page, pick your course, and keep your hall ticket number close. The whole check takes a couple of minutes.

## Four courses covered in today's Osmania University results

Today's release covers the UG (CBCS) Semester 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations held in September 2026. Four courses are part of it.

- B.Sc Semester 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations, September 2026
- B.Com Semester 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations, September 2026
- BBA Semester 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations, September 2026
- B.A. Semester 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations, September 2026

These are the instant and makeup exams. Students who had to reappear for a paper or clear a pending subject took these exams in September. If your course is not in this list, your result may come in a separate release. Osmania University puts out results course by course, so check the exact link for your course on the official page.

> The Semester 6 instant/makeup results are live now for B.Sc, B.Com, BBA and B.A. students. Check your marks on the official portal and save a copy before you log out.

## How to check your Osmania University Sem 6 result

Go to the <a href="https://www.osmania.ac.in/examination-results.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Osmania University results page</a> and follow these steps.

1. Open osmania.ac.in/examination-results.php in your browser.
2. Look for the link for your course: B.Sc, B.Com, BBA or B.A. Sem 6 (Inst./Makeup) Examinations, September 2026.
3. Open the result link for your course.
4. Enter the details the page asks for, such as your hall ticket number.
5. Submit and view your result on the screen.
6. Download or print the result and save a copy for your records.

The portal can slow down when thousands of students check at the same time. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Use only the official website. Random links shared on social media or messaging groups may lead to fake pages, so do not enter your hall ticket number anywhere else.

## What to do after you see your result

Read every line of your result carefully. Check your name, hall ticket number, subject names and marks. Small errors do happen, and it is easier to fix them early.

The result you see online is the provisional copy. Your college will tell you when the original marks memo is ready for collection. Keep a printout of the online result with you until then, since you may need it for admission to the next semester or for applying to other courses.

## If your result shows a problem

Some students may find a subject marked absent or a paper they expected to clear still showing as failed. Do not panic. First, match the result with your hall ticket and the papers you actually wrote. If something looks wrong, contact your college office or the university examination branch right away.

Osmania University has not yet announced the dates, fees or process for revaluation or recounting for this release. A separate notice is expected on the official website. Keep checking the <a href="https://www.osmania.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Osmania University website</a> for that notice, and do not pay anyone who claims they can change your marks for a fee.

Students who cleared all their Semester 6 papers can now move ahead with their next step, whether that is the next semester, a postgraduate application, or a job search. Those who still have a backlog should watch for the next supplementary exam notice from the university.
