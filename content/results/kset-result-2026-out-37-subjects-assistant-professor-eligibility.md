**The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has released the KSET Result 2026 on October 26, and candidates can now check their qualifying status on the official portal.** The Karnataka State Eligibility Test, held on October 11, covers 37 subjects. It decides who is eligible to apply for Assistant Professor posts in universities and colleges across Karnataka.

If you sat for the exam, this is the result you have been waiting for. The merit list is live on cetonline.karnataka.gov.in, and your scorecard shows your marks across Paper 1 and Paper 2. Do not rely on screenshots or links shared in WhatsApp groups. The KEA portal is the only place where the genuine list appears.

## How to check your KSET 2026 result in five minutes

Go to the official portal first. The steps are simple, but one wrong click can land you on a fake result page, so type the address yourself instead of following a forwarded link.

- Open the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA portal</a> and find the KSET 2026 section on the homepage
- Click the link for the KSET Result 2026 or the merit list PDF
- Enter your application number and date of birth where the portal asks for them
- Check your name, roll number, subject, and marks in both papers
- Download the scorecard and take a clear printout for your records

If the merit list opens as a PDF, use the search option in your browser or PDF reader to find your roll number. Save a copy on your phone and on your computer. You will need it again during document verification and whenever you apply for a teaching post.

Students tracking other teaching eligibility exams should also watch the [UGC NET December 2026 registration deadline](/education-news/ugc-net-december-2026-registration-deadline-oct-28/), which closes on October 28. That exam is the national-level route to the same kind of Assistant Professor eligibility.

## 40% for General, 35% for reserved: what the cut-off really means

KEA has set the minimum aggregate marks at 40% across both papers for General category candidates and 35% for reserved category candidates. These are the marks you must cross to be considered for the eligibility list.

Crossing the mark is necessary, but it is not the whole story. Only about 6% of the candidates who appeared in both papers make the final eligibility list, under the UGC merit rules that govern state eligibility tests. Your subject and category matter here. A score that clears the minimum in one subject may still fall short of the final list in a subject where many candidates scored high.

That is why two candidates with similar marks can get different outcomes. One may find a place on the list and the other may not. Check the merit list for your own subject and category instead of comparing your score with friends from other subjects.

> "The qualifying mark opens the door. The merit list decides who walks through it. Check your own subject list, not someone else's score."

## Document verification is next: keep these papers ready

Candidates who qualify will have to appear for document verification. KEA has not asked candidates to rush anywhere yet, but the smart move is to get your file ready now. Verification usually fails on small things: a missing certificate, a name spelled differently on two documents, or a category certificate that has expired.

Keep these ready:

- A printout of your KSET 2026 result or scorecard
- Your KSET admit card from the October 11 exam
- Your degree and postgraduate degree certificates and marksheets
- Your category certificate, if you claimed a reserved category
- A valid government photo ID that matches the name on your application

Check that the name and date of birth match across every document. If you spot a mismatch, start the correction process with the issuing authority right away. Corrections take time, and a verification desk will not wait for them.

## Qualifying is eligibility, not a job offer

This point trips up many first-time candidates. KSET gives you eligibility. It does not give you a job. You still have to apply for Assistant Professor vacancies as colleges and universities announce them, and each institution runs its own selection process under its own rules.

Think of the result as a certificate that says you meet the standard. It stays useful across future vacancies in Karnataka, as long as you meet the other conditions in each recruitment notice, such as the required degree in the subject. Read every vacancy notice carefully. Some posts ask for a PhD or NET qualification in addition to subject eligibility, and some ask for teaching experience.

If your name is not on the list this time, the result still tells you something useful. Your scorecard shows where you lost marks across the two papers. Use it to plan your next attempt instead of guessing. Many teachers qualify on their second or third try, after fixing the paper where they were weakest.

Check your result only through the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA portal</a>, save your scorecard, and get your documents in order. The list is out today. The next step is yours.
