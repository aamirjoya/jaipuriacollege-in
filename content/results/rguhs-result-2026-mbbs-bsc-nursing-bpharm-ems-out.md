**The Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) has released the 2026 results for MBBS, BHMS, B.Pharm and B.Sc Nursing.** Students across Karnataka can now check their scores and download their marksheets from the university's Examination Management System (EMS) portal. The latest update also covers the B.Sc Nursing main and final-year results that many students had been waiting for.

The results are live on the official portal at [rguhs.karnataka.gov.in/rguresult](https://rguhs.karnataka.gov.in/rguresult). You only need your university registration number to see your marks. The university releases results in batches, so if your course is not listed yet, it should appear in a later round.

## RGUHS result 2026: which courses are out

The results declared so far cover undergraduate and yearly examinations in several medical and paramedical courses. MBBS, BHMS, B.Pharm and B.Sc Nursing are confirmed live, and the B.Sc Nursing main and final-year results are part of this round. Students from affiliated colleges across Karnataka can check their course on the portal.

Some core medical batches were declared earlier as part of the regular evaluation cycle. This round adds the remaining undergraduate results and completes another stage of the university's 2026 result process. The portal's course list is the final word on what is available, so check there rather than trusting a forwarded link.

Health science results have been coming thick and fast this month. Kerala University of Health Sciences also [released its MBBS, BAMS and M.Sc Nursing results](/results/kuhs-result-2026-mbbs-bams-msc-nursing-nov-13/) on 13 November, and [Andhra University put out its UG and PG semester scorecards](/results/andhra-university-result-2026-ug-pg-semester-scorecards-out/) last week. If you study in Karnataka, the [results section](/results/) has the latest updates from universities across India.

## How to check your RGUHS result on the EMS portal

Keep your registration number ready. It is the same number printed on your hall ticket, and the portal will not accept a wrong or incomplete entry.

1. Go to the official EMS result portal at <a href="https://rguhs.karnataka.gov.in/rguresult" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rguhs.karnataka.gov.in/rguresult</a>.
2. Enter your university registration number exactly as it appears on your hall ticket.
3. Pick your course, stream and exam session from the options shown.
4. Click on View Result to see your subject-wise marks and pass status.
5. Download the marksheet PDF and save a clear copy for your records.

The online marksheet shows subject-wise marks, maximum marks for each subject, the minimum marks needed to pass, and practical exam scores. Read every line before you use the marksheet for an admission form, a job application or a higher-studies application. A small spelling mistake in your name or a wrong subject code is easier to fix now than after you have submitted it somewhere.

> Check the name, registration number and subject-wise marks on the downloaded marksheet before you use it for any admission or job form. If anything looks wrong, raise it with your college office at once.

## The portal may be slow, so do not panic

Result days bring heavy traffic, and the RGUHS portal has slowed down on past result days. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Do not pay any website or agent that promises a faster result. The EMS portal is the only official place to check, and the result is free.

Students who find a mistake in their marks can use the university's re-evaluation and answer-script photocopy process. Applications for these services go through the online system, and your college can guide you on the exact steps and the last date. Do not wait too long. These windows close, and late requests are rarely accepted.

## What to do after you see your result

Passing students should keep both a digital and a printed copy of the marksheet. Many admission and job forms ask for it, and the original marksheet from the university can take time to reach your college. Final-year students should also ask their college office about degree certificates and the next steps for registration or internship, where they apply.

Students who did not clear a subject should check the supplementary exam schedule with their college. RGUHS holds supplementary exams for eligible students, and the dates usually appear on the university website and college notice boards soon after the results. Start your preparation now instead of waiting for the official notice.

The 2026 examination cycle is still running, with more course results expected in the coming weeks. Check the EMS portal for your course, and keep your registration number safe until every result you need is in hand.
