**The University of Kashmir has released four UG Professional results on November 26, 2026.** B.Tech and B.E students in the 8th and 4th semesters can check their scores now, and so can BUMS students waiting on the re-evaluation of the 1st Professional exam held in April 2026. All four results are live on the university's official results portal, <a href="https://egov.uok.edu.in/results" target="_blank" rel="noopener">egov.uok.edu.in/results</a>.

The four entries sit together in the UG Professional category on the portal. Each one carries its own notification number, and that number is the surest way to open the right result. The table below lists all four, with the exam session each one covers.

## All four Kashmir University results released on November 26

| Notification No. | Course and examination | Exam session |
|---|---|---|
| 54 | B.Tech/B.E 8th Semester (Regular/Backlog) | July 2025 |
| 05 | Re-evaluation, BUMS 1st Prof | April 2026 |
| 13 | B.Tech/B.E 8th Semester | June-July 2026 |
| 2 | B.Tech 4th Semester | July-August 2026 |

BUMS is the Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery course. The BUMS entry in this round is a re-evaluation result, which means it covers students who had asked for their April 2026 exam papers to be checked again. The other three entries are fresh results for engineering students.

## Two B.Tech 8th semester results are live, so open the right one

This is the part where students slip up. The portal lists two separate B.Tech/B.E 8th Semester results, both dated November 26. They look the same at first glance, but they belong to different exam sessions.

Notification No. 54 covers the Regular/Backlog examination held in July 2025. Notification No. 13 covers the examination held in June-July 2026. If you open the wrong one, your roll number will not show up, and you may think your result is missing when it is sitting in the other list.

Before you click, match two things with your own exam: the notification number and the session. The 4th semester result is easier to spot. It is the only B.Tech 4th Semester entry in this round, listed as Notification No. 2 for the July-August 2026 session.

## How to check your Kashmir University result

The portal keeps it simple. There is no separate login page to hunt for. Open the official results page and work from the list.

1. Go to <a href="https://egov.uok.edu.in/results" target="_blank" rel="noopener">egov.uok.edu.in/results</a>.
2. Find the notification that matches your course, semester and exam session.
3. Check the notification number once more, then click View Result beside it.
4. Enter the details the page asks for and open your result.
5. Save the PDF or take a printout for your records.

Use only the official portal. Result links shared on WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels often point to old lists or wrong pages, and the university has said nothing about any other channel for these results. If the page loads slowly, wait and try again instead of refreshing again and again.

## What to do after you see your result

Read the whole marksheet before you close it. Check your name, roll number and the marks in each subject. A small spelling error in your name is easier to fix now than when the final marksheet reaches your college.

If your result shows a backlog, the next step is the supplementary or backlog examination. The university posts the schedule for those exams as a separate notice, so keep an eye on the official website. Students who cleared everything should watch for the notice on marksheet distribution, since the online result is provisional and the printed marksheet comes later.

The university has been releasing results in batches through November. Earlier this month it put out the [BG 4th semester result](/results/kashmir-university-bg-4th-semester-nep-result-2026-batch-2024/) for the 2024 batch, and the B.Tech 7th and 8th semester supplementary results came out on November 20. Students tracking [other university results](/results/) this week can also check the [Calicut University revaluation result](/results/calicut-university-bhm-revaluation-result-nov-25-2026/), which came out on November 25. For anything about your own result, the university website at <a href="https://www.kashmiruniversity.net" target="_blank" rel="noopener">kashmiruniversity.net</a> is the place to look.
