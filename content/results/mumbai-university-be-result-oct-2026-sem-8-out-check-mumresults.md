**Mumbai University has released the 8th-semester results for six B.E. branches, and students can now check their scores on the university's official result portal.** The latest list went live on 16 October 2026 for Civil, Computer, and Electrical Engineering, while results for the AI-related branches were published a day earlier. If you appeared for your final semester this year, your scorecard is waiting.

The results are for Semester VIII under the CBCS 'C' Scheme. That means this is the final semester for most students in the batch — the scores on this list decide your degree classification. Keep reading for the full course list, how to check, and what to do if something looks wrong.

## Which branches are out so far

Three batches of B.E. Semester VIII results have been published on the official portal over the past two weeks:

- **Released 16 October 2026:** B.E. Civil Engineering (1T00638), B.E. Computer Engineering (1T00738), B.E. Electrical Engineering (1T00838)
- **Released 15 October 2026:** B.E. CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) (1T01878), B.E. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (1T01828), B.E. Mechatronics Engineering (1T01538)
- **Released 8 October 2026:** B.E. CSE (IoT & Cyber Security) (1T01888), B.E. Mechanical Engineering (1T01438), B.E. Information Technology Engineering (1T01238), B.E. Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (1T01038)

More branches may be added in the coming days. If your branch is not on the list yet, check the portal again in a few days — the university usually uploads results in batches.

## How to check your scorecard on mumresults.in

Go to the official result portal at <a href="https://www.mumresults.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mumresults.in</a>. The portal lists every declared result by program code and examination name, newest first. Look for your course code — for example, 1T00738 for Computer Engineering Sem VIII — and open the result PDF.

Inside the PDF, results are listed by seat number. Find yours and note your marks in each subject. Save a copy of the PDF on your phone or laptop, because students often need it for job applications and higher-study admissions before the official marksheet arrives.

If your result is not opening, try again after some time. The portal can slow down when thousands of students check at once, especially on result day. Confirm that you picked the right course code and semester before assuming something is wrong.

## What to do after seeing your result

First, read the PDF carefully. Match your name, seat number, and course against your hall ticket. Small mismatches in spelling happen more often than students expect, and fixing them gets harder the longer you wait. If anything looks off, tell your college's examination cell right away — they raise correction requests with the university.

Second, remember that the online PDF is for information only. Your official marksheet and degree documents will come from your college separately. Many students need the marksheet for [university results](https://jaipuriacollege.in/results/) related formalities like campus placements and PG applications, so ask your college office when the printed copies are expected.

Third, if you have a backlog or revaluation plan, start on it now. Colleges usually open revaluation applications within days of the result declaration, and the window does not stay open long. Missing it means waiting for the next cycle.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I check the Mumbai University B.E. Sem 8 result 2026?**
On the university's official result portal, mumresults.in. Select your program and semester from the list, then open the result PDF to find your score.

**Which B.E. branches have results declared in October 2026?**
As of 16 October 2026: Civil, Computer, and Electrical Engineering. Results for CSE (AI & ML), AI & ML, and Mechatronics were released on 15 October 2026, and IoT & Cyber Security, Mechanical, IT, and Electronics & Telecommunication on 8 October 2026.

**Is the online result the final marksheet?**
No. The PDF on the portal is for information only. Your college will issue the official marksheet and degree documents separately — collect them from your college office when announced.

**What if my name or marks look wrong in the result PDF?**
Contact your college's examination cell immediately. They can raise the correction request with the university on your behalf. Do not wait for the printed marksheet.
