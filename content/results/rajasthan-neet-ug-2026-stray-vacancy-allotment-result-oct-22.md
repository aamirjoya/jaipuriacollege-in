**The Rajasthan NEET UG Medical and Dental Admission/Counselling Board has declared the stray vacancy round seat allotment result for MBBS and BDS admissions on October 22, 2026. Students who got a seat must download their allotment letter from the official portal and report at the allotted college between October 22 and October 26, 2026.**

The board released the provisional combined merit list a day earlier, on October 21. That list has 1,793 candidates who finished the document verification and deposition formalities. The allotment result now shows which of these candidates got a college seat in this round.

## What has been declared

The stray vacancy round covers MBBS and BDS seats under the Rajasthan state quota. The allotment result PDF is available on the official counselling portal. Candidates can search the list with their registration ID or NEET UG roll number to find their allotted college and course.

The Rajasthan NEET UG stray vacancy round is the last phase of the state counselling. It fills seats that remained vacant after the earlier three rounds. So for students in the merit list, this is the final chance to get a government or private medical college seat through the state quota this year.

A merit list does not mean you have a seat. College allotment is a separate step. It depends on your merit position, your category, the fresh choices you filled, and the seats available in each college.

## Rajasthan NEET UG stray vacancy round: key dates

| Event | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Provisional combined merit list | October 21, 2026 |
| Provisional seat allotment result | October 22, 2026 |
| Allotment letter printing | October 22 to 26, 2026 (till 2 PM on Oct 26) |
| One-year tuition fee payment | October 22 to 26, 2026 (till 5 PM on Oct 26) |
| Reporting at allotted college | October 22 to 26, 2026 (till 8 PM on Oct 26) |

The board has given two different cut-off times on the last day. Print your allotment letter by 2 PM on October 26, and finish the tuition fee payment by 5 PM the same day. Both are part of the admission formalities you must complete before reporting.

## How to check the Rajasthan NEET UG stray vacancy allotment result

- Open the official portal at <a href="https://ug.neetrajasthan.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ug.neetrajasthan.com</a>.
- Go to the Schedule/Notifications section on the homepage.
- Click the link for the stray vacancy round seat allotment result PDF.
- Use the PDF search to find your registration ID, NEET UG roll number, or name.
- Check the allotted college, course, and category against your records.
- Download the PDF and keep a printout ready for the reporting day.

> The stray vacancy round is the final phase of the state counselling. If your name is in the merit list of 1,793 candidates, this is the last shot at an MBBS or BDS seat under the Rajasthan quota this year.

## What to do after you get a seat

Download the allotment letter first. The download window is open from October 22 to October 26, but the last printout must be taken by 2 PM on October 26. Without the allotment letter, the college will not start your admission.

Next, pay the one-year tuition fee shown in your allotment letter. The payment deadline is 5 PM on October 26. Keep the fee receipt safe, because the college will ask for it during reporting.

Report in person at the allotted college before the deadline. Carry your original documents, including the NEET UG 2026 scorecard, Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets, photo ID, domicile and category certificates, and the bond documents you deposited during verification. Candidates who miss the reporting window risk losing the allotted seat.

## Why this round matters for Rajasthan aspirants

Earlier this week, the board also published the provisional state merit list and an updated seat matrix, including BDS management seats at colleges such as Pacific Dental College and Hospital, Udaipur. The stray vacancy round brings the counselling process to a close for the 2026-27 session.

Students who did not get a seat in Rounds 1, 2, or 3 — and newly registered candidates who completed the verification between October 18 and 20 — were eligible for this round. Fresh college choices had to be filled by 11:45 PM on October 21, so the allotment is based only on the choices submitted for this round.
