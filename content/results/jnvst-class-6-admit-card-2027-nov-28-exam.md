**The Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) has released the JNVST Class 6 admit card, and the exam is less than a month away.** Students who registered for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) for Class 6 admission in the 2027-28 session can now download their hall tickets from the official website. The Phase 1 exam for summer-bound Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) is on November 28, 2026, and it will be held offline.

Parents should not leave the download for the last week. A slow website, a forgotten registration number, or a spelling mistake on the hall ticket all take time to fix. Download the card today, check every detail on it, and keep two printouts ready, one for the exam day bag and one at home.

## What the JNVST Class 6 admit card is for

The [JNVST Class 6 admit card](/results/) is the entry pass for the selection test. Without it, a student cannot enter the exam centre. It carries the student's name, registration details, exam centre address, reporting time, and exam day guidelines. The centre printed on the card is the only centre where the student can sit for the test, so read it carefully instead of assuming the nearest school is the venue.

JNVs are residential schools run by the central government, and the selection test is the way into Class 6. The test checks three skills, and the full paper adds up to 80 questions for 100 marks over two hours.

## Download your hall ticket in 5 steps

Go to the official NVS website to get the card. Do not trust forwarded links on WhatsApp or social media, since fake result and admit card pages appear around every big exam. The steps are:

- Open the <a href="https://navodaya.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official NVS website</a> and look for the admit card link on the homepage.
- Click the link for the JNVST Class 6 admit card.
- Enter your registration number and date of birth.
- Click Submit. Your hall ticket will appear on the screen.
- Download it, save a copy on your phone, and take a clear printout.

Students who downloaded the [FMGE admit card](/results/fmge-october-2026-admit-card-oct-27-exam-oct-31/) this week will find the process familiar. The login details are different, but the idea is the same: use the official portal only, and keep the printout safe.

## Check these details the moment you download it

> "A small error on the admit card can cost a student the whole exam day, so the check after downloading matters as much as the download itself."

Match the name and registration details on the card with the application form. Check the exam centre address and the reporting time. If anything looks wrong, contact the authorities through the official NVS website at once. Do not wait till November 27, when offices are hard to reach and corrections may not be possible.

Keep the admit card safe even after the exam. You may need your roll number later, when the result stage begins, in the same way students needed their details to check the recent [NPTEL result](/results/nptel-result-2026-jul-dec-october-batch-scorecard/) scorecards.

## What the Nov 28 paper looks like

The selection test has three sections. Mental Ability carries 40 questions for 50 marks, with 60 minutes for that section. Arithmetic has 20 questions for 25 marks, with 30 minutes. Language also has 20 questions for 25 marks, with 30 minutes. Together, that is 80 objective-type questions for 100 marks in two hours.

Since the paper is offline, students will answer on paper at the centre. Practice with a timer helps here. A student who spends too long on one section can run out of time for the next, and the section-wise time limits make pacing part of the test.

## Exam day: what to carry and what to avoid

Carry a hard copy of the admit card and a government-issued ID. Reach the centre before the reporting time printed on the card. At the gate, students are frisked and asked to show their documents to the checking staff before they enter their halls.

Leave barred items at home. Unfair means during the exam can lead to punishment and cancellation of the paper, and talking during exam hours is also against the rules. A calm, early arrival beats a rushed one. Eat before leaving home, pack the admit card and ID the night before, and plan the route to the centre in advance so a traffic jam does not eat into the reporting time.
