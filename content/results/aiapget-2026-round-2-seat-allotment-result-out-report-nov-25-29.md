**The AYUSH Admissions Central Counselling Committee has released the [AIAPGET 2026 Round 2 seat allotment result](/results/) today, 24 November 2026. If you registered for this round, your allotted college and course are now in the PDF for your stream. You have five days to report, from 25 to 29 November.**

The result covers postgraduate MD and MS seats under the all-India quota in AYUSH colleges across the country. AACCC has put out a separate PDF for each of the four streams, so open the one that matches your course.

AIAPGET is the entrance test for postgraduate AYUSH courses, and AACCC runs the all-India counselling for these seats. State quotas run separately through state authorities, so this result covers only the all-India seats.

## Four PDFs, one for each course

| Course | Result | Reporting dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ayurveda | PDF on aaccc.gov.in | 25 to 29 November 2026 |
| Siddha | PDF on aaccc.gov.in | 25 to 29 November 2026 |
| Unani | PDF on aaccc.gov.in | 25 to 29 November 2026 |
| Homoeopathy | PDF on aaccc.gov.in | 25 to 29 November 2026 |

Each PDF lists the candidates who got a seat in that stream, with the college and course allotted to them. Check the file for your course only. A name in the Ayurveda PDF means nothing if you applied for Homoeopathy.

> Report to your allotted institute between November 25 and 29. That is the window to finish your admission, and missing it can cost you the seat.

## How to check your allotment

The result comes as a PDF, so keep your roll number ready before you open it.

- Open the <a href="https://aaccc.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AACCC portal</a>.
- Go to the PG Counselling section on the home page.
- Find the AIAPGET 2026 Round 2 seat allotment result link.
- Open the PDF for your course.
- Search for your roll number with Ctrl+F and check the college and course.
- Download the PDF and save a copy before you close the page.

The PDF shows your All India Rank, the quota your seat came from, the name of the allotted institute, the postgraduate course, your allotted category and candidate category, and the allotment remarks. Read every line. If any detail looks wrong, note it down before you report, and raise it through the portal.

## This round was rescheduled before it opened

Round 2 did not run on its first dates. The counselling was put on hold on 27 October, and on 17 November [AIAPGET Round 2 counselling](/admissions/aiapget-2026-round-2-counselling-withheld-nov-17/) was still withheld, with no fresh date in sight. AACCC then issued a re-revised schedule. Registration reopened on 20 November and ran till 2 PM on 22 November, with choice filling and locking in the same window. Allotment processing took place on 23 November, and the result is out today. Round 1 allotment had come out on 5 November.

That is why this result matters more than a routine round. Candidates who waited through the hold now have five days to act. If you took part in Round 2, check the PDF today rather than waiting for the last day. The portal slows down when thousands of candidates log in at once, and the last day leaves no room to fix a mistake.

## What to do after you find your name

Start with the college. Your allotted institute will issue its own reporting instructions, and those are the ones you must follow. Save the result PDF, keep your AIAPGET admit card and scorecard, degree certificates and a photo ID in one folder, and carry your originals with a set of photocopies when you report.

Do not trust an allotment list shared on WhatsApp or Telegram. The PDF on aaccc.gov.in is the only list that counts, and a forwarded screenshot can be old, edited or from another round.

## If your name is not in the list

No seat in Round 2 does not end your chances. AACCC has not announced the next round yet, so keep checking the portal for the next schedule. Keep your documents ready so you can register fast when the window opens.

AYUSH counselling is running alongside the medical rounds this week. The [NEET PG Round 2 provisional allotment](/results/neet-pg-round-2-provisional-allotment-out-nov-20-2026/) came out on 20 November, and the [Karnataka UG AYUSH Round 3 final allotment](/results/karnataka-ug-ayush-round-3-final-allotment-result-nov-23-2026/) was published on 23 November. If you are tracking more than one counselling, check each portal separately. One result says nothing about the other.
