**The Staff Selection Commission has released the [SSC CPO admit card 2026](/results/) for Paper 1, and candidates who appear on November 23 can download their hall tickets right now.** The Sub-Inspector exam runs from November 23 to November 28, 2026, and this drive will fill 1,871 posts in Delhi Police and the Central Armed Police Forces.

The admit card is the one paper you cannot enter the exam hall without. It shows your exam date, shift, reporting time, and centre address. Read it the moment you download it, because a wrong centre or shift can cost you the exam.

## 1,871 Sub-Inspector posts across Delhi Police and CAPFs

This drive covers Sub-Inspector posts in Delhi Police and in five central forces: BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB. The CISF list also includes the Sub-Inspector (Fire) post. The total vacancy count is 1,871, and the figure is tentative, so the final number may change slightly when the Commission updates it.

Paper 1 is a computer-based test and the first stage of selection. Candidates who clear it face the Physical Standard Test and the Physical Efficiency Test, then Paper 2, a medical examination, and document verification. Only those who clear every stage make it to the final list.

## Hall tickets are coming out in phases, not all at once

The admit cards are being released in phases. Right now, only candidates whose exam falls on November 23, 2026, can download theirs. If your exam is on November 24 or later, your hall ticket will appear about 2 to 3 days before your exam date. Check your login every day as your exam date gets near, and download the card as soon as it shows up.

> "Download the admit card the day it appears, print two copies, and read the reporting time twice. On exam day, the portal is slow and the queue outside the centre is long."

## How to download the SSC CPO admit card

Get your hall ticket from the official portal in a few steps:

- Go to the official SSC website at <a href="https://ssc.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ssc.gov.in</a>.
- Open the candidate login section on the homepage.
- Enter your registration number or username, your password, and the captcha code shown on the screen.
- Submit the details to open your candidate dashboard.
- Click on the admit card link for the SSC CPO Paper 1 exam.
- Check your name, roll number, exam date, shift, and centre address, then download the PDF and take a printout.

The [SSC GD PET and PST admit card](/results/ssc-gd-pet-pst-admit-card-2026-out-nov-26-physical-test/) and the [SSC JE Paper 1 admit card](/results/ssc-je-paper-1-admit-card-2026-nov-2-exam/) followed the same pattern, with hall tickets opening a few days before each exam date. The CPO release works the same way, so there is no need to panic if your card is not visible yet.

## What to check on your admit card before exam day

Once the PDF is in your hands, check your name, photograph, roll number, exam date, shift, and centre address one by one. They should match your ID proof. If any detail is wrong, contact the SSC regional office for your region at once. Do not wait till the exam morning. A spelling error in the name or a wrong photograph can cause trouble at the entry gate, and the staff there cannot change the card on the spot.

Plan your travel now. If your centre is in another city, book your ticket today and reach the city a day early if you can. Reach the centre at least an hour before the reporting time. Late entry is not allowed.

Carry the printed admit card and a valid photo ID proof such as Aadhaar, voter ID, or passport. Keep a second printout in your bag as a backup. Do not carry a mobile phone, smartwatch, or any electronic gadget inside the hall. These are not allowed and can lead to disqualification.

## What comes after Paper 1

Paper 1 is only the first step, but it decides who moves ahead. The result will come after the exam window closes on November 28. Candidates who clear the cut-off will be called for the physical tests, then Paper 2, and the final merit list comes after the medical examination and document verification. Keep your original certificates ready, from Class 10 and 12 marksheets to caste and domicile certificates, because the verification stage asks for them in original.

For now, the task is simple. Download the card, check the details, print two copies, and plan your travel. The exam starts in three days, and the candidates who sort out the small things early are the ones who walk into the hall calm.
