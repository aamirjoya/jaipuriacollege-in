**The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board has released the [Rajasthan CET 12th Level admit card](/results/) for download today, November 26, 2026.** Candidates appearing for the Common Eligibility Test (Senior Secondary Level) can get their hall ticket from the official recruitment portal, <a href="https://recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in</a>, or through their Rajasthan SSO ID. The exam will be held on December 1, 2 and 3, 2026.

The admit card is a provisional e-admit card. It carries your roll number, exam date, shift, centre address and reporting time, along with your photo and signature. Download it early and take a clear printout, because the portal gets busy as the exam dates come closer.

> "The board will not send provisional admit cards to candidates separately."
>
> RSSB notice on the CET 12th Level admit card

## Key dates for the Rajasthan CET 12th Level exam

| What | Date |
|---|---|
| Exam city slip released | November 21, 2026 |
| Admit card released | November 26, 2026 |
| Exam dates | December 1, 2 and 3, 2026 |
| Exam mode | Offline, two shifts a day |
| Official portal | recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in |

The city intimation slip came out on November 21 and told candidates their exam city in advance. The admit card is different. It gives the exact centre, not just the city. Do not mix up the two documents. The slip alone will not get you into the exam hall.

## How to download the admit card

There are two ways to get your hall ticket. Use the one that works for you.

1. Open <a href="https://recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in</a> and go to the admit card section. Pick CET 2026 12th Level Recruitment, enter your application number and date of birth, fill in the captcha and click Get Admit Card.
2. Log in at <a href="https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/signin" target="_blank" rel="noopener">sso.rajasthan.gov.in</a> with your SSO ID and password. Open the Recruitment Portal on the dashboard, go to the admit card section and select CET 2026 12th Level Recruitment.

Once the hall ticket appears on screen, save the PDF and print it. Keep a second copy at home. If the portal does not load on the first try, wait a few minutes and try again instead of refreshing again and again.

## Check these details the moment you download it

Read every line on the admit card before exam day. Your name spelling, photograph, roll number, exam date, shift and centre address should all match your application. If anything is wrong, raise it with the board through the official website instead of waiting till the last day. A wrong centre address or a wrong shift can cost you the exam.

Carry the printed admit card and a valid photo ID to the centre. The board has said the admit card is a must for entry, and the centre details on it are what you need to plan your travel and reporting time. Reach the centre early. The reporting time printed on your hall ticket is the time you must be there, not the time the exam starts.

## What the Rajasthan CET is for

The Rajasthan CET Senior Secondary Level is the screening test for direct recruitment to non-gazetted government posts in Rajasthan where Class 12 is the minimum qualification. The board runs it to shortlist candidates, and the scores are then used for recruitment to these posts. If you applied this year, this admit card is your entry pass to the first stage.

Students who are tracking other admit cards this week can also check the [CLAT 2027 admit card](/results/clat-2027-admit-card-out-nov-21-download-exam-dec-6/), which came out on November 21 for the December 6 exam, and the [SSC CPO admit card](/results/ssc-cpo-admit-card-2026-paper-1-out-nov-23-exam/) for the Sub-Inspector exam. For the latest updates from the board, the official site to watch is <a href="https://rssb.rajasthan.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">rssb.rajasthan.gov.in</a>.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was the Rajasthan CET 12th Level admit card 2026 released?

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board released it on November 26, 2026. Candidates can download it from recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in or through their Rajasthan SSO ID.

### When is the Rajasthan CET 12th Level exam 2026?

The exam is scheduled for December 1, 2 and 3, 2026, in offline mode, with two shifts each day. Your admit card shows your exact date, shift and centre.

Your application number and date of birth, or your registered Rajasthan SSO ID and password. Keep both ready before you open the portal.
