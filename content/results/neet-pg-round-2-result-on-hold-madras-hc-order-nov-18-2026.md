**The NEET PG 2026 Round 2 result will not come out today. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has put the seat allotment on hold after the Madras High Court stayed the admission process on November 17.**

Candidates waited for the final allotment list on November 17. It never came. Late that evening, MCC issued a notice saying seat processing for Round 2 had been suspended. The result will be declared only after the committee gets clarification or fresh directions from the court. MCC has not announced a new date.

The hold comes from a case filed by Sri Venkateshwaraa Medical College Hospital and Research Centre against the Government of India and others. The case is registered as Writ Petition No. 46350 of 2026, along with three miscellaneous petitions. On November 17, the Madras High Court passed an interim order in this case.

## What the court order says

The court ordered an interim stay on all further steps in the admission process. The stay also covers the joining of students through MCC and the Centralised Admission Committee (CENTAC). The order, quoted in the MCC notice, says:

> "In view of the present facts and circumstances of this case, there shall be an order of interim stay on all further proceedings in the process of admission and also the joining of the students by CENTAC and MCC."

Joining of students and collection of fees will stay subject to clarification from the High Court's Division Bench. In plain words, no college can admit a Round 2 candidate and no candidate can join a college until the court says so.

## Reporting dates now on hold

The delay hits the whole Round 2 calendar. Under the earlier schedule, candidates allotted seats under the All India Quota were to report to their colleges between November 18 and 24. Verification of joined candidates was set for November 25. Those dates cannot move ahead while the stay is in force.

MCC issued the hold through Notice No. 10, dated November 17, 2026. The notice tells candidates to keep checking the official website, <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a>, for the next update. Do not trust result links shared on Telegram or WhatsApp groups. The only safe copy of the result will be the one on the MCC website.

The provisional result came out on November 16, as planned. The final list was due the next day, November 17. That final list is the one now stuck. A seat shown in the provisional list can still change, so candidates should not treat it as confirmed. Like other [exam results](/results/) announced this month, the Round 2 list will be published as a PDF that candidates can search with their roll number.

## Round 3 dates still stand, for now

MCC published an updated NEET PG 2026 counselling schedule on November 16. Under it, Round 3 counselling runs from November 26 to December 4. The stray vacancy round for All India Quota seats runs from December 16 to 24. These dates still stand on paper, but they depend on what the court decides next. If the stay drags on, MCC will have to revise the schedule again.

Round 2 carries more weight than usual this year. The National Medical Commission approved [9,783 new PG seats](/education-news/neet-pg-2026-9783-new-seats-total-68305/), taking the total to 68,305. Those seats were added to the Round 2 matrix, which is why so many candidates are watching this result. The allotment covers MD, MS, DNB and postgraduate diploma seats in medical colleges across the country.

## How to check the result when it comes out

When MCC lifts the hold and publishes the final list, follow these steps:

1. Go to the official MCC website at <a href="https://mcc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mcc.nic.in</a>.
2. Open the PG Medical Counselling section.
3. Find the link for the Round 2 final seat allotment result.
4. Open the PDF and search for your roll number with Ctrl+F.
5. Note the allotted college and course, then read the reporting instructions in the same notice.

The MCC website slows down when a big result goes live. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Keep your documents ready in the meantime, because the reporting window may be short once the result is out.

## What candidates should do now

First, do not book travel or pay any fee based on the provisional list. The [NEET PG Round 2 provisional result](/results/neet-pg-round-2-provisional-result-nov-16-final-nov-17/) released on November 16 can still change in the final list. Second, check mcc.nic.in once or twice a day instead of refreshing it all day. Third, keep your original documents ready: allotment letter, NEET PG admit card and scorecard, MBBS degree and marksheets, internship completion certificate, registration certificate, and photo ID.

The wait is hard, and the court case is outside the candidates' control. The result will come only after the court gives its direction. Until then, the safest move is to stay ready and watch the official website.
