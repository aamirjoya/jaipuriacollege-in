**Panjab University has declared eight examination results on 12 October 2026.** Students from B.Voc, M.Com, M.Sc, M.E and Pre-PhD programmes can now check their marks online. The results are published as PDFs on the university's official result portal, and you only need your roll number to find your entry.

## Which Panjab University results are out today

The October 12 release covers both undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Vocational and professional students are included alongside regular degree programmes. This is part of the university's phased result declaration for the 2025-26 cycle.

- **B.Voc. Food Science and Technology**, 4th Semester
- **B.Voc. Medical Lab Technology**, 2nd and 4th Semesters
- **M.E. Electrical Engineering** (Instrumentation and Control), 7th Spell
- **M.Sc. Chemistry**, 2nd Semester Special Chance
- **M.Com**, 4th Semester Golden Chance
- **Pre Ph.D. Fashion and Lifestyle Technology**, April and May 2026 examinations

Open the entry that matches your exact course and exam session. The university lists multiple sessions on the portal, including April/May 2026 and May/June 2026 (Golden Chance), so make sure you pick the right one. ## How to check your Panjab University result 2026

The whole process takes just a few minutes. Keep your admit card or roll number slip ready before you start. You need the <a href="https://results.puexam.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official Panjab University result portal</a>, which is run by the university's examination branch.

1. Go to results.puexam.in in your browser.
2. Choose the right exam session from the dropdown, such as April/May 2026 or May/June 2026 (Golden Chance).
3. Find your examination in the list, for example "B.Voc. (Medical Lab Technology) 4th Semester".
4. Open the result PDF for your course.
5. Search the PDF for your roll number using Ctrl+F on a computer or Find on your phone.
6. Check your name, marks and result status carefully, then download or save the PDF.

Many students will be opening the portal at the same time. If a page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Your result will not disappear.

## What to verify before you close the PDF

Do not stop at just seeing "pass" or "fail". Go through your entry line by line and match every detail with your hall ticket.

- Your name, roll number and registration number should match exactly.
- Subject-wise marks, internal assessment scores and the total should all be present.
- The pass or fail status and any special remark should make sense for your course.

If something looks wrong, a missing subject, a blank column, or a misspelled name, contact your department or the university examination branch right away. The online PDF is provisional. Your original marksheet will be issued separately by the university, and corrections follow the official process. Students waiting for revaluation can watch the <a href="https://puchd.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Panjab University website</a> for the next circulars, which carry the deadline and fee details.

Students from other programmes are still waiting. The university declares results in batches across departments, so keep checking the result portal for your course name over the coming days.

### Which courses had their results declared by Panjab University on 12 October 2026?

### What if my roll number does not show up in the PDF?
