**Adikavi Nannaya University has declared the AKNU UG 2nd Semester Result 2026.** Students of BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA and BVoc from the 2024 AB and 2025 AB batches can now check their marks online. The results cover the semester exams held in April 2026, and the links are live on the university portal.

If you wrote these exams, this is the moment you have been waiting for. Keep your hall ticket number ready before you open the portal, because the page will ask for it along with a captcha. The whole check takes less than a minute when the site is running well.

## AKNU UG 2nd Semester Result 2026: what is out

The university has released the 2nd semester results for all six undergraduate degree courses: BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, BCA and BVoc. Both the 2024 AB and 2025 AB batches are covered. Regular and backlog students who appeared for the April 2026 exams will find their updated marks on the same portal.

Adikavi Nannaya University, based in Rajamahendravaram in Andhra Pradesh, serves affiliated colleges across five districts in the Godavari region. The university was set up in March 2006 and holds a NAAC B+ grade. All its UG and PG results now come through one central portal.

The same day also brought more results. The university released the LLM 2nd Semester (Regular and Backlog) results for the July 2026 exams, along with the PG Instant 3rd Semester and PG Instant 4th Semester results for the September 2026 exams. PG students from those batches can check their marks on the same portal.

## How to check your AKNU UG result in 6 steps

1. Open the <a href="https://aknu.edu.in/results" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official AKNU results portal</a> at aknu.edu.in/results. It is the same portal AKNU uses for all its [university results](/results/).
2. Enter your hall ticket number or registration number exactly as printed on your hall ticket.
3. Type the captcha code shown on the screen.
4. Click Submit or Get Result.
5. Your marks memo will appear with subject-wise marks, total marks, SGPA and result status.
6. Download the PDF or take a printout and keep it safe for admission and scholarship work.

A single wrong digit in the hall ticket number gives you an error, so type it slowly and check it twice before you submit. If the portal feels slow, wait a few minutes and try again. Result days bring heavy traffic, and the site usually settles down within an hour.

> The result you see online is a provisional marks memo. The original marksheet comes later, through your college.

## What your AKNU marks memo shows

Your online marks memo lists your name, hall ticket number, course and semester, and subject-wise marks with internal and external components. It also shows your total marks, grade, SGPA and whether you have passed. AKNU follows a 10-point grading system, and you need at least 40 percent in each subject, including both internal and external parts, to pass that subject.

Check every detail on the memo as soon as it loads. Look at the spelling of your name, your hall ticket number and the marks in each subject. If anything looks wrong, tell your college examination branch right away. Small errors are far easier to fix when you report them early.

## Not happy with your marks? Revaluation and recounting

Students who feel their marks do not match their performance can apply for revaluation (RV) or recounting (RC). The university releases a separate notification for this process shortly after the main results. That notice will carry the application form, the fee per subject and the last date to apply.

Submit the filled form with the fee receipt to your college examination cell before the deadline. The university has not announced the revaluation dates yet, so keep checking the <a href="https://aknu.edu.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AKNU official website</a> for the notice. After revaluation, the revised results appear on the same portal. Marks can go up, go down or stay the same.

## Failed in a subject? Supplementary exams are next

Students who did not pass one or more subjects can appear for the supplementary or backlog exams. These exams let you clear the subject without repeating the whole year. The university will release the supplementary exam notification after the results, and applications go through your college. Hall tickets for the supplementary exams are issued before the exams begin.

The notification usually comes within days of the result, and the window to apply can be short. Check the university website every day this week if you need to apply.

## What to do right after checking your result

Download the provisional marks memo and save a copy on your phone and your computer. Take two printouts and keep them with your other academic papers. You may need the memo for scholarships or any verification your college asks for.

If you passed all subjects, your next semester work starts from here. If you have a backlog, plan for the supplementary exams now instead of waiting for the last date. If you plan to apply for revaluation, note the notification date the moment it appears, because late applications are not accepted.
