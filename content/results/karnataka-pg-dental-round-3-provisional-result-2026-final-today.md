**The Karnataka Examinations Authority has released the third-round provisional seat allotment result for PG Dental admission 2026. The final allotment list will follow today, November 3, after 6 PM, and that final list is the one that decides your admission.**

If you took part in the third round of Karnataka PG Dental counselling for MDS seats, check the provisional list now. It tells you which college and course you have been allotted for now. But remember, this list is provisional. Seats can still change in the final list after KEA looks at the objections raised by candidates.

KEA published the provisional result on November 2, and the authority's own post on the same day invited candidates to raise any objection by 11 AM on November 3. That window closes this morning, so if you spot a mistake in your allotment, do not wait till evening to flag it.

## Final result today, then a tight three-day admission window

The schedule after today moves fast. Candidates must pay the fee, after deducting the caution deposit already paid, and submit their original documents between November 4 and November 6, 2026. The last date to report to the allotted college is November 7, 2026.

That leaves almost no slack. Students who wait till November 7 to arrange documents or travel will be cutting it very close. Get your original certificates, fee receipt, and allotment letter ready as soon as the final list is out today.

> Check the provisional list now, and keep your documents ready. The final list comes out this evening, and the admission window after that is only three days long.

## How to check the KEA PG Dental Round 3 provisional result

Follow these steps to download the provisional allotment list from the official portal:

- Go to the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA portal</a> at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.
- Open the PG Dental section and look for the Round 3 provisional allotment result link.
- Download the allotment PDF and search for your name or rank in the list.
- Note the college and course allotted to you, and compare it with the choices you had filled.
- If anything looks wrong, raise your objection before the 11 AM deadline on November 3.

The provisional list is a PDF, so save a copy on your phone as well as your computer.

## What the provisional list means, and what it does not

A provisional allotment is not a confirmed seat. KEA calls it provisional for a reason. Objections from candidates can lead to corrections, and the final list published today evening is the binding one. Reporting to a college on the basis of the provisional list alone would be a mistake.

At the same time, the provisional list is a strong signal. Most allotments stay the same in the final list unless an error is found. So use today to plan: check the distance to the allotted college, the fee you need to pay after the caution deposit adjustment, and the documents the college will ask for at reporting.

This third round is the mop-up round of Karnataka PG Dental counselling for the 2026-27 session. Option entry for this round closed at 10 AM on November 2, after KEA revised the schedule in late October. Candidates who missed that window cannot join this round now.

## Do not mix this up with KEA's other ongoing rounds

KEA is running several counselling processes at the same time, and it is easy to open the wrong list. The [Karnataka NEET UG special stray vacancy round](/admissions/karnataka-kea-stray-vacancy-191-medical-seats-nov-11-result/) is a separate process for MBBS and BDS seats, with its own result date of November 11. The [Karnataka PGCET 2026 extended round](/admissions/karnataka-pgcet-2026-extended-round-option-entry-nov-4/) covers MBA, MCA, and MTech seats, not dental seats.

If you are a dental candidate, stick to the PG Dental section of the KEA portal. The result you need is labelled PG Dental Round 3, and the [results section](/results/) on this site will carry updates if KEA changes the schedule further.

Do not rely on forwarded PDFs on social media. Fake allotment lists circulate during every counselling round. The only list that counts is the one on <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cetonline.karnataka.gov.in</a>, and the only reporting deadline that counts is November 7.
