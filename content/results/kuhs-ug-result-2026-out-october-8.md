**The Kerala University of Health Sciences released four UG results yesterday, October 8.** Nursing, BHMS and BSMS students who appeared in supplementary and regular examinations can now check their marks online on the university's official portal.

If you were waiting on your result, the wait is over. Here is what came out, who it covers, and exactly how to check it.

## Four results, one update: what KUHS declared on October 8

The KUHS result 2026 notice update covers four separate examinations:

- **Result:** Seventh Semester B.Sc Nursing Degree Supplementary Examinations, August 2026
- **Withheld Result:** First BHMS Supplementary Examinations, April 2026
- **Result:** Final Professional BSMS Degree (2016 Scheme) Supplementary Examinations, September 2026
- **Rank List:** Third (Final) Professional BSMS Degree (2021 Scheme, 2021 Admission) Regular Examinations, July 2026

The biggest group in this update is the B.Sc Nursing students. The seventh semester supplementary result covers students who re-appeared in the August 2026 session after not clearing one or more papers earlier. For BSMS students, this is also a big one — the final professional result decides whether you complete the degree and move toward internship.

> "Students should check the official university notice for their exact course and session before downloading. The result page lists each examination separately, so match your course name and scheme before you open the link."

## How to check your KUHS result on kuhs.ac.in

The university has published everything online. Keep your register number ready — that is the detail the portal usually asks for. Follow these steps:

1. Open the official KUHS website at <a href="https://kuhs.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">kuhs.ac.in</a>.
2. Go to the results section from the main menu.
3. Find the entry that matches your exact examination — for example, "Seventh Semester B.Sc Nursing Degree Supplementary Examinations, August 2026".
4. Open the result link under that title.
5. Enter your register number or the details the page asks for, and submit.
6. Check your name, subject-wise marks and overall status carefully. Save or print a copy.

The KUHS website can run slow right after a result update because thousands of students log in at the same time. If the page does not load, wait a few minutes and try again. Use the official site only — fake result websites often appear on result days, and they sometimes ask for money or personal details.

## What the KUHS result notice means for you

A supplementary result is simple: if it says pass, you move on to the next semester. If it says fail in any paper, check the revaluation or retotaling notice on the university website. KUHS usually publishes instructions for answer-book copies and score sheets along with each result notification, so read the full notice, not just the result list.

For students whose result is listed as **withheld**, there is no need to panic. A withheld result usually means the university needs a document or clarification from your college. The fastest way to resolve it is through your college examination cell, not through random helplines online.
