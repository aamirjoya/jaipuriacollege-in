**The Karnataka Examinations Authority will publish the UG AYUSH 2026 Round 3 final allotment result today, November 23, after 11 AM.** The provisional list came out on November 22, and 2,471 candidates found seats in it. If you took part in this round of counselling, the next few hours will decide which college you join.

This round covers undergraduate AYUSH courses in Karnataka, including BAMS, BSMS, BUMS and BHMS. Only candidates who qualified in NEET UG 2026 could take part. The [Karnataka UG AYUSH Round 3 final allotment result](/results/) will be available on the official KEA portal, and you will need your login details to check it.

## What happened in Round 3 so far

KEA issued the third-round schedule in a notification dated November 18, 2026. Candidates filled fresh options for the seats on offer, and the provisional allotment result was published on November 22 after 4 PM. The provisional list gave 2,471 candidates a seat.

The final result is the list that counts. KEA checks the provisional list for errors and seat changes before it publishes the final version. A few allotments can change between the two lists, so check the final list even if your name was in the provisional one.

Round 3 also brought new seats into play. KEA added 245 BAMS seats across four private Ayurveda colleges in Belagavi, Vijayapura, Dharwad and Hubballi. These seats were part of the fresh option entry for this round, which gave candidates more colleges to pick from.

## What to do after the result comes out

Getting a seat is only the first step. You have three days to finish the formalities, and the last date to report to your college is November 26, 2026. Missing a deadline can cost you the seat.

- Open the <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official KEA website</a> and go to the UGNEET 2026 AYUSH counselling section
- Click the Round 3 final allotment result link and log in with your details
- Check the course and college allotted to you, and download the allotment list
- Pay the prescribed fee and download your seat confirmation slip between November 23 and 25
- Report to the allotted college with your original documents by November 26

Keep a printed copy of the confirmation slip and the fee receipt. Colleges ask for these at the time of reporting, along with your NEET UG 2026 scorecard, admit card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, and category or domicile certificates if they apply to you.

## Why this round matters for AYUSH aspirants

AYUSH counselling in Karnataka runs on the state quota through KEA, while the all-India quota runs separately through the Ayush Admissions Central Counseling Committee. State rounds like this one are where most Karnataka candidates get their seats, and Round 3 is often the last full round before stray vacancy rounds begin.

The 245 new BAMS seats show that the seat matrix is still changing late in the process. Colleges that got approval or added seats late can enter the matrix in later rounds, which is why candidates should keep checking the KEA portal even after a round ends.

If you do not get a seat in the final list, do not give up yet. KEA usually announces stray vacancy rounds after the main rounds close. Watch the portal for the next notice, and keep your documents ready so you can act fast when it comes.
