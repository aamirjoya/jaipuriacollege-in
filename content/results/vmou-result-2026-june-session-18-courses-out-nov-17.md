**Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota, has declared the [VMOU result 2026](/results/) for 18 more programmes of the June 2026 session, and students can check their marks on the official results page now.**

The fresh list carries the date November 17, 2026, and covers the June 2026 exam session, shown as JUNE26 on the results page. BA Part-I students get their result in this batch, along with MBA, MCA, MCom, MA and diploma students. If your course is on the list, your result is live. Open the official page and check it with the details the page asks for.

VMOU is Rajasthan's open university, and its distance learners study from home across the state. For them, the results page is the only place to get the official marksheet. No result is sent by post, so every student has to check online.

## These 18 courses are in the November 17 list

The university has put out results for one undergraduate course, five MA courses, five professional PG courses, five diploma courses and two PG diploma courses. Here is the full list with programme codes.

- Bachelor of Arts (Part-I), code BA-I
- MA Economics (Semester-III), code MAECN-3
- MA Education (Semester-II), code MAEDN-2
- MA English (Final), code MAEG-F
- MA Sanskrit (Semester-II), code MASAN-2
- MA Sociology (Semester-II), code MASON-2
- MBA 2Y (Year-I), code MBA-I
- MBA (Semester-II), code MBAN-2
- MCA (Part-I), code MCAQ-I
- MCom (Semester-II), code MCOMN-2
- MSc Computer Science (Semester-II), code MSCCSN-2
- Diploma in Computer Application (Semester-II), code DCANOL-2
- Diploma in Watershed Management (Semester-I), code DWSMN-1
- Diploma in Watershed Management (Semester-II), code DWSMN-2
- Diploma in Yoga Science, code DYS
- Diploma in Yoga Science (Semester-II), code DYSN-2
- PG Diploma in Computer Application, code PGDCA
- PG Diploma in Guidance and Counselling, code PGDGC

BA Part-I is the only UG course in this batch. That makes this list most useful for first-year BA students and for PG students in MBA, MCA, MCom and MA courses.

> If your course is on the list, your result is already live. Check it on the official page only, and save a copy before you close the tab.

## 29 more courses were declared a day earlier

VMOU did not release all June 2026 results at once. A day before this batch, on November 16, the university listed results for 29 more programmes of the same June 2026 session. That list covers BA Part-II lateral entry, BBA, BCA, BCom, BEd, Journalism, BSc lateral entry and several MA and MSc courses.

So the two batches together cover 47 programmes so far. Results are coming out in lots, and more courses should follow in the coming days. Students whose course is missing from both lists will have to wait for the next lot. BA Part-II and Part-III, for example, are not in the November 17 list.

This is the same week VMOU also declared the [RSCIT result 2026](/results/rscit-result-2026-october-exams-out-nov-17/) for the October 4 and 11 exams. That result came out on November 17 as well, on the separate RKCL portal. Open school students had their own result this week too, with the [TOSS SSC and Inter results](/results/toss-ssc-inter-result-october-2026-out-nov-18/) declared on November 18.

## How to check your VMOU result 2026

Follow these steps on the official page. The page lists every declared result with its programme code and date.

- Open the <a href="https://online.vmou.ac.in/results.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official VMOU results page</a>.
- Find your programme in the declared results list and check that the programme code and the JUNE26 session match your course.
- Open the result link given for your programme.
- Enter the details the page asks for and submit.
- Check your name, programme and subject details on the result that opens.
- Save or print a copy for your records, and keep it safe till the original marksheet arrives.

If any detail on your result looks wrong, report it to the university through its official channels. Do not trust a result link shared on social media or on any other website. The <a href="https://www.vmou.ac.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">VMOU website</a> and its results page are the only safe places to check.

## What to do after you see your result

First, read every line of your result. Match your name and programme code with your admit card. A small spelling error now is easy to fix, but it can cause trouble later at admission or at a job verification.

Second, save a digital copy and take a printout. The online result is the proof of your marks till the university issues the original marksheet. Keep both copies in a safe place.

Third, if your course is not on either list yet, do not panic. VMOU is releasing results in batches, and your course may appear in the next lot. Keep checking the official results page every day instead of waiting for a message from someone else.
