**The Karnataka Examinations Authority has released the KSET 2026 hall ticket today, October 3, 2026. The State Eligibility Test for Assistant Professor posts will be held on October 11, which gives candidates just eight days to download the admit card and wrap up their final preparation.**

This one matters to every teaching aspirant in the state. Passing KSET is a must if you want an Assistant Professor job in Karnataka. Colleges and universities accept either the national UGC NET or the state-level KSET, and for most Karnataka candidates, this test is the more direct route. This year the exam covers 37 subjects.

The hall ticket had been expected a couple of days earlier. Reports say the release was earlier planned for October 1 and was pushed back by two days. It is now live on the official KEA portal, so if you registered, go and download it today instead of leaving it for the last evening.

## KSET 2026: the key facts in one place

Here is everything confirmed about the exam so far:

- **Exam name:** Karnataka State Eligibility Test for Assistant Professor, 2026 (KSET-2026)
- **Conducting body:** Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)
- **Hall ticket released:** October 3, 2026
- **Exam date:** October 11, 2026 (Sunday)
- **Subjects:** 37
- **Papers:** Two: Paper-I (general aptitude on teaching and research) and Paper-II (your chosen subject)
- **Official website:** cetonline.karnataka.gov.in

## How to download the KSET hall ticket 2026 in 6 steps

The admit card is available only online. Follow these steps:

1. Go to the official KEA portal at <a href="https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cetonline.karnataka.gov.in</a>.
2. Look for the KSET 2026 hall ticket or admit card link on the homepage.
3. Click it to reach the login page.
4. Enter your application number and password or date of birth as login details.
5. Your hall ticket will appear on the screen. Check every detail carefully.
6. Download the PDF, take a clear printout, and keep it safe for exam day.

Do not rely on screenshots alone. A printed copy is the safest option because some centres ask for a hard copy at the gate. If you are unsure about the general process, our guide on [how to download your admit card](/results/how-to-download-university-admit-card/) walks through the common steps used by most portals.

## Check these 10 details the moment it downloads

Open the PDF and read it line by line before anything else. Your hall ticket should show:

- Your full name as entered in the application
- Application number or roll number
- Your photograph
- Your signature
- Exam date and exam time
- Exam centre name and complete address
- Reporting time
- The subject and paper details
- Your category, if applicable
- The exam-day instructions printed at the bottom

If anything looks wrong, like a spelling mistake in your name, a blurred photo, or the wrong subject, report it to the KEA at once. Use the contact or helpline section on the official portal. Corrections closer to the exam get harder, not easier.

> "Download the hall ticket today, not on October 10. The day you discover an error is the day you run out of time to fix it."

## One week to go: how to use these eight days

With the exam on October 11, you have one short week. Paper-I tests teaching aptitude, research aptitude, and general awareness, so daily revision of key concepts beats fresh study now. For Paper-II, solve previous years' papers in timed sittings; familiarity with the pattern reduces exam-day panic.

Visit your exam centre a day early if it is new to you. Check the travel time, the entry gate, and where you need to report. A relaxed morning makes a real difference to your first hour in the hall.

Also keep your documents ready tonight: the printed hall ticket, a valid photo ID, and a couple of pens. Pack them in one folder so there is no morning scramble.

## Why KSET matters for your teaching career

Government, aided, and private institutions in Karnataka all require either KSET or NET qualification before they can appoint you as an Assistant Professor. Qualifying gives you an eligibility certificate that holds across all higher education institutions in the state. For candidates who studied in Karnataka and plan to teach here, this one certificate removes the biggest barrier between a postgraduation and a classroom of your own.

Other admit cards are also out this season. The [IGNOU PhD admit card](/results/ignou-phd-admit-card-2026-out/) was released recently for research aspirants, so if you applied to more than one exam, download each one from its own official portal and do not mix them up.
