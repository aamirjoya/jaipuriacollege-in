**The [NEET UG Round 6 result](/results/) is one step from the finish line. The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has released the provisional allotment for this stray vacancy round, and the final result is expected today, 20 November 2026.**

This round is for BDS and BSc Nursing seats only. It is not a fresh MBBS round. Students who took part should check the provisional list now, because the final list comes next, and reporting to the allotted college starts tomorrow, 21 November.

## Provisional allotment is out: check every detail now

MCC has put out the provisional allotment for Round 6. Students who applied in this round can open the PDF on the MCC portal and search for their roll number. The list shows who has been allotted a seat, and in which college and course.

The provisional list is not the last word. MCC gave students time to point out mistakes in it. That window closed at 11:30 am on 19 November. Students had to raise any problem through the Candidates Grievances and Query Redressal System on the candidate login portal. MCC will use those complaints to prepare the final list.

So if your name is on the provisional list, read it line by line. Check the college name, the course, and your own details. If you raised a problem before the deadline, MCC will factor it into the final list. If you did not, the final list will go by the provisional one.

## Final result today: what the schedule says

MCC published the Round 6 schedule in a revised notice, and the dates have held so far. Seat allotment processing took place on 19 November. The result is due today, 20 November.

[Registration for this round](/admissions/neet-ug-round-6-bds-bsc-nursing-registration-nov-17-2026/) opened on 17 November, and choice filling ran over 17 to 19 November. Now only the result and reporting are left. MCC will also share the allotted candidates' data with state counselling authorities today. That matters because states run their own stray vacancy rounds in parallel, and they need the all-India data to weed out students who already have a seat.

> If your name is in the provisional list, keep your documents ready today. The final list is the one that counts, and reporting starts tomorrow.

## Reporting runs from 21 to 28 November

Students who get a seat in the final list must report to the allotted college between 21 and 28 November, up to 5 pm on 28 November. That is the last date to join. Miss it, and the seat can go to another student.

Download the allotment letter from your candidate login once the final result is out. Carry it with your original documents when you report. Colleges check the letter and the documents together before they confirm the admission.

## How to check the NEET UG Round 6 final result

Follow these steps on the official portal. The result comes as a PDF, so keep your roll number ready.

- Open the <a href="https://mcc.nic.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official MCC portal</a>.
- Go to the UG medical counselling section.
- Look for the Round 6 special stray vacancy result link.
- Open the result PDF and search for your roll number with Ctrl+F.
- If you have a seat, log in and download your allotment letter.
- Save a copy of the letter and the result PDF before you close the page.

Do not trust a result link shared on social media or on any other website. The MCC portal is the only safe place to check.

## If your name is not on the list

A missing name in the provisional list does not always mean the end of the road. The final list is prepared after the grievance window, so a few changes are possible. Check the final list today before you give up on this round.

But be honest with yourself too. This is a stray vacancy round, and the number of seats is small. If the final list does not have your name, look at the state stray vacancy rounds instead of waiting for a fresh all-India round. State rounds run in parallel, and your state counselling site will have the dates.

## The PG round is on a different track

MCC is also dealing with a delay on the PG side. The [NEET PG Round 2 result](/results/neet-pg-round-2-result-on-hold-madras-hc-order-nov-18-2026/) is on hold after a Madras High Court order dated 17 November. That hold does not touch this UG round. Round 6 for BDS and BSc Nursing is running on its own schedule, and the result is due today.
