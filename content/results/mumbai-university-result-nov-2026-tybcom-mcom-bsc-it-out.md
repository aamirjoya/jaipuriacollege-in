**The University of Mumbai has released seven semester result PDFs in two days, and final-year BCom students are first in line.** The new results came out on November 20 and 21, 2026, on the official results portal, mumresults.in. They cover TYBCom, MCom, BSc IT, BPharm and two more courses, so students in these courses can now check their marks.

The [Mumbai University result](/results/) update on November 20 brought six programmes in one go. T.Y.B.Com Banking and Insurance Semester VI got two separate PDFs, one of them marked 7 Grade Point. M.Com Semester III, B.Sc Information Technology Semester VI, B.Pharm Semester VIII, B.A. German Studies Semester I and the integrated B.A. M.A. Pali Semester X were in the same batch. Each result is a course-wise PDF, so open the one that matches your programme code and semester.

A day later, on November 21, the result for T.Y.B.Com (Accounting and Finance) Semester VI came out. It follows the CBCGS pattern with a 7 Grade Point scale, and the notice lists its result code as 2C00456. This is the final semester for third-year BCom students, so the list matters for those who plan to apply for jobs or higher studies next.

## How to check your Mumbai University result

The university puts up course-wise PDFs on its results portal, so there is no login and no personal dashboard for these results. You only need your seat number. Follow these steps:

- Open <a href="https://www.mumresults.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mumresults.in</a>, the official University of Mumbai results website.
- Find the result listing dated November 20 or 21, 2026.
- Search for your course name or programme code.
- Open the result PDF for your course and semester.
- Press Ctrl+F, or Find in page on your phone, and type your seat number.
- Check your name, subjects and grades, then download the PDF and save a copy.

Some PDFs also show the PRN, so keep that handy if your seat number does not show up at first. For T.Y.B.Com Banking and Insurance, check both PDFs, since the course has two separate lists.

## What the 7 grade point tag means

A few students get confused by the words CBCGS and 7 Grade Point on the notice, so here is the simple version. CBCGS stands for Choice Based Credit and Grading System, the pattern Mumbai University uses for these courses. The 7 Grade Point tag means the result uses a seven-point grading scale. The notice does not print the full grade table, so use your grade card or the university ordinance if you need the exact grade-to-marks break-up.

## What to do after you see your result

> The online result is only for quick reference. Your college will issue the original marksheet, and that is the document that counts for jobs and admissions.

First, read every line of your result with care. Check the spelling of your name, your seat number, the subject names and the grades. A small error now is easy to fix, and a wrong name on a marksheet can cause trouble later. If anything looks wrong, tell your college examination section right away. The university asks students to report errors to their college or to the examination department.

Second, save a digital copy of the PDF on your phone and in one more place, like your email or a drive. Keep it till your college hands you the original grade card. The PDF on the portal is not the original marksheet, and you will need the original for most formal work.

Third, watch for the next notices. The result notice says nothing about revaluation or verification dates yet. Mumbai University usually puts up those notices separately, so check the portal again in a few days if you plan to apply for revaluation.

## Mumbai University results are coming in batches

This is not the first result round from the university this season. The [Mumbai University MCom Sem 4 result](/results/mumbai-university-mcom-ma-sem4-result-oct-2026/) came out on October 2, covering MCom, MA Economics and Political Science, and the [Mumbai University BE Sem 8 result](/results/mumbai-university-be-sem8-result-oct-2026/) followed on October 5 for EXTC, Electrical and five more branches. The November 20 and 21 lists keep that run going.

If your course is not in this list, do not worry. The university releases results course by course through result season, and new PDFs keep appearing on the portal. Keep your seat number ready and check <a href="https://www.mumresults.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mumresults.in</a> again in a day or two.
