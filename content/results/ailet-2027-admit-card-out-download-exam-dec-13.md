**NLU Delhi has released the AILET 2027 admit card, a week before the date it had first announced. Registered candidates can download the hall ticket from the official website, nationallawuniversitydelhi.in. The download window stays open till 1:30 pm on December 6, 2026. The exam is on Sunday, December 13, from 2 pm to 4 pm.**

The admit card was first scheduled for November 30. NLU Delhi issued a notice on November 23 and opened the download link the next morning, November 24, at 11 am. Students who registered for the All India Law Entrance Test can now see their allotted exam centre on the hall ticket.

AILET is the entrance test for law programmes at National Law University Delhi. It covers the BA LLB (Hons), LLM and PhD courses. The university runs its own test, separate from CLAT, which is used by the other national law universities.

## Key dates to remember

| Event | Date |

The December 6 deadline matters more than it looks. The download link closes at 1:30 pm that day, and the university will not send the hall ticket by post or email. Anyone who waits till the last hour risks a slow portal or a login problem with no time left to fix it.

## How to download the AILET 2027 admit card

1. Go to the <a href="https://nationallawuniversitydelhi.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official NLU Delhi website</a>.
2. Click on the candidate login tab.
3. Log in with your registration ID and password.
4. Click on the AILET admit card tab.
5. Check every detail on the hall ticket, including your name, photo, exam centre and timing.
6. Download the admit card and take a printout.

Check the printed copy as soon as you download it. If your name, photo or exam centre looks wrong, write to ailetadmissions@nludelhi.ac.in or raise it through the AILET portal. Do not wait till exam week to raise a problem.

## What to carry on exam day

A printed copy of the admit card is a must. Entry will not be allowed without it, and a soft copy on a phone does not count. Carry a valid photo ID along with the hall ticket.

Reach your allotted centre at least one hour before the exam begins. That gives you time to find your hall and your seat. Candidates will not be allowed to enter the centre 15 minutes after the exam starts, so plan for traffic and a long queue at the gate.

## If you need a scribe

PwD candidates who need a scribe have a separate deadline. They must update their details and upload the supporting documents through their AILET portal account by December 3, 2026. NLU Delhi has said it will not accept late submissions for scribe requests, so this date cannot be pushed.
