**The Rajasthan JET 2026 second provisional seat allotment list is scheduled for release today, October 28, 2026. Students who took part in the main online counselling for undergraduate agriculture and allied science courses can check their allotment on the official JET counselling portal. If you get a seat in this round, you have only two days to act, because acceptance, fee payment and upward assessment requests all close at midnight on October 30.**

Swami Keshwanand Rajasthan Agricultural University (SKRAU), Bikaner, is running the counselling. The second list follows the first provisional list, which came out on October 21. Candidates who were allotted seats in round one had to finish their formalities by October 23 midnight. Now the process moves to round two, and the same tight window applies again.

## What the second list means for you

A place in the second provisional list means a college and course have been allotted to you for now, based on your rank, your category and the choices you filled. It is called provisional because the seat is yours only after you complete the next steps. Simply seeing your name or roll number in the list does not finish your admission.

Students who did not get a seat in round one, or who asked for upward assessment, should also check this list with care. Upward assessment is the option for candidates who got a seat but want a higher-preference college or course. If a better option opens up in this round, it will show in your updated allotment.

Rajasthan JET fills seats in courses such as B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, Horticulture, Forestry, Fisheries Science, Dairy Technology and Food Technology across state agriculture and veterinary universities in Rajasthan.

| Event | Date |

| Second provisional allotment list | October 28, 2026 |

| Third provisional allotment list | November 2, 2026 |

Keep this table saved on your phone. The gap between the second list and its deadline is just two days, and the portal gets slow when thousands of students log in at the same time. Do not leave the fee payment for the last hour.

## How to check the Rajasthan JET second allotment list

- Open the <a href="https://jetskrau2026.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">official JET counselling portal</a>.
- Go to the JET 2026 counselling or seat allotment section.
- Open the link for the second provisional allotment list.
- Log in with your registered username or registration number and password, if the portal asks for it.
- Check the allotted college, course and category shown against your details.
- Download or print the allotment details and keep them safe till admission is complete.

If the link is not live when you first check, wait a while and try again. Lists are often uploaded during the day, and the portal can be slow right after release.

> In round one, a missed deadline meant the provisional seat offer was cancelled on its own. Treat October 30 midnight as a hard stop, not a soft reminder.

## What to do after you get a seat

Accept the seat first, then pay the fee shown on your dashboard. Both steps sit inside the same October 30 midnight deadline. If you want a better college or course, submit the upward assessment request within the same window instead of sitting idle. You cannot ask for an upgrade after the deadline passes.

Read the instructions on the portal before you pay. The counselling process also carries refund and forfeiture rules for the option form fee, and those rules decide what you get back if you later give up the seat.

Next, get your documents ready for reporting. The schedule puts college reporting on November 5 and 6, up to 5 PM. You will need your original documents at the allotted college, so keep your marksheets, certificates and allotment printout in one folder now. A missing paper on reporting day can hold up your admission even after you have paid the fee.

Students still waiting after round two should watch November 2. That is when the third provisional allotment list is due, with acceptance and fee payment closing on November 4 midnight. The counselling does not end with this round, but each round after this one leaves fewer seats on the table.
